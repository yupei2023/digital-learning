三种小作品怎么做How to make the three small things

三种作品，各三步。规格故意很小：导图 1 个中心 + 3 个分支 · 闪卡 10 张 · 视频 ≤ 2 分钟。第一次做，14 分钟只够做到“能发布”——那就是本周要的。三种都有一个共同的第一步：中心是主修课里的一个概念，不是一章、不是整门课。

Three kinds of artifact, three steps each. The specs are deliberately small: a map with 1 centre + 3 branches · a deck of 10 cards · a video ≤ 2 minutes. A first attempt in 14 minutes gets you to “publishable” — which is all this week asks. All three share step one: the centre is one concept from your core course, not a chapter, not the whole course.

A · 思维导图（类别 3 · 画出来）A · Mind map (category 3 · draw it)

  1. 定中心。主修课里的一个概念，写在中间。写完问自己：它有三个能说出名字的部分吗？说不出，说明选大了或选小了。Set the centre. One concept from the core course, in the middle. Then ask: does it have three parts I can name? If not, it is too big or too small.
  2. 三个分支，每个分支下至少两片叶子。叶子用你自己的话，不抄课程原句；写不出叶子的分支，就是你还没懂的那一块——留着，作品说明里正好写它。Three branches, at least two leaves under each. Leaves in your own words, not the course's sentences; a branch you cannot give leaves to is the part you have not understood yet — keep it, it goes straight into your artifact note.
  3. 导出图片。XMind 桌面版：文件 → 导出 → PNG（免费版带水印，允许）。Freeplane：文件 → 导出 → 图片。幕布：右上角 → 导出 → 图片。手机做的就截全图。Export an image. XMind desktop: File → Export → PNG (the free tier adds a watermark, which is fine). Freeplane: File → Export → image. 幕布: top right → Export → image. On a phone, take a full screenshot.

B · 闪卡组（类别 6 · 考自己）B · Flashcard deck (category 6 · test yourself)

  1. 定范围。主修课里的一组术语或一个概念的十个问题。问在正面，答在背面；一张卡只问一件事。“X 是什么”可以，“X 和 Y 有什么区别、为什么”更好——第 8 周说过，提取越费力，记得越牢。Set the range. One set of terms, or ten questions about one concept, from the core course. Question on the front, answer on the back; one card asks one thing. “What is X” is fine; “how do X and Y differ, and why” is better — as Week 8 said, the harder the retrieval, the stronger the memory.
  2. 做 10 张。Anki 桌面版：添加 → 正面 / 背面 → 添加；不用注册（同步账号是选做，只用邮箱）。下载只从官网 apps.ankiweb.net（待实测）。纸卡也可以：10 张卡片拍照，拼成一张图。Make 10. Anki desktop: Add → Front / Back → Add; no account needed (sync is optional, email only). Download only from the official site apps.ankiweb.net (to verify). Paper cards work too: photograph 10 cards and tile them into one image.
  3. 考自己一次，再导出。合上课程资料，把 10 张过一遍，记下答错的几张——这就是“它帮我学了什么”。导出：Anki 文件 → 导出 → 卡组（.apkg），或者截一张“浏览”界面的图（能看到 10 张的正面）。Test yourself once, then export. Course materials closed, go through all 10 and note which you got wrong — that is your “what it helped me learn”. Export: Anki File → Export → deck (.apkg), or a screenshot of the Browse view showing the 10 fronts.

C · 讲解短视频（类别 1 · 做出来）C · Explainer video (category 1 · make it)

  1. 先写三句提纲。它是什么 · 一个例子 · 最容易搞混的地方。三句写在一页笔记里，录的时候屏幕上就放这页——不露脸，也不用背。Write a three-sentence outline first. What it is · one example · the thing most easily confused. Put the three sentences on one page of notes and keep that page on screen while recording — no face, nothing to memorise.
  2. 录屏 + 旁白，≤ 2 分钟。系统自带录屏（macOS ⇧⌘5 · Windows Win+G · 手机自带录屏）够用；要选“内置麦克风”（微课 2 的示范微评里卡住的那一步）。说错了就从头再录一遍——两分钟，重录比剪辑快。Screen + voice, ≤ 2 minutes. The built-in recorder is enough (macOS ⇧⌘5 · Windows Win+G · your phone's recorder); select the built-in microphone (the step that stuck in micro-lesson 2's model review). If you misspeak, start over — at two minutes, re-recording beats editing.
  3. 回放一遍再发布。听自己讲，标出“我说到这里时不确定”的地方——那就是“它帮我学了什么”。文件通常是 .mp4 或 .mov，几十 MB 以内。Play it back before publishing. Listen to yourself and mark where you sounded unsure — that is your “what it helped me learn”. The file is usually .mp4 or .mov, a few dozen MB at most.

隐私与版权（发布前对一遍）Privacy & copyright (check before publishing)

  • 讲解视频不露脸、不露全名；屏幕上不要出现你的邮箱、他人姓名或学校全名。The video shows no face and no full name; nothing on screen shows your email, others' names, or the full school name.
  • 不用别人的图片、音乐、配音；课程截图只用于说明，不整页复制。No one else's images, music or voice; course screenshots only to illustrate, never a whole page copied.
  • 导图和卡片是你自己的话；AI 生成的整张导图或整组卡不算本单元作品（AI 只能帮改文字）。Map and cards are in your own words; a whole map or deck generated by AI does not count as this module's artifact (AI may only help with wording).
  • 你的网站保持“不被搜索”设置。Your site keeps its no-index setting.
