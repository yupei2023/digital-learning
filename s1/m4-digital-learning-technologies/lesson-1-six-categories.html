金山、铲子和镐头The gold mountain, the spade and the pick

2021 年我在校内开这门课时，写过一句话：互联网就如金山一般，而在线学习工具就像能在金山上帮你挖金子的铲子和镐头。那一年我要求每一类工具“至少熟练掌握三个”。五年过去，我把这句话改了——不是工具越多越好，而是面对一个真实的任务，你认得六类工具各能做什么，然后选一件、用起来。地图的用处不是让你走遍每条路，而是让你知道自己在哪、要去哪。（这个比喻有一处不准：金子挖出来就是金子，而工具“挖”出来的东西——一张导图、一组卡——只有你自己再用它学，才算数。）

When I first taught this course at school in 2021, I wrote: the Internet is like a mountain of gold, and online learning tools are the spades and picks that help you dig. That year I asked for “at least three tools mastered in every category”. Five years on, I have changed the sentence — more tools are not better; what matters is that facing a real task, you recognise what each of the six categories can do, then choose one and use it. A map is not for walking every road; it is for knowing where you are and where you are going. (One place the metaphor fails: gold dug up is simply gold, but what a tool “digs up” — a map, a deck — only counts once you study with it yourself.)

六类，各解决一个学习问题Six categories, six learning problems

这个分类来自 Dabbagh、Marra 和 Howland 的《有意义的在线学习》，2021 年的校本教材也用它。我按“个体学习者”把第 2 类和第 6 类的名字改了——不改内容，只把视角从“老师怎么用”换成“我怎么用”。每类记一句话：它替我解决什么问题。

The classification comes from Dabbagh, Marra and Howland's Meaningful Online Learning, which the 2021 school textbook also used. I have renamed categories 2 and 6 for individual learners — same content, but the viewpoint shifts from “how a teacher uses it” to “how I use it”. Remember one line per category: what problem it solves for me.

同一件工具，可以属于两类——看你拿它做什么One tool can belong to two categories — it depends on what you do with it

分类分的不是工具，是用法。用手机录音把一个概念讲一遍：如果是为了发给别人听，它是第 1 类“做出来”；如果是合上书讲、讲完自己听哪里卡了，它是第 6 类“考自己”。用在线文档给自己列一张术语表：写的过程是第 3 类“画出来”；放在网上随时接着写，是第 2 类“放出去”。所以微评里的“做什么”一栏要写清楚：我拿它对我的任务做了什么，类别才站得住。

The categories sort uses, not tools. Recording yourself explaining a concept on your phone: if it is to send to someone, that is category 1, “make it”; if you explain with the book closed and then listen for where you stumbled, it is category 6, “test yourself”. Listing your course's terms in an online document: the writing is category 3, “draw it”; keeping it online to continue anywhere is category 2, “share it”. That is why the “what it does” line of a micro-review must say what I did with it for my task — only then does the category hold.
