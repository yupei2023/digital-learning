微评四栏模板The four-line micro-review

一则微评 = 四行字，写给下一个要选工具的同学。每栏一句话；“卡在哪”不许空着。三则微评写完，再写一句选择理由。

One micro-review = four lines, written for the next learner choosing a tool. One sentence per line; “what got stuck” may not be blank. After three reviews, write one sentence of reason.

栏Line写Write检查Check
工具 · 类别Tool · category____ · 第 __ 类（1 做出来 · 2 放出去 · 3 画出来 · 4 走进去 · 5 找得回 · 6 考自己）____ · category __ (1 make it · 2 share it · 3 draw it · 4 step into it · 5 find it again · 6 test yourself)免费？只用邮箱？不用 VPN？Free? Email only? No VPN?
做什么What it does我拿它对____（我的任务）做了____。I used it on ____ (my task) to ____.写的是我做的事，不是介绍页。What I did, not the product page.
好在哪What worked试用时真的发生的一件好事：____。One good thing that really happened: ____.不是“好用 / 方便”。Not “good / convenient”.
卡在哪What got stuck具体到那一步：____。（装不上 / 要手机号 / 打不开——都算）Down to the step: ____. (Couldn't install / needs a phone number / wouldn't open — all count)不许空着。当场写。Never blank. Written on the spot.
我会不会继续用Would I keep using it会 / 不会 / 看情况——条件：____。Yes / no / it depends — condition: ____.有半句条件。Half a sentence of condition.
选择理由（三则写完再写）Reason (after all three)
针对____这个任务，我选____，因为____；放弃____，因为____。For the task ____, I chose ____ because ____; I gave up ____ because ____.
两个“因为”后面都是微评里写过的事。Both “because” clauses point to something in the reviews.
