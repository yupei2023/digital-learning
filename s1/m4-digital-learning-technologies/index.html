如此生活三十年，我买了很多很多书。上大学时逛地坛书市，去的时候坐公交车，回来就不得不打车——买得太多，扛不动了。工作以后，我差不多每个月拿三分之一的工资买书，路过书店，本着“贼不走空”的原则，怎么也要带走一两本。终于在一次搬家之前，我鼓足勇气清理了大部分纸质书；朋友们拿走想要的，剩下的当废纸卖了一万多块钱。我的大脑，并没有像那些卷帙浩繁的藏书一样无所不知。我这才深刻地明白：“藏”书不等于“读”书。

For thirty years I bought books — many, many books. As a student I went to the Ditan book fair by bus and came home by taxi, because I had bought more than I could carry. Once I was earning, roughly a third of each month's salary went on books; passing a bookshop, I could not leave empty-handed. Then, before one move, I gathered the courage to clear most of my paper books: friends took what they wanted, and the rest went for scrap — over ten thousand yuan of it. My brain had not become as all-knowing as those shelves. Only then did I truly understand: owning books is not reading books.

学习工具也一样。手机里装了十个学习 App，不等于学会了什么。这两周你只做一件事：围绕你正在学的主修外部课程里的一个真实任务，试用三类工具各一件，写下每一件“好在哪、卡在哪”，然后用其中一件，亲手做出一件小东西——一张思维导图、一组闪卡，或者一段两分钟的讲解视频。工具不是用来“藏”的，是用来给主修课当铲子和镐头的。

Learning tools are the same. Ten study apps on your phone are not ten things learned. These two weeks you do one thing: take one real task from the core external course you are already studying, try one tool from each of three categories, write down what worked and what got stuck for each — and then make one small thing with the tool you chose: a mind map, a flashcard deck, or a two-minute explainer video. Tools are not for collecting; they are the spade and pick you bring to your core course.

核心问题Essential questions

  • 六类数字化学习工具各解决什么学习问题？What does each of the six tool categories do for a learner?
  • 工具越多越好吗？——面对一个真实的学习任务，我怎样做出有理由的选择？Are more tools better? Given a real learning task, how do I choose — and say why?
  • 我能用自己选的工具独立做出一件东西吗？Can I make something with the tool I chose?

学完本单元，你应能By the end of this module you should be able to

  1. ① 说出六类数字化学习工具各自解决的学习问题，并为每一类举出一个自己能用到的免费工具例子。① Name the learning problem each of the six tool categories solves, and give one free tool you could actually use for each.
  2. ② 围绕主修外部课程里的同一个真实学习任务，试用三类工具各一件，各写一则微评（做什么 · 好在哪 · 卡在哪 · 我会不会继续用），并说明最终选哪一件、为什么。② For one real task from your core external course, try one tool from each of three categories and write a micro-review of each (what it does · what worked · what got stuck · would I keep using it), then say which one you chose and why.
  3. ③ 用所选的那一件工具独立做出一件小作品（思维导图 / 闪卡组 / ≤ 2 分钟讲解短视频），发布到个人网站，并用一两句话说明它帮自己学了什么。③ Make one small artifact with the tool you chose (a mind map / a flashcard deck / a ≤ 2-minute explainer video), publish it on your own website, and add one or two sentences on what it helped you learn.

预计用时Time

每周 120 分钟以内，其中 60 分钟是你的主修外部课程——本单元的“真实任务”和作品内容都从这 60 分钟里来。剩下 60 分钟：看/读约 20 · 做约 30 · 反思与日志约 10——工具试用和做作品都记在“做”里，紧接着主修课那一小时做，不占那 60 分钟。标注“选做”的内容不计入。第一次装软件、第一次做作品会比表里写的慢——这是正常的，没有迟交处罚。Within 120 minutes a week, of which 60 minutes are your core external course — this module's “real task” and the artifact's content both come out of those 60 minutes. The other 60: watch & read ~20 · do ~30 · reflect & log ~10 — tool trials and the artifact sit in “do”, done right after the core-course hour, never out of its 60 minutes. Items marked optional don't count. Installing a tool or making an artifact for the first time runs slower than the table says — that is normal, and there is no late penalty.

本单元的 AI 标注：✅ 辅助AI designation for this module: ✅ Assisted
两项任务都允许使用课程认可清单中的 AI 工具辅助文字——例如帮你把微评的句子改通顺、检查作品说明的语句——并在作业结尾附两三句“AI 使用说明”：用了什么工具、它帮了什么忙。完全不用 AI 同样可以拿满分。但请注意：微评里的“卡在哪”必须是你自己试用时真的卡住的地方；任务 2 的作品必须是你自己用所选工具做出来的——AI 生成的导图、卡组或视频不算本单元的作品。认可清单在第 1 周《关于本课程与 AI》页公布。Both tasks allow AI tools from the course's approved list for the writing — e.g., smoothing the sentences of a micro-review or checking the wording of your artifact note — with a two–three-sentence AI use note at the end: which tool, what it helped with. Full marks are equally reachable without AI. But note: the “what got stuck” line must be where you really got stuck, and the Task 2 artifact must be made by you with the tool you chose — a mind map, deck or video generated by AI does not count as this module's artifact. The approved list is on the Week 1 “About this course and AI” page.
