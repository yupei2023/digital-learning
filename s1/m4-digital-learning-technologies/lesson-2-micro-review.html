怎样写一则有用的微评How to write a useful micro-review

微评不是广告，也不是评分。它是写给下一个要选工具的同学看的四行字——让那个人不用再踩一遍你踩过的坑。所以最有价值的一行永远是“卡在哪”。

A micro-review is not an advertisement and not a rating. It is four lines written for the next learner choosing a tool — so that person need not fall into the hole you fell into. That is why the most valuable line is always “what got stuck”.

这个“下一个同学”不是虚指：第 14 周导师会把大家微评里最常见的“卡在哪”匿名汇总给教师，用来修订微课 1 工具表的“注意”列；同一周，导师还会把全班的微评汇成一份「同学们的工具推荐」清单（只写化名、工具名、一句好处、一句卡点）发回给每一个人。这门课没有讨论区、不互访网站，这份清单就是你和同学之间真实的那条线——你写下的那一行，同学与导师都会读到。The “next learner” is not a figure of speech: in Week 14 the mentors collect the most common “what got stuck” lines, anonymised, for the instructor, who uses them to revise the “watch out” column of micro-lesson 1's table; in that same week the mentors also compile the cohort's reviews into one “classmates' tool picks” list — pseudonym, tool name, one benefit, one snag — and send it back to everyone. This course has no forum and no visiting each other's sites, so that list is the real line between you and your classmates: the line you write is read by classmates and mentor alike.

微评 · 系统自带录屏（macOS ⇧⌘5）· 类别 1 做出来Micro-review · built-in screen recorder (macOS ⇧⌘5) · category 1, make it

做什么：对着我的读本第 08 章的大纲，录了一段 90 秒的讲解：为什么“藏书不等于读书”。What it does: with the outline of Reader Ch. 08 on screen, I recorded 90 seconds explaining why owning books is not reading them.

好在哪：不用装、不用注册，按下 ⇧⌘5 三秒后就在录了；录完的文件直接出现在桌面上。What worked: nothing to install, no account; three seconds after ⇧⌘5 it was recording, and the file landed on the desktop when I stopped.

卡在哪：第一遍录完没有声音——麦克风默认是“无”，要在“选项”里选内置麦克风。另外，录完发现自己说了七次“然后”，删不掉，只能重录。What got stuck: the first take had no sound — the microphone defaults to “none” and must be picked under Options. Also, I said “and then” seven times and there is no way to cut it; I had to re-record.

我会不会继续用：会——两分钟以内的讲解够用；超过两分钟、要剪辑，就换 OBS 或别的。Would I keep using it: yes — enough for anything under two minutes; beyond that, or if I need to edit, I would switch to OBS or something else.

注意“卡在哪”那一栏：它救了下一个人两次重录。也注意最后一栏的条件——“两分钟以内”，这就是下一个人判断“我该不该用它”的依据。

Notice the “what got stuck” line: it saves the next person two re-takes. And notice the condition in the last line — “under two minutes” — which is exactly what the next person needs to decide whether this tool is for them.

选择理由：一条句干Your reason: one sentence stem

针对____这个任务，我选____，因为____；放弃____，因为____。For the task ____, I chose ____ because ____; I gave up ____ because ____.

两个“因为”后面都要是微评里写过的事。“因为它好用”不算；“因为它第二天自动只考我答错的 3 张，而导图画完就不会再看”才算。放弃的那件不一定差——只是对这个任务不合适。这就是读本里那句话：选择的本质是放弃。

Both “because” clauses must point to something written in the reviews. “Because it's good” doesn't count; “because it quizzed me the next day only on the 3 cards I got wrong, while the mind map never got looked at again” does. The tool you give up is not necessarily worse — it simply doesn't fit this task. That is the reader's line: to choose is to give up.

三件事，帮你写快一点Three things that make it faster

  1. 试用当场写“卡在哪”——过一天就忘了是哪一步。Write “what got stuck” on the spot — a day later you forget which step it was.
  2. 每栏一句话。四栏加起来比这段文字还长，就是写多了。One sentence per line. If the four lines run longer than this paragraph, you have written too much.
  3. 本单元允许 AI 帮你改句子；但“卡在哪”只有你知道，AI 写不出来。AI may help you polish sentences in this module; but only you know where you got stuck — AI cannot write that line.
