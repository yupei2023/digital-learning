六类工具，各解决什么问题？Six kinds of tools — what does each one solve?

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

“真实任务”是什么？我的主修课这周没有作业。What is a “real task”? My core course has no assignment this week.

不是作业，是你这周学主修课时本来就要做的一件事：记住一组术语、理清一节课的结构、把一个概念给自己讲明白、把课程资料存好以后找得回来。任选一件，写具体：“记住第 3 周的 15 个术语”，而不是“学好这门课”。

Not an assignment — something you would do anyway while studying the course this week: memorise a set of terms, sort out the structure of a lesson, explain one concept to yourself, keep the course materials where you can find them again. Pick one and make it concrete: “remember the 15 terms from week 3”, not “do well in the course”.

工具要注册手机号，怎么办？The tool wants a phone number to sign up.

换一件。本课程的工具规则是：免费、只用邮箱、不用手机号、不用 VPN。微课 1 每类都列了至少一件不注册或只用邮箱的工具；实在没有，就把“要手机号”写进微评的“卡在哪”，换同一类里的另一件——这本身就是一次有理由的选择。

Switch tools. The course rule is: free, email only, no phone number, no VPN. Micro-lesson 1 lists at least one no-account or email-only tool per category; if none works, write “needs a phone number” under “what got stuck” and take another tool from the same category — that is a justified choice in itself.

电脑装不了软件（学校电脑 / 家里没有电脑）。I can't install software (school computer / no computer at home).

六类里每一类都有不用装的选项：浏览器里打开的 PhET、全景网站、在线文档、浏览器书签；手机自带的录屏、录音；甚至纸卡加一张间隔表。“装不上”写进“卡在哪”，然后选不用装的那件。

Every category has a no-install option: PhET and panorama sites in the browser, online documents, browser bookmarks; the screen or voice recorder built into your phone; even paper cards with a spacing table. Write “couldn't install” under “what got stuck”, then choose the one that needs no installing.

两件工具可以是同一类的吗？Can the two tools come from the same category?

不可以——本单元要比的是“不同类的工具对同一个任务各能做什么”，两件同类工具比不出这一点。三件来自三个不同类别，其中至少两件来自能做作品的第 1、3、6 类——第 14 周做作品时，你就在这两件里真正选一件。本周两件里只排进了一件 1、3、6 类的，下周的第三件就从那三类里再挑一类；已经排进两件的，第三件从其余类别里任挑。

No — the module compares what tools of different kinds can do for the same task, which two tools of one kind cannot show. Three tools from three different categories, at least two of them from the artifact categories 1, 3 and 6 — in Week 14 you choose between those two for the artifact. If this week's pair includes only one of categories 1, 3 and 6, take next week's third tool from another of those three; if it already includes two, pick any remaining category.

“用它做 5 分钟真事”是什么意思？What does “5 real minutes” mean?

装好之后，用它对你的真实任务做 5 分钟：真的做 5 张卡、真的画出中心和两个分支、真的录 30 秒讲解。不是看它的介绍页，不是点一遍所有菜单。5 分钟之后你才有资格写“好在哪”和“卡在哪”。

After setting it up, spend 5 minutes using it on your real task: actually make 5 cards, actually draw the centre and two branches, actually record 30 seconds of explanation. Not reading its product page, not clicking through every menu. Only after those 5 minutes can you honestly write “what worked” and “what got stuck”.

我的主修课还没选定 / 想换一门。I haven't chosen my core course / I want to switch.

先在 菜单 上选定再做本周任务——真实任务必须来自一门具体的课。在校学生换课请先邮件导师确认（第 7 周的规则）。

Choose from the menu first, then do the task — the real task must come from one specific course. Enrolled students: email your mentor to confirm a switch (the Week 7 rule).

仍不清楚？先看上面的常见问题，再邮件导师或教师（24–48 小时内回复；导师邮箱见开课信）。导师不替你装软件，也不做远程操作——但会告诉你换哪一件。Still unclear? Check the FAQ above, then email your mentor or the instructor (reply within 24–48 h; mentor addresses are in the welcome letter). Mentors do not install software for you or operate your computer remotely — but they will tell you which tool to switch to.
