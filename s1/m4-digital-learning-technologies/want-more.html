做Do

  • 做第二件作品——换一种：做了导图的做 10 张卡，做了卡的录 2 分钟视频。同一个中心内容，两种输出，比一比哪一种让你发现了更多“原来没懂”的地方。作品集周可以一起放进去。Make a second artifact — a different kind: if you made a map, make 10 cards; if you made cards, record 2 minutes. Same centre, two outputs; compare which one exposed more of what you hadn't understood. Both can go into the portfolio.
  • 把地图上的第六件用起来：六类里你一件都没试的那几类，寒假各试一件——不用写微评，只在日志里记一行“会 / 不会继续用”。Use the rest of the map: over the break, try one tool from each category you haven't touched — no review needed, just one log line: keep / drop.
  • 删几个图标：起点的两个数字之间的差，今天就处理掉一部分。删掉的每一个，写一个词：为什么。Delete a few icons: close part of the gap between your two starting-point numbers today. For each one deleted, write one word: why.
