不要饿死在富饶之地：工具越多越好吗？Don't starve in a land of plenty: are more tools better?

讲给一个不懂的人听，卡住的地方就是你没懂的地方。本周的作品——导图、闪卡、视频——都是在做同一件事：输出。Explain it to someone who doesn't know it; where you stall is where you haven't understood. This week's artifact — map, deck or video — does the same thing: it makes you produce.

2021 年的问题，反过来问The 2021 question, turned around

2021 年这门课的第四模块，核心问题是：“为什么我们掌握的在线学习工具越多越好？”那时我要求每类至少熟练掌握三个。五年后我把这个问题反过来：工具越多越好吗？你在起点写的两个数字已经给了答案——装了七个，上周打开过两个。另外五个，是“藏”，不是“用”。

In 2021 the fourth module of this course asked: “Why is it better to master as many online learning tools as possible?” I required at least three per category. Five years later I turn the question around: are more tools better? The two numbers you wrote at the starting point already answer it — seven installed, two opened last week. The other five are owned, not used.

藏，不等于用Owning is not using

读本第 08 章讲的是书：三十年里我买了那么多，路过书店“贼不走空”，搬家前卖废纸卖了一万多块，大脑却没有因此“无所不知”。藏书不等于读书。工具和书一模一样，甚至更糟——书至少占地方，会提醒你它没被读；App 只占一个图标，装上那天的满足感，和学会它带来的满足感，是同一种感觉，所以你很容易以为装上就是学会了。

Reader Ch. 08 is about books: thirty years of buying, never leaving a bookshop empty-handed, selling the lot for scrap before a move — and a brain no more all-knowing for it. Owning books is not reading them. Tools are exactly like books, or worse — a book at least takes up space and reminds you it is unread; an app takes up one icon, and the satisfaction of installing it feels identical to the satisfaction of having learned it, which is why it is so easy to mistake one for the other.

这两周你试的三件工具里，一定有一件“好用但我不会再用”。这不是浪费——你现在知道它对你的任务不合适了，而不是让它在手机里躺一年。微评里那一栏“我会不会继续用”，就是把“藏”和“用”分开的那条线。

Among the three tools you tried, there is surely one that is “good, but I won't use it again”. That is not waste — you now know it does not fit your task, instead of letting it lie on your phone for a year. The review line “would I keep using it” is the line that separates owning from using.

选择的本质是放弃To choose is to give up

读本里还有一句：时间是排他的。同样的一个小时，拿起这本书，就放下了其他所有本。工具也一样：主修课这周的 60 分钟，用来做 10 张卡，就没有用来画导图。所以“选择理由”的句干里有两个“因为”——选它因为什么，放弃它因为什么。放弃的那件工具不一定差，只是在这一个小时、对这一个任务，它输了。能说清楚“为什么放弃”，才算做了选择；否则只是又装了一个。

The reader has another line: time is exclusive. In the same hour, picking up this book means putting down every other. Tools too: this week's 60 core-course minutes spent making 10 cards are minutes not spent drawing a map. That is why the reason stem has two “because” clauses — why I chose this, why I gave that up. The tool you give up is not necessarily worse; in this hour, for this task, it lost. Only when you can say why you gave it up have you chosen; otherwise you have merely installed one more.

做出来的那一刻The moment it exists

还有一件读本里没说、但视频说了的事：做本身就是学。费曼的办法是讲给一个不懂的人听——讲到哪里卡住，哪里就是你没懂的。导图画到第三个分支写不出叶子，闪卡做到第七张发现两个术语分不清，视频录到第二遍才意识到例子举错了——那一刻你学到的，比看三遍视频都多。这就是为什么本单元的作品分最高：不是因为作品好看，而是因为做出来是你真的用了工具、真的碰到了自己不懂的地方的证据。

One more thing the reader does not say but the video does: making is itself learning. Feynman's method is to explain to someone who doesn't know — where you stall is where you haven't understood. A third branch you cannot give leaves to, a seventh card where two terms blur, a second take where you notice the example was wrong — in that moment you learn more than three replays of a video give you. That is why the artifact carries the most points in this module: not because it looks good, but because its existence is the evidence that you really used the tool and really met what you didn't understand.

带着这句话去做任务 2：Take this sentence into Task 2:一件小作品做完，留下一件工具，删掉几个图标——这两周就没有饿死在富饶之地。One small thing made, one tool kept, a few icons deleted — and these two weeks you did not starve in a land of plenty.
