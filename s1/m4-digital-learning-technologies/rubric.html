M4 量规M4 Rubric

量规的三行就是本单元的三条目标。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。

The three rows are the module's three objectives. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence.

目标（任务 · 分值）Objective (Task · Pts)达标Meets发展中Developing尚未达标Not yet证据不足No evidence
① 说出六类数字化学习工具各自解决的学习问题，并为每一类举出一个自己能用到的免费工具例子① Name the learning problem each of the six tool categories solves, and give one free tool you could actually use for each
任务 1 · 4 分Task 1 · 4 pts		4 · 六类齐全；每类一句“解决什么学习问题”，说的是学习者遇到的问题（“合上书考自己”），不是工具的功能列表；每类一个例子，符合工具规则（免费、只用邮箱、不用 VPN），而且是自己能装、能打开的。All six categories; one line per category naming the learner's problem (“test myself with the book closed”), not a feature list; one example per category that meets the tool rules (free, email only, no VPN) and that the learner can actually install or open.3 · 六类齐全，但一两类的“解决什么问题”写成了功能（“可以画图”），或一两个例子不符合工具规则 / 只是抄了微课里的名字而自己用不到。All six present, but one or two “what it solves” lines are features (“it can draw”), or one or two examples break the tool rules / are copied from the lesson but unusable by this learner.1 · 缺两类以上，或多数“解决什么问题”写不出来，或例子多数不可用。Two or more categories missing, or most “what it solves” lines absent, or most examples unusable.0 · 未发布 / 打不开 / 不是本人所写。Not published / unreachable / not the learner's own.
② 围绕主修外部课程里的同一个真实学习任务，试用三类工具各一件，各写一则微评（做什么 · 好在哪 · 卡在哪 · 我会不会继续用），并说明最终选哪一件、为什么② For one real task from your core external course, try one tool from each of three categories and write a micro-review of each (what it does · what worked · what got stuck · would I keep using it), then say which one you chose and why
任务 1 · 5 分（第 14 周评）Task 1 · 5 pts (scored in Week 14)		5 · 真实任务是主修课里一件具体的事；三件工具来自三个不同类别，都对着这同一个任务；三则微评四栏齐全，“卡在哪”写的是试用时真的发生的事（装不上也算）；选择理由用了“选____因为____；放弃____因为____”的结构，“因为”指向微评里写过的事。The real task is one concrete thing from the core course; three tools from three different categories, all facing that same task; all three reviews have all four lines, and “what got stuck” is something that really happened in the trial (failing to install counts); the reason uses “chose ____ because ____; gave up ____ because ____”, and the “because” points to something written in the reviews.4 · 缺一样：三件里有两件同类；或一则微评没有“卡在哪”（只有“好用”）；或选择理由是“好用 / 方便”，没有指向试用时的事；或任务写得笼统（“学好这门课”）但微评仍是真的。One thing missing: two of the three tools share a category; or one review lacks “what got stuck” (only “it's good”); or the reason is “it's good / convenient” with no trial event behind it; or the task is vague (“do well in the course”) though the reviews are real.2 · 只有一两则微评，或微评明显没有试用（只抄介绍页），或三件工具不是对着同一个任务，或没有选择理由。Only one or two reviews; or reviews clearly without a trial (copied from a product page); or the three tools face different tasks; or no reason given.0 · 未发布 / 打不开 / 不是本人所写。Not published / unreachable / not the learner's own.
③ 用所选的那一件工具独立做出一件小作品（思维导图 / 闪卡组 / ≤ 2 分钟讲解短视频），发布到个人网站，并用一两句话说明它帮自己学了什么③ Make one small artifact with the tool you chose (a mind map / a flashcard deck / a ≤ 2-minute explainer video), publish it on your own website, and add one or two sentences on what it helped you learn
任务 2 · 6 分Task 2 · 6 pts		6 · 作品本身在网站上（图片可见 / 视频可播放或可下载）；是用任务 1 里选定的那件工具做的；中心内容来自主修课；达到最低规格（导图 1 中心 + 3 分支 / 闪卡 10 张问答 / 视频 ≤ 2 分钟不露脸）；“它帮我学了什么”指向一个具体的概念或发现（“做到第三张卡才发现我分不清 A 和 B”）；符合隐私与版权规则。The artifact itself is on the site (image visible / video playable or downloadable); made with the tool chosen in Task 1; its centre comes from the core course; meets the minimum spec (map: 1 centre + 3 branches / deck: 10 Q–A cards / video: ≤ 2 min, no face); “what it helped me learn” names a specific concept or discovery (“at card three I realised I couldn't tell A from B”); privacy and copyright rules kept.4 · 作品在网站上，但低于最低规格（2 个分支 / 6 张卡）并如实标了“未完成”；或“帮我学了什么”是套话（“更熟悉了”）；或作品与主修课关系松（中心是别的课）；或用的不是任务 1 选定的工具而没有说明为什么换。On the site but below the minimum spec (2 branches / 6 cards), honestly marked “unfinished”; or the learning sentence is boilerplate (“I'm more familiar now”); or the link to the core course is loose (the centre is another subject); or a different tool than the one chosen in Task 1, with no explanation.2 · 只有截图或文字描述，作品本身不在网站上；或作品明显不是本人用工具做的（如整张导图由 AI 生成）；或内容与主修课无关。Only a screenshot or description; the artifact itself is not on the site; or clearly not made by the learner with the tool (e.g., a whole map generated by AI); or unrelated to the core course.0 · 未发布 / 打不开 / 露脸或露全名或含他人素材（先要求删除重发）。Not published / unreachable / shows a face or full name or contains others' material (ask for removal and re-post first).

规范要求（不计分，但缺少则该任务记“证据不足”）Form requirements (a gate, not points — missing means “No evidence”)

  • 作品发布在自己的网站“作品集”页，链接可打开；在校学生已登记链接：任务 1 第 13 周登记（M4-1），任务 2 第 14 周登记（M4-2）；第 14 周补第三则后任务 1 链接不变，不用重新登记，只在 M4-1 行备注“已补第三则”。Published on the learner's Portfolio page and reachable; enrolled students registered the links: Task 1 in Week 13 (M4-1), Task 2 in Week 14 (M4-2); after the third review is added the Task 1 link is unchanged — no re-registration, just the “third review added” note on the M4-1 row.
  • 当周三行日志已写（第 11–14 周写的是主修课）。The week's three-line log is written (Weeks 11–14 are about the core course).
  • 使用 AI 者附了 AI 使用说明；作品本身由 AI 生成的按学术诚信规则处理。AI users added a use note; an artifact generated by AI falls under the integrity policy.
  • 工具规则：试用的工具免费、只用邮箱注册（或不注册）、不用手机号、不用 VPN；需要手机号的工具只能“认得”，不能作为试用件。Tool rules: trial tools are free, email-only (or no account), no phone number, no VPN; a tool that needs a phone number may be named on the map but not used as a trial.
  • 隐私与版权：截图与作品不含他人姓名或信息；讲解视频不露脸、不露全名；不使用他人的图片、音乐。Privacy and copyright: no one else's names or information in screenshots or artifacts; no face or full name in the video; no one else's images or music.

合成How the 15 points add up

任务 1 = 第 ① 行（4 分）+ 第 ② 行（5 分）= 9 分；任务 2 = 第 ③ 行（6 分）；单元合计 15 分。任务 1 的两行都在第 14 周第三则微评登记后再评（第 13 周只发布，不评分）。导师在登记表上分别记两项任务的分数与等级，并在一周内邮件反馈。Task 1 = row ① (4) + row ② (5) = 9; Task 2 = row ③ (6); 15 in total. Both Task 1 rows are scored after the third review is registered in Week 14 (Week 13 is publish-only). Mentors record both tasks' scores and levels on the tracking sheet and email feedback within a week.
