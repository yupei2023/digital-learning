4 · 跟做（写在草稿里，3 分钟）4 · Follow-along (in your draft, 3 min)

做作品之前，先在草稿里写三句——这三句就是作品的骨架，也是微课 3 里“第一步”要的东西：Before making anything, write three sentences in your draft — they are the skeleton of the artifact, and the “step one” micro-lesson 3 asks for:

  1. 我的作品的中心是主修课里的哪一个概念 / 哪一组术语？（一个，不是一章。）The centre of my artifact is which concept / which set of terms from the core course? (One, not a chapter.)
  2. 它的三个分支 / 十张卡的题目 / 视频要讲的三句话，分别是什么？Its three branches / the ten card questions / the three sentences the video will say — what are they?
  3. 我用哪件工具做，它导出成什么（图片 / 截图 / 视频文件），发到网站的哪一页？Which tool do I make it with, what does it export as (image / screenshot / video file), and which page of my site does it go on?

第 2 句写不出来，说明第 1 句选大了——缩小到一个更小的概念。If sentence 2 won't come, sentence 1 is too big — shrink to a smaller concept.

✅ 核心要点（写完再看）✅ Key takeaways (open after writing)

六类工具各解决一个学习问题：做出来 · 放出去 · 画出来 · 走进去 · 找得回 · 考自己；同一件工具属于哪一类，看你拿它做什么；有理由的选择是“针对这个任务，选它因为____，放弃它因为____”，理由里有一件试用时真的发生的事；“藏”工具不等于“用”工具，选择的本质是放弃；作品做出来的那一刻，你才知道自己哪里没懂。

Each of the six categories solves one learning problem: make it · share it · draw it · step into it · find it again · test yourself. Which category a tool belongs to depends on what you do with it. A justified choice reads “for this task I chose X because ____ and gave up Y because ____”, with one real event from the trial inside the reason. Owning tools is not using them; to choose is to give up. And only when the artifact exists do you find out what you had not understood.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

视频怎么“发布到个人网站”？我不想传到 B 站。How do I “publish” a video on my site? I don't want to upload to Bilibili.

不需要传 B 站（上传要手机号，本课程不要求）。两种办法都算发布：把视频文件直接上传到你的网站页面（建站平台的“视频”或“文件”模块，2 分钟的录屏通常几十 MB 以内）；或者放一个能打开的文件链接（网站自带的文件存储）。要求只有一条：导师点开链接能看或能下载。

No Bilibili upload needed (it requires a phone number, which the course never asks for). Either counts as publishing: upload the video file straight to a page of your site (the builder's “video” or “file” block — a 2-minute screen recording is usually a few dozen MB at most), or place a working file link (the site's own file storage). The only requirement: your mentor can play or download it from the link.

作品做到一半，时间到了。I ran out of time halfway through the artifact.

发布做到一半的版本，写清“做到了哪一步”。量规看的是：中心来自主修课、是你自己用工具做的、发布了、说了它帮你学了什么。一张只有中心和两个分支的导图，诚实地标着“未完成”，也比一张描述“我打算做什么”的文字强。下周（作品集周）可以再补。

Publish the half-made version and say how far you got. The rubric looks for: the centre comes from the core course, you made it yourself with the tool, it is published, and you say what it helped you learn. A map with a centre and two branches, honestly marked “unfinished”, beats a paragraph describing what you meant to make. You can finish it in portfolio week.

第三件工具该从哪一类里挑？作品必须用第三件做吗？Which category does the third tool come from? Must the artifact use it?

第三件从你还没试过的类别里挑：第 13 周只排进一件 1、3、6 类的，就从那三类里再挑一类；已经有两件的，其余类别任挑。作品不必用第三件做——你在自己试过的两件能做作品的工具（导图 / 闪卡 / 录屏）里选一件，说明选它、放弃另一件的理由，微评照写。

Pick the third from a category you haven't tried: if Week 13 covered only one of categories 1, 3 and 6, take another of those three; if it covered two, any remaining category. The artifact need not use the third tool — you choose between the two artifact-capable tools you have tried (map / cards / recording), say why you chose one and gave up the other, and the review still gets written.

可以用手机做作品吗？Can I make the artifact on my phone?

可以。三种作品都有手机做法：导图用手机版 XMind 或幕布（免登录部分）截图导出；闪卡用 Anki 手机版或纸卡拍照拼图；讲解视频用手机自带录屏 + 对着笔记讲。规格不变。

Yes. All three have a phone route: a mind map in the phone version of XMind or 幕布 (the no-login part), exported as a screenshot; flashcards in Anki's phone app, or paper cards photographed and tiled; an explainer video with the phone's built-in screen recorder while you talk over your notes. The specs stay the same.

寒假还要继续记主修课的日志吗？Do I keep the core-course log over the break?

不要求。寒假的学习是选做，不计入任何评分；想继续，用同一页接着记就好。第 15 周起，日志回到写本课程。

Not required. Study over the break is optional and never graded; if you continue, keep using the same page. From Week 15 the log returns to this course.
