M4 · 第 13–14 周任务 · 9 分 · ✅ 辅助M4 · Weeks 13–14 task · 9 pts · ✅ Assisted

任务 1 · 三件工具，一个任务Task 1 · Three tools, one task

地图 + 三则微评 + 理由Map + 3 reviews + reason中文或英文Chinese or English约 30 + 8 分钟（首次更久）~30 + 8 min (longer at first)

要做什么What to do

在你网站的“作品集”页新建一条“M4 任务 1 · 三件工具，一个任务”。它有三部分：(1) 我的六类地图——六类各一句“它替我解决什么学习问题”+ 一个我能用到的免费工具；(2) 我的真实任务——主修课这两周里的一件具体的事；(3) 三则微评——三个不同类别各一件工具，每件“装好 + 用它对这个任务做 5 分钟真事”，再写四栏；最后一句选择理由。本周（第 13 周）发布：地图 + 真实任务 + 微评 1、2；第 14 周补：微评 3 + 选择理由。这一页是活的，两周都在写它。

On your Portfolio page create an entry “M4 Task 1 · Three tools, one task”, in three parts: (1) my six-category map — one line per category on what learning problem it solves for me, plus one free tool I could use; (2) my real task — one concrete thing from my core course these two weeks; (3) three micro-reviews — one tool from each of three different categories, each “set up + 5 real minutes on this task”, then the four lines; and one sentence of reason at the end. Publish in Week 13: map + real task + reviews 1 and 2; add in Week 14: review 3 + your reason. The page is alive — you write on it both weeks.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 工具规则：免费 · 只用邮箱注册（或不注册）· 不用手机号 · 不用 VPN。要手机号的工具可以写在地图上“认得”，但不能作为试用件。Tool rules: free · email-only sign-up (or none) · no phone number · no VPN. A tool that needs a phone number may appear on the map as “known”, but not as a trial.
  • 试用的定义：装好（或打开）+ 用它对你的真实任务做 5 分钟真事。看介绍页不算。A trial means: set up (or opened) + 5 real minutes on your real task. Reading the product page doesn't count.
  • 三件工具来自三个不同类别，都对着同一个任务；其中至少两件来自能做作品的第 1、3、6 类——第 14 周你在这两件之间选定作品工具。Three tools from three different categories, all facing the same task; at least two from the artifact categories 1, 3 and 6 — in Week 14 you choose your artifact tool between those two.
  • 每则微评四栏齐全；“卡在哪”不许空着。Every review has all four lines; “what got stuck” may not be blank.
  • 截图不含他人信息、不含你的全名；结尾附 AI 使用说明（或“本文未使用 AI”）。Screenshots show no one else's information and not your full name; end with an AI use note (or “No AI was used”).

你写的“卡在哪”不会白写，也不是只写给导师看的：第 14 周导师会把大家微评里最常见的“卡在哪”匿名汇总给教师，用来修订微课 1 工具表的“注意”列；同一周，导师还会把全班的微评汇成一份「同学们的工具推荐」清单（只写化名、工具名、一句好处、一句卡点）发回给每一个人。读者是同学与导师。Your “what got stuck” lines are not written into the void, and not written for the mentor alone: in Week 14 the mentors collect the most common ones, anonymised, for the instructor, who uses them to revise the “watch out” column of micro-lesson 1's table; in that same week they also compile the cohort's reviews into one “classmates' tool picks” list — pseudonym, tool name, one benefit, one snag — and send it back to everyone. Your readers are your classmates and your mentor.

步骤（第 13 周）Steps (Week 13)

  1. 写六类地图：每类一句“替我解决什么”，用微课 1 表格里的“它替我解决什么问题”那一列检查——写的是我的问题，不是工具的功能。例子优先写你自己已经在用、或这周真的装了 / 打开过的一件（符合工具规则即可）；微课 1 的表用来检查写法，不要照抄你自己用不到的名字。Write the map: one line per category on what it solves for me, checked against the “what it solves for me” column in micro-lesson 1's table — it names my problem, not the tool's features. Prefer an example you already use, or one you really installed or opened this week (as long as it meets the tool rules); use micro-lesson 1's table to check the wording, and don't copy names you could not actually use.
  2. 写真实任务：主修课这两周里具体的一件事。检查：它能在 5 分钟里被一件工具“碰”到吗？“学好这门课”碰不到；“记住第 3 周的 15 个术语”碰得到。Write the real task: one concrete thing from the course these two weeks. Check: can a tool touch it in 5 minutes? “Do well in the course” — no; “remember the 15 terms from week 3” — yes.
  3. 先想好下周做哪种作品（导图 / 闪卡 / 讲解视频，对应第 3、6、1 类），把其中一类排进本周。选两个类别（至少一个来自第 1、3、6 类），各挑一件工具（先看“注意”列，避开要手机号的）。紧接着主修课的时段装好，用它对任务做 5 分钟真事；当场写四栏。Think ahead to next week's artifact (map / cards / explainer video — categories 3, 6 and 1) and fit one of those categories into this week. Choose two categories (at least one from 1, 3 and 6) and one tool from each (read the “watch out” column first; avoid phone-number tools). Right after a core-course slot, set it up and spend 5 real minutes on the task; write the four lines on the spot.
  4. 截图隐私：截图里不要有他人姓名、你的全名、邮箱；用标注工具涂掉（第 3、5 周学过）。Screenshot privacy: no one else's names, not your full name or email; block out with the annotation tool (Weeks 3 and 5).
  5. 发布——三行做法：① 打开你的网站编辑器，进入“作品集”页；② 新建一条，标题“M4 任务 1 · 三件工具，一个任务”，粘贴模板并填好（第三则和理由先留空）；③ 点“发布”，复制这一页的链接。Publish — three self-contained steps: ① open your site editor and go to the Portfolio page; ② add an entry titled “M4 Task 1 · Three tools, one task”, paste and fill the template (leave the third review and the reason blank for now); ③ press Publish and copy the page's link.
  6. （在校学生）把链接登记给导师：登记表 → [网址将在开课信中公布]。三样：学生编号、任务编号（M4-1）、作品链接。(Enrolled) Register the link: Tracking sheet → [address in the welcome letter]. Three fields: student number, task number (M4-1), link.
  7. 写三行日志（写的是主修课）；按表学主修课第三周（60 分钟）。Write the three-line log (about the core course); do your third core-course hour by the timetable (60 min).

步骤（第 14 周 · 第三则与理由）Steps (Week 14 · the third review and the reason)

  1. 从你还没试过的类别里挑第三件：第 13 周只排进一件 1、3、6 类的，就从那三类里再挑一类；已经有两件的，其余类别任挑。对同一个任务做 5 分钟真事，当场写四栏。Pick the third tool from a category you haven't tried: if Week 13 covered only one of categories 1, 3 and 6, take another of those three; otherwise any remaining category. Spend 5 real minutes on the same task; write the four lines on the spot.
  2. 在两件能做作品的工具之间选定作品工具，写选择理由，用句干：“针对____这个任务，我选____，因为____；放弃____，因为____。”两个“因为”后面都要是微评里写过的事。Choose your artifact tool between the two artifact-capable ones, then write the reason with the stem: “For the task ____, I chose ____ because ____; I gave up ____ because ____.” Both “because” clauses must point to something in the reviews.
  3. 回到网站上同一条，填上第三则和理由，重新发布（链接不变，不用重新登记；在校学生在登记表的 M4-1 那行备注“已补第三则”）。Return to the same entry on your site, fill in the third review and the reason, and republish (same link; no re-registration — enrolled students add “third review added” in the M4-1 row's note).

常见问题FAQ

软件装不上 / 官网打不开。The software won't install / the site won't open.

把它写进“卡在哪”，这一则微评就算完成了（“我会不会继续用：不会——装不上”）。然后换同一类里不用装的那件（微课 1 每类都有）。下载只从官网域名（微课 1 的表里已括注：OBS obsproject.com · XMind xmind.cn · Anki apps.ankiweb.net，均待实测）；搜索结果里的“高速下载”“下载器”不要点。导师不替你装软件，也不做远程操作。

Write it under “what got stuck” and the review is complete (“would I keep using it: no — couldn't install”). Then switch to the no-install option in the same category (micro-lesson 1 has one per category). Download only from the official domains (bracketed in micro-lesson 1's table: OBS obsproject.com · XMind xmind.cn · Anki apps.ankiweb.net, all awaiting access tests); never click the “high-speed download” links in search results. Mentors do not install software for you or operate your computer remotely.

我已经在用一件工具了，还要“试用”吗？I already use a tool — do I still have to “try” it?

可以把它作为三件之一，但仍要对这个任务用 5 分钟并写四栏——熟悉的工具往往“卡在哪”写不出来，那就写“它对这个任务做不到的一件事”。另外两件必须是别的类别。

It can be one of the three, but you still spend 5 minutes on this task and write the four lines — with a familiar tool “what got stuck” is often hard to write, so write “one thing it cannot do for this task”. The other two must come from different categories.

微评可以写英文吗？Can the reviews be in English?

可以，中文或英文都行；一则里不要混着写。

Yes — Chinese or English; just don't mix within one review.
