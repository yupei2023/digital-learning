怎样讲好一次学习经历
How to tell a learning story

教师示范 The instructor's own story（2021 年课堂讲稿 · 2021 class script）

我小学转过两次学。五年级从农村小学转进城里的学校，英语课上老师讲 be 动词，我在本子上写下“必动词”，被老师写在黑板上，全班哄堂大笑。数学老师按分数排座位，几次考试后我坐到了靠墙倒数第二排。我妈说以后家长会她不去了，太丢人。同学给我起外号叫“乡巴佬”。我一度怀疑自己智商有问题。

转机是一场跳绳比赛。班里没有男生会跳双摇，我也不会。比赛前一天下午，我站在操场边看六年级选手怎么跳——发现要跳得更高、手摇得更快。我试了几下，能跳一两个；休息一下再来，能跳三四个。就这样练了一个晚上，第二天报名，拿了年级第三。班主任和同学都来祝贺我。我第一次觉得，我能给集体做贡献。

后来学长笛，因为是初学者，被分到和二、三年级小朋友一起的初级班。我年龄大、手长、气足，几周后就成了班里吹得最好的，老师每次让我示范。回家我乐此不疲地练——因为吹得好，所以有兴趣；因为有兴趣，所以练得多；因为练得多，所以吹得更好。两个月后我进了高级班。现在我明白了：我不是笨，我只是练习不够。

I changed primary schools twice. In grade 5 I moved from a village school to a city one. In English class the teacher said “be verb” and I wrote the Chinese character that sounds like “be” — she put it on the blackboard and the whole class laughed. The maths teacher seated us by test score; after a few tests I was in the second-to-last row by the wall. My mother said she would not attend parents' meetings any more — too embarrassing. Classmates nicknamed me “country bumpkin”. I doubted my own intelligence.

The turn came at a skipping-rope contest. No boy in our class could do double-unders; neither could I. The afternoon before, I stood at the edge of the playground watching the sixth-graders — they jumped higher and turned the rope faster. I tried: one or two. Rested, tried again: three or four. One evening of that, and the next day I placed third in the grade. My teacher and classmates congratulated me. For the first time I felt I could contribute.

Then the flute. As a beginner I was put with second- and third-graders. Being older, with longer arms and more breath, I was soon the best in the class and the teacher's demonstration player. At home I practised endlessly — playing well made it interesting, interest made me practise, practice made me better. Two months later I moved up to the advanced class. Now I understand: I was never stupid. I was under-practised.

读本第 03 章里，作者从全班倒数变成老师，转机是一场跳绳比赛。注意他怎么讲这件事：不是“我努力了，然后我成功了”，而是——

In Ch. 03 the author goes from the bottom of the class to becoming a teacher, and the turning point is a skipping-rope contest. Notice how he tells it — not “I tried hard and succeeded”, but:

  1. 场景：五年级，班里没人会跳双摇，眼看要记零分。Scene: grade 5, nobody in class can do double-unders, a zero is coming.
  2. 方法：先观察六年级选手怎么跳——发现要跳得更高、手摇得更快。Method: he observed the older students — jump higher, turn faster.
  3. 方法：小步尝试，跳出一两个；休息再来，跳出三四个。Method: small attempts, one or two; rest and retry, three or four.
  4. 条件：一个晚上的专注练习；一个“为班级争分”的动机。Conditions: one evening of focused practice; a motive — points for the class.
  5. 结果 + 认可：年级第三；同学祝贺；“我不是白痴”。Result + recognition: third in the grade; classmates' congratulations; “I'm not stupid.”
  6. 它教会我的关于学习的事：落后是练习不足，不是智商问题；被认可 → 想练 → 练得多 → 更好，是一个可以复制的循环。What it taught him about learning: falling behind means too little practice, not low IQ; recognition → desire → practice → improvement is a loop you can repeat.

你的 2–3 分钟 Your 2–3 minutes

用同样的六步讲你自己的故事。最容易丢的是第 2–4 步——方法和条件。量规只看这一点：你有没有说清楚“是什么让学习发生了”。“我努力了”“我有天赋”“运气好”都不算归因。

Tell your own story in the same six steps. The steps people drop are 2–4 — methods and conditions. The rubric checks exactly this: did you say what made learning happen? “I worked hard”, “I'm talented”, “I got lucky” are not attributions.

好的归因长这样 Good attributions look like：“我把歌词抄在便利贴上贴在镜子上，每天刷牙时念一遍”“我先看别人怎么做，再拆成三步练”“我妈每天晚上陪我读十分钟”“我关了手机”。“I wrote the lyrics on sticky notes on the mirror and read them while brushing my teeth.” “I watched someone first, then practised it in three steps.” “My mum read with me ten minutes every night.” “I turned my phone off.”

录制 Recording

  • 安静环境，衣着得体，不拍到别人；不说真实姓名和学校全名。Quiet place, appropriately dressed, nobody else in frame; no real name or full school name.
  • 先写六步提纲，再录；讲错了没关系，重录一次比剪辑省时间。Outline the six steps, then record; if you stumble, re-record — faster than editing.
  • 2–3 分钟。超过 3 分钟，砍掉“场景”里的细节，留下“方法”。2–3 minutes. If over, cut scene details and keep the methods.
