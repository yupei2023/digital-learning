到底什么是学习？What is learning, really?

上学 ≠ 学习Schooling ≠ learning

孔子有三千弟子，学有所成、名字留在孔庙里的，不到八十人。苏格拉底一生教出的著书立说的学者，七位。就算你的老师是孔子或苏格拉底，“上学”这件事本身也不能保证你学到东西。马克·吐温说得干脆：“我从来不会因为上学而影响我受教育。”

Confucius had three thousand students; fewer than eighty are honoured in his temples. Socrates produced seven scholars who wrote books. Even with Confucius or Socrates as your teacher, being in school guarantees nothing. Mark Twain put it bluntly: “I have never let my schooling interfere with my education.”

一个定义A definition

学习是获取新的理解、知识、行为、技能、价值观、态度和偏好的过程。注意三个词：新的（原来不会、现在会了）、过程（不是一次考试的分数）、行为（不只是“知道”，还包括“会做”）。

Learning is the process of acquiring new understanding, knowledge, behaviours, skills, values, attitudes and preferences. Three words matter: new (you couldn't before; now you can), process (not one test score), and behaviours (not just knowing, but doing).

它发生在哪里？Where does it happen?

可能在教室，可能在家，可能在球场——但一定 100% 发生在你的大脑里。《西游记》里孙悟空学艺的地方叫“灵台方寸山，斜月三星洞”，谜底是一个“心”字。古人说的“心”，就是我们今天说的大脑。学习，就是在建造自己的大脑。所以：坐在教室里，不等于在学习；大脑没有开工，学习就没有发生。

Maybe in a classroom, at home, on a court — but always, 100%, in your brain. In Journey to the West, the Monkey King learns his arts at a place whose name is a riddle for the character “heart” — the ancients' word for what we call the brain. Learning is building your own brain. Sitting in a classroom is not learning; if the brain is not at work, no learning happens.

两个门卫Two gatekeepers

情绪Emotion
学习之门The door to learning
动机Motivation

把守学习大门的有两个门卫：情绪和动机。情绪不对、动机不足，人就“身体在教室，大脑在门外”。我们叫不醒装睡的人，也教不会装作学习的人。所以，下次你觉得“学不进去”，先别怪自己笨，先问：是哪一个门卫在拦我？

Two gatekeepers guard the door: emotion and motivation. Wrong mood or weak motive, and the body sits in class while the brain waits outside. You cannot wake someone pretending to sleep, and you cannot teach someone pretending to learn. Next time you “can't study”, don't call yourself stupid — ask which gatekeeper is blocking you.

知行合一Mind and hand

MIT 的校训是 mens et manus——“心智与手”，翻译成中文就是“知行合一”。真正的学习是一个环：读、听、看 → 思考、实践 → 存回大脑，变成经验 → 遇到新问题时再拿出来用，用完了还可能被改写。只有输入没有输出，不叫学习。

MIT's motto is mens et manus — mind and hand; in Chinese, “knowing and doing as one”. Real learning is a loop: read, listen, watch → think, practise → store as experience → reuse on a new problem, and maybe rewrite. Input without output is not learning.

（选看）教师 2021 年在课堂上是怎么讲这一课的——八条讲稿要点(Optional) How the instructor taught this lesson in 2021 — eight talking points

这一课我在 2021 年给七、八年级上过。当年讲稿的主线和读本第 02 章一致；上面五个小标题已经是它的浓缩版，这八条是原始提纲，只给想看“老师当年怎么讲”的同学。I taught this lesson to grades 7–8 in 2021. Its line matches Reader Ch. 02; the five headings above are already the condensed version. These eight points are the original outline, for anyone curious how the lesson was taught.

  1. 上学 ≠ 受教育：马克·吐温的一句话；两个虚构的反例——《西线无战事》（一部一战小说）里，保罗的中学老师鼓动全班参军；《鲁西西外传》（郑渊洁的童话）里，唧唧唧大学只教人死记硬背——上学本身也可能是坏教育。Schooling ≠ education: Twain's line, and two fictional counter-examples — in All Quiet on the Western Front (a World War I novel), Paul's schoolteacher talks the whole class into enlisting; in Zheng Yuanjie's children's tale Lu Xixi, “Jijiji University” teaches nothing but rote memorising — schooling itself can be bad education.
  2. 孔子的“成功率”：海南崖城孔庙里牌位上有名有姓的弟子不到八十人，除以三千，约 2.6%；苏格拉底一生七位著书立说的学生。按今天某些学校的教师考核，两位老师都会失业。Confucius's “success rate”: under 80 named disciples on the tablets of the Yacheng temple in Hainan, out of 3,000 (~2.6%); Socrates, seven scholars who wrote books. By some schools' teacher appraisals today, both would be fired.
  3. 学习的定义与它发生的地方：一定在大脑；“心理学”这个译名其实把大脑的事放进了心里；脑死亡才是死亡——如果记忆也能移植，那个人还是他吗？The definition, and where it happens: always the brain; the Chinese word for “psychology” literally puts the brain's business in the heart; brain death is death — if memory could be transplanted, would the person still be themselves?
  4. 《一百年后的学校》（1900 年法国想象画）：把书放进机器、把知识灌进学生大脑——先觉得可笑，再觉得可怕：这就是洗脑式的灌输。“The school in the year 2000” (a 1900 French painting): books fed into a machine, knowledge pumped into students' heads — funny at first, then frightening: spoon-feeding as brainwashing.
  5. 知行合一：MIT 的 mens et manus；《中庸》的“博学之，审问之，慎思之，明辨之，笃行之”。Mind and hand: MIT's mens et manus, and the Zhongyong's “study widely, inquire carefully, think prudently, discern clearly, practise earnestly”.
  6. 两个门卫：情绪与动机；“威逼”与“利诱”（都加引号）；如果都不管用，就移植你自己的成功经验。Two gatekeepers: emotion and motivation; “stick” and “carrot” (both in quotation marks); if neither works, transplant a success of your own.
  7. 两个可移植的例子：我自己从“乡巴佬”到教师的故事（第 9 周微课 3 完整讲述）；Barbara Oakley 26 岁才开始学数学，把军队训练出的专注力“移植”到学习上。Two transplantable examples: my own story from “country bumpkin” to teacher (told in full in Week 9, micro-lesson 3), and Barbara Oakley, who started maths at 26 and transplanted her army-trained focus into study.
  8. 两个身体条件：睡眠让记忆归档；大脑占体重 2% 却耗氧 20%——上课犯困，先开窗、先运动。Two bodily conditions: sleep files memories away; the brain is 2% of body weight but uses 20% of the oxygen — sleepy in class? open a window and move before blaming yourself.
