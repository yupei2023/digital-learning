M1 · 第 8 周任务 · 4 分 · ✅ 辅助M1 · Week 8 task · 4 pts · ✅ Assisted

任务 1 · 我的成功学习经历Task 1 · My successful learning experience

录音或录像Audio or video2–3 分钟2–3 min约 45 分钟完成（提纲 + 录制 + 发布）~45 min to make (outline + record + publish)

要做什么What to do

录一段 2–3 分钟的音频或视频，讲一次你真的学会了什么的经历，按六步讲（第 8 周页面第 5 节有图；教师本人的完整示范见微课 3）：场景 → 方法（先观察）→ 方法（小步试、休息再试）→ 条件 → 结果与认可 → 它教会我的关于学习的事。发布到你网站的“作品集”页，标题写“M1 任务 1 · 我的成功学习经历”。

Record 2–3 minutes of audio or video telling one time you really learned something, in six steps (figure in Week 8, section 5; the instructor's full worked example is in micro-lesson 3): scene → method (observe first) → method (small attempts, rest, retry) → conditions → result & recognition → what it taught me about learning. Publish on your Portfolio page titled “M1 Task 1 · My successful learning experience”.

  1. 场景：什么时候、在哪、什么事Scene: when, where, what
  2. 方法：先观察Method: observe first
  3. 方法：小步试；休息再试Method: small attempts; rest and retry
  4. 条件：时间、地点、动机、谁帮了你Conditions: time, place, motive, who helped
  5. 结果与认可Result & recognition
  6. 它教会我的关于学习的事What it taught me about learning

橙色的第 2–4 步是量规只看的地方。录之前把这张图放在手机上，讲到哪一步看一眼。The orange steps 2–4 are all the rubric looks at. Keep this figure on your phone while recording and glance at it as you go.

选看 · 录之前的 4 分钟Optional · 4 minutes before you record

冲浪练习：过程与结果 · 约 4 分钟 · B 站 UP 主 _愚人金_（Coursera《学会如何学习》中英字幕转载）Process versus product (Learning How to Learn 3.1.4) · ~4 min · Bilibili uploader _愚人金_ (re-upload of Coursera's Learning How to Learn with bilingual captions) · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗
量规只看“方法和条件”，不看“结果多好”——Oakley 把这叫“关注过程而不是结果”。四分钟听她怎么区分两者，再检查你的六步提纲：第 2–4 步写的是过程吗？不计入本周 120 分钟。The rubric scores methods and conditions, not how good the result was — Oakley calls this focusing on process rather than product. Four minutes on the difference, then check your six-step outline: are steps 2–4 about process? Not counted in this week's 120 minutes.

步骤Steps

  1. 选一次经历。不必伟大——会骑车、会一首歌、会一种题型都算。Pick one experience. It need not be grand — riding a bike, a song, a type of problem all count.
  2. 写六步提纲，每步一行。Outline the six steps, one line each.
  3. 录制：手机自带的录音软件，或电脑录屏（第 3 周 B2 教过）都可以。安静环境，得体衣着，不拍到别人，不说真实姓名和学校全名。Record with your phone's own voice recorder, or the screen recorder from Week 3 (B2). Quiet, appropriately dressed, nobody else in frame; no real name or full school name.
  4. 听一遍：方法和条件说清楚了吗？超过 3 分钟就删场景细节。Listen back: are methods and conditions clear? Over 3 minutes — cut scene details.
  5. 发布：① 打开你的网站编辑页 → “作品集”页 → 新建一条，标题“M1 任务 1 · 我的成功学习经历”；② 用建站平台的上传功能把文件传上去；③ 放不下，就把文件放进你的云盘，生成分享链接（第 7 周 B6 教过），把链接贴进这一条。发布后自己打开一次确认能播放。Publish: ① open your site editor → Portfolio page → new entry titled “M1 Task 1 · My successful learning experience”; ② upload the file with the site builder's upload tool; ③ if it is too big, put it in your cloud drive, create a share link (Week 7, B6) and paste the link into the entry. Open it once yourself to confirm it plays.
  6. 在作品下面写一句话：“这次经历里，让学习发生的关键是____。”Under the artifact, write one line: “The key that made learning happen was ____.”
  7. 如果用了 AI（例如帮你理提纲），附 AI 使用说明。在校学生把链接登记到导师的登记表（学生编号 · M1-T1 · 链接）。If you used AI (e.g., to tidy the outline), add the AI use note. Enrolled students register the link on the mentor's tracking sheet (student ID · M1-T1 · link).

评分 · 量规第 ② 行（4 分）Scoring · Rubric row ② (4 pts)

达标 · 4Meets · 4发展中 · 3Developing · 3尚未达标 · 2Not yet · 2证据不足 · 0No evidence · 0
一次具体经历（有时间、地点、事情）；把成功归因于具体的方法或条件（做了什么、在什么条件下）；说出它教会自己的关于学习的一点；2–3 分钟，清楚可听。One specific experience (when, where, what); success attributed to specific methods or conditions; one lesson about learning; 2–3 min, audible.经历具体，但归因笼统（“我努力了”“我有天赋”“运气好”）或只有结果没有过程。Specific experience but vague attribution (“I worked hard”, “talent”, “luck”) or result without process.经历不具体（泛泛而谈“我学英语”），或没有归因。Experience not specific (“I learned English” in general), or no attribution.未发布、无法打开，或不是本人经历，或不符合录制规范（拍到他人、泄露个人信息）。Not published / unreachable / not the learner's own / violates recording rules (others in frame, personal information disclosed).
