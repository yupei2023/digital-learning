M1 · 第 9 周任务 Week 9 task · 6 分 pts · ✅ 辅助 Assisted

任务 2 · “我的学习观”短文
Task 2 · “My view of learning” mini-essay

300–500 字/词 characters or words中文或英文 zh or en约 40 分钟 ~40 min

要做什么 What to do

写一篇 300–500 字的短文，说清楚你现在对“学习”的看法。五段结构见微课 4。发布到你网站的“作品集”页，标题写“M1 任务 2 · 我的学习观”。

Write 300–500 words on what you now think learning is. Five-paragraph structure in micro-lesson 4. Publish on your Portfolio page as “M1 Task 2 · My view of learning”.

硬性要求 Hard requirements

  • 定义用自己的话——照抄读本定义按“发展中”计。Definition in your own words — a copied definition rates Developing.
  • 至少两个读本概念，写明章号，并说明它怎样解释你的例子。At least two reader concepts, with chapter numbers, explaining your example.
  • 至少一对策略：一种有效、一种效果有限，各说为什么。At least one pair of strategies — effective and limited — with reasons.
  • 结尾附 AI 使用说明（或“本文未使用 AI”）。End with an AI use note (or “No AI was used”).

Define learning; effective vs. ineffective		自己的话给出定义；至少一对有效/无效策略并说明为什么；有亲身例子。Own-words definition; a strategy pair with reasons; a personal example.定义接近照抄，或策略只列出没有说明为什么，或缺例子。Near-copied definition, or strategies without reasons, or no example.定义模糊，且无策略对比。Vague definition and no strategy comparison.未发布 / 不是本人所写。Not published / not the learner's own.
