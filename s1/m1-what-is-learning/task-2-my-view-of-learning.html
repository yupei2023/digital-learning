M1 · 第 9 周任务 · 6 分 · ✅ 辅助M1 · Week 9 task · 6 pts · ✅ Assisted

任务 2 · “我的学习观”短文Task 2 · “My view of learning” mini-essay

300–500 字/词300–500 characters or words中文或英文Chinese or English约 50 分钟（草稿 + 写作 + 发布）~50 min (draft + write + publish)

要做什么What to do

写一篇 300–500 字的短文，说清楚你现在对“学习”的看法。五段结构见微课 4。发布到你网站的“作品集”页，标题写“M1 任务 2 · 我的学习观”。

Write 300–500 words on what you now think learning is. Five-paragraph structure in micro-lesson 4. Publish on your Portfolio page as “M1 Task 2 · My view of learning”.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 定义用自己的话——照抄读本定义按“发展中”计。Definition in your own words — a copied definition rates Developing.
  • 用至少两个读本概念解释你的经历或看法——写明章号，说清它怎样解释你的例子；只提到名字不算。Use at least two reader concepts to explain your experience or view — chapter numbers named, and how each explains your example; name-dropping does not count.
  • 至少一对策略：一种有效、一种效果有限，各说为什么。At least one pair of strategies — effective and limited — with reasons.
  • 结尾附 AI 使用说明（或“本文未使用 AI”）。End with an AI use note (or “No AI was used”).

两位教授用两分半把整门课“学习是什么、怎样学更有效”收成几句话——这正是你第五段“一句话学习观”要做的事。看完先合上视频，用一句话说出他们的学习观，再写你自己的。不计入本周 120 分钟。Two professors compress a whole course — what learning is and how to do it better — into a few sentences, which is exactly the job of your fifth paragraph. Close the video, state their view of learning in one sentence, then write yours. Not counted in this week's 120 minutes.
