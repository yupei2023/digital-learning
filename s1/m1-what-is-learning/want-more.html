做Do

  • 一周提取实验：选一门正在学的课，本周复习时不重读，只用“合上书回忆 → 翻开核对”。周日在日志里多写一行：感觉如何？A one-week retrieval experiment: pick a course you are taking; this week, no rereading — only “close the book and recall → open and check”. Add a line to your log on Sunday: how did it feel?
  • 七种动机因素自查（读本第 04 章）：挑战、好奇、掌控、幻想、竞争、合作、认可——哪两种最能让你开工？把答案存进作品集备用，第 15 周会用到。Seven-motivator self-check (Reader Ch. 04): challenge, curiosity, control, fantasy, competition, cooperation, recognition — which two get you going? Save the answer in your portfolio; Week 15 will use it.
