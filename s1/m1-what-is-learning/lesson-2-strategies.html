睡眠是“条件”，不是“方法”——但没有它，方法都打折。 Sleep is a condition, not a method — but without it every method is discounted.

学习好的人有秘密武器吗？有——但不是天赋，而是一些看起来更费劲、实际上更有效的做法。心理学家比较过学生常用的十种学习方法（Dunlosky 等，2013），结论让很多人意外。

Do good learners have a secret weapon? Yes — not talent, but a handful of practices that feel harder and work better. Psychologists compared ten common study techniques (Dunlosky et al., 2013); the results surprise most students.

为什么我们偏爱无效的方法？Why do we prefer the weak ones?

因为它们让人感觉良好。重读和划线很顺畅，提取练习很痛苦——你会发现自己记不住。但“顺畅”是一种错觉，“费劲”才是大脑在建造的信号。读本第 02 章说学习 100% 发生在大脑里——那些让大脑真正开工的方法，才是有效的方法。

Because they feel good. Rereading and highlighting flow; retrieval hurts — you discover what you don't know. But fluency is an illusion, and effort is the signal that the brain is building. Ch. 02 says learning happens 100% in the brain — the methods that make the brain work are the ones that work.

两个条件 Two conditions

方法之外还有条件：睡眠（记忆在睡眠中整理，熬夜学习是拆东墙补西墙）和不分心（一边刷手机一边背单词，大脑其实在两件事之间来回切换，两件都做不好）。讲你的学习经历时，别忘了这一类“条件”。

Beyond methods there are conditions: sleep (memory is consolidated during sleep; all-nighters rob Peter to pay Paul) and no multitasking (studying with your phone open means the brain keeps switching — and does both badly). When you tell your learning story, remember conditions like these.
