M1 量规M1 Rubric

量规的三行就是本单元的三条目标。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。

The three rows are the module's three objectives. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence.

目标（任务 · 分值）Objective (Task · Pts)达标Meets发展中Developing尚未达标Not yet证据不足No evidence
① 用自己的话界定“学习”，并举例区分有效与无效的学习策略① Define learning in your own words, with examples that separate effective from ineffective strategies
任务 2 · 3 分Task 2 · 3 pts		3 · 自己的话给出定义；至少一对有效/无效策略并说明为什么；有亲身例子。Own-words definition; a strategy pair with reasons; personal example.2 · 定义接近照抄，或策略只列出未说明，或缺例子。Near-copied, or no reasons, or no example.1 · 定义模糊且无策略对比。Vague and no comparison.0 · 未发布 / 不是本人所写。Not published / not the learner's own.
② 讲述一次自己的成功学习经历，并归因于具体的方法或条件② Tell one successful learning experience of your own and attribute it to specific methods or conditions
任务 1 · 4 分Task 1 · 4 pts		4 · 一次具体经历；归因到具体方法或条件；说出它教会自己的关于学习的一点；2–3 分钟，清楚可听。Specific; attributed to methods/conditions; a lesson about learning; 2–3 min, audible.3 · 经历具体但归因笼统（努力/天赋/运气），或只有结果没有过程。Specific but vague attribution (effort/talent/luck), or result without process.2 · 经历不具体或没有归因。Not specific or no attribution.0 · 未发布、无法打开、非本人经历，或违反录制规范。Not published, unreachable, not the learner's own, or violates recording rules.

规范要求（不计分，但缺少则该任务记“证据不足”）Form requirements (a gate, not points — missing means “No evidence”)

  • 作品发布在自己的网站“作品集”页，链接可打开；在校学生已登记链接。Published on the learner's Portfolio page and reachable; enrolled students registered the link.
  • 当周三行日志已写。The week's three-line log is written.
  • 使用 AI 者附了 AI 使用说明；短文里明显由 AI 代写的部分按学术诚信规则处理。AI users added a use note; AI-written passages fall under the integrity policy.
  • 录制规范：不拍到他人、不泄露真实姓名与学校全名。Recording rules: no one else in frame, no real name or full school name.

合成How the 10 points add up

任务 1 = 第 ② 行（4 分）；任务 2 = 第 ① 行 + 第 ③ 行（3 + 3 = 6 分）；单元合计 10 分。导师在登记表上分别记两项任务的分数与等级，并在一周内邮件反馈。Task 1 = row ② (4); Task 2 = rows ① + ③ (3 + 3 = 6); 10 in total. Mentors record both tasks' scores and levels on the tracking sheet and email feedback within a week.
