5 · 本周任务5 · This week's task

任务 1 · 我的成功学习经历（录音或录像，2–3 分钟）· 4 分Task 1 · My successful learning experience (audio or video, 2–3 min) · 4 pts

讲一次你真的学会了什么的经历——可以是一首曲子、一个游戏、一道题型、一项运动、一门语言。重点不是结果，而是：你具体做了什么，在什么条件下，让学习发生了？

Tell about one time you really learned something — a piece of music, a game, a type of problem, a sport, a language. The point is not the result but: what exactly did you do, and under what conditions, that made learning happen?

怎么讲：六步How to tell it: six steps

  1. 场景：什么时候、在哪、什么事Scene: when, where, what
  2. 方法：先观察——看别人怎么做Method: observe first — watch how others do it
  3. 方法：小步试；休息再试Method: small attempts; rest and retry
  4. 条件：时间、地点、动机、谁帮了你Conditions: time, place, motive, who helped
  5. 结果与认可：发生了什么，谁看见了Result & recognition: what happened, who noticed
  6. 它教会我的关于学习的事What it taught me about learning

橙色的第 2–4 步是量规唯一看的地方：你有没有说清楚“是什么让学习发生了”。“我努力了”“我有天赋”“运气好”都不算归因。The orange steps 2–4 are all the rubric looks at: did you say what made learning happen? “I worked hard”, “I'm talented”, “I got lucky” are not attributions.

好的归因长这样：Good attributions look like:“我把歌词抄在便利贴上贴在镜子上，每天刷牙时念一遍”“我先看别人怎么做，再拆成三步练”“我妈每天晚上陪我读十分钟”“我关了手机”。“I wrote the lyrics on sticky notes on the mirror and read them while brushing my teeth.” “I watched someone first, then practised it in three steps.” “My mum read with me ten minutes every night.” “I turned my phone off.”

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我不想露脸，可以吗？Can I stay off camera?

可以。只录音频，或者录屏幕（比如一边翻你当年的笔记一边讲）都行。

Yes. Audio only, or a screen recording (e.g., flipping through your old notes while you talk), is fine.

我的网站放不下音频/视频文件怎么办？My site won't take the file.

先试建站平台自带的文件上传；放不下，就把文件传到你的云盘，生成分享链接（第 7 周 B6 会教），在网站上放链接即可。不要传到需要手机号注册、又没有“仅链接可见”的视频网站。

Try the site builder's own file upload first; if the file is too big, put it in your cloud drive, create a share link (Week 7, B6, teaches this) and link it from your site. Avoid video sites that need a phone number and have no unlisted mode.

用中文还是英文讲？Chinese or English?

都可以。想练英语的同学可以先用中文讲一遍，再用英文讲一遍。

Either. If you want English practice, record once in Chinese, then once in English.

我想不出“成功”的经历。I can't think of a success.

把标准放低：任何一件你现在会、以前不会的事都算——系鞋带、骑车、一首歌的歌词。关键是回忆“我当时是怎么学会的”。

Lower the bar: anything you can do now that you couldn't before — tying shoelaces, riding a bike, the lyrics of a song. The point is recalling how you learned it.
