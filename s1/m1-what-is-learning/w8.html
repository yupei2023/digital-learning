10 · 常见问题 FAQ

我不想露脸，可以吗？Can I stay off camera?

可以。只录音频，或者录屏幕（比如一边翻你当年的笔记一边讲）都行。Yes. Audio only, or a screen recording (e.g., flipping through your old notes while you talk), is fine.

我的网站放不下音频/视频文件怎么办？My site won't take the file.

把文件传到 B 站（设为“不公开”）或你的云盘，在网站上放链接即可——B2 和 B6 教过的方法。Upload it to Bilibili (unlisted) or your cloud drive and link it from your site — as taught in B2 and B6.

用中文还是英文讲？Chinese or English?

都可以。想练英语的同学可以先用中文讲一遍，再用英文讲一遍。Either. If you want English practice, record once in Chinese, then once in English.

我想不出“成功”的经历。I can't think of a success.

把标准放低：任何一件你现在会、以前不会的事都算——系鞋带、骑车、一首歌的歌词。关键是回忆“我当时是怎么学会的”。Lower the bar: anything you can do now that you couldn't before — tying shoelaces, riding a bike, the lyrics of a song. The point is recalling how you learned it.

仍不清楚？先看页面底部的常见问题，再邮件导师或教师（24–48 小时内回复）。Still unclear? Email your mentor or the instructor (reply within 24–48 h).
