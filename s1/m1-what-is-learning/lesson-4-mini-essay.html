怎样写“我的学习观”How to write “My view of learning”

学习的本质是什么？ · 约 7.5 分钟 · B 站 UP 主 艾伦-麦克法兰（剑桥大学人类学教授 Alan Macfarlane）What is the essence of learning? · ~7.5 min · Bilibili uploader Alan Macfarlane (Professor of Anthropology, Cambridge) · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗
一种鲜明的学习观：“学习不是传递事实——事实可以留在书里和电脑里。”你的短文可以同意他，也可以反驳他——但要说出理由。A sharp view of learning: “learning is not transmitting facts — facts can stay in books and computers.” Your essay may agree or argue back — with reasons.

“学习观”就是你对“学习是什么、怎样才算学会、怎样学更有效”的看法。300–500 字，五段就够。

A “view of learning” is what you think learning is, what counts as having learned, and how to learn better. 300–500 words; five short paragraphs are enough.

¶写什么What to write对应量规Rubric row
1我的定义：用自己的话说“学习是……”。禁止照抄读本的定义；可以改写、可以反驳。Your definition, in your own words: “Learning is…”. Don't copy the reader's; rephrase or even argue with it.①
2一个例子：上周录的那次经历，或另一次。一两句话即可。One example — last week's story or another. A sentence or two.①
3读本概念 × 2：至少两个，写明章号，说明它怎样帮你解释自己的例子。例如“两个门卫”（02）、“知行合一”（02）、“良性循环”（03）、“功夫既是本事也是时间”（03）、“内部动机”（04）、“多巴胺”（04）、“七种动机因素”（04）。Two reader concepts, with chapter numbers, and how each explains your example. Options: the two gatekeepers (02), mind and hand (02), the virtuous cycle (03), “gongfu = skill and time” (03), internal motivation (04), dopamine (04), the seven motivators (04).③
4有效 vs 无效：一种你打算多用的策略、一种你打算少用的，各说为什么。Effective vs. ineffective: one strategy you'll use more, one you'll use less — with reasons.①
5一句话学习观：如果只能留一句，你会说什么？Your view in one sentence: if only one line survived, what would it be?①

一个开头的例子A sample opening

“以前我以为学习就是把老师讲的记住。现在我觉得，学习是把一件我原来不会的事，变成我不看提示也能做出来的事。上周我讲了自己学会双摇跳绳的经历……”

“I used to think learning meant remembering what the teacher said. Now I think learning is turning something I couldn't do into something I can do without looking at the hints. Last week I told the story of learning double-unders…”

AI 使用说明怎么写The AI use note

如果你用了 AI，在文末加两三句：If you used AI, add two–three sentences at the end:

“AI 使用说明：我用（工具名）检查了语句是否通顺，并请它给我第 3 段的概念举一个例子；例子我没有采用，因为和我的经历不符。观点和例子都是我自己的。”

“AI use note: I used (tool) to check my sentences and asked it for an example for paragraph 3; I didn't use the example because it didn't match my experience. The ideas and examples are my own.”

不用 AI 就写“本文未使用 AI”。If you didn't use AI, write “No AI was used.”
