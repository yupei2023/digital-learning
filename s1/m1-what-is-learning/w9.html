4 · 跟做 Follow-along（10 分钟，写在草稿里）

在动笔之前，先用三句话回答（草稿，不用发布）：Before writing, answer in three sentences (draft only):

  1. 如果“学习”是一个动词，我会怎么解释它？（不许用“获取知识”四个字）If “learning” is a verb, how would I explain it — without the phrase “acquire knowledge”?
  2. 上周我录的那次经历，用读本的哪个概念可以解释？Which reader concept explains the story I recorded last week?
  3. 我现在用的学习方法里，哪一个我打算换掉？为什么？Which of my current study methods will I replace, and why?

这三句话就是你短文的骨架。These three sentences are the skeleton of your essay.

单元收尾 Module wrap-up（再加两行 two more lines）：

✅ 核心要点 Key takeaways：学习 100% 发生在大脑中；上学≠学习；把守学习大门的是情绪和动机；有效的策略（提取、间隔、自我解释）看起来更费劲，却更有效；成功可以归因，因此可以复制。
Learning happens in the brain; schooling ≠ learning; emotion and motivation guard the door; effective strategies (retrieval, spacing, self-explanation) feel harder but work better; success can be attributed — and therefore repeated.

10 · 常见问题 FAQ

可以用英文写吗？Can I write in English?

可以，中文或英文，或者双语。按字数/词数要求即可。Yes — Chinese, English, or both.

“引用读本概念”要怎么写？How do I “cite a reader concept”?

写出概念 + 章号 + 你的理解，例如：“读本第 02 章说学习有两个门卫——情绪和动机。我发现自己被拦在门外的，通常是情绪。”Concept + chapter + your take, e.g., “Ch. 02 says two gatekeepers guard learning — emotion and motivation. What usually stops me at the door is emotion.”

我用 AI 帮我改了句子，算作弊吗？I used AI to fix sentences — is that cheating?

不算，本单元是 ✅ 辅助。观点和例子必须是你的；在结尾附两三句 AI 使用说明。让 AI 替你写整篇，才是作弊。No — this module is ✅ Assisted. The ideas and examples must be yours; add an AI use note. Having AI write the whole essay is cheating.

上周的任务 1 还没做完怎么办？Task 1 is still unfinished.

先做本周任务，再补上周的——导师登记表以周日为参考点，但没有迟交处罚。Do this week's task first, then catch up — no late penalty.
