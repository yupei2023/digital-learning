10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

可以用英文写吗？Can I write in English?

可以，中文或英文，或者双语。按字数/词数要求即可。

Yes — Chinese, English, or both. Just meet the length requirement.

“引用读本概念”要怎么写？How do I “cite a reader concept”?

写出概念 + 章号 + 你的理解，例如：“读本第 02 章说学习有两个门卫——情绪和动机。我发现自己被拦在门外的，通常是情绪。”

Concept + chapter + your take, e.g., “Ch. 02 says two gatekeepers guard learning — emotion and motivation. What usually stops me at the door is emotion.”

我用 AI 帮我改了句子，算作弊吗？I used AI to fix sentences — is that cheating?

不算，本单元是 ✅ 辅助。观点和例子必须是你的；在结尾附两三句 AI 使用说明。让 AI 替你写整篇，才是作弊。

No — this module is ✅ Assisted. The ideas and examples must be yours; add a two–three-sentence AI use note. Having AI write the whole essay is cheating.
