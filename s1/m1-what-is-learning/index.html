什么是学习？What is Learning?

10 分10 pts✅ 辅助✅ Assisted每周 ≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min/week两周Two weeks

先想一个问题：你上过多少节课？几千节总有了。那么——其中有多少节，你是真的在学习？马克·吐温说：“我从来不会因为上学而影响我受教育。”上学和学习，是两码事。这两周我们不讲任何数字工具，只做一件事：把“学习”这件你每天都在做的事，看清楚。

Start with a question: how many lessons have you sat through? Thousands. In how many of them were you actually learning? Mark Twain said he never let his schooling interfere with his education. Going to school and learning are two different things. For two weeks we put the digital tools aside and do one thing only: look clearly at “learning” — something you do every day.

核心问题Essential questions

  • 为什么我们应该学习？Why should we learn?
  • 什么是学习？What is learning?
  • 学习好的人有什么秘密武器吗？Do good learners have a secret weapon?

Barbara Oakley 用五分钟回答第三个核心问题：学习好的人有没有秘密武器？她的答案是运动、练习和时间——不是天赋。看完把你自己的猜想写在日志第一行。不计入每周 120 分钟。Barbara Oakley takes five minutes on the third essential question: do good learners have a secret weapon? Her answer is exercise, practice and time — not talent. Afterwards, write your own guess as the first line of your log. Not counted in the weekly 120 minutes.

学完本单元，你应能By the end of this module you should be able to

  1. 用自己的话界定“学习”，并举例区分有效与无效的学习策略。Define learning in your own words, with examples that separate effective from ineffective strategies.
  2. 讲述一次自己的成功学习经历，并归因于具体的方法或条件。Tell one successful learning experience of your own and attribute it to specific methods or conditions.
  3. 用读本中至少两个概念解释自己的学习经历或学习观。Use at least two concepts from the reader to explain your own learning experience or view of learning.
