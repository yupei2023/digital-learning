第一学期 Semester 1 · 第 8–9 周 Weeks 8–9 · 单元 Module 1

什么是学习？
What is Learning?

10 分 pts✅ 辅助 Assisted每周 ≤ 120 分钟 ≤ 120 min/week两周 Two weeks

先想一个问题：你上过多少节课？几千节总有了。那么——其中有多少节，你是真的在学习？马克·吐温说：“我从来不会因为上学而影响我受教育。”上学和学习，是两码事。这两周我们不讲任何数字工具，只做一件事：把“学习”这件你每天都在做的事，看清楚。

Start with a question: how many lessons have you sat through? Thousands. In how many of them were you actually learning? Mark Twain said he never let his schooling interfere with his education. Going to school and learning are two different things. For two weeks we put the digital tools aside and do one thing only: look clearly at “learning” — something you do every day.

核心问题 Essential questions

  • 为什么我们应该学习？Why should we learn?
  • 什么是学习？What is learning?
  • 学习好的人有什么秘密武器吗？Do good learners have a secret weapon?

学完本单元，你应能 By the end of this module you should be able to

  1. 用自己的话界定“学习”，并举例区分有效与无效的学习策略。
    Define learning in your own words, with examples that separate effective from ineffective strategies.
  2. 讲述一次自己的成功学习经历，并归因于具体的方法或条件。
    Tell one successful learning experience of your own and attribute it to specific methods or conditions.
  3. 在“我的学习观”短文中至少引用读本中的两个概念。
    Cite at least two concepts from the reader in your “My view of learning” mini-essay.

这三条也是本单元量规的三行评分标准。These three are also the three rows of the module rubric → 量规 Rubric
