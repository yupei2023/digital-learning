任务 · 为课程账号完成安全设置Task · Secure your course accounts

截图 · 诊断 · 清单 · 规则卡Screenshot · diagnosis · checklist · card发布 + 登记链接Publish + register the link约 50 分钟（别赶）~50 min (unhurried)

要做什么What to do

给你的课程账号——第 1 周的邮箱、第 3 周的建站平台——逐个完成安全升级：救援通道先修好，换上强而唯一的密码，打开平台提供的两步验证或登录保护，用"三问"巡一遍隐私设置。然后在你的网站上发布一份安全升级报告：一张已打码的设置状态截图 + 一句自我诊断 + 三行安全清单 + 你的文件命名规则卡；最后把链接登记到登记表。这不是一次作业，这是你以后每换一个新账号都要重演的仪式。

Upgrade your course accounts one by one — Week 1's email and Week 3's site builder: repair the rescue channel first, fit each with a strong unique password, switch on whatever two-step verification or sign-in protection the platform offers, and sweep the privacy settings with the three questions. Then publish a security upgrade report on your site: one redacted screenshot of a settings status + one self-diagnosis sentence + the three-line security checklist + your file-naming rule card; finally register the link. This is not homework so much as the ceremony you will repeat for every new account you ever open.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 两个课程账号都照看到：邮箱 + 建站平台（愿意的话再加自己的常用账号，加多少是你的自由）。邮箱最先——它是其余一切的总后台。Both course accounts tended: email + site builder (add personal accounts beyond that at your own pleasure). Email first — it is the master office behind everything else.
  • 一张打码截图：任一账号的安全设置状态画面（例如"密码已更新""两步验证已开启"那一行）。照微课 4 的图打码：邮箱地址、手机号、二维码、恢复代码全部遮住；密码本身在任何截图里都不出现。遮到只剩一行状态也完全可以。One redacted screenshot: a security-settings status view from either account (say, the row reading "password updated" or "two-step verification ON"). Mask it per micro-lesson 4's figure: email addresses, phone numbers, QR codes and recovery codes all covered; the password itself appears in no screenshot, ever. Masking down to a single status row is perfectly acceptable.
  • 一句自我诊断 + 三行安全清单（写"做了什么"，绝不写密码内容）。先照四角图写一句：这四个对手，我这周挡住了哪几个？还有哪一个没挡，为什么？——四个里挡住两三个是很正常的一周；把没挡住的那个和原因如实写出来，与"四个全挡住了"同样达标。再写三行：① 我换了 __ 个账号的密码（哪几个）；② 两步验证 / 登录保护：我开在了 __ ——或如实写"平台没有此功能 / 我在 __ 找过没找到"；③ 隐私设置我检查了 __，改动了 __（网站"不被搜索收录"的开关复查在内；有一处拿不准、最后决定保留或清空的格子，把它和你的理由写进这一行）。One self-diagnosis sentence + the three-line security checklist (record what was done — never a password's content). First, one sentence written against the four-corner map: which of these four adversaries did I block this week? which one is still open, and why? — blocking two or three of the four is an ordinary week; naming the one you did not block, and why, meets the bar exactly as "all four" does. Then the three lines: ① I changed the passwords on __ accounts (which ones); ② two-step verification / sign-in protection: switched on at __ — or, honestly, "the platform lacks it / I searched __ and found none"; ③ privacy settings I reviewed: __, and changed: __ (the site's no-search-indexing switch re-checked among them; if one field left you unsure and you kept or cleared it in the end, write that field and your reason into this line).
  • 命名规则卡：一句适用于你全部文件的命名规则（在第 2 周"课程-周-内容-化名"基础上扩展）+ 两个真实文件的改名示例（改前 → 改后）+ 一行"它们住在哪"（文件夹或云端位置）。写法见下方教学卡。The naming rule card: one sentence of naming rule covering all your files (extending Week 2's course-week-content-pseudonym) + two real files renamed as evidence (before → after) + one line on where they live (folder or cloud location). The teaching card below shows how.
  • 已发布 + 已登记：报告发布在你网站的作品集页（新条目或新建一页都行），链接登记到登记表，任务编号 B4；本周三行日志照常另写在日志页。公开学习者：链接发进自己的邮箱（主题"我的安全升级报告 · 化名"）。Published + registered: the report lives on your site's Portfolio page (a new entry or a new page, either way), the link on the tracking sheet, task number B4; the week's three-line log still goes on the log page as usual. Open learners: email the link to yourself (subject "my security upgrade report · pseudonym").
  • 🚫 独立：清单与规则卡自己写；密码和验证码不进任何聊天框、翻译框或生成式 AI。设置过程可以请家长在旁——把关的是你，动手的也是你。🚫 Independent: the checklist and rule card written by you; passwords and verification codes enter no chat box, translation box or generative AI. A parent may sit alongside during setup — but the judging hand and the working hand are both yours.

先说保命的一条：救援通道第一，换锁第二The life-saving rule first: rescue channel before new locks
改密码的事故只有一种——新密码忘了、旧密码回不去。所以本任务的顺序不可颠倒：第 2 步（修救援通道）没做完，不动第 3 步（换密码）。彩排页的救援卡和"临时抄写规则"（头两周允许抄在纸上放家里，熟练后销毁）此刻正式生效。顺便点破一个以后处处能用的动作：先修后路，就是把"不可逆"改造成"可逆"——今后任何大改动之前，都先给自己留一条回头路。Password changes have exactly one accident: the new password forgotten, the old one gone. Hence this task's order is not negotiable: step 3 (new passwords) waits until step 2 (the rescue channel) is done. The rehearsal's rescue card and temporary-copy rule (two weeks on paper at home, destroyed once fluent) take effect now. And name the move once, for it serves everywhere hereafter: repairing the escape route first is how an irreversible action is rebuilt into a reversible one — before any big change from now on, first leave yourself a road back.

步骤Steps

  1. 列账号清单（4 分钟）：写下本周要照看的账号——邮箱、建站平台，加上你愿意顺手升级的个人账号。按重要程度排序：邮箱永远第一（微课 2 讲了为什么）。现在的密码还在家长手里的账号，先和家长约好一起动手的时间——本周哪天能开工，取决于这个约定。List the accounts (4 min): write down this week's charges — email, site builder, plus any personal accounts you care to upgrade along the way. Order them by importance: email always first (micro-lesson 2 explained why). Where a current password still lives with your parents, agree a time to sit down together first — which day the week's work can start hangs on that appointment.
  2. 修好救援通道（8 分钟）：把彩排第三幕"答不上来"的项补上——恢复邮箱还收得到信吗？密保答案记得吗？在账号的"安全设置"里把失效的恢复方式更新掉。这一步没完成，不进下一步。唯一的例外出口：这个账号没有任何可用的恢复方式、能补的又只有绑手机号（邮箱账号自己常是这样——它是别的账号的总后台，自己的后台却可能是空的）？那么"临时抄写规则"就是你的救援通道：动手改之前，先把现在这个密码按纸上规矩抄好（放家里固定安全处、不出门、不拍照），等新密码顺利登录过两次再销毁——然后就可以进第 3 步，并在三行清单里如实写明（第 ① 行末尾加半句"此账号无恢复方式，救援通道用的是纸上底稿"就行）。Repair the rescue channel (8 min): fix whatever drew a blank in rehearsal Act 3 — does the recovery inbox still receive? Are the security answers remembered? Update any dead recovery route inside the account's security settings. No moving on until this step stands. The one sanctioned exit: the account has no working recovery route at all, and the only addable one is a phone number (often true of the email account itself — the master office behind everything else, whose own backstage may be empty)? Then the temporary-copy rule IS your rescue channel: before changing anything, copy the current password onto paper under the paper rules (one fixed safe place at home, never leaving the house, never photographed), keep it until the new password has signed you in twice, then destroy it — proceed to step 3, and record it honestly in the checklist (half a sentence at the end of line ①: "this account has no recovery route; the paper copy was the rescue channel").
  3. 换上强而唯一的密码（12 分钟）：从邮箱开始，用微课 1 的四步给每个账号造各自的密码短语（彩排练过的手艺，这次的词只属于你，不写进任何公开的地方）。入口通常在"设置 → 账号安全 → 修改密码"。改一个，立即按救援卡写的"家"收好一个，再改下一个。Fit strong, unique passwords (12 min): starting with email, forge each account its own passphrase by micro-lesson 1's four steps (the rehearsed craft — this time the words are yours alone and written in no public place). The door is usually Settings → Account security → Change password. Change one, house it at once in the home your rescue card names, then take the next.
  4. 打开第二道门（10 分钟）：在每个账号的"账号安全"里找两步验证——也可能叫二次验证、登录保护、设备锁——找到就打开，按提示绑定你的恢复方式。建站平台的开关位置见开课信（各平台不同）。翻遍了没找到、或平台确实没有？如实写进清单第 ② 行，就算完成——诚实的记录和打开的开关同样达标（量规明文）。Open the second door (10 min): in each account's security area, hunt for two-step verification — possibly named secondary verification, sign-in protection or device lock — switch it on and bind your recovery route as prompted. The site builder's switch is described in the welcome letter (platforms differ). Searched everywhere and found none, or the platform truly lacks it? Write that honestly into checklist line ② and it counts as done — an honest record meets the bar exactly as a flipped switch does (the rubric says so in writing).
  5. 三问巡查隐私设置（9 分钟）：打开每个账号的个人资料页和隐私设置，用微课 4 的三问过一遍——这一格暴露了什么？谁能看到？拼起来能定位到我吗？选填的清空，公开范围收紧，头像和昵称按课程隐私四条办。这三个问题的答案由你判断——遇到拿不准的一格（比如平台请你绑定手机号，或一个说不清用途的开关），用三问做一次自己的决定，把这一格和你的决定写进证据（清单第 ③ 行）。最后复查你网站的"不被搜索引擎收录"开关（第 3 周设过的那个）。Sweep privacy with the three questions (9 min): open each account's profile page and privacy panels and walk them through micro-lesson 4's three questions — what does this field reveal? who can see it? pieced together, does it locate me? Empty the optional fields, tighten the visibility switches, hold avatar and nickname to the course's four privacy rules. The answers belong to your judgment — when a field resists the defaults (a platform asking to bind a phone number, a switch of unclear purpose), run it through the three questions, decide for yourself, and write that field and your decision into the evidence (checklist line ③). Finish by re-checking your website's no-search-indexing switch (the one set in Week 3).
  6. 取证、写卡、发布登记（7 分钟）：截一张设置状态图，照微课 4 的图打码；照四角图写下那句自我诊断，再写三行安全清单和命名规则卡（见下）；一起发布到网站的作品集页，重新点"发布"，用访客的眼睛看一遍（截图里真的没有漏网的隐私吗？），再把链接登记到登记表（公开学习者：发进自己的邮箱）。Evidence, card, publish, register (7 min): capture one settings-status screenshot and mask it per micro-lesson 4's figure; write the self-diagnosis against the four-corner map, then the three-line checklist and the naming rule card (below); publish them together on your site's Portfolio page, press Publish again, review it with a visitor's eyes (does the screenshot truly leak nothing?), then register the link (open learners: email it to yourself).

命名规则卡怎么写：给每个文件一个能被找到的名字Writing the naming card: a findable name for every file

第 2 周你给课程文件立过规矩：课程-周-内容-化名（数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-晨星.pdf）。它好用的原因不神秘：文件名回答了"这是什么、什么时候的、谁的"，三个月后照样一眼认出。本周把同一个原理推广到你的全部文件——模板：用途-时间-内容-谁，四段按需取舍，化名原则照旧（文件名里不用真名）。In Week 2 your course files got their rule: course-week-content-pseudonym (DL-W2-self-intro-Chenxing.pdf). Its magic is no mystery: the name answers what this is, when it was, and whose — still legible at a glance three months on. This week the same principle extends to all your files — template: purpose-time-content-who, four segments kept or dropped as needed, the pseudonym principle unchanged (no real names in filenames).

文件住哪：一门课一个家——比如"文档 / 数字化学习 / 第5周 /"；用云盘的，云端照同一套结构建文件夹，名字同一套规则。桌面不是家，是门厅：路过可以，过夜要收。你的规则卡三样齐：一句规则 + 两个真实改名（改前 → 改后）+ 一行"它们住在哪"。Where files live: one course, one home — say Documents / Digital Learning / Week 5 /; if you use a cloud drive, build the same structure there under the same naming rule. The desktop is not a home but a hallway: passing through is fine, staying overnight is not. Your rule card carries all three parts: the one-sentence rule + two real renames (before → after) + one line on where they live.

顺手一件事（选做）：讲义《账号安全卡》把本周全部要点——密码短语四步、一号一钥、四种"家"、第二道门、三问、永不外传清单、救援卡、文件命名——装进了一页可打印的卡。打一张贴在家里，全家都用得上。One small extra (optional): the handout Account security card packs the whole week — the four passphrase steps, one key per door, the four homes, the second door, the three questions, the never-share list, the rescue card, file naming — onto one printable page. Print one for the wall at home; the whole household can use it.

评分 · 量规第 ① ② 行（3 + 2 = 5 分）Scoring · Rubric rows ① ② (3 + 2 = 5 pts)

第 ① 行看安全升级本身：打码合格的状态截图、点到名的自我诊断、三行清单落到实处、已发布已登记——"平台没有两步验证，我找过 __"的诚实记录与"已开启"同分。第 ② 行看命名规则卡：规则一句 + 真实改名两例 + 住处一行。评的是把自己的数字世界收拾妥当的动作，不评文笔。完整量规Row ① scores the security upgrade itself: a properly masked status screenshot, a self-diagnosis that names its adversaries, a three-line checklist grounded in what happened, published and registered — the honest record "the platform lacks two-step verification; I searched __" scores the same as "switched on". Row ② scores the naming rule card: one rule sentence + two real renames + one line of address. What earns points is the act of putting your digital world in order, never the prose. 常见问题FAQ

我把自己锁在门外了——新密码想不起来。I locked myself out — the new password won't come back to me.

深呼吸：这正是第 2 步先修救援通道的原因，而你修过了。走"忘记密码"，用恢复邮箱或密保开门，再造一把新的（这次立刻按救援卡收好）。开不了门再邮件导师——附一张打码截图说明卡在哪一步。不扣分：锁门事故是本周教学计划的一部分，处理它就是在学它。

Breathe: this is exactly why step 2 repaired the rescue channel first — and you did repair it. Take the forgot-password door, open up with the recovery inbox or security answers, forge a new key (housed properly this time, per the rescue card). If the door still won't give, email your mentor with a masked screenshot of where it jammed. No deduction: the lockout accident is part of this week's syllabus, and handling it is learning it.

我的平台找不到两步验证的开关。My platform shows no two-step verification switch.

很正常——不是每个平台都有，叫法和位置也五花八门。先在"设置 → 账号安全"一带找齐这些名字：两步验证、二次验证、登录保护、设备锁。真没有，就在清单第 ② 行如实写"我在 __ 找过，平台没有此功能"——这行照样满分。你的搜索本身就是证据。

Entirely normal — not every platform offers one, and the names and locations wander. Search the Settings → Account security area for all the aliases: two-step verification, secondary verification, sign-in protection, device lock. If it truly is not there, write into checklist line ②, honestly, "I searched __; the platform lacks the feature" — the line still earns full marks. The search itself is the evidence.

截图里到处是敏感信息，越遮越没剩什么了。My screenshot is wall-to-wall sensitive details — masking leaves almost nothing.

遮就对了。证据只需要一样东西：一行看得出"做了"的状态（"密码已更新""两步验证已开启"）。哪怕整张图只剩那一行绿字、其余全是色块，也是合格的证据。宁可遮多，不可漏一——漏了的导师会提醒你改，不扣分，但要重新发布。

Then mask away. Evidence needs exactly one thing: a status row showing it happened ("password updated", "two-step verification ON"). A screenshot reduced to that single green line amid solid blocks is fully qualified evidence. Over-mask rather than under-mask — anything missed draws a mentor's reminder, costs nothing, but must be re-published.

家里的电脑是共用的，浏览器还老提示"保存密码"。Our home computer is shared, and the browser keeps offering to save passwords.

共用电脑上一律点"不保存"（跟做卡 6）。你的密码短语放脑子和家里的纸本就够。顺手做件好事：把这周学的讲给共用这台电脑的家人听——家里每个人的账号安全，就是你的账号安全。

On a shared machine, always decline (rehearsal card 6). Your passphrases live in your head and the paper notebook at home — that suffices. And do one good deed while you're there: teach this week's lessons to the family sharing that computer. Every household member's account security is also yours.

能不能把新密码写进报告，证明我真的换了？May I write the new password into the report, to prove I really changed it?

绝对不能。发布出来的密码等于没有密码——不管打没打码。证据永远是状态（"已更新"那一行），不是内容。报告里若出现任何真实密码，导师会立刻请你换掉它并重新发布。

Absolutely not. A published password is no password — masked or otherwise. Evidence is always the status (the "updated" row), never the content. Any real password appearing in a report will have your mentor asking you to change it at once and republish.

我是公开学习者，怎么"评分"？I am an open learner — how do I get scored?

发布报告并把链接发给自己之后，打开量规页逐行自评，分数记进日志。一个建议（选做）：给家里人当一回"安全顾问"，帮长辈检查有没有到处共用的密码——能把"撞库"讲明白，这一课就真的学会了。

After publishing and emailing yourself the link, self-score row by row against the rubric and note it in your log. One suggestion (optional): serve a shift as the family's security consultant and help an elder check for reused passwords — if you can explain credential stuffing clearly, the lesson is truly yours.
