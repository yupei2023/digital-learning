账号、密码与隐私Accounts, Passwords & Privacy

5 分5 pts🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min一周One week

开学到现在，你已经在数码世界安了两个家：第 1 周准备的邮箱，第 3 周上线的个人网站。上周你还学到一句要紧的话：能不给的信息就不给。你的教师在给家长的一篇文章里，把数码世界比作一个"陌生国度"——资源丰饶，但钓鱼网站和诈骗，可能只与你的日常生活隔着一"键"。进入陌生国度，安全第一。

Since the course began you have made two homes in the digital world: the email address readied in Week 1 and the personal website launched in Week 3. Last week added one weighty sentence: information you can withhold, withhold. In an essay for parents, your instructor once called the digital world a "foreign country" — rich in resources, yet with phishing pages and scams perhaps a single tap away from daily life. In a foreign country, safety comes first.

本周就做一件实事：给这两个家换上结实的锁。你会学到为什么"长"比"复杂"重要、为什么一个密码到处用才是真正的危险、密码多了放在哪里、两步验证是怎样的第二道门，还有一套受用终身的判断力——发出任何一张截图、填出任何一格信息之前，先问自己三个问题。

This week does one concrete thing: fits those two homes with solid locks. You will learn why length beats complexity, why one password everywhere is the real danger, where a crowd of passwords should live, how two-step verification adds a second door — and a piece of judgment that serves for life: before any screenshot travels or any form field is filled, ask yourself three questions.

核心问题Essential question

  • 我的账号安全吗？Are my accounts safe?

学完本周，你应能By the end of this week you should be able to

  1. ① 为课程账号（邮箱、建站平台）完成安全设置——换上强而唯一的密码、打开平台提供的两步验证或登录保护、检查隐私设置——并在自己的网站上发布一份"安全升级报告"：一张已打码的设置状态截图（绝不含密码本身）+ 一句照四角图写的自我诊断（这四个对手，我这周挡住了哪几个？还有哪一个没挡，为什么？）+ 三行安全清单（换了什么 · 开了什么 · 查了什么，不写具体密码）；链接登记到导师的登记表（公开学习者：把链接存进自己的邮箱）。① Complete the security setup on your course accounts (email, site builder) — a strong, unique password on each, the platform's two-step verification or sign-in protection switched on, privacy settings reviewed — and publish a "security upgrade report" on your own site: one redacted screenshot of a settings status (never the password itself) + one self-diagnosis sentence written against the four-corner map (which of the four adversaries did I block this week, which one is still open, and why?) + a three-line security checklist (what was changed · what was switched on · what was reviewed, with no actual password written); register the link on your mentor's tracking sheet (open learners: file the link in your own inbox).
  2. ② 写出你的"文件命名规则卡"：在第 2 周"课程-周-内容-化名"的基础上，定一句适用于你全部文件的命名规则，附两个真实文件的改名示例（改前 → 改后）和一行"它们住在哪"（文件夹或云端的位置），放进本周的报告。② Write your "file-naming rule card": building on Week 2's course-week-content-pseudonym rule, one sentence of naming rule for all your files, two real files renamed as evidence (before → after), and one line on where they live (the folder or cloud location), added to this week's report.
  3. ③（不计分 · 完成即可）完成本周的"安全彩排"跟做三幕：用一次性例子练造密码短语、给几条流传的密码说法分一次真假、把救援通道（恢复邮箱与"忘了新密码怎么办"卡）先备好。全程不动真账号，成果留在你自己的设备上。③ (ungraded · completing it is enough) The week's security-rehearsal follow-along, three acts: build a passphrase on a throwaway example, sort a handful of circulating password claims into true and false, and prepare the rescue channel (recovery email and the "what if I forget the new password" card) in advance. No real account is touched; everything stays on your own devices.

本周你还会学到：密码泄露和"撞库"是怎么回事、"永不外传"清单上有哪些信息（验证码在最前面）、密码管理器有哪几条路可选。这些不进量规——第 5 周页面的自测题帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: how password leaks and credential stuffing work, what belongs on the never-share list (verification codes at the top), and the routes a password manager can take. None of it is graded — the Week 5 self-quiz checks it for you, ungraded.

四个对手，四把锁Four adversaries, four locks

"我的账号安全吗"这个问题，要先问清楚"防谁"才答得出。本周四段微课其实是四个不同的回答——下面这张图把它们摆在一起看：四个角是四个对手，各有各的进门方式；每个角里那把锁，就是对付它的那一课。The question "are my accounts safe?" becomes answerable only after a second question: safe from whom? This week's four micro-lessons are four different answers — and the figure below sets them side by side: four corners, four adversaries, each with its own way in; the lock named inside each corner is the lesson that stops that one.

本单元的 AI 标注：🚫 独立AI designation: 🚫 Independent
本周不使用生成式 AI。还有一条本周专属的提醒：密码和验证码不该出现在任何输入框里——不进聊天软件、不进翻译框（上周那条规则的第三款）、更不进任何"帮你测密码强度"的陌生网站。你的密码只出现在两个地方：登录框，和你自己选定的保管处（微课 3 讲）。No generative AI this week. And one reminder unique to this module: passwords and verification codes belong in no input box — not in chat apps, not in translation boxes (clause three of last week's rule), and never on some stranger's "test your password strength" page. A password appears in exactly two places: the login form, and the home you chose for it (micro-lesson 3).
