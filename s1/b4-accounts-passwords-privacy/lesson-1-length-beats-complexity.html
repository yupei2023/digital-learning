长度胜过复杂Length beats complexity

P@ssw0rd! 看起来戒备森严——大写、符号、数字齐了——其实弱得很。为什么？因为猜你密码的不是人，是程序：它一秒钟能试上亿次，还背熟了人类爱用的所有花招（a→@、o→0、结尾加个 !）。对程序来说，真正的墙只有一堵：长度。密码每长一位，它要试的次数就翻几十倍——短而花哨的密码几分钟出局，长而朴素的密码它要试到天荒地老。

P@ssw0rd! looks heavily armed — capital, symbol, digit, all present — and is feeble. Why? Because the thing guessing your password is not a person but a program: it tries hundreds of millions of combinations a second and knows every human trick by heart (a→@, o→0, the ! tacked on the end). To a program only one wall matters: length. Every added character multiplies its work dozens of times over — a short fancy password falls in minutes, a long plain one outlasts the machine's patience.

密码短语：四步造一把长钥匙The passphrase: a long key in four steps

  1. 挑四个互不相干的词——越有画面越好：绿茶 · 风筝 · 台灯 · 骆驼。别挑和你有关的（宠物名、生日、学校），那些程序猜得最快。Pick four unrelated words — the more vivid the better: green-tea · kite · lamp · camel. Nothing about you (pet names, birthdays, school) — those the program guesses first.
  2. 连成一串，用拼音或英文都行，中间藏一个数字或大写：Lvcha7fengzhengTaidengLuotuo——28 位，程序面前是一堵高墙。String them together, in pinyin or English, a digit or capital tucked inside: Lvcha7fengzhengTaidengLuotuo — 28 characters, a high wall to any program.
  3. 在心里配一幅小画：一只骆驼开着台灯，喝着绿茶放风筝。画面越荒唐，你记得越牢——对程序毫无帮助，对你的记性帮助巨大。Give it a mental picture: a camel flying a kite by lamplight, sipping green tea. The more absurd the scene, the better you remember it — useless to the program, priceless to your memory.
  4. 量一量：12 位是及格线，16 位以上更安心。数一下第 2 步那串有多长，够了就成。Measure it: 12 characters is the passing line, 16 or more is real comfort. Count the string from step 2; long enough is done.
一件顶要紧的小事One small thing that matters greatly
上面那串骆驼密码不能真用——它印在一个公开网页上，等于贴在了城门口。凡是出现在书里、视频里、网页上的"示例密码"，都已经进了猜密码程序的字典。方法拿走，词自己挑，挑完不告诉任何人，也不写在任何公开的地方。The camel string above must never be used for real — printed on a public page, it might as well be posted on the city gate. Every "example password" that has appeared in a book, a video or a webpage is already in the guessing programs' dictionaries. Take the method, choose your own words, and once chosen, tell no one and write it in no public place.

两个流传很广的说法，趁早澄清Two widely circulated claims, cleared up early

说法The claim实情The reality
"密码必须塞满特殊符号才安全。""A password is only safe when stuffed with special symbols."长度重要得多。Tr0ub4dor&3 式的符号杂技难记又不经猜；四个词连成的长句好记而难破。近年各国网络安全机构的指南都转向了这一条：先求长，再谈花。Length matters far more. Symbol acrobatics like Tr0ub4dor&3 are hard to remember and easy to crack; four plain words strung long are the reverse. National cybersecurity guidelines in recent years have converged on exactly this: length first, decoration later.
"密码要定期全部换一遍。""All passwords should be changed on a regular schedule."新指南同样改了口：没有泄露迹象的强密码不必按日历硬换——频繁强制换密码，只会把人逼向"密码123 → 密码124"。该换的时刻只有一种：它可能泄露了（平台出事、异地登录提醒、或它和别的账号共用——微课 2 讲）。The newer guidelines reversed this too: a strong password with no sign of leaking needs no calendar-driven change — forced rotation only drives people to "password123 → password124". One moment alone demands a change: it may have leaked (a platform breach, a strange-location alert, or the password is shared across accounts — micro-lesson 2's subject).
