B4 账号、密码与隐私 · 第 5 周（数字基本功第 4 周，共 6 周）B4 Accounts, Passwords & Privacy · Week 5 (Digital Basics week 4 of 6)

先彩排，再换锁Rehearse first, then change the locks

任务 · 5 分Task · 5 pts🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min

请从上到下按顺序做。本周结束时，你的邮箱和网站会换上强而唯一的密码、开好第二道门，你的网站上会多出一份"安全升级报告"——而你会带走一套受用终身的判断力：三个问题，问完再发。Work top to bottom. By the end of this week your email and website will wear strong, unique passwords behind a second door, your site will hold a "security upgrade report" — and you will carry away a lifetime's piece of judgment: three questions, asked before anything travels.

0 · 本周为什么不先动手0 · Why this week does not start hands-first

前两周你都是先动手再上课：截图删得掉，译文重来不花钱——可逆的动作，先试后学。密码不一样：改急了、记岔了，会把自己锁在自己家门外；验证码给错一次，收不回来。不可逆的动作，先彩排后登台——第 2 周你给自己发测试邮件彩排过，本周同理。所以顺序是：四段微课 → 跟做"安全彩排"（全程不动真账号）→ 然后才是真账号的升级。别跳。For two weeks you leapt in first and studied after: screenshots delete, translations redo for free — reversible actions get tried before studied. Passwords differ: changed in haste and misremembered, they lock you out of your own house; a verification code given wrongly cannot be recalled. Irreversible actions get rehearsed before the stage — exactly as Week 2 had you rehearse on a test email to yourself. Hence this week's order: four micro-lessons → the security-rehearsal follow-along (no real account touched) → and only then the real upgrade. No skipping.

1 · 本周目标1 · This week's objectives

  • ① 为课程账号（邮箱、建站平台）完成安全设置——换上强而唯一的密码、打开平台提供的两步验证或登录保护、检查隐私设置——并在自己的网站上发布一份"安全升级报告"：一张已打码的设置状态截图（绝不含密码本身）+ 一句照四角图写的自我诊断（这四个对手，我这周挡住了哪几个？还有哪一个没挡，为什么？）+ 三行安全清单（换了什么 · 开了什么 · 查了什么，不写具体密码）；链接登记到导师的登记表（公开学习者：把链接存进自己的邮箱）。① Complete the security setup on your course accounts (email, site builder) — a strong, unique password on each, the platform's two-step verification or sign-in protection switched on, privacy settings reviewed — and publish a "security upgrade report" on your own site: one redacted screenshot of a settings status (never the password itself) + one self-diagnosis sentence written against the four-corner map (which of the four adversaries did I block this week, which one is still open, and why?) + a three-line security checklist (what was changed · what was switched on · what was reviewed, with no actual password written); register the link on your mentor's tracking sheet (open learners: file the link in your own inbox).
  • ② 写出你的"文件命名规则卡"：在第 2 周"课程-周-内容-化名"的基础上，定一句适用于你全部文件的命名规则，附两个真实文件的改名示例（改前 → 改后）和一行"它们住在哪"（文件夹或云端的位置），放进本周的报告。② Write your "file-naming rule card": building on Week 2's course-week-content-pseudonym rule, one sentence of naming rule for all your files, two real files renamed as evidence (before → after), and one line on where they live (the folder or cloud location), added to this week's report.
  • ③（不计分 · 完成即可）完成本周的"安全彩排"跟做三幕：用一次性例子练造密码短语、给六条流传的说法分真假、把救援通道先备好。全程不动真账号，成果留在你自己的设备上。③ (ungraded · completing it is enough) The week's security-rehearsal follow-along, three acts: build a passphrase on a throwaway example, sort six circulating claims into true and false, and prepare the rescue channel in advance. No real account is touched; everything stays on your own devices.

本周你还会学到：密码泄露和"撞库"是怎么回事、"永不外传"清单（验证码在最前面）、密码管理器的几条路。这些不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: how leaks and credential stuffing work, the never-share list (verification codes first), and the routes a password manager can take. None of it is graded — the self-quiz checks it for you, ungraded.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我怕改坏——不改行不行？现在也能登录啊。I'm afraid of breaking something — can't I just skip it? Everything logs in fine now.

"现在能登录"和"现在安全"是两回事：撞库试你密码的时候不会提前打招呼。本周的顺序已经把风险拆到最小——救援通道先修好，改坏了也回得来（任务页第一条 FAQ 专门讲"锁在门外了怎么办"）。而且这是计分任务：动手做，出问题算课程的教学内容，不算你的事故。

"Logs in fine" and "is safe" are different facts: credential stuffing sends no advance notice before trying your password. The week's ordering has already shrunk the risk to its minimum — the rescue channel is repaired first, so even a botched change walks back (the task page's first FAQ covers the lockout). And this is the graded task: do it — anything that goes wrong is course content, not your accident.

家长想帮我弄，或者想知道我的新密码。My parents want to do it for me, or to know my new password.

欢迎家长在旁边一起做——本周的内容对全家都有用。密码要不要给家长留一份，是你们家自己商量决定的事：未成年人的账号由家长保管一份备份，是很多家庭合理的安排；把备份写进家里的纸本就好。课程的规矩只管一条：密码不进网络——不发消息、不发邮件，包括发给家长。当面写在纸上。

Parents alongside are welcome — this week's content serves the whole household. Whether a parent keeps a copy of your password is your family's own decision: a guardian holding a minor's backup is a reasonable arrangement in many homes; write it into the household paper notebook. The course insists on one thing only: passwords never travel the network — no messages, no emails, not even to parents. Hand it over on paper, in person.

我别的账号也全是同一个密码，一下子全换太吓人了。All my other accounts share one password too — changing everything at once feels overwhelming.

不用一口气。本周硬性要求只有两个课程账号；其余按重要程度慢慢来：先邮箱（已做）、再涉及钱的、再社交，一周换一两个，一个月就换完了。方向对了，速度随你。

No need for one heroic sitting. The hard requirement is only the two course accounts; the rest go by importance at your own pace: email first (done), then anything touching money, then social — one or two a week finishes the lot inside a month. With the direction right, the speed is yours.

平台让我绑定手机号才能开两步验证——课程不是说不给手机号吗？The platform wants a phone number for two-step verification — doesn't the course say withhold phone numbers?

好问题，两条规则管两件事。"不要手机号"管的是注册新工具——能用邮箱就不给手机号，这条不变。而你已经在用的账号上，绑手机是平台的一项安全功能，绑不绑由你和家长商量（第 ② 问：这个号码交给了谁？）。不想绑就选"验证发到邮箱"的方式，或如实写进清单——都算完成。还有一种情况：这个账号自己就是那个邮箱——"验证发到邮箱"发的是它自己，绕了个圈。别卡在这里：走任务第 2 步写明的纸上出口（改之前先把现密码按纸上规矩抄好），同样算完成。

Good question — two rules governing two different things. "No phone numbers" governs signing up for new tools: where email suffices, the number is withheld, unchanged. On an account you already use, binding a phone is a security feature of the platform, and whether to bind is for you and your parents (question ②: who receives this number?). Prefer not to? Choose email-based verification, or record it honestly in the checklist — either counts as done. One more case: the account is itself that email — "send the code to the email" loops back to its own door. Do not stall there: take the paper exit written into task step 2 (copy the current password onto paper, under the paper rules, before changing anything), and that too counts as done.

收到短信说"您的账号存在异常，点此验证"。A text message says "unusual activity on your account — tap here to verify".

不点。短信里的链接是钓鱼最常用的门。要查真假，自己打开官方 App 或在浏览器里输入官网地址看账号状态——正门永远比短信里递来的门可靠。顺手把这条短信讲给家里人听：他们收到的版本可能更逼真。

Don't tap. Links inside text messages are phishing's favourite door. To check the claim, open the official app yourself or type the site's address into the browser and look at the account there — the front door always beats a door handed to you in a message. Then tell the family about the text: the version they receive may look even more convincing.

截图里有我的账号信息，怎么处理来着？My screenshot shows account details — what was the drill again?

第 2 周的手艺原样用：标注工具打码，或裁掉那一块。本周的分寸看微课 4 那张图——红处必遮（邮箱、手机号、二维码、恢复代码），绿处恰恰要留（"已开启"的状态行）。宁可遮多，不可漏一。

Week 2's craft, unchanged: mask with the annotation tool or crop the region away. This week's proportions are in micro-lesson 4's figure — red must be covered (addresses, numbers, QR, recovery codes), green is precisely what stays (the "ON" status row). Over-mask rather than under-mask.

这周正好赶上长假怎么办？What if a holiday week lands on top of this?

按课程的假期规则：长假那一周是缓冲周，不开新单元，用来补做和休息（具体见日历页）。账号不会跑——假期结束接着升级就是。没有迟交处罚。

The course's holiday rule applies: a holiday week is a buffer week — no new module, just catch-up and rest (see the calendar). Your accounts will wait; resume the upgrade after the break. No late penalty.
