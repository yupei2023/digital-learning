发出之前的三个问题Three questions before anything travels

第 3 周你学过一句判断：发布内容前先问「这条信息能帮陌生人定位到真实的我吗？」那时它管的是你网站上的文字和照片。本周把同一个判断放大到一切要出门的信息——注册表单里的一格、个人资料页的一栏、发出去的一张截图。规则背不完，判断力可以带走：三个问题，问完再发。

In Week 3 you learned one judgment: before publishing, ask "could this information help a stranger locate the real me?" Back then it governed the words and photos on your website. This week the same judgment widens to everything that leaves your hands — a field in a sign-up form, a row on a profile page, a screenshot about to travel. Rules run out; judgment travels with you: three questions, asked before anything is sent.

三个问题The three questions

  1. ① 这一格暴露了什么？别看表单让你填什么，看你交出了什么：昵称格里写了真名？头像是正脸照？"所在地"精确到了区？上周的习惯在这里升级——能不给的信息就不给，选填的格子默认留空。① What does this field reveal? Look not at what the form requests but at what you would surrender: a real name inside the nickname box? A face photo as the avatar? A "location" precise to the district? Last week's habit graduates here — information you can withhold, withhold, and optional fields stay empty by default.
  2. ② 谁能看到它？同一条信息，去处不同，分量完全不同：发给导师的邮件只有一个人看；填进注册表单，是交给了那家公司的服务器（上周"不把个人信息粘进翻译框"的原因，正在这里——粘进任何网页框的文字都会上传，而且收不回）；发布到网站上，就是给了全世界。发之前，把去处想一遍。② Who can see it? The same information weighs differently by destination: an email to your mentor reaches one person; a sign-up form hands it to that company's servers (here at last is the reason behind last week's "no personal information in the translation box" — text pasted into any web box is uploaded, and cannot be recalled); publishing it on your site gives it to the world. Trace the destination before sending.
  3. ③ 和别的信息拼起来，能不能找到真实的我？这是最容易漏的一问。单独看都无害的碎片，拼起来就是坐标：校服照片 + 小区门口的街景 + 生日蛋糕的日期 = 陌生人能找到你本人。所以判断一条信息，别只看它自己，要看它和你已经公开的一切加在一起是什么。③ Pieced together with everything else, does it locate the real me? The most-missed question of the three. Fragments harmless alone become coordinates together: a school-uniform photo + the street view at the compound gate + the date on a birthday cake = a stranger finding the actual you. Judge no piece of information by itself — judge it added to everything you have already made public.

三问不是让你什么都不发——这门课整年都在请你发布作品。它是把"发什么、不发什么"的决定权，从习惯手里交回你的判断手里。三问守住的还有一样东西：追不到真人的页面，才敢写真话——隐私护的不只是你的安全，还有你日志里的诚实。第 3 周建站时你读过这句话，本周它有了完整的理由。The three questions are not a ban on sharing — this course spends the whole year asking you to publish work. They move the decision of what travels and what stays out of habit's hands and back into your judgment's. And the three questions guard one more thing: only a page that cannot be traced to the real you dares to tell the truth — privacy protects not just your safety but the honesty of your log. You read that line when your site was born in Week 3; this week it carries its full reason.

用三问巡一遍你的隐私设置Sweep your privacy settings with the three questions

每个账号都有一页"个人资料"和几页"隐私设置"——那就是三问的练兵场。本周任务会带你巡查课程账号：资料页的每一格（头像、昵称、生日、地区……）过一遍第 ① 问；每个"谁可以看"的开关过一遍第 ② 问；最后把你的网站不被搜索引擎收录的开关复查一次——第 3 周设过它，它是第 ② 问最大的一个开关。Every account carries a profile page and a few privacy panels — the three questions' training ground. This week's task walks the course accounts: every profile field (avatar, nickname, birthday, region…) through question ①; every who-can-see switch through question ②; and finally a re-check of your website's no-search-indexing switch — set in Week 3, it is question ②'s single biggest lever.
