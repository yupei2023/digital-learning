账号安全卡The account security card

开新账号、换新设备、听到泄露消息的时候，把这张卡拿出来。它管的不只是这门课——是你以后的每一个账号。

Take this card out whenever a new account opens, a new device arrives, or leak news breaks. It governs not just this course but every account you will ever own.

这一栏Section内容What it says一句话记住Remember it as
密码短语四步The passphrase, four steps① 挑四个互不相干、画面感强的词（与你无关）→ ② 拼音或英文连成一串，中间藏个数字或大写 → ③ 心里配一幅荒唐小画 → ④ 量长度：12 位及格，16 位安心。书里网页上出现过的示例不能用。① Four unrelated vivid words (nothing about you) → ② strung in pinyin or English, a digit or capital tucked inside → ③ one absurd mental picture → ④ measure: 12 passes, 16 comforts. Examples ever printed anywhere are unusable.长度胜过复杂；荒唐才好记。Length beats complexity; absurd is memorable.
一号一钥One key per door泄露多发生在网站那头，你拦不住；你能决定丢一把钥匙丢几扇门。重要账号各配各的密码，邮箱最先——它能重置其余一切。听到泄露消息：改该账号 → 改所有共用过的 → 邮箱优先。Leaks happen at the website's end, beyond your control; what you decide is how many doors one lost key opens. Important accounts get individual passwords, email first — it resets everything else. On leak news: change that account → every account that shared → email first.复用才是真正的危险。Reuse is the real danger.
密码的四种"家"The four homes脑子（少数最重要的）· 家里的纸本（不出门、不夹课本、不拍照）· 浏览器（前提：自己的设备 + 有锁；共用电脑点"不保存"）· 密码管理器（KeePassXC 离线免费无需账号；Bitwarden 只需邮箱、待实测）。永远不放：聊天记录、云文档、备忘录明文、陌生"测强度"网站。Memory (the few that matter most) · the paper notebook at home (never leaves, never in a textbook, never photographed) · the browser (your own locked device only; decline on shared machines) · a manager app (KeePassXC offline, free, accountless; Bitwarden email-only, to verify). Never-homes: chats, cloud docs, plain notes, strangers' "strength testers".给每把钥匙一个家，家不上网。Every key gets a home; the home stays offline.
第二道门The second door两步验证 = 密码（你知道的）+ 你拿着的东西（发到邮箱/手机的确认）。开关多在"设置 → 账号安全"，名字五花八门：两步验证、二次验证、登录保护、设备锁。找不到就如实记下——诚实的记录同样算完成。Two-step verification = the password (something you know) + something you hold (a confirmation to inbox or phone). The switch lives around Settings → Account security under many names: two-step, secondary verification, sign-in protection, device lock. Not found? Record it honestly — that counts as done.小偷捡到钥匙，还差你手里那道门。A thief with the key still lacks the door in your hand.
发出之前的三问The three questions① 这一格暴露了什么？（选填默认留空）② 谁能看到它？（发给一个人 ≠ 交给一家公司 ≠ 给全世界；粘进网页框的收不回）③ 和我已公开的一切拼起来，能不能定位到真实的我？（碎片拼起来就是坐标）。① What does this field reveal? (optional fields stay empty) ② Who can see it? (one person ≠ a company's servers ≠ the world; what enters a web box cannot be recalled) ③ Pieced with everything already public, does it locate the real me? (fragments become coordinates).三个问题，问完再发。Three questions, asked before it travels.
永不外传清单The never-share list密码（好朋友也不给）· 验证码（真客服永远不要它；来要的都是骗局）· 身份证号 · 家庭住址 · 学校全名 · 正脸照片 · 他人的个人信息。短信里的链接不点，要查就自己打开官方入口。Passwords (best friends included) · verification codes (genuine service never asks; whoever asks is a scam) · ID numbers · home address · full school name · face photos · other people's information. Links inside text messages stay untapped; check claims through the official door you open yourself.无论对方是谁，理由多动听——不给。Whoever asks, however moving the reason — no.
救援卡与临时抄写Rescue card & temporary copies换锁前先确认"忘记密码"的门在哪、恢复方式还活着。救援卡（纸上）：账号名 · 密码的"家"在哪 · 忘了去哪找回——卡上不写密码。新密码头两周可抄在纸上放家里固定安全处，用熟销毁。Before changing a lock, confirm where the forgot-password door stands and that the recovery route breathes. The rescue card (paper): account name · which home holds the password · where to recover — no password on the card. A new password may live two weeks on paper in one fixed safe place at home, destroyed once fluent.救援通道第一，换锁第二。Rescue channel first, new locks second.
文件的名字与家Files: names and homes模板"用途-时间-内容-谁"（课程文件即"课程-周-内容-化名"），四段按需取舍，文件名不用真名。一门课一个家（文件夹），云端同一套结构同一套名字。桌面是门厅：路过可以，过夜要收。Template purpose-time-content-who (course files: course-week-content-pseudonym), segments kept as needed, no real names in filenames. One course, one home folder; the cloud mirrors the same structure and names. The desktop is a hallway: passing yes, overnight no.名字答"是什么·何时·谁的"，三个月后见分晓。A name answers what, when, whose — three months later tells.
求助的固定姿势The standing way to ask for help
一张标注好的截图（红框圈重点 · 隐私先打码 · 永远不含密码或验证码）+ 三句话：我想做什么 · 我做了什么 · 出现了什么。主题照旧："事由 · 化名（学生编号）"。One annotated screenshot (red box on the point, private parts masked first, never containing a password or code) + three sentences: what I tried · what I did · what happened. Subject as always: matter · pseudonym (student ID).
