B4 量规B4 Rubric

量规的三行就是本周的三条目标。第 ① ② 行计分（3 + 2 = 5 分），第 ③ 行不计分、完成即可。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。本任务评的是把账号照看妥当的动作与记录的诚实——"平台没有两步验证，我找过"与"已开启"同样达标；不评文笔，不评你用的设备新旧。

The three rows are this week's three objectives. Rows ① ② carry points (3 + 2 = 5); row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence. What this task scores is the act of tending your accounts and the honesty of the record — "the platform lacks two-step verification; I searched" meets the bar exactly as "switched on" does. Prose is never scored, nor is the age of your devices.

本周的知识点（泄露与撞库、永不外传清单、密码管理器的几条路）不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。隐私规则是全课程的底线（见下方"规范要求"）：截图误放了提醒改正，不扣分；但真实密码出现在任何发布物里，按安全事故处理——立即换掉那个密码并重新发布。This week's knowledge points (leaks and stuffing, the never-share list, the manager routes) are not scored here — the self-quiz checks them, ungraded. The privacy rules are the course-wide floor (Form requirements below): a slip in a screenshot draws a reminder and a fix, never a deduction; but a real password appearing in anything published is treated as a security incident — that password is changed at once and the piece republished.
