密码放哪里，和第二道门Where passwords live, and the second door

前两课把你逼进了一个真问题：密码要长、还要一号一钥——那几十把钥匙，放哪里？靠脑子硬背所有密码的人，最后都会走回"到处用同一个"的老路。真正管用的答案是：给密码安一个家。家有好几种，各有各的代价，选适合你的那种。

The last two lessons cornered you into a real question: passwords must be long AND one per account — so where do all those keys live? Everyone who tries to memorise every password ends up back on the same-password-everywhere road. The working answer: give your passwords a home. Homes come in several kinds, each with its own price; choose the one that fits you.

四种"家"，各有代价Four homes, each with its price

选"家"之前先学会一问：这把锁防的是谁？——是猜密码的程序（微课 1）、"网站那头"的失窃（微课 2）、打电话来要验证码的骗子，还是身边拿得到你设备的人。下表每一行的"代价"，其实都是这一问的答案。Before choosing a home, learn one question: whom is this lock against? — the guessing program (lesson 1), the theft at the website's end (lesson 2), the caller after your verification code, or the person nearby who can reach your device. Every "price" in the table below is really an answer to that question.

家The home好在哪里The good代价与前提（诚实版）The price, honestly
脑子 + 密码短语Memory + passphrase零依赖、零成本；配上微课 1 的"荒唐小画"，两三个账号完全记得住。Zero dependencies, zero cost; with micro-lesson 1's absurd mental picture, two or three accounts are perfectly memorable.只够管少数几个最重要的账号（邮箱必在其中）；账号一多就撑不住。Scales only to a few top accounts (email among them); beyond that it collapses.
纸质密码本A paper notebook别小看它——写在纸上、放在家里固定的安全处，网上的小偷永远够不着。对中学生是完全体面的选择。Do not scorn it — written on paper and kept in one fixed safe place at home, it sits forever beyond any online thief's reach. An entirely respectable choice for a teenager.防的是网络，防不了身边——所以不带出家门、不夹进课本、不贴屏幕、不拍照存进手机相册（拍了照，纸本的全部好处就清零了）。和别人同住一间？"安全处"的标准是不被随手看到，不是保险箱——带锁的抽屉、家里放证件的地方、一本只有你会翻的书都算；实在没有这样的地方，就和家长商量一个，或走"脑子 + 临时抄写"的路。It guards against the network, not the room — so it never leaves home, never rides inside a textbook, never sticks to a screen, and is never photographed into a phone gallery (one photo cancels every advantage paper had). Sharing a bedroom? The standard for the safe place is not casually seen, never "vault" — a drawer that locks, the spot where the family keeps its documents, a book only you ever open all qualify; if no such place exists, agree one with a parent, or take the memory-plus-temporary-copy road.
浏览器自带的密码管理The browser's built-in manager最顺手：Edge、Chrome、Safari 都会主动提出"保存密码"，下次自动填好，还能替你生成强密码、提醒你哪个密码泄露过。The most convenient: Edge, Chrome and Safari all offer to save passwords, autofill them next time, generate strong ones for you, and warn when a saved password shows up in a leak.一个硬前提：这台设备是你自己的，而且有锁屏密码。在家人共用、没有分开账户的电脑上，任何人打开浏览器就等于拿到你的全部钥匙——共用电脑上请不要点"保存"。One hard precondition: the device is yours and it locks. On a shared family computer without separate user accounts, anyone opening the browser holds all your keys — on shared machines, decline the "save" offer.
密码管理器软件Dedicated manager apps整箱钥匙一个主密码管住。两个课程核对过的免费选项：KeePass（推荐桌面版 KeePassXC）——完全离线、开源免费、不需要注册任何账号；Bitwarden——免费、只需邮箱注册、多设备同步 待实测to verify。两者都不要手机号。The whole keychest behind one master password. Two free options the course has vetted on paper: KeePass (desktop edition KeePassXC recommended) — fully offline, open-source, free, no account of any kind; and Bitwarden — free, email-only sign-up, multi-device sync 待实测to verify. Neither asks for a phone number.KeePass：密码库文件自己保管，主密码忘了无人能救，跨设备同步要自己动手。Bitwarden：官网并不是每个地方都打得开，课程也未实测——你那里打得开再用（和 DeepL 一个待遇）；打不开毫无损失。两者都是进阶选项，本周任务不要求装任何软件。KeePass: you guard the vault file yourself, a forgotten master password is beyond all rescue, and syncing across devices is your own project. Bitwarden: its site does not open from everywhere, and the course has not tested it — use it only if it opens where you are (the DeepL treatment); if it won't, you lose nothing. Both are advanced options, and this week's task requires installing nothing.
这门课的推荐（两三个课程账号足够用）This course's recommendation (enough for two or three course accounts)
邮箱的密码短语记在脑子里；全部账号在家里的纸本上留个底。自己的电脑上可以让浏览器帮忙记不那么要紧的账号。想升级成整箱管理，先从 KeePassXC 试起（"想学更多"有入门视频）。永远不放的地方：聊天记录、云文档、手机备忘录明文、以及任何来路不明的"密码强度测试"网站。Keep the email passphrase in your head; keep a backstop of all accounts in the paper notebook at home. On your own computer, let the browser carry the less critical accounts. When ready to upgrade to a full keychest, start with KeePassXC (Want-more has a primer video). Never-homes: chat histories, cloud documents, plain phone notes, and any stranger's "password strength tester" page.

第二道门：两步验证The second door: two-step verification

再强的密码也只是一道门。两步验证加上第二道：登录时除了密码（你知道的东西），还要一样你拿着的东西——发到你邮箱或手机的确认、你按下的"是我本人"。小偷就算捡到了钥匙，还是进不来，因为第二道门在你手里。However strong, a password is still only one door. Two-step verification adds a second: signing in takes not just the password (something you know) but something you hold — a confirmation sent to your inbox or phone, an "it's me" you approve. A thief who picks up the key still cannot enter, because the second door is in your hand.

开关在哪里？各平台叫法不一：两步验证、二次验证、登录保护、设备锁……位置也各异，但几乎都住在"设置 → 账号安全"一带。本周任务会带你在自己的邮箱里找一遍；建站平台的对应开关，开课信里写明（各平台不同）。找不到、或平台确实没有这项功能？如实记下来就算完成——量规写明了这条诚实通道。Where is the switch? Platforms name it differently — two-step verification, secondary verification, sign-in protection, device lock — and place it differently, but it nearly always lives around Settings → Account security. This week's task walks you through finding it in your own email account; the site builder's switch is described in the welcome letter (platforms differ). Can't find it, or the platform truly lacks the feature? Recording that honestly counts as done — the rubric spells out this honest route.

锁讲完了。最后一课讲的不是锁，是判断——哪些信息本来就不该出门。So much for locks. The final lesson is not about locks at all, but about judgment — which information should never leave the house in the first place.
