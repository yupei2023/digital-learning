做Do

  • 给家里做一次密码体检——问家里人一个问题："你有几个账号共用同一个密码？"然后把撞库的道理讲给他们听，陪他们把最重要的那个账号（多半是支付或邮箱）换成独有的密码短语。能教会别人，才算真的会。Run the family password check-up — ask at home: "how many of your accounts share one password?" Explain credential stuffing, then sit with them while the most important account (usually payments or email) gets its own passphrase. Teaching it is the proof of knowing it.
  • 立一条验证码家规——和家里人约定一句话："谁来要验证码都不给，家里人也先当面确认。"贴在路由器旁边也不为过。骗局最爱找的不是你，是家里最不设防的那个人。Set the family verification-code rule — agree one sentence at home: "no one gets a verification code, and even family confirms face to face first." Posting it beside the router would not be excessive. Scams hunt not you but the least-guarded person in the house.
  • 清理一个旧账号——想一个你早就不用的账号（旧游戏、旧论坛）。还登得上的：改成独有密码、清掉个人资料，或干脆注销。这叫"缩小攻击面"：门少了，锁才看得过来。Retire one old account — recall an account long abandoned (an old game, an old forum). If it still opens: give it a unique password and strip the profile, or close it outright. This is called shrinking the attack surface: fewer doors, and the locks stay watchable.
  • 下载文件夹改名十连——打开"下载"文件夹，挑十个最近的文件，按你的新规则一口气改名、归位。计时——多半不到十分钟，而三个月后的你会来道谢。The downloads-folder rename streak — open Downloads, take the ten most recent files, and in one sitting rename and re-home them by your new rule. Time it — usually under ten minutes, and the you of three months hence sends thanks.
