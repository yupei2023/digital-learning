安全彩排The security rehearsal

前两周你都是先动手再上课——因为截图删得掉、译文重来不花钱。本周反过来：改密码这件事带着"不可逆"的脾气，改得急了会把自己锁在门外。所以先在这里彩排：三幕都在"演习场"里做，不动任何真账号，错多少次都免费。彩排完，明天再拿真账号开工（本周任务）。

For two weeks you leapt in hands-first — because screenshots delete and translations redo for free. This week reverses the order: changing passwords has an irreversible streak, and a rushed change can lock you out of your own door. So rehearse here first: all three acts happen on the practice ground, no real account touched, every mistake free of charge. Rehearsal done, the real accounts open for business tomorrow (this week's task).

第一幕 · 用一次性例子造密码短语（8 分钟）Act 1 · Build a passphrase on a throwaway example (8 min)

什么样的密码最安全？我们应该如何设置密码？ · 2:15 · B 站 UP 主 雷雨室长 · 2022（计入跟做分钟）——动手前两分钟热身：强弱密码再对照一遍。What makes a password safe, and how should we set one? · 2:15 · Bilibili uploader 雷雨室长 · 2022 (counted in the follow-along minutes) — a two-minute warm-up laying weak and strong side by side before your hands start. · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗

  1. 设定一个虚构委托人：化名"晨星"的同学要给图书馆账号配一把新钥匙。你来当锁匠——因为是虚构账号，这把钥匙永远不会真用，练坏了也没关系。Meet your fictional client: a student pseudonymed Chenxing needs a new key for a library account. You are the locksmith — the account is fictional, so this key will never be used for real, and ruining it costs nothing.
  2. 走微课 1 的四步：挑四个互不相干、画面感强的词 → 拼音或英文连成一串、中间藏个数字或大写 → 心里配一幅荒唐小画 → 数长度（12 位及格，16 位安心）。写在草稿纸上，改到满意。Walk micro-lesson 1's four steps: four unrelated, vivid words → strung together in pinyin or English with a digit or capital tucked inside → one absurd mental picture → count the length (12 passes, 16 comforts). Draft on scratch paper and revise until it pleases you.
  3. 自检三条：和"晨星"本人无关吗？不在任何书、网页、视频里出现过吗？明天还记得住那幅小画吗？三个"是"，锁匠出师。把草稿撕掉——练习密码用完即毁，这个习惯也是彩排的一部分。Three self-checks: nothing about Chenxing personally? Never printed in any book, page or video? Will the little picture still be there tomorrow? Three yeses and the locksmith is certified. Tear up the draft — practice passwords are destroyed after use, and that habit is part of the rehearsal too.

第二幕 · 老说法分检台（9 分钟）Act 2 · The claim-sorting bench (9 min)

下面六张卡片，每张一条流传已久的说法。先自己判：真还是假？心里有了答案再点开看。全对不稀奇，错了更值——错的那张就是你带进本周任务的护身符。Six cards below, each carrying a long-circulated claim. Judge first — true or false? — then click open to check. A perfect score is unremarkable; a miss is worth more — the card you missed is the charm you carry into this week's task.

卡 1 · "我的密码从没告诉过任何人，所以不可能泄露。"Card 1 · "I never told my password to anyone, so it cannot have leaked."

假。这是一条流传最久的老观念——泄露大多不发生在你这头，而发生在网站那头：平台被偷了数据库，你守口如瓶也没用。所以微课 2 的结论才成立：你防不住泄露，但能用"一号一钥"决定泄露的代价。

False. The oldest myth of them all — most leaks happen not at your end but at the website's: the platform's database is stolen, and your perfect silence is beside the point. Hence micro-lesson 2's conclusion: you cannot prevent the leak, but one-key-per-account decides what the leak costs.

卡 2 · "密码够复杂，就可以放心地到处用同一个。"Card 2 · "A complex enough password can safely be used everywhere."

假。再强的钥匙，配了十把就等于放了十处。撞库试的不是强度，是"同一对邮箱 + 密码在别处灵不灵"。强度救不了复用。

False. The strongest key, copied ten times, lies in ten places. Credential stuffing does not test strength — it tests whether the same email-password pair works elsewhere. Strength cannot rescue reuse.

卡 3 · "密码必须每个月换一次才安全。"Card 3 · "Passwords must be changed monthly to stay safe."

假。各国安全机构的新指南都放弃了这条：硬换只会逼出"密码123 → 密码124"。该换的时刻只有"它可能泄露了"。

False. National security guidelines have all dropped this one: forced rotation just breeds "password123 → password124". The only moment that demands a change is "it may have leaked".

卡 4 · "自称客服的人打电话来核对验证码，配合一下没关系。"Card 4 · "Someone calling themselves customer service asks to verify your code — cooperating is harmless."

假，而且是最危险的一张卡。验证码等于限时万能钥匙；真客服永远不需要它。来要的一律是骗局——挂断，不解释。

False — and the most dangerous card on the bench. A verification code is a time-limited master key; genuine customer service never needs it. Whoever asks is running a scam — hang up, no explanation owed.

卡 5 · "把密码写在纸上，太老土，肯定不安全。"Card 5 · "Writing passwords on paper is hopelessly backward and obviously unsafe."

假。放在家里固定安全处的纸本，网上的小偷永远碰不到——它防的正是最大的那个威胁。真正不安全的是纸本离家：贴屏幕、夹课本、拍照存进手机。

False. A notebook in one fixed safe place at home sits beyond every online thief's reach — it guards against precisely the largest threat. What is unsafe is the notebook leaving home: stuck to a screen, tucked in a textbook, photographed into a phone.

卡 6 · "浏览器弹出'保存密码'，在哪台电脑上都可以放心点。"Card 6 · "When the browser offers to save a password, it is safe to accept on any computer."

假。前提是"这台设备是你自己的、而且有锁"。在家人共用、不分账户的电脑上点了"保存"，等于把钥匙串挂在了客厅——共用电脑上请点"不保存"。

False. The precondition is "the device is yours and it locks". Accepting on a shared family computer without separate user accounts hangs your keyring in the living room — on shared machines, decline.

第三幕 · 先备好救援通道（8 分钟）Act 3 · Prepare the rescue channel first (8 min)

锁匠换锁前，先确认窗户能开——万一新锁出问题，人不能困在屋里。明天动真账号之前，把三样救援先备好：Before changing a lock, a locksmith checks that a window opens — if the new lock misbehaves, nobody gets trapped inside. Before tomorrow touches real accounts, three rescues get readied:

  1. 找到"找回密码"的门：打开你邮箱的登录页（只看不改），找到"忘记密码"链接，点进去看一眼它会问你什么——发确认到恢复邮箱？回答密保问题？看清楚就退出来。知道救援门在哪，明天才敢换锁。Find the forgot-password door: open your email provider's login page (look, change nothing), find the "forgot password" link, and peek at what it would ask — a confirmation to a recovery address? Security questions? Then back out. Knowing where the rescue door stands is what makes tomorrow's lock-change safe.
  2. 确认恢复方式还活着：如果账号设过恢复邮箱或密保，问自己——那个邮箱现在还收得到信吗？密保答案还记得吗？答不上来的，明天任务第 2 步会先修这一项。恢复邮箱那一栏本来就是空的、密保也从没设过？在邮箱账号上这很常见——邮箱是别的账号的总后台，它自己的后台却常常空着。不用慌：任务第 2 步为这种情况写明了一条纸上的出口（改之前先把现密码抄进家里的纸本），你不会被锁在门口。Confirm the recovery route still breathes: if the account has a recovery address or security questions, ask — does that inbox still receive mail? Are the answers still remembered? Whatever draws a blank becomes task step 2 tomorrow. Recovery address empty and security questions never set? Common for the email account itself — the master office behind every other account often has an empty backstage of its own. No alarm: task step 2 spells out a paper exit for exactly this case (the current password goes onto paper at home before anything changes), so the door will not shut on you.
  3. 写"救援卡"（纸上，留着明天用）：每个课程账号一行——账号名 · 新密码打算放在哪个"家"（微课 3 选的那种，顺手写半句为什么——这把锁防的是谁）· 忘了去哪里找回。卡上不写密码本身。另外记下临时抄写规则：换新密码的头两周，允许把它抄在纸上、放家里固定安全处，用熟了就销毁——绝不贴屏幕、不夹进要带出门的本子、不拍照。Write the rescue card (on paper, kept for tomorrow): one line per course account — the account name · which "home" the new password will live in (the kind chosen in micro-lesson 3 — add half a sentence on why: whom is this lock against?) · where to recover it if forgotten. The password itself never appears on the card. Note the temporary-copy rule too: for the first two weeks after a change, the new password may be written on paper kept in one fixed safe place at home, destroyed once fluent — never stuck to a screen, never inside a notebook that leaves the house, never photographed.
彩排完毕Rehearsal complete
你现在会造长钥匙、拆穿老说法、备好了救援通道——三样都留在草稿和纸卡上，没有动过任何真账号。明天开工：本周任务按"救援先行 → 换密码 → 第二道门 → 隐私巡查"的顺序，把真账号逐个升级。You can now forge a long key, see through the old claims, and your rescue channel stands ready — all of it on scratch paper and cards, no real account touched. Tomorrow the work begins: this week's task upgrades the real accounts in order — rescue first → passwords → the second door → the privacy sweep.
