想好词，再搜索Choose your words, then search

§0 你已经见过那条大街了。为什么同一个问题，有人一搜就中，有人翻三页还空手？差别多半不在运气，而在喂给搜索引擎的词。搜索引擎不是人：它不懂客气话，不猜你的心思，只拿你给的词去几十亿个网页里做匹配。所以本周的第一门手艺是：把心里的问题，翻译成机器读得懂的几个词。

In §0 you saw the street for yourself. Why does one person strike a useful result at once while another turns three pages and comes back empty-handed? Rarely luck — usually the words fed to the engine. A search engine is not a person: it hears no politeness, guesses no intentions, and simply matches your words against billions of pages. So the week's first craft is this: translating the question in your head into a few words a machine can read.

把问题拆成词：三步Breaking a question into words: three steps

  1. 删掉客套和口水："请问一下大家""到底是怎么回事呀"——这些词机器全当噪音。心里的问题："我们家猫为什么总在半夜疯跑？"Delete the politeness and the padding: "excuse me everyone", "what on earth is going on" — all noise to the machine. The question in your head: "why does our cat always go wild in the middle of the night?"
  2. 留下具体的名词和关键动作，一般三四个词最顺手：对象 + 现象 + 你要的东西。上面的问题拆出来就是：猫 夜间 活跃 原因。太少（只搜"猫"）会淹死在结果里；太多（整句照抄）会一无所获。Keep the concrete nouns and the key action — three or four words sit best: the thing + the behaviour + what you want. The question above breaks into: cat nocturnal activity reason. Too few words (just "cat") and you drown; too many (the whole sentence verbatim) and you starve.
  3. 用"写文章的人"的词，不用你自己的词。你管它叫"疯跑"，兽医的文章会写"夜间活跃"或"应激行为"。搜索是在找别人写下的页面——所以要猜：懂行的人会怎么称呼这件事？猜不出？先随便搜一次，从结果标题里把行话捡回来，再搜第二次。Use the words of whoever wrote the page, not your own. You call it "going wild"; a vet's article says "nocturnal activity" or "stress behaviour". Searching means finding pages other people wrote — so guess: what would someone who knows call this? Can't guess? Search once anyway, harvest the trade words from the result titles, and search again.

一次搜索，不是搜一次One search is not a single search

上面第 3 步藏着本周最有用的一句话：猜不出行话，就先随便搜一次，从结果标题里把词捡回来，再搜第二次。会搜的人不是第一次就中，而是愿意搜第二次、第三次——搜索是个回路，不是一锤子买卖。Step 3 above hides the week's single most useful sentence: when you cannot guess the trade words, search once anyway, harvest them from the result titles, and search again. Skilled searchers do not strike gold first try; they are simply willing to search a second and third time — searching is a loop, not a one-shot.

搜不到？换词，换问法Nothing good? Swap words, re-ask

这一招The move怎么做How
同义词替换Swap in synonyms"效果"换"作用"，"办法"换"方法"或"技巧"——一个词换掉，结果常常整页换新。Swap "effect" for "impact", "way" for "method" or "technique" — one word changed often renews the whole page of results.
放大或缩小Zoom out or in搜"布偶猫夜间嚎叫"没结果，放大成"猫 夜间 叫"；搜"猫"太泛，缩小成"幼猫 半夜 玩耍"。词的大小决定网撒多宽。"Ragdoll cat nocturnal yowling" finds nothing? Zoom out to "cat night crying". "Cat" is an ocean? Zoom in to "kitten midnight play". Word size sets the width of the net.
换个角度重新问Re-ask from another angle"为什么半夜疯跑"卡住了，就换成"怎么让猫晚上安静"——同一件事，从"原因"问不到，从"办法"常能问到。重新提问不是认输，是高手的常规动作。Stuck on "why the midnight zoomies"? Ask "how to calm a cat at night" instead — the same matter, unreachable through "why", often opens through "how". Re-asking is not defeat; it is what skilled searchers do routinely.
换一种语言Switch language中文搜完英文再搜一次（关键词让第 4 周的翻译工具帮你转）——两种语言等于两个互联网，很多问题在另一边答案更全。After Chinese, search once in English (Week 4's translation tools convert the keywords for you) — two languages are two internets, and many questions are answered more fully on the other side.
一条随身的规矩One rule that travels with you
搜索框也是输入框——第 5 周的三问在这里照常值班。查自己的事，别把真名、学校、住址当关键词打进去：你输入的每个词，都会成为那家公司服务器上的一条记录。The search box is an input box — Week 5's three questions stay on duty here. When researching your own matters, never type your real name, school or address as keywords: every word you enter becomes a record on that company's servers.

词选对了，网还可以收得更紧——引号、site: 这些"限定词"，微课 2 见。With the words right, the net can be drawn tighter still — quotes, site: and their kin await in micro-lesson 2.
