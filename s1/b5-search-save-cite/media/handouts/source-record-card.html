三行来源卡The three-line source card

写作业查资料、给别人转发消息、三个月后想找回那个页面的时候，把这张卡拿出来。它管的不只是这门课——是你以后用到的每一条信息。

Take this card out when homework sends you searching, before you forward a claim, and whenever a page must be found again months later. It governs not just this course but every piece of information you will ever use.

想词三步Choosing words, three steps① 删客套和口水话 → ② 留三四个具体的词（对象 + 现象 + 你要的东西）→ ③ 用"写文章的人"会用的词。搜不到就换：同义词、放大缩小、换角度重新问、换语言再搜。① Delete politeness and padding → ② keep three or four concrete words (thing + behaviour + what you want) → ③ use the words the page's author would use. Nothing good? Swap: synonyms, zoom out or in, re-ask from another angle, switch language.机器读词不读心。Engines read words, not minds.
四根绳子The four drawstrings"…" 原句精确匹配（引短语别引长句）· site: 只搜一个网站（冒号后不加空格）· filetype:pdf 只要文件（不认就把 pdf 当普通关键词）· 时间筛选在结果页的"搜索工具"里。一次加一根，看结果有没有变准。"…" exact phrases (quote phrases, not long sentences) · site: one site only (no space after the colon) · filetype:pdf for documents (ignored? make pdf a plain keyword) · time filters live in the results page's tools menu. One drawstring at a time, watching whether results sharpen.是快捷键，不是魔法。Shortcuts, not magic.
收藏三步Saving, three moves收藏（星号）→ 挑文件夹（按主题建，名字沿"用途-时间-内容"规则）→ 改标题（改成自己的话）。共用电脑上不登录浏览器账号做同步。Bookmark (the star) → choose the folder (built by topic, named by the purpose-time-content rule) → retitle (in your own words). No browser sign-ins for sync on shared computers.收藏夹是门厅，带来源的笔记才是家。Bookmarks are a hallway; a note with its source is home.
三行来源卡The three-line card① 标题 ② 作者或机构（查不到写"未署名"——那本身是信号）③ 网址 + 访问日期（网页会变，日期是你的时间戳；书用页码代网址）。抄走一段话，趁页面还开着立刻补这三行。① Title ② author or institution (or "unsigned" — itself a signal) ③ URL + access date (pages change; the date is your timestamp; for books, a page number stands in). The moment a passage travels into your notes, write the three lines while the page is still open.凡用必留痕，痕迹能追回。Whatever you use leaves a trace, and the trace leads back.
一眼三看The three glances① 谁在说（机构还是个人？页顶页脚找署名）② 什么时候说的（没日期的"最新"要警惕）③ 它引了谁（能不能追到原始出处？只有"专家表示"的打问号）。流传广、点赞多、投票高，都不在三看之内。① Who says it (institution or individual? bylines live at head and foot) ② when (beware the undated "latest") ③ what it cites (does a thread lead to the original? bare "experts say" earns a question mark). Circulation, likes and votes appear nowhere among the three.先信不等于永远信。First trust is not forever trust.
摘要不是来源A summary is not a source结果页顶端机器拼的"智能回答"只是路标：明天可能变，也没人署名负责。点进它引用的真实页面，卡上记那个页面。The machine-stitched answer atop a results page is only a signpost: it may read differently tomorrow, and nobody signs it. Open the real page behind it and record that page.路标不算目的地。A signpost is not the destination.
搜索框守则Search-box rules搜索框也是输入框：真名、学校、住址不当关键词。带"广告"角标的结果整条跳过。搜出来互相矛盾？记下来——那是发现。The search box is an input box: real names, school and address are never keywords. Skip every result wearing the "ad" tag. Contradictory results? Record them — that is a find.你输入的每个词都会留下记录。Every word you type becomes a record.
每月自检The monthly check随机点开自己三条旧收藏：还认得它是什么吗？还知道当初为什么存吗？两问有一问答不上，说明该回到"收藏三步"了。Open three old bookmarks at random: do you still recognise what each is? Still know why you saved it? One blank answer means the three saving moves need another visit.存住 = 还找得到 + 还知道为什么。Kept = findable again + reason remembered.
本周一句话This week in one sentence
找得到，是本事；存得住，是习惯；追得回，是信用——三行来源卡把三件事装进了一张卡。Finding is skill; keeping is habit; being traceable is credit — and the three-line card carries all three in one place.
