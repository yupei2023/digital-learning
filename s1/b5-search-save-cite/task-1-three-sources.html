要做什么What to do

本周任务一页装两件事。第一件：为你自选的真实问题找到三个来源，每个记成一张三行来源卡（标题 · 作者或机构 · 网址 + 访问日期），各配一句"第一眼可信或存疑，因为——"。第二件：到外部课程菜单浏览至少三门课的介绍页，挑两门各试学约 20 分钟，每门写两行"试学小记"，再加一句两门的对比。问题一句 + 三张卡 + 两则小记 + 一句对比合成一页"检索报告"，发布到你网站的作品集页，链接登记。第一件练的是受用终身的检索与记录；第二件为下周的选课备好第一手感受——两件事用的是同一双刚练出来的眼睛。

This week's task fits two matters on one page. First: find three sources for a real question of your own, each recorded as a three-line card (title · author or institution · URL + access date) with one sentence — "trustworthy or doubtful at first glance, because —". Second: browse at least three course introductions on the external course menu and sample two courses for ~20 minutes each, with two lines of sampling notes per course plus one sentence comparing the two. One line of question + three cards + two notes + one comparison make one "search report", published on your site's Portfolio page, link registered. The first matter trains search-and-record for life; the second banks first-hand impressions for next week's choice — and both run on the same freshly trained pair of eyes.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 问题是你自己的：来自功课，或纯粹的好奇心，一句话写得清楚。一条安全线：别选会暴露隐私的问题（涉及真名、住址、学校的不选）——搜索框也是输入框。The question is yours: from schoolwork or pure curiosity, stateable in one line. One safety line: choose nothing that exposes private facts (real names, addresses, your school are off the table) — the search box is an input box.
  • 三个来源，三张卡：每张三行齐全。查不到作者写机构；连机构都没有，如实写"未署名"。三个来源别全出自同一个网站。Three sources, three cards: all three lines on each. No author? Record the institution; neither? An honest "unsigned". And the three sources must not all come from one website.
  • 来源是能追到的真实页面：结果页顶端的"智能摘要"不算——点进它引用的页面去。视频也行（标题 · UP 主或机构 · 网址 + 日期照记）；书和课本也行（网址一行改写"页码"）。A source is a real, traceable page: the results-page summary does not count — open the page behind it. A video qualifies (title · uploader or institution · URL + date as usual); so does a book (the URL line becomes a page number).
  • 每张卡一句"第一眼"：可信或存疑都行，但要给理由——用一眼三看（谁在说 · 何时说 · 引了谁）里的至少一看。三个来源互相矛盾？照实写，那是发现不是麻烦。One first-glance sentence per card: trust or doubt, either — with a reason drawn from at least one of the three glances (who says it · when · what it cites). Sources contradicting each other? Record it straight; that is a find, not a fault.
  • 菜单浏览 + 两门试学 + 小记：介绍页至少三门（第 4 周读过的那门算数）；试学每门约 20 分钟；小记每门两行——课名 + 一句自己的发现或感受（抄介绍页不算）。打不开的课，如实记下课名——那是课程实测要用的第一手数据。Menu browsing + two samples + notes: at least three introductions (the one you read in Week 4 counts); ~20 minutes per sampled course; two lines of notes each — the course name + one discovery or impression of your own (copying the blurb does not qualify). Any course that will not open, record by name — first-hand data for the pre-launch access tests.
  • 已发布 + 已登记：报告发布在你网站的作品集页（新条目或新建一页都行），链接登记到登记表，任务编号 B5；本周三行日志照常另写在日志页。公开学习者：链接发进自己的邮箱（主题"我的检索报告 · 化名"）。Published + registered: the report lives on your site's Portfolio page (a new entry or a new page), the link on the tracking sheet, task number B5; the week's three-line log still goes on the log page. Open learners: email the link to yourself (subject "my search report · pseudonym").
  • 🚫 独立：三张卡和每句判断由你自己写；本周不使用生成式 AI，机器拼的摘要也不进报告。家长陪着逛菜单，完全欢迎。🚫 Independent: the cards and every judgment sentence written by you; no generative AI this week, and no machine-stitched summary enters the report. A parent browsing the menu alongside is entirely welcome.

步骤Steps

  1. 定下问题（1 分钟）：§0 那一分钟已经替你试搜过它，这里只做确认。第 5 周日志第 3 行写过的那个优先。检验两条：真实（我确实想弄明白）、安全（不含隐私）。一时想不出？替补任选："中学生每天需要多少睡眠""人类为什么要冬眠——哦不对，为什么不冬眠"。Settle the question (1 min): §0's minute already gave it a trial search; here you only confirm. The one from Week 5's log line 3 comes first. Two checks: real (I genuinely want this settled) and safe (nothing private in it). Blank? Take a spare: "how much sleep does a teenager need", or "why don't humans hibernate".
  2. 搜索并挑出三个来源（7 分钟）：微课 1 的拆词起手，结果不理想就换词或收紧（微课 2）——至少换两种问法，别在第一页第一条上定终身。从结果里挑三个不同来源的页面，顺手存进跟做建的收藏夹文件夹。Search and shortlist three sources (7 min): open with micro-lesson 1's word-breaking; when results disappoint, swap words or tighten (micro-lesson 2) — at least two different askings — don't settle on the very first result on page one. Pick three pages from different sources and file them into the follow-along's bookmark folder as you go.
  3. 三张来源卡 + 第一眼（8 分钟）：照微课 4 的图逐行填：标题 · 作者或机构 · 网址 + 今天的日期；每张卡下面写一句"第一眼可信/存疑，因为——"。跟做里那张初稿卡，现在正好转正。想要一页对照表？打印讲义《三行来源卡》就是为这一步做的——本周全部要点在一页上。Three cards + first glances (8 min): fill line by line after micro-lesson 4's figure: title · author or institution · URL + today's date; beneath each card, one sentence — "trustworthy / doubtful at first glance, because —". The draft card from the follow-along graduates here. Want the whole craft on one page while you write? The printable Three-line source card handout was made for exactly this step.
  4. 浏览三门课程介绍页（4 分钟）：打开菜单，四组十二门里点开至少三门的介绍页（第 4 周任务读过的那门算一门；当时走了"连读 2–3 门英文介绍"兜底路线的同学，这一步几乎是复习）。凭直觉记下最吸引你的两门。拿不准哪门打得开？先从中文平台的课点起——第 6 门（微软，简体中文，部分内容不用账号）和第 5、10、11 门（中国大学MOOC，介绍页不注册也能看）；国际平台的课打不开不是你的错，换下一门就是。Browse three course intros (4 min): open the menu and click into at least three of the twelve introductions (the course you read for Week 4's task counts as one; if you took that week's read-2–3-blurbs fallback, this step is nearly revision). Note by instinct the two that pull at you. Not sure which pages will open from home? Start with the Chinese-platform courses — #6 (Microsoft, Simplified Chinese, partly account-free) and #5, #10, #11 (icourse163, whose intro pages open without registering); an international course that won't open is not your failure — move to the next.
  5. 试学两门（40 分钟，每门约 20）：给每门一段完整的 20 分钟。看什么：介绍页 → 课程大纲 → 第一节或预览视频。心里带着三个小问题：它讲什么？怎么讲（视频、文字还是练习）？我跟得上吗（语言和难度）？英文课用第 4 周的翻译手艺，理直气壮。不强制注册——公开内容够看；真要注册，先告诉家长，再用第 5 周的手艺（邮箱注册 + 强密码 + 选填留空）。20 分钟一到就停：本周只是试，正式学第 11 周才开始。第一门就打不开、页面一直转圈？别耗着——换中文平台那几门先试（第 5、6、10、11 门），"打不开"写进小记照样满分。想注册但家长今晚不在？当晚只看公开部分，注册改天——试学不必等注册。Sample two courses (40 min, ~20 each): give each one unbroken 20-minute stretch. What to look at: the introduction → the syllabus → the first lesson or preview video. Carry three small questions: what does it teach? How (video, text, exercises)? Can I keep up (language and difficulty)? For English courses, Week 4's translation craft applies without apology. No registration required — the open content suffices; if you do register, tell your parents first, then use Week 5's craft (email sign-up + strong passphrase + optional fields empty). Stop at 20 minutes: this week samples; real study begins in Week 11. First choice stuck on a loading spinner? Don't wait it out — try the Chinese-platform courses first (#5, #6, #10, #11); "wouldn't open", written into the note, still earns full marks. Want to register but no parent home tonight? Sample the open content tonight and register another day — sampling never waits on registration.
  6. 小记、一句对比、发布、登记（5 分钟）：每门两行小记（课名 + 一句自己的发现或感受），两则小记后加一句对比——两门相比，此刻哪一门更让你想回去？为什么（下周可以反悔）；问题 + 三卡 + 两小记 + 一句对比合成一页，发布到网站的作品集页，重新点"发布"，用访客的眼睛过一遍（卡里没有你的隐私吧？），再把链接登记到登记表（公开学习者：发进自己的邮箱）。Notes, one comparison, publish, register (5 min): two lines per sampled course (name + one discovery or impression of your own), then one comparing sentence — of the two, which one calls you back right now, and why (free to change your mind next week); question + three cards + two notes + one comparison onto one page, published on your site's Portfolio page; press Publish again, review with a visitor's eyes (no private details in the cards?), then register the link (open learners: email it to yourself).

试学小记怎么写：两行，够下周用Writing the sampling note: two lines that feed next week

小记不是课程评论，是给一周后的你留的便条——下周写"我的外部课程"选择页时，它就是你的第一手证据。格式：第一行课名（照菜单抄，别记错对象）；第二行一句真话——"视频短、字幕全，20 分钟看完了第一课，还想看第二课"，或者"介绍很吸引人，但大纲全是长文档，我大概坚持不了八周"。诚实比热情有用：劝退你的发现和吸引你的发现一样值钱。两则小记写完，再补一句对比：两门相比，此刻哪一门更让你想回去？为什么。写下的只是此刻的倾向——下周的正式选择完全可以反悔；评分看的是这句在场、出自亲身，不评你选得"对不对"。The note is not a course review; it is a memo to the you of one week hence — first-hand evidence for next week's "My external course" page. Format: line one, the course name (copy it from the menu, so the note points at the right course); line two, one true sentence — "short videos, full subtitles, finished lesson 1 in 20 minutes and wanted lesson 2", or "an alluring introduction, but the syllabus is all long documents and I doubt I'd last eight weeks". Honesty is worth more than enthusiasm: a finding that warns you off is worth exactly as much as one that draws you in. With both notes written, add one comparing sentence: of the two, which one calls you back right now, and why? It records a leaning, not a verdict — next week's official choice is free to differ; what is scored is that the sentence is there and first-hand, never whether you chose "correctly".

评分 · 量规第 ① ② 行（3 + 2 = 5 分）Scoring · Rubric rows ① ② (3 + 2 = 5 pts)

第 ① 行看检索与记录：真实的问题、三张三行齐全的卡（"未署名"如实注明同样齐全）、每卡一句有理由的第一眼、已发布已登记。第 ② 行看试学：两则各两行的小记 + 一句两门对比，看得出亲身试过——"打不开，换了一门"或"走了菜单英文介绍的兜底路"的诚实记录同样达标。评的是检索与记录的动作，不评你的问题"高不高级"，也不评你最后信了谁。完整量规Row ① scores search and record: a real question, three complete three-line cards (an honest "unsigned" counts as complete), a reasoned first-glance sentence on each, published and registered. Row ② scores the sampling: two two-line notes plus one comparing sentence, bearing the marks of a real visit — and the honest record "wouldn't open, switched courses" or "took the menu-blurb fallback route" meets the bar just the same. What earns points is the act of searching and recording — never how sophisticated your question is, nor whom you ended up believing. Full rubric → 量规页rubric.html

常见问题FAQ

前两个来源好找，第三个怎么也不满意。Two sources came easily; the third refuses to satisfy.

先换问法（微课 1 的表）再换阵地：视频站搜一次（B 站的科普区藏着大量好来源）、英文搜一次（翻译工具帮忙）、或者干脆问家里的书架——书也是来源，页码就是它的"网址"。还不满意，就选"最不差"的那个，把你的保留意见写进第一眼那句："存疑，因为找不到它引用的出处"——这句写出来，第三张卡反而是最见功力的一张。

Swap the asking first (micro-lesson 1's table), then swap terrain: search a video site once (Bilibili's science channels hide fine sources), search once in English (translation tools assist), or consult the bookshelf at home — a book is a source, its page number standing in for the URL. Still unsatisfied? Take the least-bad one and write your reservation into its first-glance line: "doubtful — I cannot trace what it cites." That sentence makes the third card the most skilled of the three.

我的三个来源可以都是视频吗？May all three of my sources be videos?

可以，三行卡照样写：标题 · UP 主或机构 · 网址 + 访问日期。只提醒一句：三个来源别全出自同一个网站——换个阵地，常能看到另一种说法。一眼三看对视频同样管用：这个 UP 主是谁？视频是哪年发的？它引了谁的数据？

Yes — the card writes the same: title · uploader or institution · URL + access date. One reminder stands: the three must not all come from one website — a change of terrain often reveals another telling. And the three glances work on video too: who is this uploader? What year was it posted? Whose data does it cite?

"智能摘要"看不到它引用了哪个页面，来源入口在哪？The "smart summary" shows no link to the page it draws on — where is the way in?

各家引擎的摘要长得不一样：多数在摘要旁边或底部有小字的来源角标或链接图标，点它就到真实页面。实在找不到入口，就无视摘要，往下滚到普通结果列表里挑。还有一种情况值得记下来：一个说法只在摘要里出现、下面的结果里怎么也追不到——那它就没有可追的来源，这个发现本身正好写进"第一眼"那句（"存疑，因为追不到出处"）。

Summaries differ by engine: most carry a small source tag or link icon beside or beneath the text — click it and you reach the real page. If no way in appears, ignore the summary and scroll down to the ordinary result list. One case is worth recording: a claim that lives only in the summary and cannot be traced in the results below has no traceable source — that finding belongs word for word in your first-glance sentence ("doubtful — I cannot trace where it came from").

试学的平台一定要注册吗？页面提示登录才能看视频。Must I register to sample? The page wants a login before the video plays.

不一定。先把不需要登录的看完：介绍页、大纲、评价，常常已经够 20 分钟。确实想进去：先告诉家长（外部课程线的约定），看菜单"注册 · 手机号"一列的说明，然后用第 5 周的手艺——邮箱注册、独有的密码短语、选填留空。不想注册就换一门试，或在小记里如实写"未注册，看的是公开部分"——照样达标。

Not necessarily. Exhaust the login-free parts first: the introduction, syllabus and reviews often fill 20 minutes by themselves. If you truly want in: tell your parents first (the external thread's agreement), read the menu's sign-up column, then apply Week 5's craft — email sign-up, a unique passphrase, optional fields empty. Prefer not to? Sample another course, or note honestly "not registered; sampled the open content" — the bar is met either way.

20 分钟的试学，到底"学"什么？Twenty minutes of sampling — sampling what, exactly?

别求学会任何知识点——试的是合不合身。顺序：介绍页（它承诺教什么）→ 课程大纲（路有多长、每周多陡）→ 第一节或预览视频（老师的讲法你受不受用）。20 分钟结束时能回答三小问（讲什么 · 怎么讲 · 跟得上吗），试学就圆满了。两门都试完，你对"我下周选谁"多半已经心里有数——这正是本周的目的。（报告里那句对比，就是把这份"心里有数"写下来。）

Aim to learn no content at all — you are trying it on for fit. The order: the introduction (what it promises), the syllabus (how long the road, how steep each week), the first lesson or preview (whether the teaching style agrees with you). If at minute 20 you can answer the three small questions — what · how · can I keep up — the sample is complete. After both, you will probably already know which course you would pick next week — and that is exactly the point. (The comparing sentence simply writes that knowledge down.)

我是公开学习者，怎么"登记"和"评分"？I am an open learner — how do registering and scoring work for me?

发布报告后，把链接发进自己的邮箱存档（主题"我的检索报告 · 化名"），再打开量规页逐行自评，分数记进日志。下周的"我的外部课程"选择页对你同样适用——公开学习者的外部课程，自己选、自己学、自己见证。

After publishing, email the link to yourself for the archive (subject "my search report · pseudonym"), then self-score row by row against the rubric and note the score in your log. Next week's "My external course" page applies to you unchanged — an open learner's external course is chosen, studied and witnessed by the learner alone.
