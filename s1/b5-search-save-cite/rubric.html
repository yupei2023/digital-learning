B5 量规：三关怎么计分B5 Rubric: how the three tiers score

每一关一行。分数一关一关往上加：起点营地 3 分（及格）→ 山脊 +1.5 = 4.5 → 登顶 +0.5 = 5。导师按顺序计分：起点营地至少 2 分，才给山脊计分；山脊至少 1 分，才给登顶计分。在校学生由导师评分，公开学习者用同一张表自评。One row per tier. Points stack up the mountain: Base Camp 3 (the pass mark) → Ridge +1.5 = 4.5 → Summit +0.5 = 5. Mentors score in order: the Ridge counts once Base Camp has at least 2; the Summit once the Ridge has at least 1. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table.

评的是检索和记录的动作，以及记录是否诚实。不评问题深浅、不评你最后信了哪个来源、不评文笔。随时补交：每次更新后给导师发一句"已更新"的邮件，导师据此重评。What is scored is the act of searching and recording, and the honesty of the record — never how deep your question runs, which source you end up trusting, or your prose. Add or fix anytime: after each update, email your mentor a one-line "updated" note and the row is re-scored.

缺项怎么数（起点营地）Counting omissions (Base Camp)

下面每一条算一个缺项；同一种问题出现在几张卡上，每张各算一个。Each item below is one omission; the same problem on several cards counts once per card.

  • 问题没写，或写得看不出要查什么。The question is missing, or too unclear to tell what is being looked up.
  • 一张卡少了一行（最常见：访问日期）。一张卡少两行，算两个。A card is missing a line (most often the access date). Two lines missing on one card count as two.
  • 一个"来源"停在结果页的智能摘要，没点进真实页面；或与问题明显无关。One "source" stops at a results-page smart summary, or is plainly unrelated to the question.
  • 三个来源全来自同一个网站。All three sources come from one website.
  • 少一门想试的课（一门都没写，算两个）。A course pick is missing (no picks at all count as two).
  • 一门课名后面没有理由（两门都没理由，算两个）。A course pick has no reason (no reason on either pick counts as two).

两位导师评分前，先一起用同一份样例报告各评一次，对齐尺度。Before scoring, the two mentors each score the same sample report once and compare, so both apply the same thresholds.

不计分，但要做Ungraded, but do it

  • 搜索三连（起点营地第 3 步）：完成即可，导师不检查。The three searches (Base Camp step 3): completing is enough; mentors don't check it.
  • 隐私底线：报告里不出现真名、照片、学校全名或住址；截图先打码。出现了就改，不扣分。Privacy floor: no real names, face photos, full school names or addresses in the report; mask screenshots first. A slip means a fix, never a deduction.
  • 别人的话带上出处：引用一句话就配上它的来源卡；把别人的段落当成自己的判断，按学术诚信处理。Others' words carry their source: a quoted sentence travels with its card; passing off someone's passage as your own judgment falls under the integrity policy.
  • 🚫 独立：卡片和判断句都由你自己写；家长陪你一起逛课程菜单，非常欢迎。🚫 Independent: the cards and judgment sentences are yours; a parent browsing the course menu with you is very welcome.

B5 的 5 分属于第一学期"数字基本功 B1–B6，共 30 分"。链接登记（或"已更新"邮件）后一周内，导师会邮件反馈。迟交不扣分。B5's 5 points belong to Semester 1's "Digital Basics B1–B6, 30 points". Your mentor replies by email within a week of the link landing (or of an "updated" email). Late work costs nothing.
