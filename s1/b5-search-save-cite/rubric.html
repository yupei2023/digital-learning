B5 量规B5 Rubric

量规的三行就是本周的三条目标。第 ① ② 行计分（3 + 2 = 5 分），第 ③ 行不计分、完成即可。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。本任务评的是检索与记录的动作，和记录的诚实——"这门课打不开，我换了一门"与"顺利试学"同样达标；不评你问题的深浅，不评你最后信了哪个来源，也不评文笔。

The three rows are this week's three objectives. Rows ① ② carry points (3 + 2 = 5); row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence. What this task scores is the act of searching and recording, and the honesty of the record — "the course wouldn't open, so I switched" meets the bar exactly as a smooth sample does. Never scored: how deep your question runs, which source you ended up trusting, or your prose.

本周的知识点（限定词的脾气、收藏夹的治法、一眼三看的细节）不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。隐私规则是全课程的底线（见下方"规范要求"）：报告里误放了隐私，导师提醒改正，不扣分。This week's knowledge points (the operators' temperaments, the bookmark cure, the fine grain of the three glances) are not scored here — the self-quiz checks them, ungraded. The privacy rules are the course-wide floor (Form requirements below): private details slipping into a report draw a reminder and a fix, never a deduction.

规范要求（不计分的底线）Form requirements (the ungraded floor)

  • 报告遵守课程隐私四条：不放真名、正脸照片、学校全名、可定位的信息；来源卡里不出现你或他人的个人信息；若附截图，按第 3、5 周的规矩先打码。误放了由导师提醒改正，不扣分——改了重新发布即可。The report keeps the course's four privacy rules: no real names, face photos, full school names or locating details; no personal information — yours or anyone's — inside the source cards; any screenshot masked first by Weeks 3 and 5's rules. A slip draws a mentor's reminder and a fix, never a deduction — edit and republish.
  • 别人的话标明来路，不冒充自己的话——这正是本周所练。报告里引用来源的句子带着它的卡，完全正确；整段抄来冒充自己的判断，相应行按学术诚信规则处理。Others' words carry their origin and are never passed off as yours — the very craft this week trains. A quoted sentence travelling with its card is exactly right; a passage copied and dressed as your own judgment puts the row under the integrity policy.
  • 🚫 独立：三张卡与判断句由自己写；由生成式 AI 或他人代写的，相应行记 0 并按学术诚信规则处理。家长在旁一起逛菜单、一起试学，完全欢迎。🚫 Independent: the cards and judgment sentences written by you; rows written by a generative AI or another person score 0 under the integrity policy. A parent browsing and sampling alongside is entirely welcome.
