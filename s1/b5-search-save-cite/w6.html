B5 检索、保存与引用 · 第 6 周（数字基本功第 5 周，共 6 周）B5 Search, Save & Cite · Week 6 (Digital Basics week 5 of 6)

找得到，还得追得回Find it — and be able to trace it back

任务 · 5 分Task · 5 pts🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min

请从上到下按顺序做。本周结束时，你的网站上会多出一份"检索报告"——你为自己的一个真实问题找到了三个来源，每个都留下了能被追回去的三行记录；你还逛过了外部课程菜单、试学了两门课，为下周的选课备好了第一手感受。这一周就是把台阶图那三级从下往上走一遍——微课造台阶，任务站在第三级上。Work top to bottom. By the end of this week your website will hold a "search report" — three sources found for a real question of yours, each leaving a three-line record that can be traced back; and you will have browsed the external course menu and sampled two courses, first-hand impressions banked for next week's choice. The week is one climb up the three steps of the ladder — the lessons build the steps, the task stands on the third.

0 · 先搜一次（1 分钟）0 · One search first (one minute)

别看任何微课，现在就把你的问题（第 5 周日志第 3 行写的那个；没写就用替补："中学生每天需要多少睡眠"）原样输进任何一个搜索引擎，看一眼结果的第一页，回答三个小观察：最上面的几条有没有带"广告"角标？各条结果分别是谁发的（机构？个人？看不出来）？有没有两条结果互相矛盾？No lesson yet — type your question (the one on Week 5's log line 3; the spare, if you never wrote one: "how much sleep does a teenager need") into any search engine exactly as it is, look at page one of the results, and make three small observations: do the top entries carry an "ad" marker? Who posted each result (an institution? an individual? impossible to tell)? Do any two results contradict each other?

看到了吧——搜索引擎不是答案机，是一条什么人都在上面摆摊的大街。为什么本周敢让你先动手？因为搜索和第 3 周的截图、第 4 周的翻译一样是可逆的动作：搜错了换个词重搜就是，一分钱不花。可逆的动作，先试后学——四段微课这就把这条大街的走法教给你。And there it is — a search engine is not an answer machine but a street where anyone may set up a stall. Why does this week dare to start hands-first? Because searching, like Week 3's screenshots and Week 4's translations, is reversible: a bad search costs a re-typed word and nothing more. Reversible actions get tried before studied — and the four micro-lessons now teach you how to walk that street.

1 · 本周目标1 · This week's objectives

  • ① 用本周的搜索技巧，为一个自选的真实问题（来自功课或你自己的好奇心）找到三个来源，每个都用"三行来源卡"记录——标题 · 作者或机构 · 网址 + 访问日期——并各写一句"它第一眼看上去可信或存疑，因为——"；把这份"检索报告"发布到你网站的作品集页，链接登记到导师的登记表（公开学习者：把链接存进自己的邮箱）。① Using this week's search skills, find three sources for a real question of your own choosing (from schoolwork or your own curiosity), record each on a "three-line source card" — title · author or institution · URL + access date — with one sentence each on why it looked trustworthy or doubtful at first glance; publish the "search report" on your site's Portfolio page and register the link (open learners: file the link in your own inbox).
  • ② 浏览外部课程菜单里至少三门课的介绍页，挑两门各试学约 20 分钟，并在报告里为每门写两行"试学小记"（课名 + 一句发现或感受），两则小记后再写一句对比——两门相比，此刻哪一门更让我想回去？为什么（下周可以反悔）——下周写"我的外部课程"选择页，靠的就是它。② Browse at least three course introduction pages on the external course menu, sample two courses for about 20 minutes each, and add two lines of "sampling notes" per course to the report (course name + one discovery or impression), plus one comparing sentence after the notes — of the two, which one calls me back right now, and why (free to change your mind next week) — next week's "My external course" page stands on exactly this.
  • ③（不计分 · 完成即可）完成本周的"搜索三连"跟做：同一个问题搜三次——直接问 → 加引号或换词 → 再收紧一步——亲眼看结果怎样随问法变化，并把最有用的一条存进新建的收藏夹文件夹、配上它的三行来源。③ (ungraded · completing it is enough) The week's "three searches" follow-along: one question searched three ways — straight → quoted or re-worded → tightened once more — the changing results watched with your own eyes, and the most useful hit saved into a new bookmark folder with its three source lines.

本周你还会学到：限定词（引号、site:、filetype:）什么时候好使、什么时候别指望；收藏夹怎么治乱；网页会变——所以要记访问日期；"一眼三看"的第一眼判断。这些不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: when the operators (quotes, site:, filetype:) help and when not to count on them; the cure for tangled bookmarks; that web pages change — hence the access date; and the three-glance first read of a source. None of it is graded — the self-quiz checks it for you, ungraded.

  1. 微课 1 · 想好词，再搜索（6 分钟）Micro-lesson 1 · Choose your words, then search (6 min)——搜索引擎读关键词不读客套话；把问题拆成词；搜不到就换词、换问法。 — engines read keywords, not politeness; break the question into words; when results disappoint, swap words and re-ask.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

搜出来的第一页全是广告和带货，正经内容在哪？Page one is wall-to-wall ads and product plugs — where is the real content?

先认标记：结果条角上的"广告"两个字（各引擎都被要求标出来，但常标得很小）。带角标的整条跳过，从第一条不带角标的开始读。还嫌闹，用微课 2 的两招：site: 限定到一个正经网站里搜，或换更具体的关键词——广告买的是热门词，你的词越具体，摊贩越追不上你。

Learn the marker first: the little "ad" tag on a result's corner (every engine is required to show it, and most show it small). Skip every tagged entry and start reading at the first untagged one. Still too noisy? Two moves from micro-lesson 2: search inside one serious site with site:, or sharpen your keywords — advertisers buy the popular words, and the more specific yours are, the fewer stalls can chase you.

我的三个来源说法互相矛盾，怎么办？My three sources contradict each other — now what?

恭喜，这不是失败，是本周最有价值的发现——把矛盾如实写进报告，正是"记录来源"的意义所在：别人能沿着你的三张卡自己去看、自己去判断。你不必当裁判；用"一眼三看"说出你暂时更信哪个和为什么，就已经超额完成本周。真正的裁判功夫（追踪说法的源头、横向对照）第二学期有一整个单元来练。

Congratulations — that is not failure but the week's most valuable find. Write the contradiction into the report honestly; it is exactly what recording sources is for: others can follow your three cards, look for themselves and judge for themselves. You need not referee; saying which one you trust for now and why, by the three glances, already exceeds this week's bar. The real refereeing craft — tracing a claim to its origin, reading laterally — gets a whole module of its own in Semester 2.

试学的课程页打不开。The course I want to sample won't open.

老朋友了——第 4 周你就见过它。菜单里的外部链接都标着"待实测"，打不开不是你的错。国际平台（Coursera、edX 等）并不是在每个地方都打得开——拿不准就先从中文平台的课试起：第 6 门（微软，简体中文，部分内容不用账号）和第 5、10、11 门（中国大学MOOC，介绍页不注册也能看）。按顺序补救：① 换菜单里另一门；② 都打不开，把菜单页切成英文，读每门课自带的介绍（第 4 周的兜底老路），试学小记里如实写"外部页面未能打开，读的是菜单内介绍"。两条路同样达标，而且你记下的"打不开"课程名正是课程开课前实测要用的第一手数据——导师每周都在收集。（公开学习者：菜单页顶部的"待实测"说明写着"打不开请邮件告诉我们"——同一条回路。）

An old acquaintance — you met it in Week 4. Every external link on the menu is marked "to verify"; a dead page is not your failure. The international platforms (Coursera, edX and friends) do not open from everywhere — when unsure, start with the Chinese-platform courses: #6 (Microsoft, Simplified Chinese, partly usable without an account) and #5, #10, #11 (icourse163, whose introduction pages open without registering). Recover in order: ① switch to another course on the menu; ② if none opens, flip the menu page to English and read each course's own blurb (Week 4's fallback road), noting honestly "external page unreachable; menu blurb read". Both routes meet the bar — and the course names you record as unreachable are precisely the first-hand data the pre-launch access tests need; mentors collect them weekly. (Open learners: the menu page's top "to verify" note asks you to email the course about any page that fails — the same loop.)

试学要不要注册账号？Does sampling mean registering for accounts?

不强制。介绍页、课程大纲、预览视频这些公开内容，足够撑起 20 分钟的试学。真想登进去看更多：先告诉家长（外部课程学习线的约定），然后用第 5 周的手艺——邮箱注册、强而唯一的密码短语、选填留空。菜单的"注册 · 手机号"一列写明了每门课要什么，注册前看一眼。

Not required. Introduction pages, syllabi and preview videos carry a 20-minute sample comfortably in the open. If you genuinely want inside: tell your parents first (the external-course thread's standing agreement), then use Week 5's craft — email sign-up, a strong unique passphrase, optional fields left empty. The menu's sign-up column states what each course asks; read it before registering.

来源查不到作者是谁，卡上第二行写什么？The source names no author — what goes on the card's second line?

先找机构：页面顶部的网站名、页脚的主办单位，都算"作者或机构"——机构署名本来就比个人署名常见。真的什么都查不到，就如实写"未署名"。注意，这不是记录失败："未署名"本身就是一条可信度信息——你的"一眼三看"第一看（谁在说？）刚好用得上它。

Look for the institution first: the site's name at the top, the operator in the footer — both count as "author or institution", and institutional bylines are the more common kind anyway. If truly nothing surfaces, write "unsigned", honestly. And note: that is not a failed record — "unsigned" is itself a piece of credibility information, exactly what the first of your three glances (who says it?) feeds on.

只有手机的晚上，本周能做哪些？An evening with only a phone — how much of this week works?

§0、"搜索三连"和逛课程介绍页，手机浏览器都做得了。两句实话：试学时平台常会拼命推自家 App——能看网页版就看网页版，本课程不要求装任何 App（第 5 周的规矩照旧：装不装、注册不注册，先过三问再动手）；收藏夹的"挑文件夹、改标题"在手机浏览器里藏得比较深（微课 3 提过位置不同）。写三张卡、发布检索报告，建议回到电脑上做——攒到周末一次做完也来得及。

§0, the three searches and intro-page browsing all work in a phone browser. Two honest notes: platforms push their apps hard while you sample — use the web version where one exists; this course never requires installing an app (Week 5's rule stands: run the three questions before any install or sign-up). And the bookmark moves — picking a folder, renaming — hide deep in phone browsers (micro-lesson 3 flagged the difference).
