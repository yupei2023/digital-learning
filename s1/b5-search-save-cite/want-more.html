做Do

  • 当一次家里的"查证员"——从家人群里挑一条"转疯了"的消息，用一眼三看查一遍：谁在说？何时说的？引了谁？把查到的（哪怕是"查无来源"）讲给家里人听。骗局最爱找的不是你，是家里最不设防的那个人——这句话第 5 周说过，本周你有工具了。Serve a shift as the family fact-finder — take one message "reposted everywhere" from the family group and run the three glances: who says it? when? citing whom? Report what you find (even "no source findable") at the dinner table. Scams hunt the least-guarded person in the house — Week 5 said it, and this week you own the tools.
  • 收藏夹大扫除——打开收藏夹，给现有条目建三五个主题文件夹，归位、改名、删掉打不开的。计时——多半不到十五分钟，从此收藏才真的"收"得住。The great bookmark clean-up — open the bookmarks, raise three to five topic folders, re-home, rename, and delete the dead. Time it — usually under fifteen minutes, and saving finally starts to keep.
  • 把三行卡带进下一次作业——下回任何一科要查资料，给用到的每个来源写张三行卡附在作业后面。老师没要求？正好——习惯先于要求的人，永远显得从容。Take the card into your next homework — whichever subject next sends you searching, append a three-line card for every source you use. Not required by the teacher? All the better — whoever's habits run ahead of the requirements always looks unhurried.
  • 同一个问题，英文再搜一次——把本周问题的关键词用翻译工具转成英文，换个引擎搜一遍，比较两边的三个来源有何不同。两种语言等于两个互联网——这个发现值得亲眼看一次。Search your question once more, in English — turn this week's keywords into English with a translation tool and run them through another engine; compare the three sources each side offers. Two languages are two internets — a discovery worth seeing with your own eyes.
