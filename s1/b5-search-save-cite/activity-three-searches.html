搜索三连The three searches

§0 你已经搜过一次、见识过那条大街。现在把四段微课的手艺连起来练：同一个问题，搜三次，一次比一次准，最后把收获存好。用你自己的问题（第 5 周日志第 3 行那个；替补："中学生每天需要多少睡眠"或"猫为什么半夜疯跑"）。全程可逆——搜错了重来，一分钱不花。

In §0 you searched once and saw the street. Now chain the four micro-lessons' craft together: one question, searched three times, each sharper than the last, and the catch properly stored. Use your own question (Week 5's log, line 3; spares: "how much sleep does a teenager need" or "why do cats get the midnight zoomies"). Everything is reversible — a bad search costs nothing but a retry.

三搜一存Three searches, one save

  1. 第一搜 · 直接问（2 分钟）：把问题原样输进搜索框。这次别急着点进任何结果——只读前五条的来源名（每条结果下方那行小字：是机构？个人号？看不出来？），把最像正经机构的一条记在心里。有带"广告"角标的，数一数有几条。Search 1 · asked straight (2 min): type the question in as it is. Do not click anything yet — read only the source names of the top five (the small line under each result: an institution? a personal account? unreadable?), and keep the most institutional-looking one in mind. Count any entries wearing the "ad" tag.
  2. 第二搜 · 拆词或加引号（2 分钟）：按微课 1 把问题拆成三四个具体的词重搜；问题里有固定说法（歌词、名言、术语）就给它加上英文双引号。对比第一搜：第一页换了多少张新面孔？哪一次更接近你要的？Search 2 · broken into words, or quoted (2 min): re-search with the question broken into three or four concrete words per micro-lesson 1; if it contains a fixed phrase (a lyric, a saying, a term), wrap that phrase in straight quotes. Compare with search 1: how much of page one changed faces? Which came closer to what you wanted?
  3. 第三搜 · 再收紧一步（2 分钟）：任选一根绳子：site: 限定一个你信得过的网站类型、时间筛选"一年内"、或把关键词换成英文再搜一次（第 4 周的翻译工具帮忙）。三次搜索里，哪一次的第一页你最愿意从头读到尾？Search 3 · tightened once more (2 min): pick any drawstring — site: to a kind of site you trust, a past-year time filter, or the keywords switched into English (Week 4's translation tools assist). Of the three searches, which page one would you most willingly read top to bottom?
  4. 存一条（2 分钟）：在收藏夹里新建一个文件夹，按第 5 周的规则起名（比如"数字化学习-W6-我的问题"）；把三次搜索里最有用的一条收进去、标题改成你自己的话；再在笔记或草稿里给它写下三行来源（标题 · 作者或机构 · 网址 + 今天的日期）。这三行不白写——它就是本周任务第一张来源卡的现成初稿。Save one (2 min): create a new bookmark folder named by Week 5's rule (say "DL-W6-my-question"); file the most useful hit of the three searches into it, retitled in your own words; then, in your notes or a draft, write its three source lines (title · author or institution · URL + today's date). Those lines are not practice waste — they are the ready first draft of the task's first source card.
幕间点拨A word between acts
三次搜索，三张不同的结果榜——你刚刚亲眼证明了一件事：搜索结果不是标准答案的排行榜，只是引擎对"你想要什么"的一次猜测。问法变，猜测就变；换个人搜，榜单也不同。这正是来源要记录的原因：你看到的那个页面，别人（和三个月后的你）未必搜得到——三行卡才是可靠的回程路标。Three searches, three different leaderboards — you have just proven something with your own eyes: search results are no ranking of correct answers, only the engine's guess at what you want. Change the asking and the guess changes; a different person gets a different board. Which is exactly why sources need recording: the page you saw may never surface for someone else (or for the you of three months hence) — the three-line card is the reliable road home.
彩排完毕Rehearsal complete
你现在会拆词、会收网、手边还有一个已经开张的收藏夹文件夹和一张来源卡的初稿。开工吧：本周任务——三个来源、三张卡，再去外部课程菜单试学两门课。You can now break questions into words and draw the net tight, with a working bookmark folder and a drafted source card at hand. To work, then: this week's task — three sources, three cards, and two courses sampled from the external menu.
