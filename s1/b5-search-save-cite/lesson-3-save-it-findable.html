存住它：让三个月后的你还找得到Save it findable: for the you of three months hence

第 3 周建网站时，课程说过一句预言："收藏夹会乱。"现在检验一下：打开你的收藏夹看一眼——是不是已经躺着一排你完全不记得为什么收藏的东西？搜到好东西只是上半场；存得三个月后还找得到，才算真的拥有它。治乱的方子和第 5 周治文件的一模一样：给它们一个家，给家一个名字。

Back in Week 3, when your website was born, the course made a prophecy: "bookmark folders tangle." Test it now — open your bookmarks and look: is there already a row of things you cannot remember saving? Finding a good page is only the first half; saving it so that the you of three months hence can find it again is what makes it yours. And the cure for the tangle is the very one Week 5 applied to files: give things a home, and give the home a name.

收藏的三步，而不是一步Saving takes three moves, not one

  1. 收藏：点星号（或 Ctrl/Cmd + D）。大多数人到这里就停手了——于是三个月后收藏夹成了乱葬岗。Bookmark it: the star (or Ctrl/Cmd + D). Most people stop here — which is how, three months on, the bookmarks bar becomes a burial mound.
  2. 挑文件夹：收藏弹窗里就能选位置、新建文件夹。文件夹按主题或用途建，名字沿第 5 周的规则——"数字化学习-W6-睡眠问题"比"新建文件夹(3)"耐用一百倍。一门课一个夹，一件事一个夹。Choose the folder: the save dialog itself lets you pick a location or create a folder. Build folders by topic or purpose, named by Week 5's rule — "DL-W6-sleep-question" outlives "New folder (3)" a hundred times over. One course, one folder; one matter, one folder.
  3. 改标题：网页的默认标题常是一长串口号加站名。当场改成你自己的话——"青少年睡眠指南（疾控）"——将来扫一眼就认得。Rename it: a page's default title is often a slogan trailing a site name. Rewrite it on the spot in your own words — "teen sleep guide (CDC)" — and a future glance will recognise it instantly.

抄走一段话时，让出处跟着走When a passage travels, its source travels with it

做功课时最常见的动作：从网页上抄一段话进笔记或文档。本周起给这个动作加一个尾巴：粘贴完，紧接着在下面写上它的来路——用微课 4 马上要教的三行（标题 · 作者或机构 · 网址 + 访问日期）。趁页面还开着写，二十秒的事；等三天后想补，就要重新大海捞针了。The commonest homework move: copying a passage from a page into your notes or a document. From this week, give that move a tail: the instant you paste, write beneath it where it came from — the three lines micro-lesson 4 is about to teach (title · author or institution · URL + access date). Written while the page is still open, it costs twenty seconds; postponed three days, it costs a whole new needle-hunt.

为什么值得？三个理由，一个比一个近身：将来的你要找得回去（网页会变，笔记里的三行是备份坐标）；读你笔记的人要看得出这不是你写的（分清哪些是自己的话，哪些是别人的——这是诚实，也是对作者的起码尊重）；第 10 周的立场短文和第二学期的评鉴报告，直接要用这套三行。Why bother? Three reasons, each closer to home: future-you must be able to trace it back (pages change; the three lines in your notes are backup coordinates); a reader of your notes must be able to see these words are not yours (keeping your words and others' words distinct is honesty, and the least respect an author is owed); and Week 10's position piece and next semester's evaluation report will use these very three lines.

收藏夹是门厅，带来源的笔记才是家The bookmarks bar is a hallway; a note with its source is home
第 5 周你听过它的兄弟："桌面是门厅不是家。"收藏夹一样：路过存一下可以，真正要用的内容，要住进你的笔记——带着它的三行来源。另一条第 5 周的规矩也照常值班：共用电脑上不登录浏览器账号做多设备同步；自己的带锁设备才登录。In Week 5 you met this sentence's sibling: "the desktop is a hallway, not a home." Bookmarks likewise: passing storage is fine, but content you truly mean to use moves into your notes — with its three source lines. And another Week 5 rule stays on duty: no signing into browser accounts on shared computers for cross-device sync; only your own locked device gets a sign-in.
