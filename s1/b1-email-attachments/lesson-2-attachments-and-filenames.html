附件与文件名Attachments and file names

附件是随信寄出的“包裹”。寄包裹要做三件事：装对东西、写清标签、在信里说一声。这三件事对应本课的三个动作：添加附件、按规则命名、正文里提到。

An attachment is a parcel that travels with the letter. Sending a parcel takes three moves: pack the right thing, label it clearly, and mention it in the letter. Those map to this lesson's three actions: attach the file, name it by the rule, mention it in the body.

动作一 · 添加附件Move 1 · Attach the file

写邮件的界面上找“附件”按钮（常画成一个回形针 📎），点它，选中你的文件，等它传完——正文区上方或下方出现文件名和大小，才算添加成功。手机上操作相同，按钮通常也在写信界面里。两个小规矩：附件别超过 10 MB（本周的一页文档远小于此）；一封邮件的附件数别超过三五个，多了就打包。In the compose window, find the attach button (usually a paperclip 📎), click it, pick your file, and wait for the upload — it counts only when the file name and size appear above or below the body. Phones work the same way. Two small rules: keep attachments under 10 MB (this week's one-pager is far smaller), and no more than a handful per email — zip them if there are more.

动作二 · 按规则命名Move 2 · Name it by the rule

“新建文档1.docx”“未命名(3).pdf”“最终版真的最终版.docx”——这些名字在你自己的电脑里已经够乱，进了别人的下载文件夹就彻底成了孤儿：没人知道它是谁的、是什么、哪周的。本课程的命名规则只有一句：“New Document 1.docx”, “Untitled (3).pdf”, “final-REALLY-final.docx” — names like these are messy enough on your own computer; in someone else's Downloads folder they become orphans nobody can place: whose, what, which week? The course naming rule is one line:

课程 - 周次 - 内容 - 化名course - week - what - pseudonym
本周就是：数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-晨星.pdf（把“晨星”换成你的化名）。四段信息各答一个问题：哪门课的 · 哪一周的 · 是什么 · 谁的。以后每周的作业文件都用它；第 5 周（B4）会把这条规则扩展到你的整个文件世界。中文版或英文版命名都可以（数字化学习-W2-… 或 DL-W2-…），四段齐全即可。This week that means: DL-W2-self-intro-Chenxing.pdf (swap in your own pseudonym). Four fields, four questions answered: which course · which week · what · whose. Every weekly file from now on uses it; Week 5 (B4) extends the rule to your whole file world. Either the Chinese or the English form of the name is fine (数字化学习-W2-… or DL-W2-…) — what matters is all four fields, present and in order.

格式建议用 PDF：在任何设备上打开都长一个样，也不会被对方的软件版本改乱。文档软件里“导出 / 另存为 PDF”即可；实在导不了，.docx 也可以。改名的方法：先在电脑里把文件改好名，再添加为附件（比传上去再想办法容易得多）。Prefer PDF: it looks identical on every device and no software version can scramble it. Use “Export / Save as PDF” in your writing app; if that fails, .docx is accepted. Renaming: rename the file on your computer first, then attach — far easier than fixing it after upload.

动作三 · 正文里提到它Move 3 · Mention it in the body

在正文（通常是最后一段）写一句：“附件是我的一页自我介绍（数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-晨星.pdf）。”这句一举三得：读的人不用猜附件是什么；你写这句时自然会检查附件加了没有；很多邮箱看到正文里的“附件”两个字，还会在你忘加附件时弹窗提醒。In the body (usually the last paragraph) write: “Attached is my one-page self-intro (DL-W2-self-intro-Chenxing.pdf).” One sentence, three wins: the reader never guesses; writing it makes you check the attachment is really there; and many mail services, seeing the word “attached”, will pop a warning if you forgot it.

忘了附件怎么办If you forget the attachment

每个用邮件的人都发过“裸奔邮件”。补救本身就是规范的一部分：马上再发一封，主题在原主题后加“ · 补充附件”，第一句说明（“抱歉，刚才漏了附件，补在这封里”），附上文件。自己发现、当天补上——在这门课里不扣分。Everyone who uses email has sent the parcel-less letter. Recovering is part of the craft: send a follow-up immediately, add “ · attachment” to the original subject, open with one line (“sorry — the attachment slipped; here it is”), and attach the file. Catch it yourself, fix it the same day — no points lost in this course.
