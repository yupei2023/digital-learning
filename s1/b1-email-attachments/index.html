规范邮件与附件Email & Attachments

1987 年 9 月 14 日，北京一间实验室的计算机发出了第一封发自中国的电子邮件。那封邮件写道：“ACROSS THE GREAT WALL WE CAN REACH EVERY CORNER IN THE WORLD——越过万里长城，我们可以到达世界任一个角落。”将近四十年过去，聊天软件来了又变，电子邮件还在：升学申请、求助老师、联系陌生的专业人士、注册几乎所有正式的账号——世界的“正式通道”依然靠它。

On September 14, 1987, a computer in a Beijing laboratory sent the first email ever to leave China. It read: “ACROSS THE GREAT WALL WE CAN REACH EVERY CORNER IN THE WORLD.” Nearly forty years on, chat apps have come and gone — email is still here: school applications, asking a teacher for help, reaching a professional you have never met, registering nearly every formal account. The world's official channel still runs on it.

这周，你要发出你在这门课里的第一封邮件：一封格式规范、带附件的自我介绍，发给你的导师。它是本课程第一个计分任务——只有 5 分，但它教的东西，你会用一辈子。

This week you send your first email of this course: a properly formatted self-introduction with an attachment, addressed to your mentor. It is the course's first graded task — only 5 points, but what it teaches, you will use for life.

核心问题Essential question

  • 我能写一封得体的邮件吗？Can I write a proper email?

学完本周，你应能By the end of this week you should be able to

  1. ① 发出一封格式规范的自我介绍邮件：主题一句话说清“谁 · 什么事”，称呼得体，正文分三小段（我是谁 · 我为什么学这门课 · 一个问题或期待），署名完整——并知道 24–48 小时的回复预期。① Send one properly formatted self-introduction email: a subject line that says who and what in one line, a proper greeting, a body in three short paragraphs (who I am · why I am taking this course · one question or hope), a full sign-off — and know the 24–48-hour reply expectation.
  2. ② 正确使用附件与“收件人 / 抄送 / 回复 / 全部回复”：附件按课程命名规则命名、在正文中提到；说出收件人与抄送的分工、“回复”与“全部回复”各在什么时候用。② Use attachments and To / Cc / Reply / Reply-all correctly: name the attachment by the course file-naming rule and mention it in the body; say what To and Cc each do, and when Reply and Reply-all are each the right button.
  3. ③ 完成“学习状态自测”（第 2 周 · 不计分），并把结果邮件发给自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”）——既是本周技能的一次真实练习，也为第 16 周的对照保存基线。③ Complete the learning self-check (Week 2 · ungraded) and email the results to yourself (subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”) — real practice of this week's skill, and insurance for the Week-16 comparison.

预计用时Time

120 分钟以内：微课约 25 · 跟做约 25 · 任务约 50 · 反思与日志约 20。微课页上的短视频已算在微课的分钟里；标注“选看”的视频不计入。第一次发正式邮件会比表里写的慢——这很正常，没有迟交处罚，只有建议节奏。Within 120 minutes: micro-lessons ~25 · follow-along ~25 · task ~50 · reflect & log ~20. The short clips on the lesson pages are counted inside the lesson minutes; anything marked optional is not. A first formal email runs slower than the table says — that is normal. No late penalty, only a suggested pace.

本单元的 AI 标注：🚫 独立AI designation: 🚫 Independent
本周全程不使用 AI——包括写邮件正文和自我介绍。这类任务是给你自己的一面镜子：评的是投入与格式规范，不评文笔好坏、不评“答得对不对”。自我介绍写得朴素完全没关系——它必须是你的。No AI anywhere this week — including the email body and the self-introduction. Tasks like this are a mirror for yourself: scored on engagement and correctness of form, never on elegant prose or on “being right”. A plain self-introduction is perfectly fine — it just has to be yours.
