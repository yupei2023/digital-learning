邮件、截图与附件Email, Screenshots & Attachments

5 分5 pts🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min一周One week

1987 年 9 月 20 日 20 时 55 分，北京车道沟一间实验室里，一封电子邮件第一次从中国成功发了出去。信只有一句话：“ACROSS THE GREAT WALL WE CAN REACH EVERY CORNER IN THE WORLD——越过长城，走向世界。”六天前的第一次尝试卡在协议里的一个小错上，中德两国的工程师排查了整整一周才让它发出去。将近四十年过去，聊天软件来了又变，电子邮件还在：升学申请、求助老师、联系陌生的专业人士、注册几乎所有正式的账号——世界的“正式通道”依然靠它。

At 20:55 on 20 September 1987, in a laboratory on Chedaogou Road in Beijing, an email left China successfully for the first time. It carried one sentence: “ACROSS THE GREAT WALL WE CAN REACH EVERY CORNER IN THE WORLD.” A first attempt six days earlier had stalled on a small fault in a protocol, and engineers in China and Germany spent a week hunting it down before the message went. Nearly forty years on, chat apps have come and gone — email is still here: school applications, asking a teacher for help, reaching a professional you have never met, registering nearly every formal account. The world's official channel still runs on it.

这周，你要发出你在这门课里的第一封邮件：一封格式规范、带附件的自我介绍，发给你的导师。附件不是另写的一份文件——是你自己从屏幕上截下来、标注好、把私人信息遮住的一张图。它是本课程第一个计分任务——只有 5 分，但它教的两样东西（写一封得体的信、把屏幕上的东西变成能寄出去的证据），你会用一辈子。

This week you send your first email of this course: a properly formatted self-introduction with an attachment, addressed to your mentor. The attachment is not a separate document you write — it is one screenshot you take off your own screen, mark up, and mask. It is the course’s first graded task — only 5 points, but what it teaches (writing a proper letter, and turning what is on a screen into evidence you can send), you will use for life.

核心问题Essential question

  • 我能写一封得体的邮件吗？Can I write a proper email?

学完本周，你应能By the end of this week you should be able to

  1. ① 发出一封格式规范的自我介绍邮件：主题一句话说清“谁 · 什么事”，称呼得体，正文分三小段（我是谁 · 我为什么学这门课 · 一个问题或期待），署名完整。① Send one properly formatted self-introduction email: a subject line that says who and what in one line, a proper greeting, a body in three short paragraphs (who I am · why I am taking this course · one question or hope), a full sign-off.
  2. ② 自己截一张图当附件：在电脑或手机上截图，加标注、把私人信息打码，按课程命名规则命名并在正文中提到；收到导师回信后用“回复”按钮接一句（不回信不扣分）。② Make your own screenshot the attachment: capture it on a computer or phone, annotate it and mask anything private, name it by the course file-naming rule and mention it in the body; when the mentor’s reply lands, answer with the Reply button (not replying costs nothing).
  3. ③ 完成“学习状态自测”（第 2 周 · 不计分），并把结果邮件发给自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”）——既是本周技能的一次真实练习，也为第 16 周的对照保存基线。③ Complete the learning self-check (Week 2 · ungraded) and email the results to yourself (subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”) — real practice of this week's skill, and insurance for the Week-16 comparison.

这三条也是本单元量规的评分行（第 ③ 条不计分，完成即可）。These three are also the rubric rows (row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough). → 量规Rubric

本周你还会学到：怎样提一个带截图的问题（这门课全年的“求助姿势”）、收件人与抄送的分工、“回复”与“全部回复”各在什么时候用、24–48 小时的回复预期。这些不进量规——第 2 周页面的自测题与分箱卡帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: how to ask a question with a screenshot (this course’s all-year way of asking for help), what To and Cc each do, when Reply and Reply-all are each the right button, and the 24–48-hour reply expectation. None of it is graded — the Week 2 self-quiz and the sorting cards check it for you, ungraded.

本单元的 AI 标注：🚫 独立AI designation: 🚫 Independent
本周全程不使用 AI——包括写邮件正文、自我介绍，以及那张截图上写的标注。这类任务是给你自己的一面镜子：评的是投入与格式规范，不评文笔好坏、不评“答得对不对”。自我介绍写得朴素完全没关系——它必须是你的。No AI anywhere this week — the email body, the self-introduction, and the label you write on the screenshot included. Tasks like this are a mirror for yourself: scored on engagement and correctness of form, never on elegant prose or on “being right”. A plain self-introduction is perfectly fine — it just has to be yours.
