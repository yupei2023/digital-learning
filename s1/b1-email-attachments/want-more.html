做Do

  • 给邮箱设置签名档——在邮箱设置里找到“签名”，存入“化名（学生编号）· 数字化学习课程学生”。设置一次，以后每封邮件自动带署名。Set up an email signature — find Signature in your mail settings and save “pseudonym (student ID) · Digital Learning student”. Set once, signed forever.
  • 给收件箱建第一个文件夹（或标签）——命名“数字化学习”，把本周的往来邮件都挪进去。第 7 周（B6）整理数字工作台时，你会感谢现在的自己。Create your inbox's first folder (or label) — name it “Digital Learning” and move this week's emails in. Week 7 (B6), when the whole workspace gets organised, will thank you.
  • 用英语再写一版自我介绍邮件——照任务页的英文模板，发给自己。不用发给任何人看；写完你会发现六个部位在两种语言里一模一样。Rewrite your self-intro email in English — follow the task page's English template and send it to yourself. Nobody needs to see it; you will find the six parts identical across languages.
  • 问问家里人——他们最近一次发正式邮件是什么时候、写的什么？把你这周学的六个部位讲给他们听——能讲清楚，才是真学会。Ask at home — when did your family last send a formal email, and what for? Teach them the six parts you learned this week: explaining it clearly is the proof you own it.
