截图、彩排，与第一封邮件Screenshots, rehearsal, and the first email

本周的跟做有四幕，前两幕做出你要寄出的那件东西，后两幕彩排寄它的动作。为什么是这个顺序？因为截图是可逆的，正式邮件不可逆：截坏了删掉重截，一分钱不花；正式邮件发出去却收不回。可逆的先动手，不可逆的先彩排——收件人是你自己，怎么错都没关系。这封测试信是你本周三封邮件里的第一封、最随意的一封；后面两封（给自己的基线信、给导师的计分信）会一封比一封正式。

This week’s follow-along runs in four acts: the first two make the thing you will send, the last two rehearse the sending. Why that order? Because a screenshot is reversible and a formal email is not: a bad capture is deleted and retaken at no cost, while a sent email cannot be unsent. Reversible things get tried; irreversible ones get rehearsed — with you as the recipient, where every mistake is free. This test email is the first and most casual of the week’s three; the next two (the baseline to yourself, the graded one to your mentor) each step up in formality.

第一幕 · 两台设备各截一张（7 分钟）Act 1 · One capture on each device (7 min)

  1. 电脑：用微课 3 表里你这台电脑的截法，框选本页的标题部分截一张。然后立刻做一件事：找到它——剪贴板预览？桌面？截图文件夹？知道图去了哪里，和会截一样重要。Computer: with your machine’s method from micro-lesson 3, region-capture this page’s title. Then do one thing at once: find the file — clipboard preview? desktop? a screenshots folder? Knowing where it went matters as much as capturing it.
  2. 手机：用你手机的按键组合把任意页面截一张，到相册里找到它。（只有手机的同学：电脑那张等下次碰到电脑再补，不影响任何分数。）Phone: capture any page with your phone’s button combo, then find it in the gallery. (Phone only? The computer one waits for your next computer; it costs no points.)
  3. 顺手练一次删除重截：把不满意的那张删掉，再截一张更准的——体会一下“可逆”是什么感觉。Practise the delete-and-retake once while you are here: remove the worse capture and take a tighter one — feel what “reversible” means.

第二幕 · 截出“要寄出的那一张”，标注并打码（6 分钟）Act 2 · Capture the one you will send, then annotate and mask it (6 min)

前一幕是练手，这一幕做成品——本周任务要发给导师的附件，就在这一幕里诞生。Act 1 was practice; this act makes the real thing — the attachment your mentor receives in this week’s task is born right here.

  1. 挑一个能替你说话的画面，截下来。三个现成的方向，任选其一（也可以自己想）：① 你为这门课定下的每周两个固定学习时段（日历、备忘录、便签、课程表都行——第 1 周第 4 步定过）；② 你正在学、或最想学的一样东西的屏幕画面（一段视频、一本电子书、一个应用）；③ 你把这门课的首页收进收藏夹的样子。Pick a frame that speaks for you and capture it. Three ready directions — take one (or invent your own): ① the two weekly study slots you set for this course (a calendar, a notes app, a sticky note, a timetable — you fixed them in Week 1, Step 4); ② the screen of something you are learning or most want to learn (a video, an e-book, an app); ③ this course’s home page sitting in your bookmarks.
  2. 加标注（微课 4）：画一个框圈住你最想让导师看的那一处，旁边写一小句字说清它是什么（“周二晚 7 点，我给这门课留的时间”）。Annotate it (micro-lesson 4): draw a box around the one thing you want your mentor to see and add a short label saying what it is (“Tuesday 7pm — the time I keep for this course”).
  3. 打码：把画面里的真名、账号、别人的名字或消息盖住，或者裁掉那一块。这张图要寄给别人，所以这一步不是练习，是必须。Mask it: cover — or crop away — any real name, account detail, or other person’s name or message in the frame. This image is going to travel, so this step is not practice; it is required.
  4. 存好它。改名的事下一步不急——任务页第 1 步会带你按“课程-周-内容-化名”改成 数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-化名.png。Save it. No rush on the name — step 1 of the task page renames it course-week-what-pseudonym, into DL-W2-self-intro-pseudonym.png.

第三幕 · 热身：给自己发一封带附件的测试邮件（8 分钟）Act 3 · Warm-up: a test email, with attachment, to yourself (8 min)

跟着视频操作；各家邮箱的界面略有差别，但按钮都在类似的位置。Work along with the clip; mail services differ slightly, but the buttons live in the same places.

怎么发电子邮件？（保姆级，小白向） · 4:04 · B 站 UP 主 友人0X45F · 2025（已计入跟做分钟）How to send an email — a step-by-step beginner's guide · 4:04 · Bilibili uploader 友人0X45F · 2025 (counted in the follow-along minutes) · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗

  1. 存联系人：（在校学生）从开课信里找到导师的邮箱，加进邮箱的通讯录 / 联系人，备注导师姓氏。存过通讯录，以后写信不会敲错地址——一个字母之差，邮件就去了陌生人的信箱。Save the contact: (enrolled) take your mentor's address from the welcome letter and add it to your mail contacts, noting the family name. A saved contact means never mistyping the address — one wrong letter sends your email to a stranger.
  2. 写一封给自己的测试邮件：收件人填你自己的地址；主题写“测试 · 我的第一封带附件的邮件”；正文两三行随意；就用第二幕那张标注好的截图作附件。Compose a test email to yourself: your own address in To; subject “test · my first email with an attachment”; two or three casual lines; the annotated screenshot from Act 2 as the attachment.
  3. 发送，然后到收件箱看它：这是本活动最有用的一步——你第一次以“收件人”的眼睛看自己的邮件。主题在列表里显示成什么样？附件图标出现了吗？点开附件能打开吗？Send, then open it in your inbox: the most useful step of the whole activity — your first look at your own email through a recipient's eyes. How does the subject read in the list? Did the attachment icon appear? Does the file open?
  4. 练一次“回复”：在这封测试邮件上点“回复”，写一个字发出。看到了吗——主题自动变成“Re: …”，往来记录连在同一个话题里。Practise Reply once: press Reply on the test email and send a single word. See how the subject becomes “Re: …” and the exchange stays in one thread.

第四幕 · 火眼金睛：规范还是还差一步？（6 分钟）Act 4 · Sharp eyes: proper, or one step short? (6 min)

九张卡片，每张判断一下：这个写法“规范”，还是“还差一步”？点按钮立即看解释。不计分，答错正是练习的目的。Nine cards. Judge each: is this “proper” or “one step short”? Press a button for the instant explanation. Ungraded — wrong answers are the point of practising.

九张卡都判完了？把“还差一步”的六张在脑子里各改成规范版——那就是你待会儿要写的真邮件。All nine judged? Mentally rewrite the six “one step short” cards into their proper versions — that is the real email you are about to write.
