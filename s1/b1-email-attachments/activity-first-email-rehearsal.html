第一封邮件的彩排The first-email rehearsal

正式邮件发出去就收不回。所以先在“空场”里彩排一遍：收件人是你自己，怎么错都没关系。彩排分两幕：动手发一封测试邮件，再用八张卡片练眼力。

A formal email cannot be unsent — so rehearse on an empty stage first, with yourself as the recipient, where every mistake is free. Two acts: send a test email with your own hands, then train your eye on eight cards.

第一幕 · 热身：给自己发一封带附件的测试邮件（15 分钟）Act 1 · Warm-up: a test email, with attachment, to yourself (15 min)

跟着视频操作；各家邮箱的界面略有差别，但按钮都在类似的位置。Work along with the clip; mail services differ slightly, but the buttons live in the same places.

怎么发电子邮件？（保姆级，小白向） · 4:04 · B 站 UP 主 友人0X45F · 2025（已计入跟做分钟）How to send an email — a step-by-step beginner's guide · 4:04 · Bilibili uploader 友人0X45F · 2025 (counted in the follow-along minutes) · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗

  1. 存联系人：（在校学生）从开课信里找到导师的邮箱，加进邮箱的通讯录 / 联系人，备注导师姓氏。存过通讯录，以后写信不会敲错地址——一个字母之差，邮件就去了陌生人的信箱。Save the contact: (enrolled) take your mentor's address from the welcome letter and add it to your mail contacts, noting the family name. A saved contact means never mistyping the address — one wrong letter sends your email to a stranger.
  2. 写一封给自己的测试邮件：收件人填你自己的地址；主题写“测试 · 我的第一封带附件的邮件”；正文两三行随意；随便挑一个小文件（一张图、一个文档）作附件。Compose a test email to yourself: your own address in To; subject “test · my first email with an attachment”; two or three casual lines; any small file (a picture, a document) as the attachment.
  3. 发送，然后到收件箱看它：这是本活动最有用的一步——你第一次以“收件人”的眼睛看自己的邮件。主题在列表里显示成什么样？附件图标出现了吗？点开附件能打开吗？Send, then open it in your inbox: the most useful step of the whole activity — your first look at your own email through a recipient's eyes. How does the subject read in the list? Did the attachment icon appear? Does the file open?
  4. 练一次“回复”：在这封测试邮件上点“回复”，写一个字发出。看到了吗——主题自动变成“Re: …”，往来记录连在同一个话题里。Practise Reply once: press Reply on the test email and send a single word. See how the subject becomes “Re: …” and the exchange stays in one thread.

第二幕 · 火眼金睛：规范还是还差一步？（10 分钟）Act 2 · Sharp eyes: proper, or one step short? (10 min)

八张卡片，每张判断一下：这个写法“规范”，还是“还差一步”？点按钮立即看解释。不计分，答错正是练习的目的。Eight cards. Judge each: is this “proper” or “one step short”? Press a button for the instant explanation. Ungraded — wrong answers are the point of practising.

八张卡都判完了？把“还差一步”的五张在脑子里各改成规范版——那就是你待会儿要写的真邮件。All eight judged? Mentally rewrite the five “one step short” cards into their proper versions — that is the real email you are about to write.
