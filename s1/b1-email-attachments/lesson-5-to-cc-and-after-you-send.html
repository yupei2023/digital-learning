收件人、抄送与发出去之后To, Cc, and after you hit Send

最后一课收尾两件事：信寄给谁，和信寄走以后。写邮件的界面上有两三个收信人框，回邮件时有两个按钮——放错框、按错钮，是邮件世界最常见的两类事故；点下“发送”之后，还有等待、回信与追问的分寸。这两样都不进本周量规，自测题与跟做的分箱卡会替你检查。

The last lesson closes two loops: who the letter goes to, and what happens once it has gone. The compose window has two or three recipient boxes and the reply screen has two buttons — wrong box and wrong button are email's two commonest accidents; and after Send there is a rhythm of waiting, replying and following up. Neither is graded this week: the self-quiz and the follow-along's sorting cards check them for you.

收件人（To）To这件事需要 TA 做点什么的人：回答、批改、处理。本周就是你的导师。The person the matter asks something of: an answer, a review, an action. This week: your mentor.“请您办 / 请您回。”“Over to you.”
抄送（Cc）Cc需要知道、但不需要行动的人。TA 收到一份副本，通常不必回复。People who need to know but need not act. They get a copy and normally don't reply.“告诉您一声。”“Just so you know.”
密送（Bcc）Bcc也收到副本，但其他收件人看不到 TA。最正当的用法：群发通知时把大家都放密送，互相看不到彼此的邮箱地址——保护隐私。本周用不到，知道它存在就行。Also gets a copy, but invisible to the other recipients. Its most legitimate use: putting a whole list in Bcc for an announcement so nobody sees anyone else's address — privacy. Not needed this week; just know it exists.“悄悄告诉您。”“Quietly told.”

两个按钮：“回复”还是“全部回复”？Two buttons: Reply or Reply-all?

回复（Reply）：只回给发件人一个人。全部回复（Reply-all）：回给发件人 + 所有收件人 + 所有抄送的人。Reply goes to the sender alone. Reply-all goes to the sender + everyone in To + everyone in Cc.

判断只问一个问题：我这句话，对在场的每个人都有用吗？有用——全部回复（比如小组讨论里补充一条大家都需要的信息）；只对发件人有用——回复。经典事故：老师群发通知写“收到请回复”，有同学点了“全部回复”，于是全班六十个人都收到一封“收到”。一个人按错还好，十个人按错，就是六百封无关邮件。One question decides it: is what I'm about to say useful to everyone on this email? Useful to all — Reply-all (say, adding a fact the whole group needs). Useful only to the sender — Reply. The classic accident: a teacher's announcement says “please confirm receipt”, someone hits Reply-all, and all sixty classmates receive a “got it”. One wrong press is fine; ten wrong presses are six hundred useless emails.

这学期你几乎只需要“回复”：导师回信后，在原邮件上点“回复”继续同一个话题，而不是新写一封——这样往来记录连在一起，双方都省力。This semester you will almost always want plain Reply: when your mentor writes back, press Reply on that email to continue the same thread instead of composing fresh — the exchange stays in one place and saves everyone effort.

24–48 小时：邮件的时间刻度24–48 hours: email's unit of time

本课程的约定（课程说明里写着）：邮件教师或导师，24–48 小时内回复，节假日除外。这不是“慢”，是邮件本来的节奏——它给认真读、认真回留出时间。所以：发出之后正常生活，不用刷新收件箱；两个整天（不含节假日）过去仍无回音，再在原邮件上点“回复”礼貌追问一句：“老师您好，想确认一下上一封是否收到，谢谢！”追问不失礼——石沉大海才最耽误事，也许你的邮件真的进了垃圾箱。The course's promise (it is in the course guide): email the instructor or your mentor and expect a reply within 24–48 hours, holidays excepted. That is not slowness — it is email's native rhythm, leaving room for careful reading and a careful answer. So: send, then get on with your life; no inbox-refreshing. If two full working days pass in silence, press Reply on your own email and ask politely: “Hello — just checking the last message reached you. Thank you!” A follow-up is never rude; silence helps no one, and your email may genuinely have landed in a spam folder.

你的导师在看什么What your mentor is looking at

导师期待Mentors hope to see导师不期待Mentors are not looking for
一眼能认出是谁、什么事的主题；说清楚的正文；命好名的附件；一句真实的问题或期待——以及你敢发出第一封邮件本身。A subject that names who and what at a glance; a body that says things clearly; a well-named attachment; one real question or hope — and the fact that you dared to send a first email at all.华丽的文笔、完美的措辞、成熟的“职场腔”。本周任务评的是投入与格式规范，不评文采。朴素而清楚，就是满分的样子。Fine prose, perfect phrasing, a grown-up “professional voice”. This week is scored on engagement and correctness of form, never on style. Plain and clear IS what full marks look like.

你会收到什么：导师会回复你的这封邮件——问候你、回应你的问题、告诉你本周任务的得分与一条改进建议（评分反馈在一周内送达）。这也是你练“回复”按钮的机会：收到后回一句“收到，谢谢老师”，一次规范的邮件往来就完整了。What comes back: your mentor will reply to this very email — greeting you, answering your question, giving this week's score and one suggestion (scoring feedback arrives within a week). That reply is your chance to practise the Reply button: a one-line “received, thank you!” completes your first proper email exchange.

发送前的最后一眼The last look before Send

养成一个五秒钟的习惯——发送前从上到下扫一遍：收件人对吗？主题写了吗？称呼、三段、署名都在吗？附件加了吗、名字对吗？这五秒钟，能拦下九成的邮件事故。第 2 周页面的自评清单就是这一眼的完整版。Build a five-second habit — before Send, sweep top to bottom: right recipient? subject written? greeting, three paragraphs, signature all present? attachment on, name correct? Those five seconds catch nine of every ten email accidents. The self-review list on the Week 2 page is this look, in full.

那封发给自己的基线邮件，排在给导师写信之前The baseline email to yourself comes before the letter to your mentor
按任务页的顺序，你会先做完学习状态自测、把结果发给你自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”），再写给导师的计分邮件。三封邮件正好一封比一封正式：测试信 → 基线信 → 计分信；写到“我已完成自测”那句时，它已是事实。基线信也是第 16 周学期反思的对照起点——那时你会去邮箱里搜这个主题。In the task-page order you first finish the learning self-check and email the results to yourself (subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”), then write the graded email to your mentor. The week's three emails climb one step at a time: test → baseline → graded; by the time you type “I have finished this week's self-check”, it is a fact. The baseline is also the anchor your Week-16 reflection will search your inbox for.
