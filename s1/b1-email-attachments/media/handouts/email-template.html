规范邮件六件套The six-part email

发任何正式邮件前，从上到下过一遍这张表。本学期每周给导师的邮件都用它。

Before any formal email, run this table top to bottom. Every mentor email this semester uses it.

部位Part写Write检查Check
1 主题1 Subject【事由】 · 【我是谁】——本周：B1 自我介绍 · 化名（学生编号）[matter] · [who] — this week: B1 self-intro · pseudonym (ID)一行能看出“谁 · 什么事”。Who and what, visible in one line.
2 称呼2 Greeting＿老师，您好！Dear Mr./Ms. ____,不是“在吗”。Not “are you there?”.
3 正文3 Body三小段：我是谁 · 事情本身 · 问题或期待；段落间空一行。Three short paragraphs: who I am · the matter · a question or hope; a blank line between.一段一件事，不是一整块。One matter per paragraph, no solid block.
4 附件说明4 Attachment line“附件是＿＿（文件名）。”文件名 = 课程-周-内容-化名，如 数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-晨星.png“Attached is ____ (file name).” Name = course-week-what-pseudonym, e.g. DL-W2-self-intro-Chenxing.png附件真的加了；名字不是“新建文档1”。Really attached; not “New Document 1”.
5 结尾5 Closing祝好！/ 盼复，谢谢老师！Best regards, / Looking forward to your reply — thank you!有一句收束。The letter has its full stop.
6 署名6 Signature化名（学生编号）· 数字化学习课程学生Pseudonym (student ID) · Digital Learning student用化名，不用真实全名。Pseudonym, never the real full name.
发送前的五秒钟The five seconds before Send
收件人对吗 → 主题写了吗 → 六个部位齐吗 → 附件加了、名字对吗 → 从头到尾重读了一遍吗。Right recipient → subject written → six parts present → attachment on and named right → re-read once, top to bottom.
两个按钮The two buttons
回复 = 只回发件人（几乎总是它）。全部回复 = 回给所有人——只在“这句话对每个人都有用”时按。Reply = the sender alone (almost always the one). Reply-all = everyone — only when your line is useful to them all.
