B1 量规B1 Rubric

量规的三行就是本周的三条目标。第 ① ② 行计分（3 + 2 = 5 分），第 ③ 行不计分、完成即可。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。本任务是 🚫 独立任务——评投入与格式规范，不评文笔，不评“答得对不对”。

The three rows are this week's three objectives. Rows ① ② carry points (3 + 2 = 5); row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence. This is an 🚫 Independent task — scored on engagement and correctness of form, never on prose or on being right.

不进量规的知识点（怎样提一个带截图的问题；24–48 小时回复预期；收件人与抄送的分工、两个回复按钮的用法）由自测题与分箱卡帮你检查，不计分。第 ② 行评的是你交出的那张图：是不是你自己截的、标注了没有、私人信息遮了没有、名字对不对——不评构图，不评好不好看。The knowledge points that stay out of the rubric (asking with a screenshot; the 24–48-hour expectation; To vs Cc and the two Reply buttons) are checked by the self-quiz and the sorting cards, ungraded. Row ② scores the image you hand in: that you captured it yourself, that it is annotated, that private details are covered, that the name is right — never its composition or looks.

规范要求（不计分的底线）Form requirements (the ungraded floor)

  • 邮件与附件里使用化名与学生编号，不出现真实全名、照片、学校全名。截图里若有这些，发出前用标注工具盖住或裁掉——误放了提醒改正，不扣分。Pseudonym and student ID in the email and attachment; no real full name, photo, or full school name. If a screenshot holds any of these, cover or crop them before it travels — a slip draws a reminder and a fix, never a deduction.
  • 🚫 独立：全程未使用 AI（含正文、自我介绍、截图上标注文字的写作与润色）。使用 AI 的，相应行记 0 并按学术诚信规则处理。🚫 Independent: no AI anywhere (writing or polishing the body, the self-intro, and the label on the screenshot included). AI use scores the row 0 under the integrity policy.
  • 晚发不扣分——导师照常评分反馈，只是晚到。Late costs nothing — scoring and feedback come as usual, just later.

合成How the 5 points add up

第 ① 行（3 分）+ 第 ② 行（2 分）= B1 共 5 分，计入第一学期“数字基本功 B1–B6 共 30 分”。导师收到邮件后一周内回复评分与一条建议；本周不需要登记任何链接。Row ① (3) + row ② (2) = 5 points for B1, part of Semester 1's “Digital Basics B1–B6, 30 points”. Your mentor replies with the score and one suggestion within a week of receiving the email; nothing is registered this week.
