一封邮件的六个部位The six parts of an email

邮件不是聊天。聊天是一来一回的碎片，邮件是一次说完整件事的“信”。一封规范的邮件有六个部位，少一个，读的人就要多花一分力气猜。

Email is not chat. Chat is fragments bouncing back and forth; an email is a letter that says the whole thing once. A proper email has six parts — leave one out, and your reader spends extra effort guessing.

六个部位，从上到下The six parts, top to bottom

部位Part它做什么What it does本周的写法This week's version
1 主题1 Subject一句话说清“谁 · 什么事”。它决定你的邮件会不会被及时打开——收件箱列表里只显示这一行。Says who and what in one line. It decides whether your email gets opened promptly — the inbox list shows only this line.B1 自我介绍 · 晨星（S1-07）B1 self-intro · Chenxing (S1-07)
2 称呼2 Greeting确认“这封信是写给您的”。用对方的姓氏加合适的称谓，加一句问候。Confirms “this letter is for you”: family name plus the right title, plus a greeting.李老师，您好！（导师姓氏见开课信）Dear Mr./Ms. Li, (your mentor's name is in the welcome letter)
3 正文3 Body一段说一件事，段落之间空一行。三段以内说完，别写成一整块。One matter per paragraph, a blank line between paragraphs. Three short paragraphs beat one solid block.段 1 我是谁 · 段 2 我为什么学这门课 · 段 3 一个问题或期待¶1 who I am · ¶2 why I am taking this course · ¶3 one question or hope
4 附件说明4 Attachment line正文里提一句附件是什么。读的人不用猜，也能立刻发现“咦，附件忘了”。One line naming what is attached. The reader never guesses — and you catch a forgotten attachment instantly.“附件是我的一页自我介绍（数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-晨星.pdf）。”“Attached is my one-page self-intro (DL-W2-self-intro-Chenxing.pdf).”
5 结尾5 Closing一句礼貌的收束，给整封信一个句号。One courteous line that gives the letter its full stop.“祝好！”或“盼复，谢谢老师！”“Best regards,” or “Looking forward to your reply — thank you!”
6 署名6 Signature你是谁。本课程用化名 + 学生编号（课程里从头到尾都用化名，包括邮件）。Who you are. This course uses pseudonym + student ID throughout — email included.晨星（S1-07）· 数字化学习课程学生Chenxing (S1-07) · Digital Learning student

主题这一行，多说两句Two more words on the subject line

导师每周要看几十封学生邮件。主题“你好”“求助”“（无主题）”都等于让对方开盲盒；写清“谁 · 什么事”，你的邮件就自己排好了队。一个通用公式：【事由】 · 【我是谁】——这学期你会一直用它，比如“B3 任务提交 · 晨星（S1-07）”“请教一个网站问题 · 晨星（S1-07）”。Your mentor reads dozens of student emails a week. “Hello”, “help”, or “(no subject)” hands them a mystery box; who-and-what puts your email at the front of the queue by itself. A formula you will reuse all semester: [matter] · [who I am] — “B3 task · Chenxing (S1-07)”, “a question about my site · Chenxing (S1-07)”.

邮件和聊天软件最大的三个不同The three biggest differences from chat
① 不发“在吗？”——邮件直接把事情说完；② 不拆成十条短句连发——一封信说完一件事；③ 不指望秒回——回复以“天”为单位（本课程 24–48 小时），这不是冷淡，是邮件本来的节奏。① No “are you there?” — an email states the whole matter at once; ② no ten-message bursts — one letter, one matter; ③ no expecting instant replies — email runs on days (24–48 hours in this course); that is not coldness, it is email's native rhythm.
