学习状态自测Learning self-check

这份自测不是考试：没有对错，不计分，不影响任何评分；答案只在你自己的浏览器里显示，不会上传。它是给这学期的你拍的一张“起点照片”——第 15–16 周做学期反思时，你会用同一份题再测一次，和今天的自己并排对照。所以照实答，比“答得好看”重要一百倍。

This self-check is not a test: no right answers, no points, no effect on any score; results display only in your own browser and are never uploaded. It is a photo of your starting point — in Weeks 15–16 you retake the same items and set that self beside today's. So honest answers beat good-looking ones a hundred to one.

[placeholder] 正式题目待定[placeholder] The official items are not yet chosen
这份自测的正式题目（约 15–20 题）将在开课前确定并替换本页内容；第 16 周的复测页用同一份题。下面的 3 条样题只是示意格式：1–5 分自评，1 = 还做不到，5 = 能稳定做到并能教别人。The official items (about 15–20) will be finalised before launch and will replace this page's content; the Week-16 retake page uses the same set. The 3 sample items below only show the format: self-rate 1–5, where 1 = can't yet and 5 = do it reliably and could teach it.

样题（示意）Sample items (format only)

  1. 面对一个真实的学习任务，我能从认得的工具里选出合适的一件，并说出选它、不选别件的理由。Given a real learning task, I can choose a suitable tool from those I know, and say why it — and why not the others.
  2. 我能为自己排出每周固定的学习时段，守不住的时候能找到原因并改一处。I can schedule fixed weekly study slots for myself, and when one collapses I can find the cause and change one thing.
  3. 学完一段内容，我能用自测（合上书考自己）查出自己哪里还没懂。After studying something, I can use self-testing (book closed) to find what I have not yet understood.

最关键的一步：把基线存进你的邮箱The step that matters most: bank the baseline in your inbox

浏览器一关，本页的结果就没了。所以做完自测，立刻给你自己发一封邮件——这一步是本周任务的一部分：Close this browser tab and the results are gone. So the moment you finish, email yourself — this step is part of this week's task:

收件人：你自己的邮箱地址
主题：我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline
正文：逐项分各一行（如 “1 · 3”），最后一行合计。
      一句话：现在的我觉得最没底的是＿＿。
To: your own address
Subject: 我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline
Body: one line per item (e.g. “1 · 3”), the total on the last line.
      One sentence: right now I feel least sure about ____.

主题请一字不差——第 16 周你要靠在邮箱里搜“我的第 2 周基线”找回它。这封信一举两得：它是本周“规范邮件”技能的又一次真实练习（主题、正文、发送，一样不少），也是给三个多月后的自己存的一份时间胶囊。Use the subject word for word — in Week 16 you will find this email by searching your inbox for it. One email, two wins: another real rehearsal of this week's skill (subject, body, send — all of it), and a time capsule banked for the you of three months from now.

怎么用这一页How to use this page

  1. 按现在的真实状态逐项自评——不是“希望的自己”。没有对错。Rate your actual current state, item by item — not the self you wish for. There are no wrong answers.
  2. 点“看一下我的结果”，把逐项分抄下来。Press “Show my results” and copy the per-item scores down.
  3. 给自己发那封基线邮件（上面的模板；主题一字不差）。Send yourself the baseline email (template above; subject word for word).
  4. 第 3 周网站上线后，把这份结果再抄进网站的第一条日志——双保险。邮箱和网站，两处只要有一处在，第 16 周就能对照。When your site goes live in Week 3, copy the results into its first log entry too — a second safety net. If either the inbox or the site survives, Week 16 has its comparison.
  5. （在校学生 · 自愿）愿意的话，也可以把结果表发给导师——自愿、不影响成绩，不发完全没关系；公开学习者不需要发给任何人。(Enrolled · voluntary) If you wish, you may also send the table to your mentor — voluntary, never affecting grades; not sending it is entirely fine. Open learners send it to no one.

说明：在校学生的自测结果经学校同意、家长知情、学生自愿提交导师，不影响成绩，与登记表分开保存；公开学习者的结果只在本地显示，课程不收集。Note: enrolled students' results are shared with mentors with school approval, parent awareness and the student's consent; they never affect grades and are stored apart from the tracking sheet. Open learners' results display locally only — the course collects nothing.
