学习状态自测Learning self-check

这份自测不是考试：没有对错，不计分，不影响任何评分；答案只在你自己的浏览器里显示，不会上传。它是给这学期的你拍的一张“起点照片”——第 15–16 周做学期反思时，你会用同一份题再测一次，和今天的自己并排对照。所以照实答，比“答得好看”重要一百倍。

This self-check is not a test: no right answers, no points, no effect on any score; results display only in your own browser and are never uploaded. It is a photo of your starting point — in Weeks 15–16 you retake the same items and set that self beside today's. So honest answers beat good-looking ones a hundred to one.

同一把尺子，量两次One ruler, used twice
这 12 道题就是本课程正式的学习状态自测，覆盖第一学期真正要教给你的十二件事。第 16 周学期收尾时，你会做到一模一样的 12 道题，一个字都不改——只有同一把尺子量两次，才看得出这一学期你到底走了多远。所以今天照实答：现在打 2 分一点也不丢人，正是它给第 16 周留出了可涨的地方。These 12 items are the course's official learning self-check; between them they cover the twelve things Semester 1 actually sets out to teach you. In Week 16 you will answer the very same 12, not a word changed — only the same ruler used twice can show how far the semester moved you. So answer honestly today: a 2 right now is nothing to be ashamed of; it is precisely what leaves room to rise by Week 16.

12 道题（第 2 周基线）The 12 items (Week-2 baseline)

按现在的真实状态逐项给自己打分：1 = 完全不会 · 2 = 试过，但心里没底 · 3 = 大致做得到 · 4 = 比较有把握 · 5 = 很有把握，还能讲给别人听。没有对错，也没有"标准答案"——十二项各打一个数字，两分钟左右。Rate your actual current state, item by item: 1 = not at all · 2 = tried it, but far from sure · 3 = roughly can · 4 = fairly confident · 5 = very confident, and could explain it to someone. There are no right answers and no model answer — one number for each of the twelve, about two minutes.

  1. 我能写出一封结构完整的邮件——主题一行说清事由、称呼与正文分明、结尾有署名——按课程的命名规则带上附件并在正文里提到它，发送前再核对一遍收件人。I can write a complete, well-formed email — a subject line that says what it is about, a clear greeting and body, a proper sign-off — attach a file named by the course's rule and mention it in the body, and check the recipient once more before sending.
  2. 我能截图或录一段屏幕，把屏幕上发生的事清楚地展示给别人看，并在发出前先遮住不该露的信息。I can take a screenshot or record a stretch of my screen to show someone clearly what happened there, masking anything private before I send it.
  3. 我能建起一个属于自己的网页并持续维护它——作品发布得上去、链接别人打得开、发布之后我还会另存一份备份。I can build a web page of my own and keep it going — work gets published, the link opens for other people, and after each publish I save a backup of my own.
  4. 我能用翻译工具读懂一段英文学习材料，也能察觉译文哪里可能走了样，并想办法核对那一处。I can use a translation tool to get through a passage of English study material, notice where the translation may have gone astray, and find a way to check that spot.
  5. 我能给自己的账号设置结实的密码并安排好保管方式，也能判断一个网站值不值得把我的个人信息交给它。I can set strong passwords for my accounts and arrange a way to keep them, and I can judge whether a site deserves my personal information.
  6. 我能围绕一个学习问题检索资料，把有用的存下来，并记下它的来源，以便日后找得回去。I can search for material on a study question, save what is useful, and record where it came from so that I can find my way back to it.
  7. 我的学习文件、日程和笔记各有固定的落点；换一台设备，我也找得到自己的东西。My study files, my schedule and my notes each have a fixed place; on a different device I can still find my own things.
  8. 面对一个真实的学习任务，我能从认得的工具里选出合适的一件，并说出选它、不选别件的理由。Given a real learning task, I can choose a suitable tool from the ones I know, and say why it — and why not the others.
  9. 我会用自测、间隔复习这一类方法来学：合上书考自己，而不是把书再看一遍。I study with methods such as self-testing and spaced review: closing the book and quizzing myself, rather than reading it through again.
  10. 学完一段之后，我能说出自己哪里真的懂了、哪里还没懂，并据此改一改下一次怎么学。After a stretch of study I can say what I genuinely understand and what I do not, and change something about how I study next time.
  11. 面对一门网上课程或一份学习材料，我能判断它现在适不适合我，并说出这个判断的依据。Faced with an online course or a set of learning material, I can judge whether it suits me right now, and say what that judgement rests on.
  12. 没有人盯着的时候，我也能守住每周固定的学习时段；守不住的那一周，我能找出原因并改一处。With no one checking on me, I can hold to a fixed weekly study slot; in a week when it collapses I can find the cause and change one thing.

最关键的一步：把基线存进你的邮箱The step that matters most: bank the baseline in your inbox

浏览器一关，本页的结果就没了。所以做完自测，立刻给你自己发一封邮件——这一步是本周任务的一部分：Close this browser tab and the results are gone. So the moment you finish, email yourself — this step is part of this week's task:

收件人：你自己的邮箱地址
主题：我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline
正文：逐项分各一行（如 “1 · 3”），最后一行合计。
      一句话：现在的我觉得最没底的是＿＿。
To: your own address
Subject: 我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline
Body: one line per item (e.g. “1 · 3”), the total on the last line.
      One sentence: right now I feel least sure about ____.

主题请一字不差——第 16 周你要靠在邮箱里搜“我的第 2 周基线”找回它。这封信一举两得：它是本周“规范邮件”技能的又一次真实练习（主题、正文、发送，一样不少），也是给三个多月后的自己存的一份时间胶囊。Use the subject word for word — in Week 16 you will find this email by searching your inbox for it. One email, two wins: another real rehearsal of this week's skill (subject, body, send — all of it), and a time capsule banked for the you of three months from now.

怎么用这一页How to use this page

  1. 按现在的真实状态逐项自评——不是“希望的自己”。没有对错。Rate your actual current state, item by item — not the self you wish for. There are no wrong answers.
  2. 点“看一下我的结果”，把逐项分抄下来。Press “Show my results” and copy the per-item scores down.
  3. 给自己发那封基线邮件（上面的模板；主题一字不差）。Send yourself the baseline email (template above; subject word for word).
  4. 回到任务页接着写给导师的信——这时你可以如实写下“我已完成本周的学习状态自测，并把结果发给了自己”。Go back to the task page and write the letter to your mentor — you can now state “I have finished this week's learning self-check and emailed the results to myself” as plain fact.
  5. 第 3 周网站上线后，把这份结果再抄进网站的第一条日志——双保险。邮箱和网站，两处只要有一处在，第 16 周就能对照。When your site goes live in Week 3, copy the results into its first log entry too — a second safety net. If either the inbox or the site survives, Week 16 has its comparison.
  6. （在校学生 · 自愿）愿意的话，也可以把结果表发给导师——自愿、不影响成绩，不发完全没关系；公开学习者不需要发给任何人。(Enrolled · voluntary) If you wish, you may also send the table to your mentor — voluntary, never affecting grades; not sending it is entirely fine. Open learners send it to no one.

说明：在校学生的自测结果经学校同意、家长知情、学生自愿提交导师，不影响成绩，与登记表分开保存；公开学习者的结果只在本地显示，课程不收集。Note: enrolled students' results are shared with mentors with school approval, parent awareness and the student's consent; they never affect grades and are stored apart from the tracking sheet. Open learners' results display locally only — the course collects nothing.
