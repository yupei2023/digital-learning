任务 · 规范自我介绍邮件Task · The formatted self-intro email

带一个附件One attachment发给导师（公开学习者：自己或亲友）To your mentor (open learners: self or a friend)约 50 分钟：自测 18 + 写信 32（首次更久）~50 min: self-check 18 + email 32

要做什么What to do

本周任务分两步，顺序有讲究：先完成学习状态自测（不计分）并把结果发给自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”）；再给你的导师发一封格式规范的自我介绍邮件：主题按课程格式，称呼得体，正文三小段（我是谁 · 我为什么学这门课 · 一个问题或期待），署名完整；附上一页自我介绍文档，按命名规则命名，并在正文里提到。按这个顺序，写到“我已完成自测”那句时，它已经句句是真话。本周不用登记链接——导师收到你的邮件，就是提交。

This week's task runs in two steps, and the order matters: first complete the learning self-check (ungraded) and email the results to yourself (subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”); then email your mentor a properly formatted self-introduction: subject in the course format, proper greeting, three short paragraphs (who I am · why I am taking this course · one question or hope), full sign-off; attach a one-page self-intro document named by the rule and mention it in the body. Done in this order, the template line “I have finished this week's self-check” is true the moment you write it. Nothing to register this week — your mentor receiving the email IS the submission.

这样一来，本周你发出的三封邮件正好一封比一封正式：给自己的随意测试信（彩排）→ 给自己的固定主题基线信 → 给导师的计分信。每一封都是下一封的练习。Seen together, the week's three emails form a rising ramp, each more formal than the last: the casual test email to yourself (rehearsal) → the fixed-subject baseline email to yourself → the graded email to your mentor. Each one rehearses the next.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 主题：B1 自我介绍 · 化名（学生编号）——例如“B1 自我介绍 · 晨星（S1-07）”。你的学生编号和你登记的化名都在开课信里 [placeholder]，主题、署名、文件名三处用的是同一套。公开学习者：自拟一个化名（不用编号），并在整门课里保持一致。Subject: B1 self-intro · pseudonym (student ID) — e.g. “B1 self-intro · Chenxing (S1-07)”. Your student ID and your registered pseudonym are both in the welcome letter [placeholder]; the subject, signature and file name all use the same pair. Open learners: invent a pseudonym (no ID needed) and keep it consistent through the whole course.
  • 六个部位齐全：主题 · 称呼 · 三段正文 · 附件说明 · 结尾 · 署名（化名 + 学生编号）。All six parts present: subject · greeting · three-paragraph body · attachment line · closing · signature (pseudonym + student ID).
  • 附件：一页自我介绍文档，建议 PDF，命名为“数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-化名.pdf”；正文里提到它。Attachment: a one-page self-intro document, PDF preferred, named “DL-W2-self-intro-pseudonym.pdf”; mentioned in the body.
  • 正文里带一句：“我已完成本周的学习状态自测，并把结果发给了自己。”——导师会据此在登记表上打 ✓。Include the line: “I have finished this week's learning self-check and emailed the results to myself.” — your mentor ticks the tracking sheet from it.
  • 隐私照旧：全课程使用化名与学生编号，包括邮件与附件；不写真实全名、不放照片、不写学校全名。Privacy as always: pseudonym and student ID throughout the course, email and attachment included; no real full name, no photos, no full school name.
  • 🚫 独立：全程不使用 AI，包括写正文和自我介绍。写得朴素完全没关系——它必须是你的。🚫 Independent: no AI anywhere, body and self-intro included. Plain is perfectly fine — it just has to be yours.

附件写什么What goes in the attachment

一页“自我介绍卡”，四小节即可：① 化名与学生编号；② 我为什么学这门课（两三句——第 1 周你写过“我为什么学这门课”，整理那段就行）；③ 我每周固定的两个学习时段（第 1 周第 4 步定过）；④ 我最想在这学期学会的一件事。用任何文档软件写，导出 PDF，按规则改名。A one-page “self-intro card”, four short sections: ① pseudonym and student ID; ② why I am taking this course (two or three sentences — tidy up what you wrote in Week 1); ③ my two fixed weekly study slots (set in Week 1, Step 4); ④ the one thing I most want to learn this semester. Write it in any document app, export to PDF, rename by the rule.

步骤Steps

  1. 准备附件（10 分钟）：写好一页自我介绍卡，导出 PDF，先改好名——改完把这个名字摆进导师的下载文件夹里比一比：夹在“新建文档1”“作业.pdf”中间，它拿到的是 ✓ 还是问号？——再回邮箱。这一步建议在电脑上完成；只有手机也行：用 WPS 或系统自带的文档应用写好 → “分享 / 导出为 PDF” → 在“文件”（iPhone）或“文件管理”（安卓）里长按文件重命名，再回邮箱应用添加附件。Prepare the attachment (10 min): write the one-page card, export to PDF, rename it first — then set that name down inside your mentor's downloads folder and compare: wedged between “Untitled(3)” and “homework.pdf”, does it draw the ✓ or a question mark? — and return to the mailbox. A computer makes this easiest; phone-only works too: write in WPS or the built-in documents app → “Share / Export as PDF” → long-press the file to rename it in Files (iPhone) or the file manager (Android), then attach it from your mail app.
  2. 完成学习状态自测（12 分钟），把基线发给自己（6 分钟）：步骤在自测页，主题一字不差：“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”。这是你本周给自己发的第二封邮件——比彩排那封更正式一级。Do the learning self-check (12 min) and email the baseline to yourself (6 min): steps on the self-check page; subject word for word, “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”. Your second email to yourself this week — one notch more formal than the rehearsal.
  3. 确认收件人：跟做热身时已把导师存进通讯录；写信时从通讯录选，不手敲地址。公开学习者：收件人填自己（或约定的亲友）。Confirm the recipient: the warm-up saved your mentor in contacts; pick from contacts rather than typing. Open learners: yourself (or the friend who agreed) in To.
  4. 照模板写（15 分钟）：主题 → 称呼 → 三段 → 附件说明 → 结尾 → 署名；然后添加附件。模板里“我已完成本周的学习状态自测”那句，此时写下即为事实。Write from the template (15 min): subject → greeting → three paragraphs → attachment line → closing → signature; then attach the file. By now the template line “I have finished this week's learning self-check” is simply a fact as you type it.
  5. 对照检查（5 分钟）：用第 2 周页面的自评清单第 4–8 条逐项过一遍。Check (5 min): run items 4–8 of the Week 2 self-review list.
  6. 发送（2 分钟），然后正常生活——导师会在 24–48 小时内回复问候，评分反馈一周内送达。收到回信后在原邮件上点“回复”，说一句“收到，谢谢老师”。最后把三行日志写进草稿。Send (2 min), then get on with your week — a greeting comes back within 24–48 hours, and scoring feedback within a week. When the reply lands, press Reply and send a one-line “received, thank you!”. Then write the three-line log in your draft.

评分 · 量规第 ① ② 行（3 + 2 = 5 分）Scoring · Rubric rows ① ② (3 + 2 = 5 pts)

第 ① 行看信本身：主题、称呼、三段、署名。第 ② 行看附件与按钮：附件齐、命名对、正文提到；回信时用的是“回复”（不回信不扣分）。评的是投入与格式规范——文笔朴素不扣分，格式缺项才扣。完整量规Row ① scores the letter itself: subject, greeting, three paragraphs, signature. Row ② scores the parcel and the buttons: attachment present, named right, mentioned; the reply made with Reply (no reply costs nothing). Engagement and correctness of form — plain prose loses nothing; missing parts do. Full rubric → 量规页rubric.html

常见问题FAQ

“我为什么学这门课”那段写得很随便，能直接用吗？My Week-1 “why am I here” paragraph is rough — can I still use it?

能。整理一下句子就行——它是你上周的真实想法，比现编一段体面话更好。本任务评格式，不评文笔。

Yes. Tidy the sentences and it serves — last week's honest thought beats a freshly invented polished one. This task scores form, not prose.

我不知道该问导师什么问题。I can't think of a question for my mentor.

写“期待”也行：“希望这学期能____。”实在想问：问一件你真的好奇的事——“导师每周什么时候看邮件？”“第 3 周建网站难吗？”都是好问题。

A hope works too: “this semester I hope to ____.” Or ask something you genuinely wonder — “when in the week do you read our emails?” and “is Week 3's site-building hard?” are both fine questions.

发出去之后发现忘了附件 / 主题打错了。I sent it, then saw the attachment missing / a typo in the subject.

马上补发一封：原主题后加“ · 补充/更正”，第一句说明情况。自己发现并当天补上，不扣分——得体的补救正是本周教的技能之一。

Send the follow-up at once: add “ · correction” to the subject and explain in the first line. Caught by you and fixed the same day, it costs nothing — graceful recovery is one of this week's skills.

可以用聊天软件把自我介绍发给导师吗？Can I just send the self-intro over a chat app instead?

这周不行——本周练的正是邮件这条“正式通道”。以后每周和导师的往来也都走邮件（课程说明的约定）；私人问题同样发邮件。

Not this week — the formal channel is exactly what is being practised. All mentor contact in this course runs on email (it is in the course guide); private questions go by email too.

我是公开学习者，怎么“评分”？I am an open learner — how do I get scored?

发给自己或约定的亲友后，打开量规页对照自评；请亲友照量规念给你听更好。分数记在自己的草稿里，第 15 周做作品集时它就是一条现成的证据。量规第 ② 行的“回复”一项，以彩排里你给自己那封“回复”（或亲友的回信）计。

After sending to yourself or your friend, self-score against the rubric — better still, have the friend read it back to you. Note the score in your draft; in Week 15 it becomes ready-made portfolio evidence. For row ②'s Reply item, count the Reply you practised on your own test email (or a friend's reply).
