任务 · 规范自我介绍邮件Task · The formatted self-intro email

带一个附件One attachment发给导师（公开学习者：自己或亲友）To your mentor (open learners: self or a friend)约 32 分钟 + 自测 18 分钟（首次会更久）~32 min + 18 min self-check (longer the first time)

要做什么What to do

给你的导师发一封格式规范的自我介绍邮件：主题按课程格式，称呼得体，正文三小段（我是谁 · 我为什么学这门课 · 一个问题或期待），署名完整；附上一页自我介绍文档，按命名规则命名，并在正文里提到。发出后，完成学习状态自测并把结果发给自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”）。本周不用登记链接——导师收到你的邮件，就是提交。

Email your mentor a properly formatted self-introduction: subject in the course format, proper greeting, three short paragraphs (who I am · why I am taking this course · one question or hope), full sign-off; attach a one-page self-intro document named by the rule and mention it in the body. Then complete the learning self-check and email the results to yourself (subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”). Nothing to register this week — your mentor receiving the email IS the submission.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 主题：B1 自我介绍 · 化名（学生编号）——例如“B1 自我介绍 · 晨星（S1-07）”。公开学习者写化名即可。Subject: B1 self-intro · pseudonym (student ID) — e.g. “B1 self-intro · Chenxing (S1-07)”. Open learners: pseudonym alone is fine.
  • 六个部位齐全：主题 · 称呼 · 三段正文 · 附件说明 · 结尾 · 署名（化名 + 学生编号）。All six parts present: subject · greeting · three-paragraph body · attachment line · closing · signature (pseudonym + student ID).
  • 附件：一页自我介绍文档，建议 PDF，命名为“数字化学习-W2-自我介绍-化名.pdf”；正文里提到它。Attachment: a one-page self-intro document, PDF preferred, named “DL-W2-self-intro-pseudonym.pdf”; mentioned in the body.
  • 正文里带一句：“我已完成本周的学习状态自测，并把结果发给了自己。”——导师会据此在登记表上打 ✓。Include the line: “I have finished this week's learning self-check and emailed the results to myself.” — your mentor ticks the tracking sheet from it.
  • 隐私照旧：全课程使用化名与学生编号，包括邮件与附件；不写真实全名、不放照片、不写学校全名。Privacy as always: pseudonym and student ID throughout the course, email and attachment included; no real full name, no photos, no full school name.
  • 🚫 独立：全程不使用 AI，包括写正文和自我介绍。写得朴素完全没关系——它必须是你的。🚫 Independent: no AI anywhere, body and self-intro included. Plain is perfectly fine — it just has to be yours.

附件写什么What goes in the attachment

一页“自我介绍卡”，四小节即可：① 化名与学生编号；② 我为什么学这门课（两三句——第 1 周你写过“我为什么学这门课”，整理那段就行）；③ 我每周固定的两个学习时段（第 1 周第 4 步定过）；④ 我最想在这学期学会的一件事。用任何文档软件写，导出 PDF，按规则改名。A one-page “self-intro card”, four short sections: ① pseudonym and student ID; ② why I am taking this course (two or three sentences — tidy up what you wrote in Week 1); ③ my two fixed weekly study slots (set in Week 1, Step 4); ④ the one thing I most want to learn this semester. Write it in any document app, export to PDF, rename by the rule.

步骤Steps

  1. 准备附件（10 分钟）：写好一页自我介绍卡，导出 PDF，在电脑里先改好名，再回邮箱。Prepare the attachment (10 min): write the one-page card, export to PDF, rename it on your computer first, then return to the mailbox.
  2. 确认收件人：跟做热身时已把导师存进通讯录；写信时从通讯录选，不手敲地址。公开学习者：收件人填自己（或约定的亲友）。Confirm the recipient: the warm-up saved your mentor in contacts; pick from contacts rather than typing. Open learners: yourself (or the friend who agreed) in To.
  3. 照模板写（15 分钟）：主题 → 称呼 → 三段 → 附件说明 → 结尾 → 署名；然后添加附件。Write from the template (15 min): subject → greeting → three paragraphs → attachment line → closing → signature; then attach the file.
  4. 对照检查（5 分钟）：用第 2 周页面的自评清单第 3–6 条逐项过一遍。Check (5 min): run items 3–6 of the Week 2 self-review list.
  5. 发送（2 分钟），然后正常生活——导师会在 24–48 小时内回复问候，评分反馈一周内送达。收到回信后在原邮件上点“回复”，说一句“收到，谢谢老师”。Send (2 min), then get on with your week — a greeting comes back within 24–48 hours, and scoring feedback within a week. When the reply lands, press Reply and send a one-line “received, thank you!”.
  6. 完成学习状态自测（12 分钟）并把结果发给自己（6 分钟）：主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”——步骤在自测页。最后把三行日志写进草稿。Do the learning self-check (12 min) and email the results to yourself (6 min): subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline” — steps on the self-check page. Then write the three-line log in your draft.

评分 · 量规第 ① ② 行（3 + 2 = 5 分）Scoring · Rubric rows ① ② (3 + 2 = 5 pts)

第 ① 行看信本身：主题、称呼、三段、署名。第 ② 行看附件与按钮：附件齐、命名对、正文提到；回信时用的是“回复”。评的是投入与格式规范——文笔朴素不扣分，格式缺项才扣。完整量规Row ① scores the letter itself: subject, greeting, three paragraphs, signature. Row ② scores the parcel and the buttons: attachment present, named right, mentioned; the reply made with Reply. Engagement and correctness of form — plain prose loses nothing; missing parts do. 常见问题FAQ

“我为什么学这门课”那段写得很随便，能直接用吗？My Week-1 “why am I here” paragraph is rough — can I still use it?

能。整理一下句子就行——它是你上周的真实想法，比现编一段体面话更好。本任务评格式，不评文笔。

Yes. Tidy the sentences and it serves — last week's honest thought beats a freshly invented polished one. This task scores form, not prose.

我不知道该问导师什么问题。I can't think of a question for my mentor.

写“期待”也行：“希望这学期能____。”实在想问：问一件你真的好奇的事——“导师每周什么时候看邮件？”“第 3 周建网站难吗？”都是好问题。

A hope works too: “this semester I hope to ____.” Or ask something you genuinely wonder — “when in the week do you read our emails?” and “is Week 3's site-building hard?” are both fine questions.

发出去之后发现忘了附件 / 主题打错了。I sent it, then saw the attachment missing / a typo in the subject.

马上补发一封：原主题后加“ · 补充/更正”，第一句说明情况。自己发现并当天补上，不扣分——得体的补救正是本周教的技能之一。

Send the follow-up at once: add “ · correction” to the subject and explain in the first line. Caught by you and fixed the same day, it costs nothing — graceful recovery is one of this week's skills.

可以用聊天软件把自我介绍发给导师吗？Can I just send the self-intro over a chat app instead?

这周不行——本周练的正是邮件这条“正式通道”。以后每周和导师的往来也都走邮件（课程说明的约定）；私人问题同样发邮件。

Not this week — the formal channel is exactly what is being practised. All mentor contact in this course runs on email (it is in the course guide); private questions go by email too.

我是公开学习者，怎么“评分”？I am an open learner — how do I get scored?

发给自己或约定的亲友后，打开量规页对照自评；请亲友照量规念给你听更好。分数记在自己的草稿里，第 15 周做作品集时它就是一条现成的证据。

After sending to yourself or your friend, self-score against the rubric — better still, have the friend read it back to you. Note the score in your draft; in Week 15 it becomes ready-made portfolio evidence.
