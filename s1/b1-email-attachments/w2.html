0 · 情境引入（想一想，1 分钟）0 · The hook (think for a minute)

想象你的导师周日晚上打开收件箱：三十多封新邮件。有的主题是空的，有的写着“你好”，有的附件叫“新建文档1.docx”。其中有一封，主题写着“B1 自我介绍 · 晨星（S1-07）”，附件名一看就知道是谁的、是什么。——你希望自己的邮件是哪一封？这周只教一件事：让别人打开你的邮件之前，就已经对你有了好印象。——包括那个附件：它不是你另写的一份文件，是你自己从屏幕上截下来、标好、遮好的一张图。Picture your mentor opening the inbox on Sunday night: thirty-plus new messages. Some subjects are blank, one just says “hello”, one attachment is called “New Document 1.docx”. And one message reads “B1 self-intro · Chenxing (S1-07)”, its attachment named so you know at a glance whose it is and what it is. — Which one do you want yours to be? This week teaches exactly one thing: making a good impression before your email is even opened. — the attachment included: not a document you write separately, but one frame you capture off your own screen, mark up and mask.

1 · 本周目标1 · This week's objectives

  • ① 发出一封格式规范的自我介绍邮件：主题一句话说清“谁 · 什么事”，称呼得体，正文分三小段（我是谁 · 我为什么学这门课 · 一个问题或期待），署名完整。① Send one properly formatted self-introduction email: a subject line that says who and what in one line, a proper greeting, a body in three short paragraphs (who I am · why I am taking this course · one question or hope), a full sign-off.
  • ② 自己截一张图当附件：在电脑或手机上截图，加标注、把私人信息打码，按课程命名规则命名并在正文中提到；收到导师回信后用“回复”按钮接一句（不回信不扣分）。② Make your own screenshot the attachment: capture it on a computer or phone, annotate it and mask anything private, name it by the course file-naming rule and mention it in the body; when the mentor’s reply lands, answer with the Reply button (not replying costs nothing).
  • ③ 完成“学习状态自测”（不计分），并把结果邮件发给自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”）——既是本周技能的一次真实练习，也为第 16 周的对照保存基线。③ Complete the learning self-check (ungraded) and email the results to yourself (subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”) — real practice of this week's skill, and insurance for the Week-16 comparison.

本周你还会学到：怎样提一个带截图的问题（这门课全年的“求助姿势”）、收件人与抄送的分工、“回复”与“全部回复”各在什么时候用、24–48 小时的回复预期。这些不进量规——自测题与分箱卡帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: how to ask a question with a screenshot (this course’s all-year way of asking for help), what To and Cc each do, when Reply and Reply-all are each the right button, and the 24–48-hour reply expectation. None of it is graded — the self-quiz and the sorting cards check it for you, ungraded.

3 · 材料3 · Materials

五段微课，每段都有文字版和一段短视频，按顺序读完——前两段讲信怎么写、附件怎么装，中间两段讲那个附件怎么做出来，最后一段讲寄给谁、寄走以后：Five micro-lessons, each with a text version and a short clip; read them in order — the first two on writing the letter and packing the attachment, the middle two on making that attachment, the last on who receives it and what follows:

  1. 微课 1 · 一封邮件的六个部位（6 分钟）Micro-lesson 1 · The six parts of an email (6 min)——主题、称呼、正文、结尾、署名、附件说明，各自长什么样。 — subject, greeting, body, closing, signature, attachment line: what each looks like.
  2. 微课 2 · 附件与文件名（5 分钟）Micro-lesson 2 · Attachments and file names (5 min)——怎么添加附件、课程的文件命名规则、为什么“新建文档1”是灾难。 — attaching a file, the course file-naming rule, and why “New Document 1” is a disaster.
  3. 微课 3 · 截图：电脑与手机（7 分钟）Micro-lesson 3 · Screenshots on computer and phone (7 min)——每台设备上的两三种截法、截好的图存在哪里、什么时候用哪种。本周要寄出的附件，就从这里来。 — the two or three ways on each device, where the images land, and which way fits which moment. This is where this week’s attachment comes from.
  4. 微课 4 · 标注、打码，与带截图提问（6 分钟）Micro-lesson 4 · Annotate, mask, and ask with a screenshot (6 min)——画框、箭头、打码；以及这门课全年的“求助姿势”：一张标注好的截图 + 三句话。 — boxes, arrows, masking; and this course’s all-year way of asking for help: one annotated screenshot + three sentences.
  5. 微课 5 · 收件人、抄送与发出去之后（8 分钟）Micro-lesson 5 · To, Cc and after you hit Send (8 min)——三个框各是给谁的，两个“回复”按钮各在什么时候按；24–48 小时的回复预期、导师期待什么、着急时怎么办。 — who each box is for and when each Reply button is right; the 24–48-hour expectation, what mentors look for, and what to do when you are anxious.

    4 · 跟做（27 分钟）4 · Follow-along (27 min)

    四幕：先在两台设备上各截一张练手，再截出你要寄给导师的那一张并给它加标注、打码；然后彩排寄信的动作——把导师存进通讯录，给自己发一封带着这张截图的测试邮件，最后用九张卡片练一双“火眼金睛”。为什么这个顺序？截图截坏了删掉重来，正式邮件发出去却收不回：可逆的先动手，不可逆的先彩排。四幕都不计分——这里怎么错都没关系。Four acts: first a practice capture on each device, then the one you will actually send to your mentor — annotated and masked; then a rehearsal of the sending itself — save your mentor as a contact and send yourself a test email carrying that screenshot, and finally nine cards to sharpen your eye. Why that order? A bad screenshot is deleted and retaken; a sent email cannot be unsent — reversible things get tried, irreversible ones get rehearsed. All four acts are ungraded: this is where mistakes are free.

    10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

    我还没有电子邮箱。I don't have an email address yet.

    先回 M0 第 4 步：那页讲了怎么申请（14 岁以下请家长陪同注册）。申请好再回来，本周的内容都还在。

    Go back to M0 Step 4: it shows how to sign up (with a parent alongside if you are under 14). Everything here waits for you.

    导师的邮箱在哪里？Where is my mentor's address?

    在开课信里（导师姓名与邮箱不写在公开页面上）。开课信找不到了？邮件教师索取一份——教师邮箱也在开课信里；实在都找不到，请家长联系学校。

    In the welcome letter (mentor names and addresses never appear on public pages). Lost the letter? Email the instructor for another copy — that address is in the letter too; failing everything, ask a parent to contact the school.

    我是公开学习者，发给谁？I am an open learner — who do I send to?

    发给你自己（收件人填自己的地址），或发给一位事先说好的亲友。格式要求一模一样；发给自己还有个好处——你能亲眼看到这封邮件“收到时的样子”。之后用量规自评。

    To yourself (your own address in the To box), or to a friend or family member who agreed beforehand. The format requirements are identical — and sending to yourself lets you see exactly how the email arrives. Then self-score with the rubric.

    我不知道该截哪张图当附件。I don’t know which screenshot to attach.

    最省心的一张：你为这门课定下的每周两个固定学习时段（日历、备忘录、便签、课程表都行——第 1 周第 4 步定过）。人人都有、人人不同，而且正好给正文里“我为什么学这门课”一个看得见的证据。其他方向、以及具体怎么标注打码，见任务页。

    The easiest one: the two weekly study slots you set for this course (a calendar, a notes app, a sticky note, a timetable — you fixed them in Week 1, Step 4). Everyone has one, everyone’s differs, and it gives the body’s “why I am taking this course” something you can actually see. Other directions, and how to annotate and mask, are on the task page.

    能不能录一段屏幕当附件？Could I attach a screen recording instead?

    本周不行——不是因为它不好，是因为它太大：半分钟的录屏动辄几十兆，很多邮箱直接退回。邮件附件的规矩是“越轻越好”，一张截图几百 KB，秒收秒开。录屏第 3 周（B2）学：那时你刚建好自己的网站，终于有了一段值得录下来的东西。

    Not this week — not because it is bad, but because it is heavy: half a minute of screen recording easily runs to tens of megabytes, and many mail services simply bounce it. The rule for attachments is “the lighter the better”, and a screenshot at a few hundred kilobytes lands and opens instantly. Recording is Week 3’s lesson (B2): by then you will have just built your own website — finally something worth recording.

    我只有手机，没有电脑。I only have a phone, no computer.

    完全够用，而且手机在本周反而更顺手：截图、标注、打码、重命名、加附件，全在手机上一气呵成。自评清单里“电脑截一张”那一条，等下次碰到电脑（学校机房、家人电脑）再补，先空着不影响任何分数。

    Entirely enough — and a phone is if anything smoother this week: capture, annotate, mask, rename and attach all happen on the one device. As for the self-review item “one on the computer”, fill it whenever a computer next crosses your path (a school lab, a family machine); an empty box costs no points.

    发出去才发现写错了（附件忘了 / 主题错了）。I sent it and then saw a mistake (forgot the attachment / wrong subject).

    这是最常见的邮件事故，处理方式本身就是一项技能：马上再发一封，主题在原主题后加“ · 补充/更正”，第一句说明情况（“抱歉，刚才漏了附件，补在这封里”）。自己发现并当天补上，不扣分。

    The most common email accident — and recovering well is itself a skill: send a follow-up right away, add “ · correction” to the original subject, and open with one line of explanation (“sorry — the attachment slipped; here it is”). Catching it yourself and fixing it the same day costs nothing.

    学习状态自测答得“不好”会影响成绩吗？What if my self-check answers look “bad”?

    不会。自测没有对错、不计分、不影响任何评分；结果只在你自己的浏览器里显示，发给谁由你决定（发给自己是必做的一步，发给导师完全自愿）。它唯一的用处，是给第 16 周的你留一把对照的尺子——所以照实答，比“答得好看”重要一百倍。

    No. The self-check has no right answers, no points, no effect on any score; results display only in your own browser, and you choose who sees them (emailing yourself is the required step; emailing your mentor is entirely voluntary). Its one purpose is handing Week-16 you a ruler for comparison — so honest answers beat good-looking ones a hundred to one.

    为什么要把自测结果发给自己？Why email the results to myself?

    两个理由。一，它是本周技能的真实练习——主题、正文、发送，一样不少，只是收件人是你。二，它是给三个多月后的自己存的一份“时间胶囊”：第 15–16 周做学期反思时，你要把那时的自测和这份基线并排对照；邮箱替你保管它，比草稿纸可靠得多。第 3 周网站建好后，再把它抄进第一条日志作双保险更好。

    Two reasons. One: it is real practice of this week's skill — subject, body, send, all of it, just with you as the recipient. Two: it is a time capsule for the you of three months from now — in Weeks 15–16 the semester reflection sets that round beside this baseline, and your inbox keeps it far more safely than scratch paper. Once your site is up in Week 3, copying it into your first log entry adds a second safety net.

    仍不清楚？先看上面的常见问题，再邮件导师或教师——记得带上一张标注好的截图（微课 4 的求助姿势，从本周起就能用了；24–48 小时内回复，导师邮箱见开课信）。Still unclear?
