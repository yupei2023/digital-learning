第一封正式邮件Your first formal email

任务 · 5 分Task · 5 pts🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min

请从上到下按顺序做。这是这门课第一个计分周——如果心里有点紧张，很正常；页面会把每一步讲清楚，做完你会发现它比想象中简单。还没有电子邮箱？先看第 10 节第一条。Work top to bottom. See the first question in §10 before you start.

0 · 情境引入（想一想，1 分钟）0 · The hook (think for a minute)

想象你的导师周日晚上打开收件箱：三十多封新邮件。有的主题是空的，有的写着“你好”，有的附件叫“新建文档1.docx”。其中有一封，主题写着“B1 自我介绍 · 晨星（S1-07）”，附件名一看就知道是谁的、是什么。——你希望自己的邮件是哪一封？这周只教一件事：让别人打开你的邮件之前，就已经对你有了好印象。Picture your mentor opening the inbox on Sunday night: thirty-plus new messages. Some subjects are blank, one just says “hello”, one attachment is called “New Document 1.docx”. And one message reads “B1 self-intro · Chenxing (S1-07)”, its attachment named so you know at a glance whose it is and what it is. — Which one do you want yours to be? This week teaches exactly one thing: making a good impression before your email is even opened.

1 · 本周目标1 · This week's objectives

  • ① 发出一封格式规范的自我介绍邮件：主题一句话说清“谁 · 什么事”，称呼得体，正文分三小段（我是谁 · 我为什么学这门课 · 一个问题或期待），署名完整。① Send one properly formatted self-introduction email: a subject line that says who and what in one line, a proper greeting, a body in three short paragraphs (who I am · why I am taking this course · one question or hope), a full sign-off.
  • ② 正确使用附件与“回复”：附件按课程命名规则命名、在正文中提到；收到导师回信后用“回复”按钮接一句（不回信不扣分）。② Use attachments and Reply correctly: name the attachment by the course file-naming rule and mention it in the body; when the mentor's reply lands, answer with the Reply button (not replying costs nothing).
  • ③ 完成“学习状态自测”（不计分），并把结果邮件发给自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”）——既是本周技能的一次真实练习，也为第 16 周的对照保存基线。③ Complete the learning self-check (ungraded) and email the results to yourself (subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline”) — real practice of this week's skill, and insurance for the Week-16 comparison.

本周你还会学到：收件人与抄送的分工、“回复”与“全部回复”各在什么时候用、24–48 小时的回复预期。这些不进量规——自测题与分箱卡帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: what To and Cc each do, when Reply and Reply-all are each the right button, and the 24–48-hour reply expectation. None of it is graded — the self-quiz and the sorting cards check it for you, ungraded.

4 · 跟做（25 分钟）4 · Follow-along (25 min)

正式邮件发出去就收不回，所以先彩排：把导师存进通讯录，给自己发一封带附件的测试邮件，再用八张卡片练一双“火眼金睛”。彩排不计分——这里怎么错都没关系。A formal email cannot be unsent, so rehearse first: save your mentor as a contact, send yourself a test email with an attachment, then sharpen your eye on eight sorting cards. The rehearsal is ungraded — this is the place where mistakes are free.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我还没有电子邮箱。I don't have an email address yet.

先回 M0 第 4 步：那页讲了怎么申请（14 岁以下请家长陪同注册）。申请好再回来，本周的内容都还在。

Go back to M0 Step 4: it shows how to sign up (with a parent alongside if you are under 14). Everything here waits for you.

导师的邮箱在哪里？Where is my mentor's address?

在开课信里（导师姓名与邮箱不写在公开页面上）。开课信找不到了？邮件教师索取一份——教师邮箱也在开课信里；实在都找不到，请家长联系学校。

In the welcome letter (mentor names and addresses never appear on public pages). Lost the letter? Email the instructor for another copy — that address is in the letter too; failing everything, ask a parent to contact the school.

我是公开学习者，发给谁？I am an open learner — who do I send to?

发给你自己（收件人填自己的地址），或发给一位事先说好的亲友。格式要求一模一样；发给自己还有个好处——你能亲眼看到这封邮件“收到时的样子”。之后用量规自评。

To yourself (your own address in the To box), or to a friend or family member who agreed beforehand. The format requirements are identical — and sending to yourself lets you see exactly how the email arrives. Then self-score with the rubric.

发出去才发现写错了（附件忘了 / 主题错了）。I sent it and then saw a mistake (forgot the attachment / wrong subject).

这是最常见的邮件事故，处理方式本身就是一项技能：马上再发一封，主题在原主题后加“ · 补充/更正”，第一句说明情况（“抱歉，刚才漏了附件，补在这封里”）。自己发现并当天补上，不扣分。

The most common email accident — and recovering well is itself a skill: send a follow-up right away, add “ · correction” to the original subject, and open with one line of explanation (“sorry — the attachment slipped; here it is”). Catching it yourself and fixing it the same day costs nothing.

学习状态自测答得“不好”会影响成绩吗？What if my self-check answers look “bad”?

不会。自测没有对错、不计分、不影响任何评分；结果只在你自己的浏览器里显示，发给谁由你决定（发给自己是必做的一步，发给导师完全自愿）。它唯一的用处，是给第 16 周的你留一把对照的尺子——所以照实答，比“答得好看”重要一百倍。

No. The self-check has no right answers, no points, no effect on any score; results display only in your own browser, and you choose who sees them (emailing yourself is the required step; emailing your mentor is entirely voluntary). Its one purpose is handing Week-16 you a ruler for comparison — so honest answers beat good-looking ones a hundred to one.

为什么要把自测结果发给自己？Why email the results to myself?

两个理由。一，它是本周技能的真实练习——主题、正文、发送，一样不少，只是收件人是你。二，它是给三个多月后的自己存的一份“时间胶囊”：第 15–16 周做学期反思时，你要把那时的自测和这份基线并排对照；邮箱替你保管它，比草稿纸可靠得多。第 3 周网站建好后，再把它抄进第一条日志作双保险更好。

Two reasons. One: it is real practice of this week's skill — subject, body, send, all of it, just with you as the recipient. Two: it is a time capsule for the you of three months from now — in Weeks 15–16 the semester reflection sets that round beside this baseline, and your inbox keeps it far more safely than scratch paper. Once your site is up in Week 3, copying it into your first log entry adds a second safety net.
