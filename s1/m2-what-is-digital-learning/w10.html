0 · 起点（5 分钟，写在草稿里）0 · Starting point (5 min, in your draft)

先不看任何材料。打开一个草稿（网站日志页的草稿、手机备忘录都行），写三行：Before any materials, open a draft (a draft log entry, your phone's notes — anything) and write three lines:

  1. 我用手机或电脑学过什么？（列三件，越具体越好：一个 App、一段视频、一次搜索……）What have I learned with a phone or computer? (List three, as specific as you can: an app, a video, a search…)
  2. 其中哪一次，我真的学会了？那次我在做什么？Which of them did I really learn from? What was I doing that time?
  3. 哪一次，我其实只是在刷？那次又是怎么开始的？Which time was I really just scrolling? How did that one start?

这三行不发布、不计分。它们是你这周短文里“两个自身实例”的原料——读材料时，随时回来对照：读本说的，和我那两次一样吗？These three lines are not published or graded. They are the raw material for the “two examples from your own life” in this week's piece — while you read, come back and compare: does the reader describe what happened to me?

✅ 核心要点Key takeaways：数字化学习是用数字工具和网络进行的学习，“三无”——无远弗届、无时不刻、有教无类——是它的三项优势；大脑是硬件、心智是软件，环境越丰富越聪明，所以数字化学习能让人“重生”；但同一部手机也带来分心、能力错觉、不可靠来源和把工具当拐杖；每一项风险都有一条自己做得到的对策；一个好的立场，先替对方说话。: digital learning is learning through digital tools and networks; the “三无” — no distance, no fixed hour, no gatekeeper — are its three benefits; the brain is hardware, the mind software, and a richer environment makes you smarter, so digital learning can remake you; yet the same phone brings distraction, the illusion of competence, unreliable sources and tools-as-crutches; every risk has a mitigation you can actually do; a good position speaks for the other side first.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

“公开信”真的要寄给学校吗？Do I really have to send the letter to my school?

可以。公开信是文体，不是承诺——你不必真的寄出去。但请写得像要寄出去一样认真。想寄也可以：先给家长看一遍。

Yes. “Open letter” is a form, not a promise — you don't have to send it. Write it as if you would. If you do want to send it, show a parent first.

我觉得两边都有道理，可以不选边吗？I think both sides have a point — can I stay in the middle?

当然可以。对方的理由写完之后，你可以说“我同意一半”，然后说清楚哪一半、为什么。量规只看你有没有先把对方说到位，再把自己说清楚。

Of course. After the other side's case you can say “I agree with half of it” — then say which half and why. The rubric only checks that you did the other side justice first, then made yourself clear.

信里能写学校名字和老师名字吗？Can I name my school or teachers?

写“我的学校”即可，不写校名；也不写老师和同学的真实姓名。这是本课程的隐私规则。

Write “my school”, not its name; no real names of teachers or classmates. That is the course privacy rule.

我用 AI 帮我检查了对方理由写得够不够强，算作弊吗？I asked AI whether my other-side case was strong enough — is that cheating?

不算，本单元是 ✅ 辅助。立场、两个实例、对策必须是你的；文末附两三句 AI 使用说明。让 AI 替你写整封信，才是作弊。

No — this module is ✅ Assisted. The position, the two examples and the mitigations must be yours; add a two–three-sentence AI use note. Having AI write the whole letter is cheating.

① 在网站“作品集”页新建一条，标题“M2 任务 1 · 给我的学校的一封公开信”；② 把短文全文粘贴进去（不要只放文件链接），发布；③ 复制这一条的链接。在校学生把链接登记到导师的登记表 → [placeholder: 登记表网址]；导师邮箱见开课信。
