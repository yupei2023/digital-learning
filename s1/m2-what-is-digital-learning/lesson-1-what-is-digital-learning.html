从远程教育到数字化学习From distance education to digital learning

互联网改变了无数的事，其中就包括教育。我们把基于互联网的教育叫作“在线教育”。在此之前，人们也借助信函、电视、广播、电话，让教育跨越空间和时间——这类校园之外、跨越时空的教育统称“远程教育”。因为现在的远程教育主要靠电子设备，人们也叫它 E-learning。而当手机、平板、AI 工具都成了学习的一部分，“在线”已经不足以概括它——本课程用“数字化学习”这个更大的词：用数字工具和网络进行的一切学习，不管你在不在线，不管有没有老师。

The Internet has changed countless things, education among them. Education based on the Internet we call online education. Before it, people used letters, television, radio and telephone to carry education across space and time — all such off-campus, distance-crossing education is distance education. Because today's distance education runs on electronic devices, it is also called e-learning. And now that phones, tablets and AI tools are part of learning, “online” no longer covers it — this course uses the wider term digital learning: any learning done through digital tools and networks, whether or not you are online, whether or not there is a teacher.

1987 年 9 月 14 日，北京一间实验室的计算机发出了第一封来自中国的电子邮件，内容只有一句：“越过长城，走向世界。”1987 年我四岁，我受的教育和比我早出生十年的孩子没有太大差别；但比我晚出生十年的孩子，将完全拥抱一种被互联网改变的教育。今天的你，就是那个孩子。

On 14 September 1987 a computer in a Beijing laboratory sent China's first email. It said one thing: “Across the Great Wall we can reach every corner of the world.” In 1987 I was four; my education was not very different from that of children born ten years before me. But children born ten years after me would embrace an education wholly changed by the Internet. Today, you are that child.

“三无”The “三无”

读本第 07 章给数字化学习总结了三项优势，叫“三无”：无远弗届——不受空间的局限；无时不刻——摆脱时间的束缚；有教无类——跨越认知、种族、信仰和文化的隔阂。作者本人就是“三无”的受益者：一个全职工作的人，在火车上、酒店里、去机场的路上读完了一个学位。西昌泥路尽头的那个女孩，理论上也能。

Ch. 07 sums up digital learning's three benefits as the “三无”: no distance — space no longer limits you; no fixed hour — time no longer binds you; no gatekeeper — it crosses barriers of background, ethnicity, belief and culture. The author is a beneficiary himself: a full-time worker who finished a degree on trains, in hotels, on the way to airports. In theory, so could the girl at the end of the muddy road.

本课程是异步的：页面周一开放，周日前发布——你决定周几学。这既是自由，也是第 07 章作者每周三心跳加速的原因。

This course is asynchronous: pages open Monday, publish by Sunday — you decide which days. That is freedom, and it is also why Ch. 07's author felt his heart race every Wednesday.

带着这个问题去读第 07 章：Take this into Ch. 07:作者在火车上、酒店里、汽车副驾驶上学习——是什么让那些时刻成为“学习”，而不是“刷手机”？把答案记下来，它会出现在你的对策里。The author studies on trains, in hotels, in the passenger seat — what made those moments “learning” rather than “scrolling”? Write the answer down; it will reappear in your mitigations.
