M2 量规M2 Rubric

量规的三行就是本单元的三条目标，全部从同一篇立场短文评。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。

The three rows are the module's three objectives, all scored from the same position piece. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence.

立场不计分，理由才计分。The position is not scored; the reasoning is. 一封强的信可以支持任何一方——允许、禁止、有条件地允许都行。量规评的是推理：先替对方说话、用读本概念或亲身经历支持、优势与风险各配做得到的对策——从不评你站在哪一边。A strong letter can argue either side — allow, ban, or allow with conditions. The rubric scores the reasoning: the other side first, support from reader concepts or personal experience, benefits and risks with doable mitigations — never which side you took.

① 用自己的话界定“数字化学习”，并举出两个自身实例（一次帮了你的，一次没帮上或帮倒忙的）① Define digital learning in your own words, with two examples from your own life (one that helped, one that didn't)
任务 1 · 3 分Task 1 · 3 pts		3 · 自己的话给出定义（不是照抄读本或词典）；两个实例都是具体的一次（何时、用什么、学什么），一正一反；定义里有一条来自实例的边界（“只有当____时才算学习”）。Own-words definition (not copied from the reader or a dictionary); two specific occasions (when, which tool, learning what), one positive, one negative; the definition carries one boundary drawn from them (“it only counts as learning when ____”).2 · 定义接近照抄，或两个实例都是正面的，或实例泛泛（“我经常用手机查资料”）。Near-copied definition, or both examples positive, or vague examples (“I often look things up on my phone”).1 · 没有定义，或只有一个实例。No definition, or only one example.0 · 未发布 / 不是本人所写（含整篇 AI 代写）。Not published / not the learner's own (including a wholly AI-written piece).
② 就“学校该不该允许学生用手机 / AI 学习”表明立场，先写出对方最强的理由，再用读本概念或亲身经历支持自己的立场② Take a position on “should schools let students learn with phones / AI”, state the other side's strongest case first, then support your own with reader concepts or personal experience
任务 1 · 4 分Task 1 · 4 pts		4 · 对方的理由写在自己立场之前，而且是对方也会点头的强版本（不是“稻草人”——把对方说弱了再打）；理由写明来源（问过的人的一句原话 / 自己的经历 / 读本章节）；自己的立场清楚；支持理由至少一条来自读本概念（第 02、05、07 章均可，写明章号）或一次亲身经历。The other side comes first and is the strong version they would nod at (no “straw man” — the other side made weak and then knocked down); the reason has a source (a sentence from someone asked / own experience / a reader chapter); own position clear; at least one supporting reason drawn from a reader concept (Ch. 02, 05 or 07, chapter named) or a personal experience.3 · 对方的理由写了但很弱或很短（一句带过），或放在自己立场之后，或与范例雷同且无来源；或支持理由只是感受（“我觉得手机很有用”）。The other side is stated but weak or perfunctory, or placed after own position, or a close copy of the sample with no source; or the support is feeling only (“I think phones are useful”).2 · 没有写对方的理由，或立场不清楚。No other-side case, or position unclear.0 · 未发布 / 不是本人所写（含整篇 AI 代写）。Not published / not the learner's own (including a wholly AI-written piece).
③ 说明数字化学习的两项优势与两项风险，并为每项风险提出一条自己做得到的对策③ Explain two benefits and two risks of digital learning, with one mitigation you can actually do for each risk
任务 1 · 3 分Task 1 · 3 pts		3 · 两优势、两风险各说明为什么；每项风险配一条具体到动作的对策（“学习时把手机放到另一个房间”），而不是口号（“少玩手机”）；至少一项风险是自己经历过的（第 0 节或活动里写的条件），对策写明自己的时间 / 地点，不是微课 2 表格的原句。Two benefits and two risks, each explained; each risk has a mitigation stated as an action (“phone in another room while I study”), not a slogan (“use my phone less”); at least one risk was lived (from section 0 or the activity conditions), and its mitigation names the learner's own time / place rather than the table's sentence.2 · 四项齐了但只是列出没有说明，或对策是口号，或一项风险没有对策。All four listed but not explained, or mitigations are slogans, or one risk lacks a mitigation.1 · 优势或风险少于两项，或没有对策。Fewer than two benefits or risks, or no mitigations.0 · 同 ② 行As row ②

规范要求（不计分，但缺少则该任务记“证据不足”）Form requirements (a gate, not points — missing means “No evidence”)

  • 短文发布在自己的网站“作品集”页，链接可打开；在校学生已登记链接。Published on the learner's Portfolio page and reachable; enrolled students registered the link.
  • 本周三行日志已写。This week's three-line log is written.
  • 使用 AI 者附了 AI 使用说明；整篇明显由 AI 代写的按学术诚信规则处理。AI users added a use note; a wholly AI-written piece falls under the integrity policy.
  • 不写他人的真实姓名，不写学校全名（公开信里用“我的学校”）。No one else's real name; no full school name (write “my school” in the open letter).

合成How the 10 points add up

任务 1 = 第 ① 行（3）+ 第 ② 行（4）+ 第 ③ 行（3）= 10 分。导师在登记表上分别记三行的分数与等级，并在一周内邮件反馈。Task 1 = row ① (3) + row ② (4) + row ③ (3) = 10. Mentors record each row's score and level on the tracking sheet and email feedback within a week.
