做Do

  • 一周记录实验：这周每次用手机“学习”，在日志页记三样：开始时间、结束时间、实际做了什么。周日看一眼：有几次变成了刷？第 9 单元（第二学期）会再用到这份记录。A one-week logging experiment: every time you “study” on your phone this week, note three things on your log page — start time, end time, what you actually did. On Sunday, look: how many turned into scrolling? Module 9 (Semester 2) will use this record again.
  • 给家人讲“三无”：用三分钟向家里人解释“无远弗届、无时不刻、有教无类”，并问他们：你们那一代是怎么学的？把最有意思的一句写进日志。Explain the 三无 at home: take three minutes to explain “no distance, no fixed hour, no gatekeeper” to your family, then ask how their generation learned. Put the best line in your log. 不需要网络no link needed
