任务 1 · 给我的学校的一封公开信Task 1 · An open letter to my school

500–800 字 / 英文 350–600 词500–800 characters / 350–600 words中文或英文Chinese or English约 45 分钟写作 + 5 分钟发布~45 min to write + 5 to publish

要做什么What to do

写一封给你自己学校的公开信，回答：学校该不该允许学生用手机 / AI 学习？五段结构见微课 3。发布到你网站的“作品集”页，标题写“M2 任务 1 · 给我的学校的一封公开信”。公开信是文体，不必真的寄出；想寄先给家长看。

Write an open letter to your own school answering: should students be allowed to learn with phones / AI? Five-paragraph structure in micro-lesson 3. Publish on your Portfolio page as “M2 Task 1 · An open letter to my school”. “Open letter” is a form — you need not send it; if you want to, show a parent first.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 对方最强的理由在你的立场之前——先表态、后补对方理由的，第 ② 行最多只能得 3 分（发展中）。The other side's strongest case comes before your position — position first and the other side after means row ② can score at most 3 (Developing).
  • 第 2 段写明理由从哪来：我问了____（家人 / 老师 / 同学，一句原话）/ 我的一次经历 / 读本第 __ 章。没有来源、又和范例雷同的，按“发展中”计。理由若引的是网页等站外来源，鼓励附上第 6 周练过的三行来源卡（标题 · 作者或机构 · 网址 + 访问日期）；问话与亲历照旧不需要卡。Paragraph 2 says where the reason comes from: I asked ____ (a parent / teacher / classmate — one sentence they said) / one experience of mine / Reader Ch. __. No source plus a close copy of the sample rates Developing. If a reason cites a web page or another outside source, you are encouraged to attach the three-line source card practised in Week 6 (title · author or institution · URL + access date); spoken answers and personal experience need no card, as before.
  • 手机和 AI 二选一，只写一个也可以拿满分。Phones or AI — pick one; writing about only one can earn full marks.
  • 定义用自己的话；两个亲身实例，一正一反，具体到一次。Definition in your own words; two personal examples, one positive, one negative, each a specific occasion.
  • 至少一条支持理由来自读本概念（写明章号）或一次亲身经历。At least one supporting reason from a reader concept (chapter named) or a personal experience.
  • 两优势、两风险、两条动作式对策。“少玩手机”不是对策，“学习时手机放另一个房间”才是。至少一项风险是你自己经历过的（第 0 节或活动里写的条件），对策写你自己的时间、地点，不抄微课 2 表格的原句。Two benefits, two risks, two action mitigations. “Use my phone less” is not a mitigation; “phone in another room while I study” is. At least one risk must be one you lived (from section 0 or the conditions you wrote in the activity), and the mitigation names your own time and place — not the table's sentence.
  • 写“我的学校”，不写校名；不写老师和同学的真实姓名。结尾附 AI 使用说明（或“本文未使用 AI”）。Write “my school”, not its name; no real names of teachers or classmates. End with an AI use note (or “No AI was used”).
立场不计分，理由才计分。The position is not scored; the reasoning is. 一封强的信可以支持任何一方——允许、禁止、有条件地允许都行。量规评的是推理：先替对方说话、用读本概念或亲身经历支持、优势与风险各配做得到的对策——从不评你站在哪一边。A strong letter can argue either side — allow, ban, or allow with conditions. The rubric scores the reasoning: the other side first, support from reader concepts or personal experience, benefits and risks with doable mitigations — never which side you took.
  1. 我的定义 + 两次经历 + 一条边界
    量规 ① · 3 分    Definition + two moments + one boundary
    row ① · 3 pts
  2. 对方最强的理由 + 来源
    量规 ②    The other side's strongest case + its source
    row ②

    量规 ② · 4 分    My position + reasons (reader / experience)
    row ② · 4 pts

    量规 ③ · 3 分    Two benefits · two risks · two action mitigations
    row ③ · 3 pts
  5. 一句话立场 + 预想回音（不计分）+ AI 使用说明One-sentence position + anticipated reply (ungraded) + AI use note

步骤Steps

  1. 把第 0 节的三行和分类活动里你写的“条件”放在旁边——它们就是第 1 段和第 4 段的原料。Put your three lines from section 0 and the conditions you wrote in the activity beside you — they are the raw material for paragraphs 1 and 4.
  2. 先写第 2 段（对方的理由）。写完问自己：一个真的反对我的人读到这里，会点头吗？不会就重写。Write paragraph 2 (the other side) first. Then ask: would someone who really disagrees with me nod here? If not, rewrite.
  3. 再写第 3 段，然后第 1、4、5 段。全文 500–800 字（英文 350–600 词）。Then paragraph 3, then 1, 4 and 5. 500–800 characters (350–600 words) in all.
  4. 对照上面的硬性要求逐条检查；再对照第 10 周页面的自评清单勾选。Check each hard requirement above; then tick the self-review list on the Week 10 page.
  5. 发布：① 打开你的网站，在“作品集”页新建一条，标题“M2 任务 1 · 给我的学校的一封公开信”；② 把全文粘贴进去（不要只放文件链接），保存并发布；③ 打开发布后的页面，确认能看到全文，复制这一条的链接。Publish: ① open your site, add a new entry on the Portfolio page titled “M2 Task 1 · An open letter to my school”; ② paste the full text (not just a file link), save and publish; ③ open the published page, confirm the text shows, copy the entry's link.
  6. 在校学生把链接登记到导师的登记表（网址开课时公布，见第 10 周页面末尾的说明），填三样：学生编号、任务编号（M2-T1）、作品链接。导师邮箱见开课信。Enrolled students register the link on the mentor's sheet (URL published at launch — see the note at the foot of the Week 10 page), entering three things: student ID, task code (M2-T1), link. The mentor's email is in the welcome letter.
