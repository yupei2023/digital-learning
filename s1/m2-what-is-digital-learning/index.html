什么是数字化学习？What is Digital Learning?

10 分10 pts✅ 辅助✅ Assisted≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min一周One week

2007 年夏天，我带几个学生去四川西昌看火箭发射。火箭腾空、群山震动的那一刻，我们都说不出话。同一天下午，我们沿着一条泥泞的土路走进附近的一个彝族村寨。开门的是一个和我的学生差不多大的女孩，背上的竹篓里坐着她的弟弟；屋里的地面和门外的路一样泥泞。她没有在上学——她要照顾弟弟和生病的奶奶。那条泥路挡住的，不只是汽车，还有北京、纽约、伦敦的孩子唾手可得的学习资源。

火箭和泥路，相距不到十公里。那天以后我一直在想：有没有一条路，能让泥路尽头的孩子也够得着火箭旁边的课堂？这一周，我们把这个问题变成你自己的问题——数字化学习到底是什么？它对你，是火箭，还是另一条泥路？

In the summer of 2007 I took a few students to Xichang, Sichuan, to watch a rocket launch. When it rose and the mountains shook, none of us could speak. That same afternoon we walked down a muddy track into a nearby Yi village. The door was opened by a girl about my students' age, her little brother sitting in the basket on her back; the floor inside was as muddy as the road outside. She was not in school — she looked after her brother and her sick grandmother. That muddy road blocked more than cars: it blocked the learning resources that children in Beijing, New York and London take for granted.

The rocket and the mud road were less than ten kilometres apart. I have wondered ever since: is there a road that lets the child at the end of the mud track reach the classroom beside the rocket? This week we make that question yours — what is digital learning, really? For you, is it the rocket, or just another muddy road?

核心问题Essential questions

  • 什么是数字化学习？What is digital learning?
  • 为什么我应该学会数字化学习？Why should I learn to learn this way?
  • 它的利与弊？What are its benefits and risks?

学完本单元，你应能By the end of this module you should be able to

  1. 用自己的话界定“数字化学习”，并举出两个自身实例（一次帮了你的，一次没帮上或帮倒忙的）。Define digital learning in your own words, with two examples from your own life (one that helped, one that didn't).
  2. 就“学校该不该允许学生用手机 / AI 学习”表明立场，先写出对方最强的理由，再用读本概念或亲身经历支持自己的立场。Take a position on “should schools let students learn with phones / AI”, state the other side's strongest case first, then support your own with reader concepts or personal experience.
  3. 说明数字化学习的两项优势与两项风险，并为每项风险提出一条自己做得到的对策。Explain two benefits and two risks of digital learning, with one mitigation you can actually do for each risk.
