怎样写一封先替对方说话的公开信How to write an open letter that speaks for the other side first

吴恩达：MOOC（慕课）会取代学校吗？ · 约 4.5 分钟 · B 站 UP 主 PuNaDSmile（果壳网原创，2014）Andrew Ng: will MOOCs replace schools? · ~4.5 min · Bilibili uploader PuNaDSmile (Guokr original, 2014) · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗
MOOC（慕课）= 大规模开放在线课程，Coursera、中国大学 MOOC 都是。 一个在线教育的创始人，被问“在线会取代学校吗”——他先说了学校能做、在线做不到的事，再给自己的答案。数一数：他替“对方”说了几句？MOOC = massive open online course, such as Coursera or 中国大学 MOOC. A founder of online education asked whether online will replace schools — he first names what schools do that online cannot, then gives his answer. Count how many sentences he gives the “other side”.

规则：先替对方说话The rule: the other side speaks first

大多数人写“该不该”的文章，是先说自己的看法，再顺手贬一下对方。这一次反过来：先替对方说话，说到对方也会点头，再轮到你。这不是礼貌，是硬功夫——只有真的理解了对方最强的理由，你的立场才站得住。检验标准只有一个：如果你把“对方的理由”那一段拿给一个真的持相反意见的人看，他会说“对，我就是这么想的”，还是会说“你把我说弱了”？把对方说弱了再打，就叫“稻草人”。

Most people write a “should we?” piece by stating their own view and then taking a swipe at the other side. This time, reverse it: speak for the other side first, well enough that they would nod, and only then take your turn. This is not politeness; it is hard work — your position only stands if you have grasped the other side's strongest reason. There is one test: if you showed your “other side” paragraph to someone who really holds that view, would they say “yes, that's exactly it” — or “you made me sound weak”? Making the other side weak and then knocking it down is called a “straw man”.

立场不计分，理由才计分。The position is not scored; the reasoning is. 一封强的信可以支持任何一方——允许、禁止、有条件地允许都行。量规评的是推理：先替对方说话、用读本概念或亲身经历支持、优势与风险各配做得到的对策——从不评你站在哪一边。A strong letter can argue either side — allow, ban, or allow with conditions. The rubric scores the reasoning: the other side first, support from reader concepts or personal experience, benefits and risks with doable mitigations — never which side you took.
手机和 AI 二选一，只写一个也可以拿满分。Phones or AI — pick one; writing about only one can earn full marks. 反对手机的老师和反对 AI 的老师说的不是一回事。选一个对象写，对方的理由才写得准。Teachers who object to phones and teachers who object to AI are not saying the same thing. Pick one, and the other side's case can be written precisely.
弱版本（“稻草人”：把对方说得很弱，像扎个稻草人来打）Weak version (a “straw man”: the other side made so weak it is easy to knock down)强版本（对方会点头）Strong version (they would nod)
“有人觉得手机会让人分心。”“Some people think phones are distracting.”“反对的老师有一个我无法否认的理由：教室里三十部手机，老师看不到三十块屏幕，而我自己上学期就有过查一个词、看了二十分钟视频的经历。”“The teachers who object have a reason I cannot deny: in a room of thirty phones a teacher cannot see thirty screens — and last term I myself looked up one word and watched videos for twenty minutes.”
“有人觉得禁手机太落后了。”“Some people think banning phones is backward.”“支持允许的同学有一个真实的理由：第 07 章说的‘无远弗届’——同一道题，学校老师讲不清的，网上有十个老师讲；禁掉手机，等于把这十个老师关在门外。”“Students who support allowing phones have a real reason: Ch. 07's ‘no distance’ — for the same problem the school teacher can't make clear, there are ten teachers online; ban the phone and you lock those ten teachers out.”

五段模板（500–800 字 / 英文 350–600 词）Five-paragraph template (500–800 characters / 350–600 words)

¶写什么What to write对应量规Rubric row
1我的定义 + 两个实例：“在我看来，数字化学习是……”用自己的话；然后是第 0 节里那两次——一次真的学会了，一次其实在刷；再说一句边界：“但只有当____时它才算学习——我那两次的差别就在这里。”Your definition + two examples: “To me, digital learning is…” in your own words; then the two moments from section 0 — one real learning, one scrolling; then one boundary line: “but it only counts as learning when ____ — that is the difference between my two moments.”①
2对方最强的理由：“反对 / 支持的人有一个我无法否认的理由……”写到对方会点头，并写明理由从哪来：我问了____（家人 / 老师 / 同学，一句原话）/ 我的一次经历 / 读本第 __ 章。The other side's strongest case: “Those who object / support have a reason I cannot deny…” — until they would nod, and say where the reason comes from: I asked ____ (a parent / teacher / classmate — one sentence they said) / one experience of mine / Reader Ch. __.②
3我的立场与理由：“我仍然认为……因为……”至少一条理由来自读本概念（写明章号）或一次亲身经历。可以带条件。Your position and reasons: “I still think… because…” — at least one reason from a reader concept (chapter named) or a personal experience. Conditions are welcome.②
4两项优势、两项风险与对策：优势可用“三无”“丰富的笼子”；风险从微课 2 的四项里选两项，各配一条动作式对策。Two benefits, two risks with mitigations: benefits may draw on the “三无” and the “rich cage”; pick two of micro-lesson 2's four risks, each with an action mitigation.③
5一句话立场：如果学校只读一句，你希望是哪一句？Your position in one sentence: if the school reads only one line, which should it be?②
+（不计分）预想回音：“如果校长只回我一句，他最可能说：____；我会答：____。”(Ungraded) Anticipate the reply: “If the principal answered me with one sentence, it would most likely be: ____; and I would reply: ____.”—

对照：发展中 vs 达标Compare: Developing vs Meets

同一个立场（“应该允许，但有条件”），两种写法。先判断：哪一段在量规第 ② 行能得 4 分？

Same position (“allow it, with conditions”), two versions. Decide first: which one earns 4 points on rubric row ②?

A

有人觉得手机会让学生分心，我不这么认为。手机是工具，关键看怎么用。我用手机学英语很有效，所以学校应该允许。

Some people think phones distract students; I disagree. A phone is a tool — it depends how you use it. I learn English well on my phone, so school should allow it.

B

反对的老师有一个我无法否认的理由：上学期我自己就有过——查一个单词，二十分钟后还在看视频。教室里三十部手机，老师看不到三十块屏幕，而读本第 02 章说学习发生在大脑里，大脑不开工，坐在教室也没用。所以“禁止”是在保护那个开工的条件。我仍然认为应该允许，但只在两个条件下：查资料用固定的十分钟，其余时间手机放讲台的盒子里。理由是……

The teachers who object have a reason I cannot deny — I lived it last term: I looked up one word and was still watching videos twenty minutes later. In a room of thirty phones a teacher cannot see thirty screens, and Ch. 02 says learning happens in the brain; if the brain isn't working, sitting in class is useless. So a ban protects the condition for the brain to work. I still think it should be allowed, but only under two conditions: a fixed ten minutes for looking things up, and phones in the box on the teacher's desk the rest of the time. My reasons are…

C

支持允许的同学有一个真实的理由，我自己也享受过：第 07 章说的“无远弗届”——上学期一道物理题老师讲不清，我在网上看了两个老师讲，十分钟就懂了。禁掉手机，等于把那两个老师关在门外。但我仍然认为学校应该禁止在课堂上用手机，因为我问过我们班的英语老师，她说了一句我反驳不了的话：“我一节课看不过来三十块屏幕，我只看得见三十张脸。”读本第 02 章说学习发生在大脑里；课堂上大脑该开工的那四十分钟，值得由老师守住。网上那两个老师，放学后再请进来——这正是“无时不刻”给我们的余地。

Students who support allowing phones have a real reason, and I have enjoyed it myself: Ch. 07's “no distance” — last term the teacher could not make one physics problem clear, and I understood it in ten minutes from two teachers online. Ban the phone and you lock those two teachers out. Yet I still think the school should ban phones in class, because I asked our English teacher and she said something I cannot answer: “In one lesson I cannot watch thirty screens; I can only see thirty faces.” Ch. 02 says learning happens in the brain; the forty minutes when the brain should be at work in class deserve a teacher who guards them. The two online teachers can be invited in after school — exactly the room that “no fixed hour” gives us.

答案Answer

B 和 C 都得 4 分——立场相反，分数相同，因为量规评的是推理。C 的对方理由来自一次真实的询问（英语老师的一句原话），这正是量规第 ② 行“理由有来源”要的。B 得 4 分。A 的“有人觉得手机会让学生分心”只有一句，而且马上被“我不这么认为”推开——对方读到会说“你把我说弱了”。B 把对方的理由写成了对方也会点头的样子（具体场景 + 读本概念），然后立场依然清楚，还带着条件。注意 B 的支持理由里有一次亲身经历和一个读本概念，这正是第 ② 行“达标”要的。

B and C both score 4 — opposite positions, same score, because the rubric scores the reasoning. C's other-side case comes from a real question (one sentence from the English teacher), which is exactly what row ② means by “the reason has a source”. B scores 4. A's “some people think phones distract students” is one line, pushed aside at once by “I disagree” — the other side would say “you made me sound weak”. B writes the other side's case the way they would (a concrete scene plus a reader concept), and the position is still clear, with conditions. Note that B's support includes one personal experience and one reader concept — exactly what row ② “Meets” asks for.

AI 使用说明怎么写The AI use note

如果你用了 AI，在文末加两三句：If you used AI, add two–three sentences at the end:

“AI 使用说明：我用（工具名）检查了语句是否通顺，并请它替反对方再想一条理由；它给的理由我觉得不如我自己的经历有力，没有采用。立场、两个实例和对策都是我自己的。”

“AI use note: I used (tool) to check my sentences and asked it for one more reason on the opposing side; I found its reason weaker than my own experience and did not use it. The position, the two examples and the mitigations are my own.”

不用 AI 就写“本文未使用 AI”。If you didn't use AI, write “No AI was used.”
