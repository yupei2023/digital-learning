05 怎样用数字化学习让自己重生05 How digital learning can remake you 必读 · 约 10 分钟Required · ~10 min

本章导读Chapter at a glance

大脑是硬件，心智——经验、技能、记忆——是软件；学习就是给硬件升级软件，而且与电脑不同，大脑的硬件会被软件改造。大脑只对它认得的信号起反应（杨梅的故事：用拉丁语说，谁也不会流口水），而数字化学习让可用的信号种类成倍增加。爱因斯坦的大脑看起来很普通，是经历造就了他。住在多样、每天重新布置的环境里的小白鼠，比住在空盒子里的学得更快、突触明显更多：环境越丰富，越聪明——所以一个用网络看十种讲法的学习者，是在给自己布置更丰富的环境。最后，可塑性与态度相连：大脑记住的是你对经历的感受，乐观地看待苦读留下更好的记忆，而网络让你能靠近乐观的学习者、变成他们。

The brain is hardware and the mind — experience, skills, memories — is software; learning upgrades the software, and unlike a computer, the brain's hardware is reshaped by it. The brain only responds to signals it can read (the bayberry story — the “plum” of the 望梅止渴 tale: describe it in Latin and no one's mouth waters), and digital learning multiplies the kinds of signals available. Einstein's brain looked ordinary; his experiences made him. Mice raised in a varied, daily-rearranged environment learned faster and grew noticeably more synapses than mice in an empty box: the richer the environment, the smarter the animal — so a learner who uses the network to see a concept taught ten different ways is furnishing a richer environment. Finally, plasticity is tied to attitude: the brain keeps your feeling about an experience, so an optimistic reading of hard study leaves better memories, and the network lets you stay close to optimistic learners and become like them.

大脑是硬件，心智是软件The brain is hardware, the mind is software

电脑是依据人脑的功能特点制作出来的仿生产品。电脑的硬件保障了软件能够发挥功效，完成不同的工作。比如，我们在电脑硬盘上安装了文本编辑软件之后，就可以在电脑上写文章；安装了互联网浏览器软件之后，就可以用电脑查阅互联网上的信息。新生儿刚出生时的大脑，就像刚出厂的电脑，除了吃喝拉撒睡这样一些基本功能软件之外，没有安装任何复杂的软件，无法完成任何有挑战的任务。随着婴儿的成长和不断的学习，他逐渐开始能够使用语言，驾驶汽车，使用多种多样的工具完成复杂而有创意的生产……人类的大脑就好像电脑的硬件，而我们逐渐在大脑上积累的人生经验、学习的知识技能、储存的独特记忆，则类似于电脑软件，也就是心智。

A computer is a bionic product built on the working features of the human brain. Its hardware lets the software do its work: install a text editor and you can write on it; install a browser and you can read the Internet. A newborn's brain is like a computer fresh from the factory — apart from a few basic programs for eating, sleeping and so on, no complex software is installed and no demanding task can be done. As the baby grows and keeps learning, it gradually learns to use language, drive a car, and use all kinds of tools for complex, creative work… The human brain is like the computer's hardware, and the life experience, knowledge, skills and unique memories we accumulate on it are like the software: the mind.

心智是指一个人一系列认知能力组成的总体，这些能力可以让个体具有意识、感知外界、进行思考、做出判断以及记忆事物。可见，我们的学习过程，就是一个在大脑硬件中不断升级和提供软件功能的过程。但人比电脑强大太多了。新电脑用过几年之后，就需要更新换代，淘汰掉旧的硬件，以适应更高级软件的工作需求；而我们人类的大脑，竟然可以被心智重新改造，不断升级换代。所以，我们有了这样一个结论：运用学习，我们可以让自己变成更好的人。

The mind is the whole set of a person's cognitive abilities — those that let an individual be conscious, perceive the world, think, judge and remember. So learning is a process of continually upgrading and adding software functions to the brain's hardware. But a person is far more powerful than a computer. A new computer must be replaced after a few years, its old hardware discarded to suit more advanced software; the human brain, astonishingly, can be rebuilt by the mind and upgraded again and again. Hence our conclusion: through learning, we can make ourselves better people.

用大脑能识别的信号刺激它Stimulate the brain with signals it can recognise

我们为什么能感知到这个世界的缤纷多彩、鸟语花香？这是因为我们的身体上遍布着神经元。神经元是神经系统的基本结构和功能单位，集中分布在我们的眼、耳、鼻、舌、手这些器官之上，它们可以更有效地通过视觉、听觉、嗅觉、味觉、触觉来与这个世界互动，把来自外界的刺激传达给大脑中的神经元，形成我们对于这个世界的认识。当我们的眼睛看到一棵杨梅树上结满了青绿色的杨梅，用双手摘下两颗，捧在手上沉甸甸的，闻到它青涩的味道——这一系列视觉、触觉和味觉的刺激，可能已经让还没有真正吃到杨梅的你唇齿生津了。你听过的望梅止渴的故事，就是曹操运用了我们大脑的这个原理所实现的。但是，假如我刚才那一段有关杨梅的描述是用拉丁语说的，而你没有学过拉丁语，你就不懂我在说什么，嘴巴里也不会有什么反应。所以，要想正确地刺激大脑，我们需要运用它可以识别的信号，才能有更好的效果。在线学习，就为我们提供了更多样的刺激大脑的可能性。

Why can we perceive a world of colour, birdsong and fragrance? Because our bodies are covered with neurons, the basic structural and functional units of the nervous system. They cluster in the eyes, ears, nose, tongue and hands, where they interact with the world through sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch, passing outside stimuli to the neurons in the brain and forming our picture of the world. When your eyes see a bayberry tree heavy with green fruit, you pick two, feel their weight in your palm and smell their sourness — this chain of visual, tactile and olfactory stimuli may already have your mouth watering before you have eaten one. The story you know of “quenching thirst by looking at plums” (望梅止渴) is Cao Cao using exactly this principle of the brain. But if I had described the bayberries in Latin and you had never learned Latin, you would not understand me and your mouth would not react. So to stimulate the brain properly we must use signals it can recognise. Digital learning gives us far more ways to stimulate the brain.

环境越丰富，人越聪明The richer the environment, the smarter the person

每个人的大脑结构和功能大同小异，但每个人的心智，则由于自己走过的不同的路、经历的不同的事、结识的不同的人、看过的不同的书而天差地别。科学家研究过爱因斯坦的大脑，从结构上看并没有发现什么特殊之处，而爱因斯坦独特的人生经历和学习经历，才是使他独一无二的因素。

Every brain is much alike in structure and function, but every mind differs enormously, because of the different roads walked, things experienced, people met and books read. Scientists have studied Einstein's brain and found nothing structurally special; it was his unique life and learning experiences that made him unique.

科学家用小白鼠做实验，印证了我们的这个猜测。在实验室中，科学家们将两组小白鼠放置在不同的环境里，一段时间之后进行比较。第一组小白鼠被放在一个有多样化设施的空间中，每一天，这些设施都会被科学家们重新布置。对小白鼠来说，它们每天都要应对环境的变化，在变动的世界里找到生活的方法，这与它们天生在野外的生活模式其实是一致的——每天都需要应对外界变化的挑战。另一组对照组的小白鼠，则被放置在典型的实验室空间中，长时间生活在空空如也的盒子里。当这两组小白鼠在各自的环境中逐渐长大，快要成年之时，科学家对它们一起进行了一系列测试，结果发现：生活在复杂多变环境中的小白鼠，学习的速度更快，犯的错误更少。经过比较还发现，生活在复杂多变环境中的小白鼠，每一个视觉中枢中神经细胞上的突触，要比生活在简单实验室环境中的小白鼠多 20%–25%。由此我们可以看出，你接收到的来自环境的刺激越丰富，你就越聪明。

Scientists confirmed this guess with mice. In the laboratory, two groups of mice were kept in different environments and compared after a while. The first group lived in a space full of varied equipment, rearranged by the scientists every day; each day the mice had to cope with change and find ways to live in a shifting world — which is in fact how they live in the wild, meeting new challenges daily. The control group lived in a typical laboratory space, an empty box, for a long time. When both groups had grown up and were near adulthood, the scientists tested them together and found that the mice from the complex, changing environment learned faster and made fewer mistakes. Comparison also showed that these mice had 20–25% more synapses on the nerve cells of their visual centres than the mice from the simple laboratory environment. So: the richer the stimulation you receive from your environment, the smarter you become.

如果你明白了这个道理，你就知道为什么善用线上学习可以让你更聪明了。由于线上学习资源获取更加容易，你可以在学习某一个概念的时候，为自己提供多样化的学习材料。比如，同样是学习物理学中的牛顿三大定律，你在学完学校的课程之后，可以上网看一看世界上其他国家的课堂上，老师们是怎样教的——相对于那些不善于线上学习的同学来说，你就拥有了更多样的学习资源。再比如，你有一个问题解决不了，可以用中文在中文网站上获取答案的同时，也用英文在国外的网站上寻求大家的建议。事实上，很多学校已经在这样设置课程了。比如，我曾经组织学校的中学生参加过一个环球课堂项目，来自全世界不同国家的同龄人，可以针对一些全世界都关注的问题提供自己的解决方案，学生们通过线上学习，见识到了全世界不同文化背景的同龄人的思考结果。久而久之，经常用线上学习给自己提供复杂环境的学习者，不就会像实验中那些经历了更复杂多变环境的小白鼠一样，变得更聪明吗？

Once you understand this, you know why making good use of online learning can make you smarter. Because online learning resources are so easy to reach, you can give yourself varied materials when learning one concept. Take Newton's three laws: after finishing your school's lesson you can go online and see how teachers in other countries teach them — compared with classmates who are poor at online learning, you now have more varied resources. Or, when you cannot solve a problem, you can look for answers on Chinese websites in Chinese while asking for advice on foreign websites in English. Many schools already design courses this way. I once organised secondary-school students to join a global-classroom project in which peers from different countries proposed their own solutions to problems the whole world cares about; through online learning the students saw how peers from different cultural backgrounds think. Over time, won't a learner who keeps using online learning to give themselves a complex environment become smarter, like the mice that lived in the richer, more changeable environment?

大脑的可塑性与你的态度The brain's plasticity and your attitude

我们的大脑是非常迷人的，因为它有强大的可塑性，而这种可塑性，与你的主观意愿和感受有着非常紧密的关系。

Our brain is fascinating because it is highly plastic, and this plasticity is closely tied to your own will and feelings.

我们每一个人所经历的真实生活，对大脑中形成的记忆固然重要，但是，我们如何看待这些客观的经历——我们的主观态度和感受——对我们的影响将更加深远，因为我们的大脑所能记住的，是我们对这些经历的主观感受，甚至是我们一厢情愿的假想。比如，你已经从头至尾真实地经历了一件客观上非常痛苦的事情，把它理解为幸福的必要成分的你，也就是乐观的你，将比把这个过程看作痛苦不堪的你，也就是悲观的你，占更多的便宜。为什么这么说呢？因为乐观的你，将给你的大脑留下更多幸福的回忆；而悲观的你，则为你的大脑留下了一段痛苦的回忆。时间久了，客观经历给你的感觉早就消失不见了，而留在你大脑中的痛苦回忆，却能持续不断地向你输出负能量。

The real life each of us goes through certainly matters for the memories formed in the brain, but how we regard those objective experiences — our subjective attitude and feelings — has a far deeper effect, because what the brain remembers is our subjective feeling about the experience, even our wishful imagining of it. Suppose you have genuinely lived through something objectively very painful. The version of you that reads it as a necessary part of happiness — the optimistic you — comes out ahead of the version that sees the process as unbearable — the pessimistic you. Why? Because the optimistic you leaves the brain more happy memories, while the pessimistic you leaves it a painful memory. Long after the feeling of the objective experience has faded, the painful memory left in the brain keeps sending you negative energy.

还有更亏的呢。有的时候，你实际上并没有经历任何糟糕的事情，却自己吓唬自己，惟妙惟肖地想象出自己经历了非常糟糕的事情。这些从来未曾真正发生的事情，经过你不断偏离事实的想象之后，就真的可以成为你记忆中真实的一部分——虽然它们从来不曾出现过。

It can be worse still. Sometimes you have not gone through anything bad at all, yet you frighten yourself, vividly imagining that you have. Things that never happened, after enough imagining that drifts from the facts, can truly become part of your memory — though they never occurred.

相反，如果一个人真的经历了头悬梁、锥刺股般的学习过程，但他不认为那些痛苦是糟糕的，反而认为这是他幸福的阶梯，那么他的记忆就会认为，那些客观上看来痛苦异常的学习过程，其实是自己金不换的幸福人生的必要组成部分。

Conversely, if someone really has gone through study as hard as the old tales of tying one's hair to the beam and stabbing one's thigh with an awl, but does not regard that pain as bad — regards it, rather, as the staircase to happiness — then their memory will treat that objectively painful process of learning as a necessary part of a precious, happy life.

可见，以更积极、更乐观的态度看待我们经历的事情，对我们来说是更划算的。对于学习来说，更积极、更乐观地看待学习所经历的过程，会让我们收获更多。学习有可能让我们重生，学习有可能让我们成为我们想要成为的人。通过线上学习，你可以连接到更多优秀的、乐观的、积极的学习者，你可以看到更多你想成为的榜样，看到他们是怎样学习的。通过在线学习，你可以让自己更多地与你想成为的人在一起，多与榜样在一起，模仿他们，成为他们，成为更好的你，让自己重生。

So a more positive, more optimistic view of what we go through is the better bargain. For learning, a more positive and optimistic view of the process brings more. Learning may let us be reborn; learning may let us become the people we want to be. Through online learning you can connect with more excellent, optimistic, active learners; you can see more of the role models you want to become and how they learn. Through online learning you can spend more time with the people you want to become — stay with the role models, imitate them, become them, become a better you, and be reborn.

07 欢迎你来到这个“三无”学习时代 · “三无”、“边走边学”与结尾07 Welcome to the “三无” learning age · “三无”, “Learning on the road” and the ending 必读 · 约 12 分钟Required · ~12 min

本章导读Chapter at a glance

作者把在线学习的三项优势叫作“三无”——无远弗届、无时不刻、有教无类——并讲述自己作为全职学校工作者、丈夫和父亲，在 2019–21 年间如何在线读完硕士，包括凌晨三点的线上毕业典礼。“边走边学”：家人睡后在大连酒店里写作业；同步学习（指定时间——讨论、会议）与异步学习（LMS 上布置任务、一周截止）的区别；身体学会了一周的节奏、心脏在每个周三加速；起飞前几分钟用手机热点交作业；和相差十几个时区、从不抱怨的同学做小组作业。家人的支持让这一切成为可能。结尾：教育的障碍已缩减到只剩一个——你愿不愿意学习；以及 R.S. 托马斯的一首诗。

The author names three benefits of online learning — the “三无”: no distance, no fixed hour, no gatekeeper — and tells how, as a full-time school worker, husband and father, he earned a master's degree online during 2019–21, including a 3 a.m. online graduation. “Learning on the road”: homework in a Dalian hotel after the family slept; the difference between synchronous learning (fixed time — discussions, meetings) and asynchronous learning (tasks set on an LMS, done by a weekly deadline); a body that learned the weekly rhythm and a heart that raced every Wednesday; submitting an assignment over a phone hotspot minutes before a flight; group work with classmates twelve time zones away who never complained. His family's support made it possible. The ending: the barrier to education has shrunk to one — are you willing to learn? — and a poem by R. S. Thomas.

这是一个“三无”学习时代。什么是“三无”？即“无远弗届，无时不刻，有教无类”。这是我给在线学习总结出来的三个优势：通过在线学习，我们可以不受空间的局限，摆脱时间的束缚，跨越认知、种族、信仰和文化的隔阂……我自己就是一个“三无”学习的受益者——一个全职的 K12 学校工作人员，一个在家庭中同时承担儿子、丈夫、父亲角色的、想要不断变好的终身学习者。我在过往几年“三无”学习中积累的经验和总结的教训，也许会对你有所启发。

This is a “三无” learning age. What are the three? No distance (无远弗届), no fixed hour (无时不刻), no gatekeeper (有教无类). These are the three advantages I have summed up for online learning: through it we are no longer limited by space, bound by time, or separated by barriers of understanding, ethnicity, belief and culture… I am a beneficiary of “三无” learning myself — a full-time K12 school worker, and at home a son, a husband and a father, a lifelong learner who wants to keep getting better. The experience and lessons I gathered over several years of it may be of some use to you.

2019 年，当我拿到研究生院的录取通知书的时候，还不知道自己在未来两年中所要经历的，会是一次与新冠肺炎疫情的爆发与防控相伴而行的学习之旅；也不知道会有那么多个工作结束后的夜晚，我需要重新振奋精神，和大洋彼岸的同学们讨论、做小组项目、听讲座、写报告……更不知道会有那么多的节假日从此与我无关。与此同时，我也发现了一条更便捷、更高效、更实惠的通往自由学习之路。

In 2019, when I received my graduate-school admission letter, I did not yet know that the two years ahead would be a learning journey running alongside the outbreak and control of COVID-19; nor that on so many evenings after work I would have to rally myself to discuss, do group projects, attend lectures and write reports with classmates across the ocean; still less that so many holidays would no longer be mine. At the same time, I discovered a more convenient, more efficient and more affordable road to free learning.

当我在出差途中的酒店里，被凌晨 3 点的闹钟叫醒，睁着睡眼惺忪的双眼参加我的线上毕业典礼时，看到视频中闪现出以下的文字：

When, in a hotel on a business trip, a 3 a.m. alarm woke me and I attended my online graduation ceremony with sleepy eyes, these words flashed on the screen:

“多少个睡眼惺忪的凌晨，你还在奋笔疾书，赶在截止时间之前提交作业？

“How many bleary-eyed early mornings have you still been writing, racing to submit before the deadline?

多少个风和日丽的假期，你远离亲朋欢聚的温暖，独自去修改一篇报告？”

How many sunny holidays have you left the warmth of family and friends to revise a report alone?”

我那刚刚能看清屏幕的双眼，再次被泪水模糊了……

My eyes, which had only just managed to make out the screen, blurred again with tears…

边走边学Learning on the road

时间退回到 2019 年 7 月，我和家人坐着火车去大连旅行。白天，我们在大连博物馆参观，在星海广场流连，在旅顺口遥望历史的硝烟。一顿海鲜饺子之后，我从旁边的星巴克买上一个超大杯热美式咖啡，妻子就知道我又要熬夜了。

Rewind to July 2019: my family and I took the train to Dalian for a holiday. By day we visited the Dalian Museum, lingered at Xinghai Square and looked out over history from Lüshunkou. After a dinner of seafood dumplings I bought a large Americano at the Starbucks next door, and my wife knew I was going to stay up again.

家人睡下后，我打开笔记本电脑，开始做本周的作业。作业完成，我还需要准备和美国合作学校的项目讨论。夜阑人静之时，我戴上蓝牙耳机，接通线上会议室，与美国合作学校的校长开会。会开完已经是深夜了，躺在床上的第 2 秒我就进入了梦乡。抓紧时间休息吧，因为第二天起床，我还要陪家人去大连中山广场街拍呢。

After the family went to bed I opened my laptop and started the week's homework. That done, I still had to prepare a project discussion with a partner school in the United States. Late at night I put on my Bluetooth earphones, joined the online meeting room and met the American principal. By the time we finished it was very late; I was asleep the second I lay down. Rest fast — next morning I was taking the family to Zhongshan Square for street photos.

“线上教育”硕士项目的学习采用“同步学习”和“异步学习”两种方式展开。同步学习，需要学习者在指定时间上线完成学习任务，一般是讨论、答疑、会议；异步学习，则是教授提前在学习管理系统（LMS, Learning Management System）上布置学习任务，学习者根据自己可用的学习时间进行学习，一般是读书、查文献、写作、参与论坛讨论等。异步学习任务的完成时长一般是一周，学习者只要在一周之内、截止时间之前完成当周的学习任务即可。我就读的大学位于美国中部时区，一般会以美国每周三晚上的 23:59 作为作业提交截止时间（Deadline）。经过一段时间的线上学习后，我的身体逐渐适应了线上学习的节奏：周四、周五下班之后，集中看书、读文献；周六、周日抓紧写作业；周一、周二下班之后，继续做作业、完善作业。每一个周三到来时，我的心率都会逐渐加快，我仿佛听见我的心脏坐着一列由远而近的火车，裹挟着越来越多的压力不断向我驶来，搏动之声清晰可辨……直到我提交了作业之后，心率才再次恢复正常；然后在下一个周三临近之时，我又会经历一轮类似的心率提速。在这个过程中，我的妻子和家人给了我极大的支持，他们主动承担了更多的家庭事务，为我腾出了更多的时间专注于学习。我无法想象，如果没有家人的理解和支持，我该如何顺利拿到我的学位。

The online-education master's programme ran in two modes, synchronous and asynchronous. Synchronous learning requires learners to be online at a set time — usually for discussion, Q&A and meetings. Asynchronous learning means the professor posts tasks in advance on a learning management system (LMS) and learners study when their own time allows — usually reading, searching the literature, writing, and taking part in forum discussions. An asynchronous task usually spans a week; you simply finish that week's work before its deadline. My university is in the US Central time zone and generally set 23:59 on Wednesday, US time, as the deadline. After a while my body adapted to the rhythm: Thursday and Friday after work, reading and literature; Saturday and Sunday, writing the assignments; Monday and Tuesday after work, finishing and polishing them. As each Wednesday approached my heart rate would climb — I seemed to hear my heart riding a train coming nearer, carrying more and more pressure, every beat distinct… only after I submitted did it settle, and as the next Wednesday drew near the cycle began again. Through all this my wife and family supported me enormously, taking on more of the household so that I could concentrate on study. I cannot imagine how I would have earned my degree without their understanding and support.

有一次在去往海南三亚凤凰机场的路上，我坐在汽车的副驾驶座上，一路上头都没有抬一下，十个手指在键盘上飞快地敲击。而这之前，我已经一个通宵没有合眼了，也是以这样的状态持续阅读和写作——那个时候，我已经连续 24 小时没有睡觉了。我所想要争取的，就是在截止时间之前完成作业的提交。当时的我知道，汽车到达机场后，我能够利用的时间就只有安检和登机之间的一小段了；飞机一旦起飞，我的电脑就不能再用，作业也就无法提交。终于，在飞机起飞之前的几分钟里，我的作业通过手机热点的信号成功提交了。把电脑放好后，身子向后一靠，再一睁眼，我已经到北京了。

Once, on the way to Phoenix Airport in Sanya, Hainan, I sat in the passenger seat with my head down the whole way, ten fingers flying over the keyboard. Before that I had already gone a night without sleep, reading and writing in the same state — I had been awake for 24 hours straight. All I wanted was to submit before the deadline. I knew that once we reached the airport my only remaining window would be the minutes between security and boarding; after take-off the laptop had to close and the assignment could not be submitted. In the last minutes before departure, the assignment went through over my phone's hotspot. I stowed the laptop, leaned back, and when I opened my eyes I was in Beijing.

2019 年 10 月，我在深圳参加一个会议，白天在会场听嘉宾们分享，晚上在酒店通过网络学习，从酒店往返会场的路上，就是我的“课间休息”。我很偶然地在会场上遇到了自己早年工作时的老校长。散会之后，老校长请我吃饭，带我逛莲花山公园。站在莲花山山顶，我才发现，深圳原来是一个现代化与自然完美融合的年轻城市。

In October 2019 I attended a conference in Shenzhen: by day listening to speakers in the hall, by night studying online in the hotel, and the road between the two was my “break”. Quite by chance I ran into the old principal from my early working years. After the session he invited me to dinner and a walk in Lianhuashan Park. Only from the hilltop did I discover that Shenzhen is a young city where the modern and the natural blend perfectly.

相比于异步学习，同步学习对我的挑战更大。有的课程，教授会安排学生们进行分组活动，提交小组作业。在整个硕士项目的学习过程中，除了曾经遇到过一位在上海工作、和我共用北京时间的南非同学以外，我合作过的其他同学全都来自美国本土。教授给出不同方向的小组课题之后，同学们就根据自己的喜好选择分组。组员们进行同步讨论之前的第一件事，就是确定各自的时区，选择大家共同可用的时段。其他组员彼此之间充其量有几个小时的时差，而我在地球的另一端，和美国的时差有十几个小时之多，所以和我同组的同学们都被迫迁就我，要么选择清晨，要么选择深夜进行小组活动。但我从来没有看到过他们有任何抱怨——他们反而会安慰和鼓励我。有一次，由于处理一些临时事务，没来得及回到住所的我，不得不在马路边上用手机登录线上会议室，完成了我和同学们的同步学习。

Compared with asynchronous learning, synchronous learning was the greater challenge. In some courses the professor arranged group work with group submissions. Throughout the master's programme, apart from one South African classmate who worked in Shanghai and shared Beijing time with me, every classmate I worked with was in the United States. When the professor set group topics, students chose groups by interest, and the first thing before any synchronous discussion was to sort out everyone's time zone and find a slot all could use. My groupmates were at most a few hours apart from one another, while I was on the other side of the planet, more than ten hours away — so they had to accommodate me, meeting either in the early morning or late at night. I never once saw them complain; instead they comforted and encouraged me. Once, delayed by some sudden business and unable to get back to where I was staying, I had to log into the meeting room on my phone at the roadside to complete a synchronous session with them.

自从 2019 年 8 月 30 日学校的在线教育项目正式启动以来，我越来越感觉到，“在线教育”在穿透时间、空间和人间的过程中，在“无远弗届，无时不刻，有教无类”上体现出来的优势越来越大。人们眼下的教育障碍，史无前例地缩减到只剩一个了：你愿不愿意学习？

Since our school's online education programme officially launched on 30 August 2019, I have felt more and more that as online education cuts through time, space and people, its advantages in “no distance, no fixed hour, no gatekeeper” keep growing. The barrier to education has shrunk, as never before in history, to a single one: are you willing to learn?

只要你愿意，你就可以随时随地进入“三无”学习时代。

As long as you are willing, you can enter the “三无” learning age anytime, anywhere.

最后，我想和大家分享一首 R.S. 托马斯的诗，让我们一起感谢这个“三无”学习时代吧。

Finally, let me share a poem by R. S. Thomas, and let us be grateful together for this “三无” learning age.

围绕着你
慢慢转动着一整个世界，
辽阔而富于意义，不亚于伟大的
柏拉图孤寂心灵的任何构想。

Around you
slowly turns a whole world,
vast and full of meaning, no less than any of the great
conceptions of Plato's lonely mind.

——R.S. 托马斯

— R. S. Thomas

边学边用Learning by doing

我一边以学生的身份学习“在线教育”，一边以教师的身份学以致用，把“在线教育”用在我的工作中。2019 年 11 月，有 4 名学生报名参加了环球课堂的佛罗里达项目，他们来自不同的年级和班级。作为指导教师的我为他们搭建了线上学习系统，利用中午饭后那 30 分钟的时间，和同学们进行了 4 次面对面的线下学习。将必备的学习技巧教给他们之后，我们就利用每个周末大家都更方便的时段，以线上异步学习和同步学习相结合的方式，进行佛罗里达项目的行前准备。

While studying online education as a student, I applied it at work as a teacher. In November 2019, four students from different grades and classes signed up for a global-classroom project in Florida. As their supervising teacher I built an online learning system for them, used four lunch-break half-hours for face-to-face sessions, taught them the essential study skills, and then ran the trip preparation at weekends — when everyone was freer — combining asynchronous and synchronous online learning.

经过一个月的线上学习，每一个学生都阅读了至少一本有关佛罗里达的书籍，规划好了自己在参加高尔夫球比赛之外要进行的个人研究项目。在我们从中国出发去美国之前，我和学生们面对面相见的时间加起来只有 2 个小时，但他们已经对这个即将持续 3 周的环球课堂项目有了系统而深入的了解。佛罗里达项目结束之后，孩子们除了捧回沉甸甸的高尔夫比赛奖牌之外，还完成了自己的个人研究报告，最长的一份将近有 7 千字。

After a month of online learning, every student had read at least one book about Florida and planned a personal research project alongside the golf competition. Before we left China, my total face-to-face time with them was two hours, yet they already had a systematic, deep understanding of a project that would last three weeks. Afterwards, besides their golf medals, each completed a personal research report — the longest nearly 7,000 characters.

2020 年初，新冠肺炎疫情让整个人类社会陷入了“静默”，互联网一下子变得更加热闹和拥挤了。在恐慌和烦躁之中，如何用正确的健康知识保护自己？如何以积极的心态配合疫情防控？如何以切实的行动持续学习？我利用学到的有关“在线教育”的知识、理念和技术，带领学校的老师们创建了校内在线教学体系。同时，在课程研发的基础上，我也曾代表学校和中国科学院的科学家同台进行公益科普讲座，在视频平台直播过科普知识讲座。

In early 2020 the pandemic brought human society to a “standstill”, and the Internet suddenly became busier and more crowded. Amid panic and restlessness, how to protect yourself with sound health knowledge? How to support epidemic control with a positive attitude? How to keep learning through concrete action? Using what I had learned about online education — knowledge, ideas and technology — I led our school's teachers in building an in-school online teaching system. Drawing on that course development, I also represented the school in public science lectures alongside scientists from the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and gave live science talks on a video platform.

最初带领校内教师进行线上教学时，考虑到老师们的在线教学经验不足，为了给教师们留出更充足的准备时间，在第一轮线上教学实施过程中，我自己包揽了全校所有教学班级的所有同步线上教学任务：上午给 1–5 年级教学，下午给 6–11 年级（当时学校还没有 12 年级）教学。白天的教学工作之后，我还要继续组织教师们练习线上教学的技巧，把我白天的讲课视频分享给老师们，在好的地方做讲评，在不好的地方做提醒，不断改善线上教学的整体实施方案。如此这般，当第二周线上教学开始时，老师们已经熟悉了线上教学的各个环节，多种类型的高质量线上教学活动就从容不迫地开展了起来。

When I first led the teachers into online teaching, knowing their experience was limited and wanting to give them more preparation time, I took on every synchronous online class in the whole school in the first round myself: grades 1–5 in the morning, grades 6–11 in the afternoon (the school had no grade 12 yet). After a day's teaching I kept organising the teachers to practise online techniques, shared my recorded lessons, praised what worked and flagged what did not, and kept improving the overall plan. By the second week the teachers knew every step, and high-quality online activities of many kinds unfolded calmly.

人们看到海平面上浮出的冰山一角时，往往想象不到海平面之下冰山的巨大体量。在进行线上教学研发的过程中，作为亲历者的我深知，一种行之有效的线上教学策略，从研发试验到成功运行所需要花费的精力和时间，都如隐没在海平面之下的冰山那样——巨大，但看不见。

When people see the tip of an iceberg above the water, they rarely imagine its bulk beneath. Having lived through the development of online teaching, I know that the energy and time an effective online teaching strategy takes, from trial to smooth operation, are like the iceberg under the surface — enormous, but unseen.

在看了 300 多节课程实录，参考了数十个教学评估体系，拆解了多种行业的自动化流程标准之后，经历了将近 6 个学期的打磨，我最终成功搭建了全校的“学科教师正式评估课体系云系统”。通过这套系统，教师们可以自主、自由地录课，之后没有任何压力地任意选择一学期中自己最满意的那一节作为评估课提交评估。评估人员可以自由选择线下或线上观摩评估课；如果时间不允许同步听课，还可以通过云系统观看课堂实录，在电脑上，甚至直接用手机就可以完成第一轮的量化评估工作，极大地节省了工作量，而后再与教师进行有针对性的课后讨论。通过量化评估数据，我们可以针对教师个体的教学基本功进行分析，也可以针对教研组的整体教学情况进行数据分析，还可以就全校教师的某一项教学技能进行数据分析。数据就是新石油。站在海量教学数据的基础上，教学管理人员可以清晰地看到教师个体的成长轨迹、教研组的优势和劣势，这为全校的教师职业发展工作提供了精准的依据。在此基础上，再进行第二轮质性研究和分析，观察教师年际之间的成长和变化，就能够更有针对性地提出教师发展的下一阶段目标了……在大量节省人力、物力和时间的基础上，“学科教师正式评估课体系云系统”还提升了全校教师评估整体工作的效率和效果。

After watching more than 300 recorded lessons, consulting dozens of evaluation frameworks and dissecting automated process standards from several industries, over nearly six semesters of refinement I finally built the school's cloud system for formal lesson evaluation. Teachers record lessons freely and, without pressure, choose the one they are most satisfied with each semester to submit; evaluators observe on site or online, or watch the recording later, and complete the first quantitative round on a computer or even a phone, saving much effort before a targeted post-lesson discussion. With the data we can analyse an individual teacher's fundamentals, a department's overall picture, or one teaching skill across the whole school. Data is the new oil: on a base of abundant teaching data, managers can see each teacher's growth path and each department's strengths and weaknesses, giving precise grounds for professional development, and a second, qualitative round can then set the next stage of goals… While saving much labour, material and time, the cloud system also raised the efficiency and effect of the school's whole evaluation work.

曾经，学校的围墙分割开了学生和家长；现在，通过在线教育，学校内的很多教学活动都可以做到家长和孩子同上一堂课。这样齐抓共管、目标一致的教育教学，当然会收获更好的结果。

Once, the school wall divided students from parents; now, through online education, many school activities can be attended by parents and children in the same lesson. Teaching that is jointly supported and shares one goal naturally achieves better results.
