帮助，妨碍，还是看情况？Help, hinder, or it depends?

点一张卡片，再点一个筐，把它放进去。放进“看情况”的卡片，检查后会多出一行，请写：有效的条件是____。放错了？再点那张卡，再点别的筐。全部放完后点“检查”，结果和每张卡的解释会出现在筐的上方和卡片下面。请在你写信的那台设备上做这个活动——你写的条件只保存在这台设备的浏览器里。每一条都有解释——解释和你写的条件，比对错更重要。

Tap a card, then tap a bin. Cards in “it depends” get an extra line after checking: it helps on condition that ____. Misplaced one? Tap the card again, then another bin. Place all ten, then press Check; the result appears above the bins and each card's explanation under the card. Do this on the device you will write the letter on — your conditions are saved only in this device's browser. Every card has an explanation — the explanation and your condition matter more than the score.
