我为什么学这门课？Why am I taking this course?

一段话，150–300 字One paragraph, 150–300 words约 20 分钟~20 min保存在自己电脑里Kept on your own computer

要做什么What to do

写一段话回答：我为什么学这门课？它不计分，没有对错，也不会有人打分——它是写给你自己的。第 3 周你建好网站后，它会成为“关于我”页的第一段；第 15 周做学期作品集时，你会回头读它。

Write one paragraph answering: Why am I taking this course? It is ungraded, has no right answer and nobody scores it — it is written for you. In Week 3, once your website exists, it becomes the first paragraph of your About-me page; in Week 15, building your portfolio, you will read it again.

三个提示（选一个或都用）Three prompts (use one or all)

  1. 一次经历。你上一次在网上学会一件事，是什么时候？学的是什么？后来呢？One experience. When did you last learn something online? What was it? What happened next?
  2. 一个期待。读完课程说明，这门课里你最想学会的是哪一件事？为什么是它？One expectation. After the course guide, which one thing in this course do you most want to be able to do? Why that one?
  3. 一个担心。一个人在线学 16 周，你最担心什么？（作者在第 01 章说线上学习会拉大差距——你会站在哪一边？）One worry. Sixteen weeks of learning online alone — what worries you most? (Ch. 01 says learning online widens gaps — which side will you be on?)

步骤Steps

  1. 新建一个文档或纯文本文件，命名 M0-why.txt（用 Word 或备忘录也行，文件名一样）。Create a document or plain-text file named M0-why.txt (Word or Notes are fine; keep the name).
  2. 第一行写“我为什么学这门课”和今天是第几周（第 1 周）——不写日期。First line: “Why am I taking this course?” and the week (Week 1) — no calendar date.
  3. 写 150–300 字。中文或英文都可以。Write 150–300 words, in Chinese or English.
  4. 最后加三行日志：这周学到了什么 · 哪里卡住了 · 下周怎么办。End with the three-line log: what I learned · where I got stuck · what I'll do next week.
  5. 保存在你自己的电脑里一个以后找得到的位置（第 7 周会教你整理文件夹）。不发给任何人。Save it somewhere on your own computer you will find again (Week 7 teaches folder organisation). Send it to nobody.
为什么不发布？你的网站第 3 周才会有。这周先写，第 3 周搬过去——这样你的网站从第一天起就有内容。Why not publish? Your website does not exist until Week 3. Write it now, move it then — so your site has content from day one.
