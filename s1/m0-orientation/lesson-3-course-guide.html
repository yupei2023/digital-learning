每周怎么上The weekly routine

  • 每周一个页面。每个教学周有一张页面，从头到尾就是这一周的学习：目标 → 预计用时 → 材料 → 跟做 → 任务与量规 → 自评清单 → 自测题 → 反思提示 → 建议节奏 → 常见问题。页面本身就是学习体验，请不要直接跳到任务。One page a week. Each teaching week has one page that IS the week's learning: objectives → time → materials → follow-along → task and rubric → self-review → self-quiz → reflection → pace → FAQ. The page is the experience; please don't jump straight to the task.
  • 节奏。周一开放；建议周三前看完材料、周六前完成任务、周日前发布并写日志。没有迟交处罚——但有建议节奏，因为下一周不会等你。Rhythm. Open Monday; suggested: materials by Wednesday, task by Saturday, publish and log by Sunday. No late penalty — but a suggested pace, because next week does not wait.
  • 没有讨论区。这门课用个人反思代替讨论。遇到问题：先看页面底部的常见问题；仍不清楚，邮件导师或教师（24–48 小时内回复，节假日除外）；可预约一对一视频。No forum. Reflection replaces discussion. Questions: the FAQ at the foot of the page first, then email your mentor or the instructor (reply in 24–48 h, holidays excepted); 1:1 video by appointment.
  • 每周 2 小时。每页标明预计用时，各项相加不超过 120 分钟，外部课程也算在内；标注“选做”的内容不计入。Two hours a week. Each page states its time; the total never exceeds 120 minutes including your external course; optional items don't count.
  • 假期。遇法定节假日，当周任务顺延到下一个周日；国庆周与清明或劳动节周为缓冲周，不开新内容。具体日期见当年的“本学期日历”页。Holidays. On statutory holidays that week's task rolls to the next Sunday; National Day week and one spring holiday week are buffers with no new content. Dates live on the per-year calendar page.   • 互动都在浏览器里。勾选、自测、进度条不需要账号，进度只保存在你自己的浏览器里——换了浏览器或清了数据就会清空。All interaction runs in the browser. Checklists, quizzes and the progress bar need no account and are saved only in your own browser — a different browser, or cleared data, starts fresh.

温馨提示：在线课程并不比线下课程轻松，它更需要自我管理。建议固定每周两个时间段（各约 1 小时）专门学习本课。A reminder: online courses are not easier — they ask for more self-management. Reserve two ~1-hour blocks each week for this course.

这学期，你将真正在线学一门课。从外部课程菜单里选两门公开免费的在线课程：一门主修（学完，或学完菜单标注的子集），一门探索（只学两三节）。第 6 周试学、第 7 周在网站上写下选择、第 11–14 周每周学 1 小时（算在 2 小时之内）、寒假选做。需要注册的平台，请先告诉家长。

This semester you will really learn a course online. From the external course menu you choose two free public online courses: one core (finish it, or its named subset) and one explore (two or three lessons). Sample in Week 6, write your choice on your site in Week 7, study 1 hour a week in Weeks 11–14 (inside the 2 hours), optionally over the winter break. Tell your family before registering on any platform.

学习礼仪Learning etiquette

  1. 邮件：写清主题，称呼得体，署名完整；预期 24–48 小时内得到回复。Email: clear subject, proper greeting, full name; expect a reply within 24–48 hours.
  2. 录制：选择安静环境，衣着得体，不拍到他人。Recording: quiet space, appropriately dressed, no one else in frame.
  3. 网站内容：只发布你自己的作品与反思；引用他人内容注明来源；不发布他人的个人信息或未经证实的信息。On your site: only your own work and reflections; credit sources; no one else's personal information, no unverified claims.
  4. 自我管理：异步不等于随时——跟上建议节奏，每周把学习写进日志。Self-management: asynchronous does not mean anytime — keep the suggested pace and log every week.
  5. 数字公民：保护自己和他人的隐私；对不同观点保持好奇与善意；网络霸凌零容忍。Digital citizenship: protect privacy; meet different views with curiosity and goodwill; zero tolerance for cyberbullying.

学术诚信Academic integrity

作弊：明知内容出自他人（或 AI）之手，仍当作自己的成果提交——包括抄用他人分享的答案、让 AI 或工具代替自己完成本应独立思考的部分、把答案提供给他人抄用、在标注“独立”的任务中使用 AI。剽窃：有意或无意地将他人的原创观点或内容当作自己的——包括不注明出处、错误引用、仅改动个别字词的“复述”。在校学生的违规任务将被记零分，并可能约谈家长、影响后续课程的申请资格。

Cheating: knowingly submitting work created by someone else (or by AI) as your own — including copying shared answers, letting AI do what you were meant to think through, sharing answers for others to copy, and using AI on tasks marked Independent. Plagiarism: presenting others' original ideas or content as your own, intentionally or not — including missing attribution, wrong citation, and word-swapped “paraphrase”. For enrolled students, violations earn a zero on the task and may lead to a parent conference and loss of eligibility for further programmes.

节奏与截止Pacing and deadlines

  • 没有迟交处罚，也没有“讨论区关闭”。每周页面给出建议节奏；导师登记表以周日为“本周完成”的参考点。No late penalties and nothing “closes”. Each page gives a suggested pace; Sunday is the reference point for “done this week”.
  • 落后了：先完成当周任务，再补以前的——缓冲周就是给你补做的；哪一周没有登记，导师当周就会联系你。Behind? Do the current week first, then catch up in buffer weeks; your mentor contacts you the same week a link is missing.
  • 学期作品集与年度作品集有明确的收尾日（见当年日历页）；只有它们晚交会影响证书的发放时间。Only the two portfolios have firm closing dates (per-year calendar page); lateness there only delays your certificate.

设备要求Technical requirements

  • 稳定的网络连接；一台系统与浏览器保持更新的电脑（Chrome / Edge / Safari / Firefox 最新版）。作业建议在电脑上完成；页面在手机上也可阅读。A reliable connection; an up-to-date computer and browser (latest Chrome / Edge / Safari / Firefox). Do tasks on a computer; pages also read on a phone.
  • 麦克风与摄像头（或手机）——用于录音、录像与截图任务。Microphone and camera (or a phone) for recording and screenshot tasks.
  • 一个电子邮箱（第 2 周开始使用）；建站平台账号（第 3 周注册，只需邮箱）。An email address (used from Week 2); a website-builder account (Week 3, email-only).
  • 遇到设备或平台故障：立即邮件教师 / 导师并附截图——技术问题不会让你孤立无援，但请第一时间说。Tech trouble? Email the instructor or your mentor with a screenshot right away — you will not be left alone with it, but say so early.

家庭参与Family engagement
