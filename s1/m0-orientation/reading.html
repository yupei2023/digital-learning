01 序：为什么你必须学会数字化学习？01 Preface: Why you must learn to learn digitally 约 12 分钟~12 min

本章导读Chapter at a glance

这里的“你”指的是每一个人——学生、家长、老师，还有已经退休的爷爷奶奶。本章从家里说起：四位退休的长辈跟着七岁的孙女在微信群里学英语，因为会不会在线学习，已经关系到生活质量、学习效率，甚至安全与尊严。2021 年的《数据安全法》说明这项能力已经进入立法视野。疫情期间线上教学的“翻车现场”很多，但把责任推给学习方式，就像写错别字的学生怪那支笔；该做的是知道它的长处和局限——国际教育技术协会（ISTE）的九级数码素养就是这样一份清单。接着是好处：《西游记》里九九八十一难才取回的真经，今天一次检索就追到了纳瓦尔·拉维坎特免费的书和朗读音频；作者自己的硕士学位百分之百在线读完；中学生可以入读国外的在线高中，甚至同时读两所学校；密涅瓦大学让本科生在全世界七座城市轮转。挑战是“马太效应”：同一根网线，奖励了想学的人，把不想学的人留给了游戏和短视频，差距反而拉大。最后是危险：数码世界是一个有病毒、有钓鱼、有诈骗的陌生国度，孩子不该毫无准备地独自走进去。

“You” here means everyone — students, parents, teachers, retired grandparents. The chapter opens at home: four retired grandparents learning English in a family chat group from a seven-year-old, because learning online now touches quality of life, efficiency and even safety and dignity. China's 2021 Data Security Law shows the ability has entered lawmakers' view. The pandemic produced embarrassing online-teaching failures, but blaming the medium is like a student blaming the pen for spelling mistakes; the point is to know its strengths and limits, as ISTE's nine levels of digital citizenship suggest. Then the upside: where the monks of Journey to the West suffered eighty-one trials for scriptures, a search today traces three “health formulas” to Naval Ravikant's free book and audio in seconds; the author's own master's degree was earned entirely online; students can enrol in overseas online high schools or even two schools at once; Minerva University rotates its undergraduates through seven world cities. The challenge: online learning widens gaps — a “Matthew effect” — because it rewards those who want to learn and leaves the rest with games and short videos. Finally the danger: the digital world is a foreign country of viruses, phishing and scams, and a child should not walk into it unprepared.

为什么你必须会在线学习？注意，这里的“你”，指的是每一个人——不管你是在校的学生，还是学生家长，抑或是学校的老师，甚至是已经退休在家的爷爷奶奶。在线学习，是我们每一个人都必须会、必须掌握的。

Why must you be able to learn online? Note that “you” here means every one of us — whether you are a student at school, a student's parent, a teacher at a school, or a grandparent already retired at home. Learning online is something every one of us has to be able to do, has to master.

有的朋友会说：我都退休了，还要会在线学习吗？答案是肯定的。大家上网以“网络诈骗”和“退休人员”为关键词检索一下，就能看到触目惊心的结果。相反，善于线上学习，可以很好地改善自己的生活。

Some friends will say: I have already retired — do I still need to learn online? The answer is yes. Search online for “internet fraud” together with “retired people” and the results are alarming. Conversely, being good at learning online can improve your own life a great deal.

我爸、我妈、我的岳父、岳母现在都已经退休了。他们四个人现在每天最热衷的事情之一，就是在我们家的微信群里说英语。为什么呢？因为我七岁的女儿通过线上教学，在微信群里教他们说英语，还会对四位长辈说的英语进行点评，有模有样的。爷爷、奶奶、姥姥、姥爷想要获得孙女的关注，线上学习的热情特别高涨。其实，社会早已注意到了线上学习的重要性，因为它可以提高我们的生活质量，提升我们的学习效率，捍卫我们的生命安全和尊严。

My father, my mother, my father-in-law and my mother-in-law have all retired. One of the things the four of them are keenest on every day is speaking English in our family WeChat group. Why? Because my seven-year-old daughter teaches them English there, through online lessons, and comments on the four elders' English quite professionally. Wanting their granddaughter's attention, the grandparents on both sides throw themselves into learning online. Society noticed the importance of online learning long ago, because it can raise the quality of our lives, raise the efficiency of our learning, and defend the safety and the dignity of our lives.

在线学习已进入立法视野

Learning online has entered the view of the lawmakers

在如今这个时代，“在线学习”已经成为我国立法所关注的能力。我国在 2021 年 6 月就已经发布了《中华人民共和国数据安全法》，其中就有针对安全的考虑。比如，第一章第十一条明确表示：“国家积极开展数据安全治理、数据开发利用等领域的国际交流与合作，参与数据安全相关国际规则和标准的制定，促进数据跨境安全、自由流动。”如果这本读本的读者中有朋友现在正身处国外，那么这本读本就是一个跨境数据流动的平台了。

In our era, the ability to learn online has become something our country's legislation attends to. In June 2021 China issued the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, which includes considerations of security. Chapter 1, Article 11, for instance, states plainly: “The State shall actively carry out international exchange and cooperation in such fields as data security governance and the development and use of data, take part in the formulation of international rules and standards relating to data security, and promote the secure and free cross-border flow of data.” If any reader of this book happens to be abroad right now, then this book is itself a platform for a cross-border flow of data.

线上学习不是洪水猛兽

Online learning is not a monster

新冠疫情让绝大多数国内的家长对于线上学习的态度犹如惊弓之鸟，唯恐避之不及。太多的线上教学“翻车现场”，让人尴尬，也让人心痛。比如，有的教师线下怎么上课，线上还怎么上课；有的学生为了应付线上老师的点名和抽查，做了一个假人放在摄像头前，装作在学习；还有的不注意保护线上教学平台的密码和准入权限，使得多地的网课被入侵，入侵者在线上课堂里捣乱、骂人，甚至播放哀乐……类似的新闻，大家在网上可以看到很多。

COVID-19 left the great majority of parents in China as jumpy about online learning as a bird already startled by the bow (惊弓之鸟), anxious to keep well away from it. There were too many online-teaching pile-ups, embarrassing to watch and painful to watch. Some teachers taught online exactly as they had taught in the classroom; some students, to get past the online roll-call and the spot checks, propped a dummy in front of the camera and pretended to be studying; and some schools were careless with the passwords and access rights of their teaching platforms, so that online classes in several places were broken into, the intruders making trouble in the lesson, swearing, even playing funeral music… you can find a great deal of news like this online.

但是，这些现象并不能说明线上学习是不好的，不能说明线上学习是洪水猛兽。相对线下学习来说，线上学习有其无法取代的优势、作用和地位，也有它与生俱来的局限和短板；同样，线下学习也有自己的优势和劣势。咱们不能因为学不好，就怪罪到学习方式上去，否则就好像一个老写错别字的学生，把责任推到写字的笔上。对于我们来说，扬长避短才是更有意义的做法。这也是为什么我们必须掌握在线学习——只有这样，我们才能更好地知晓它的优势和局限。

But none of these things shows that online learning is bad, or that it is a monster to be fled from (洪水猛兽, literally “floods and fierce beasts”). Set beside learning face to face, online learning has advantages, uses and a standing that nothing can replace, and it has limits and weak points it was born with; face-to-face learning, equally, has its own strengths and weaknesses. We cannot blame the way of learning because the learning went badly — that would be like a student who keeps writing the wrong characters blaming the pen. The more meaningful course for us is to play to the strengths and work around the weaknesses. That is also why we must master online learning: only so can we know its advantages and its limits properly.

国际教育技术协会（International Society for Technology in Education，简称 ISTE）对我们这个时代的公民数码素养（Digital Citizenship）进行了描述：从最基本的第一级——人人都应享有的“信息知情权”，到第九级——主动防护可能来自数码世界的生理和心理危机，逐级递增地提出了一个合格公民的数码素养要求。

The International Society for Technology in Education (ISTE) has described the digital citizenship of the citizens of our era: from the most basic first level — the right to be informed, which everyone should enjoy — up to the ninth, actively guarding against the physical and psychological harms that can come out of the digital world, it sets out level by level what digital literacy a competent citizen requires.

在线学习，不仅应该是 K12 在校学生的必修课；对于学生家长、教师和所有的公民，特别是希望通过在线学习获得发展、实现升学梦的你来说，它都是必须掌握的基本素养。更进一步说，任何人，只要没有脱离现实社会，就必须会在线学习，因为这不仅涉及学习效果、生活质量，甚至涉及生命的尊严。

Online learning should be more than a required course for K–12 students at school; for parents, for teachers and for every citizen — and especially for you, who hope to develop through online learning and reach the school you are aiming for — it is a basic literacy that has to be mastered. To put it more strongly: anyone who has not withdrawn from real society must be able to learn online, because what is at stake is not only how well you learn and how well you live, but even the dignity of a life.

动动手指，真经就来

Move a finger, and the scriptures arrive

《西游记》是一个大家都耳熟能详的故事，本质上，它讲的是一个信息流动极其困难的故事。大家可以想一想，师徒四人历经九九八十一难去取经，这部真经难取、难求，还难以保存。而现在，我们想在中国获取来自印度或者世界各地的信息，动动手指头就可以完成了。

Journey to the West (《西游记》) is a story everyone knows by heart. At bottom it is a story about how desperately hard it once was for information to move. Think about it: the master and his three disciples endured eighty-one ordeals to fetch the true scriptures — scriptures hard to fetch, hard to obtain, and hard to keep. Today, if we want information from India or from anywhere else in the world, moving a finger is enough.

比如说，有一天，我从网上看到了这样几个非常有意思的健康公式：（第 1 条）幸福 = 健康 + 财富 + 良好的人际关系（信誉）。（第 2 条）健康 = 锻炼 + 饮食 + 睡眠。（第 3 条）锻炼 = 高强度耐力训练 + 体育运动 + 休息。

One day, for instance, I came across these very interesting health formulas online: (1) Happiness = health + wealth + good relationships (reputation). (2) Health = exercise + diet + sleep. (3) Exercise = high-intensity endurance training + sport + rest.

我很想知道这些公式是谁写的，立即上网检索，发现它的作者是印度裔美国人纳瓦尔·拉维坎特（Naval Ravikant），这些句子来自他的书《纳瓦尔宝典》。后来我找到了他的个人网站，那里不仅提供全书文本的免费下载，还可以找到免费的英文朗读音频文件。你看，这就是线上学习给我们提供的便利。

I badly wanted to know who had written them, searched at once, and found that the author is the Indian-American Naval Ravikant and that the lines come from his book The Almanack of Naval Ravikant. Later I found his personal website, which offers not only the full text of the book as a free download but also free English audio readings of it. There you are — this is the convenience that online learning gives us.

再比如说，我的硕士学位就是在线上读的，从入学活动到毕业典礼，百分之百在线完成。如果你是一个擅长在线学习的中学生，你也可以选择在中国足不出户，注册、入读国外的优质在线学校，获得毕业文凭，比如美国的斯坦福在线高中、日本的 N/S 高中等等。

Or take this: my own master's degree was read online, a hundred per cent of it, from the welcome activities to the graduation ceremony. If you are a secondary-school student who is good at learning online, you too can enrol in a good overseas online school and earn its diploma without leaving China — Stanford Online High School in the United States, or the N/S High Schools in Japan, for example.

当然了，如果精力允许，你甚至可以在国内读中学的同时，修读国外中学的学位。只要你能协调好自己的时间和活动，按要求完成学习任务，就能在同一段时间里入读两所甚至更多所不同的学校——比如一所中国的，一所美国的——在毕业之际同时从这些学校毕业，同时获得它们的毕业证书。

And of course, if your energy allows, you can even read for an overseas secondary-school qualification while attending secondary school here at home. As long as you can coordinate your own time and activities and finish the required work, you can be enrolled in two schools at once, or more — one Chinese, one American, say — graduate from them at the same time, and receive both diplomas.

目前，高等教育阶段成熟而优质的在线学习项目就更多了。有一所神秘的大学叫做密涅瓦大学，它的录取率比哈佛大学、耶鲁大学还低。想要申请这样的顶尖创新型大学去读本科，你必须非常优秀，同时还要擅长在线学习。因为密涅瓦大学是一所在线大学，没有固定的校舍和图书馆，学生大学四年要在全世界七个城市轮转学习：美国的旧金山、德国的柏林、阿根廷的布宜诺斯艾利斯、印度的孟买、中国的香港、英国的伦敦和美国的纽约。学生将像当地人一样居住、生活和学习，一边进行城市探索，一边完成学业。对密涅瓦大学的学生来说，“世界是校园，城市即教室”。同样的四年时间，密涅瓦大学的毕业生要比传统大学的学生多出许多在不同国家和城市生活与工作的经历。你猜猜，哪种学校的毕业生会更适合这个多元又多变的世界呢？

At the higher-education stage there are more mature, high-quality online programmes still. There is a mysterious university called Minerva, whose admission rate is lower than Harvard's or Yale's. To apply to a top innovative university like that as an undergraduate you must be outstanding, and good at learning online as well — because Minerva is an online university with no fixed campus and no library of its own; over four years its students rotate through seven cities around the world: San Francisco in the United States, Berlin in Germany, Buenos Aires in Argentina, Mumbai in India, Hong Kong in China, London in the United Kingdom, and New York in the United States. The students live, settle and study as local people do, exploring the city while completing their degree. For a Minerva student, “the world is the campus and the city is the classroom.” In the same four years, a Minerva graduate accumulates far more experience of living and working in different countries and cities than a student at a traditional university. Which kind of graduate, do you suppose, suits a world as varied and as changeable as this one?

在线学习带来的挑战：“马太效应”

The challenge online learning brings: the “Matthew effect”

在线学习在奖励会学习、爱学习的人的同时，也为整个社会带来了巨大的挑战。最初，人们期待线上学习可以用来弥合教育发达地区与教育欠发达地区之间的差距：原来偏远山区或经济欠发达地区，可能得不到好的师资，没有好的课程资源，现在我们通过线上就有可能把优质的课程和学习资料送过去。想象中，这样的线上教育支持，应该会为教育欠发达地区的学生带来良好的结果吧？可实际上却发现，线上学习的发展，非但没有缩减个体之间的教育差距，反而使这个差距变得更大了。为什么呢？我们再深思一下，可能就能理解了：有了线上学习的加持，想学的人学得更好了；而那些不想学习的人，即使面前优质的学习资源唾手可得，也总有不想学习的借口。

While online learning rewards those who know how to learn and love to learn, it also brings the whole of society an enormous challenge. At first people expected online learning to close the gap between well-provided and under-provided places: a remote mountain area or an economically weaker region might have no good teachers and no good course materials, and now we could send excellent courses and study materials there over the network. In the imagination, that kind of online support ought to have produced good results for students in the under-served places — oughtn't it? What we actually found was that as online learning grew, the educational gap between individuals did not shrink; it grew wider. Why? Think about it a little harder and it becomes understandable: with online learning behind them, the people who want to learn learn better; while the people who do not want to learn always have an excuse not to, even with excellent learning resources within arm's reach.

还有一种可能的原因：偏远山区和经济欠发达地区的学生，在互联网联通之前，空闲的时间可能就用来踢踢球、爬爬树、在户外跑一跑，虽然在学习能力的发展上有很明显的局限，但最起码身体锻炼得挺健康。现在有了网络，你给他送来教育资源的同时，他也能够玩网络游戏、看剧、刷短视频了。这样导致的结果就是：原来不想学习的，有了线上教育，还是不学习，依然很差；而那些想学习的和会学习的人，因为有了线上教育，则学得越来越好、越来越多。教育原本就欠发达地区的学生，因为不善于线上学习，注意力大多没有放在线上学习的资源上，这使得个体之间的学习差距拉得更大了。这就是线上学习所导致的“马太效应”：线上学习，拉大了个体之间的学习差距。

There is another possible reason. Before the internet reached them, students in remote mountain areas and economically weaker regions may have spent their free time kicking a ball about, climbing trees, running around outdoors; their chances to develop as learners were plainly limited, but at least their bodies were exercised and healthy. Now the network is there, and the same connection that brings them educational resources also lets them play online games, watch drama series and scroll through short videos. The result is this: those who did not want to study still do not study now that online education has arrived, and remain far behind; while those who want to learn and know how to learn, now that online education has arrived, learn better and learn more. Students in the places that were already under-served, not being good at learning online, mostly do not put their attention on the online learning resources — and so the learning gap between individuals is pulled wider still. This is the “Matthew effect” that online learning produces: online learning widens the learning gap between individuals.

在线学习的潜在危险

The hidden dangers of learning online

最后我想说，你必须会在线学习的另一个重要原因是：在线学习也存在潜在的危险。

Finally I want to say that another important reason you must be able to learn online is that online learning carries dangers of its own.

想象一下，你要去往一个陌生的世界，那里的人们使用着你不熟悉的语言，有着独特的文化和生活风俗。在你到达之前，如果对这个陌生世界的细节了解得越多，知道哪里有机会、哪里有风险，那么在你到达之后，所产生的尴尬和不方便，甚至是不安全，也会相应少一些。如果这个要出远门的人是你的孩子——一个未成年人，作为家长，你真的完全放心让孩子不做任何准备，就只身踏入一个陌生而危机四伏的世界吗？

Imagine you are going to an unfamiliar world, where people speak a language you do not know and have a culture and customs of their own. The more you know about the details of that world before you arrive — where the opportunities are, where the risks are — the less embarrassment, inconvenience and outright danger you will meet once you get there. And if the person setting out on that long journey is your child, a minor: as a parent, would you really be entirely at ease letting them step alone into a strange world thick with hazards, with no preparation at all?

“数码世界”对大多数家长来说，就是这样一个陌生国度——那里使用着网络语言，运行着程序，也隐藏着病毒；后门软件和钓鱼网站荆棘丛生在隐秘的角落；网络诈骗、色情、暴力及暗网空间，也许只与你的网购生活有着一“键”之隔。你对数码世界了解得足够全面和深入吗？如果你的孩子——一个未成年人——踽踽独行在这个资源丰饶但又危机四伏的国度，你真的放心吗？要想健康地进行在线学习，就需要避免这些潜在的危险和伤害，这样我们才能更安全地进行线上学习。而对于正在学习的青少年来说，如何更好地驾驭数字化学习技术，让自己的学习效率得到倍增，是我们需要认真面对的课题。

For most parents the “digital world” is exactly such a foreign country — a place with its own internet language, running its programs and hiding its viruses; where backdoor software and phishing sites grow like brambles in the unlit corners; where online fraud, pornography, violence and the dark web may be one “key” away from your online shopping. Do you know the digital world widely and deeply enough? If your child — a minor — walks that country alone, rich in resources and thick with hazards, are you truly at ease? To learn online in a healthy way we have to keep clear of these potential dangers and harms; only then is our online learning safer. And for the young people who are studying now, how to take better command of the technologies of digital learning, so that the efficiency of your own learning multiplies, is a question we need to face seriously.
