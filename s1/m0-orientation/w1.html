3 · 材料3 · Materials

课程介绍（教师的话，约 3 分钟）Course intro (from the instructor, ~3 min)

课程介绍 · 文字版Course intro · text version

你好，欢迎来到“数字化学习”。我是段玉佩，这门课的教师。先说三件事。

Hello, and welcome to Digital Learning. I'm Yupei Duan, the instructor. Three things first.

第一，这是一门什么样的课。它是公开的、异步的自学课程：没有上课时间，每个教学周有一张页面，页面本身就是这一周的学习——目标、材料、任务、量规、自评清单、自测题、反思提示，全在页内。两个学期，各 16 周。每周不超过 2 小时。

First, what kind of course this is. A public, asynchronous self-study course: no class meetings; each teaching week has one page, and the page IS that week's learning — objectives, materials, task, rubric, self-review list, self-quiz, reflection prompt, all on the page. Two semesters of 16 weeks. No more than 2 hours a week.

第二，你的作业本是你自己的网站。第 3 周你会建一个个人网站——关于我、学习日志、作品集三页。此后每周的作品发布在网站上，每周写三行日志。在校学生把链接登记给导师，导师按量规计分、邮件反馈；公开学习者用同一张量规自评。

Second, your notebook is your own website. In Week 3 you build a personal website — About me, Learning log, Portfolio. From then on every week's work is published there, with a three-line log. Enrolled students register the link with a mentor, who scores by rubric and replies by email; open learners self-score with the same rubric.

第三，这门课怎么对待 AI。多数作业允许 AI 辅助，前提是在结尾写两三句“AI 使用说明”；完全不用 AI 同样可以拿满分。标注“独立”的任务不用 AI。把 AI 做的当作自己的交上来，是作弊。怎样把 AI 用好，第二学期我们会专门研究。

Third, how this course treats AI. Most work allows AI assistance, provided you end with a two–three-sentence AI use note; full marks are equally reachable without AI. Tasks marked Independent use no AI. Submitting AI's work as your own is cheating. How to use AI well is something we study properly in Semester 2.

这一周你只需要做一件事：走完入门四步，然后写下——你为什么学这门课。别急，我们一步一步来。

This week you need to do one thing: walk the four-step map, then write down why you are taking this course. No hurry — one step at a time.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我不是在校学生，能上这门课吗？I'm not an enrolled student. Can I take this course?

能。任何人都可以作为公开学习者按周自学：同一页面、同一量规，自己评分；期末有可打印的“学习完成声明”。你不需要导师，也不需要登记链接。

Yes. Anyone may follow week by week as an open learner: same pages, same rubrics, self-scored; a printable completion statement at the end. You need no mentor and register no links.

这周没有作品要发布，那“任务”是什么？Nothing to publish this week — so what is the task?

把账号和设备准备好，写下“我为什么学这门课”并保存在自己电脑里。第 3 周建站后它会搬到网站上。

Get your accounts and devices ready, write “Why am I taking this course?” and keep it on your computer. It moves to your website in Week 3.

我没有电子邮箱怎么办？I don't have an email address.

第 4 步页面有申请方法：选一个在你所在的地方能正常收发、只需手机或邮箱验证的邮箱服务（在中国大陆请选大陆可用的），14 岁以下请家长陪同注册。第 2 周开始每周都要用它。

The Step 4 page shows how: pick an email service that works where you are (in mainland China, one that is reachable there), with a parent alongside if you are under 14. You will use it every week from Week 2.

页面显示的语言不对，怎么切换？The page opened in the wrong language. How do I switch?

页面按你浏览器的语言自动选择中文或英文。点右上角的“English / 中文”按钮可以切换，选择会保存在你自己的浏览器里，以后打开都按你的选择。

Pages follow your browser's language, Chinese or English. Press the “English / 中文” button at the top right to switch; the choice is saved in your own browser and wins from then on.
