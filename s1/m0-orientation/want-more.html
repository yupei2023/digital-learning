想学更多？Want more?

下面的资源为本单元精选，全部免费。带 待实测 标记的链接在开课前会逐一实测（其中包括从中国大陆的网络测一遍）；在你那里打不开，请邮件告诉我们。本学期不含人工智能主题的资源。

Free extensions for this module. Links marked to verify are access-tested one by one before launch, including from a mainland-China network; tell us if one fails where you are. No AI-themed resources this semester.

读Read

  • 读本第 00 篇《发刊词》——读本作者为什么写这本书。第 01 章读完还想读，就从这里开始。Reader, piece 00 “Opening words” — why the author wrote the reader. If Ch. 01 left you wanting more, start here. [placeholder: 发刊词是否放入读本页 whether the opening piece joins the reader page]
  • 《中华人民共和国数据安全法》——第 01 章提到的法律，原文可在国家法律法规数据库检索；只读第一章即可。The Data Security Law of the PRC — the law Ch. 01 mentions; search the national laws database and read Chapter 1 only. 待实测to verify

看Watch

  • 在 B 站搜索“在线学习 自律”或“Coursera 学习经验”，选一段 10 分钟以内、由学生自己讲的视频，看他们怎样安排一周的时间。Search Bilibili for “在线学习 自律” or “Coursera 学习经验”; pick a clip under 10 minutes told by a student, and watch how they plan their week.

做Do

  • Microsoft 数字素养（简体中文）：“使用电脑”“上网获取信息”两个模块，每个约 1.5 小时，自定进度，部分格式无需账号——给第 2–7 周的数字基本功热身。这门课也在外部课程菜单上；若你打算选它做主修，这里不必重复。Microsoft Digital Literacy (Simplified Chinese available): the “Work with computers” and “Access information online” modules, ~1.5 h each, self-paced, some formats need no account — a warm-up for Digital Basics in Weeks 2–7. It is also on the external course menu; skip if you plan to choose it as your core course. 待实测to verify
  • 一周时间记录：从今天起七天，每次打开手机或电脑“学东西”时记下开始和结束时间。周日数一数——你真实的“每周 2 小时”从哪里来？A one-week time log: for seven days, note the start and end time whenever you open a phone or computer “to learn something”. On Sunday, add it up — where do your real “2 hours a week” come from?
