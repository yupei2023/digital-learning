准备账号与设备Get set up

这一周不注册任何新平台——建站平台第 3 周才注册，外部课程第 6 周才试学。今天只做五件事，让下周的第一封邮件和后面十五周都顺畅。

No new platforms this week — the website builder comes in Week 3 and external courses in Week 6. Today, five things only, so that next week's first email and the fifteen weeks after it run smoothly.

  1. 一个能用的电子邮箱（10 分钟）A working email address (10 min)
    第 2 周起每周都要用。已有邮箱：登录一次，确认能收能发。没有邮箱：请家长帮你选一个家里常用、在你所在的地方能正常收发的邮箱服务申请，14 岁以下请家长陪同。建议用“英文名或化名 + 数字”做地址，不含真实姓名和生日。把地址写进你的记事本。Used every week from Week 2. Already have one: log in once and confirm you can send and receive. Don't: ask your family to help you sign up for a service they already use that works where you are, with a parent alongside if under 14. Use an English name or pseudonym plus digits as the address — no real name or birthday. Write the address in your notes.
  2. 设备与浏览器（5 分钟）Device and browser (5 min)
    在电脑上打开本页——作业建议在电脑上做。把浏览器更新到最新版（Chrome / Edge / Safari / Firefox 都行）。把课程网址加入书签，手机上也存一份。确认电脑或手机能录音、拍照。Open this page on a computer — do tasks on a computer. Update the browser to its latest version (Chrome / Edge / Safari / Firefox). Bookmark the course address, on your phone too. Check that the computer or phone can record audio and take photos.
  3. 固定两个学习时段（5 分钟）Fix two weekly study blocks (5 min)
    在日历、手机提醒或纸上写下每周固定的两个时段，各约 1 小时——比如“周二晚 7–8 点”“周六上午 10–11 点”。第 7 周你会学更好的日程工具；现在纸也行。In a calendar, a phone reminder or on paper, write two fixed weekly blocks of about an hour each — e.g., “Tuesday 7–8 pm”, “Saturday 10–11 am”. Week 7 teaches better scheduling tools; paper is fine for now.
  4. 告诉家长（3 分钟）Tell your family (3 min)
    和家里人说一句：这学期你在上一门在线课，每周约 2 小时；第 3 周会用邮箱注册一个建站平台，第 6 周会注册一两个外部课程平台。在校学生的家长会收到开课信。Tell your family: you are taking an online course this semester, about 2 hours a week; in Week 3 you will register a website builder with an email address, and in Week 6 one or two external course platforms. Enrolled students' families receive the welcome letter. [placeholder: 开课信 welcome letter]
  5. （在校学生）找到你的导师（2 分钟）(Enrolled) Find your mentor (2 min)
    在开课信里找到导师的姓名与邮箱，存进邮箱通讯录。第 2 周的第一封邮件就是发给导师的。登记表的网址也在开课信里。Find your mentor's name and email in the welcome letter and save them in your contacts. Week 2's first email goes to your mentor. The tracking-sheet address is in the letter too. [placeholder: 导师姓名与邮箱见开课信 mentor names & emails from the welcome letter] [placeholder: 登记表网址 tracking-sheet URL]
这一步的“证据”The “evidence” for this step
不需要截图、不需要提交。回到第 1 周页面，勾自评清单第 7 条即可——它只保存在你自己的浏览器里。第 5 周（B4）会教你怎样为这些账号做安全设置。No screenshot, nothing to submit. Go back to the Week 1 page and tick item 7 of the self-review — it is saved only in your browser. Week 5 (B4) teaches how to secure these accounts.
