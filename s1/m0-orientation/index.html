绪论：这是一门什么样的课？Orientation: What kind of course is this?

不计分Ungraded🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min一周One week

欢迎你。这门课没有教室，没有上课时间，也没有讨论区——只有一张又一张页面，和你自己。听起来有点孤单？其实不是：每一页都是为“一个人也能学完”写的，而且有两位导师每周看着在校同学的进度。这一周不学新东西，只做一件事：搞清楚这门课怎么上，然后告诉自己，为什么要上它。

Welcome. This course has no classroom, no class time and no forum — only a series of pages, and you. Sounds lonely? It isn't: every page is written so that one person can finish it alone, and two mentors watch enrolled students' progress every week. This week teaches nothing new. It does one thing: work out how this course runs — and tell yourself why you are taking it.

核心问题Essential questions

  • 这是一门什么样的课？What kind of course is this?
  • 我为什么学它？Why am I here?
  • 我准备好一个人在线学习了吗？Am I ready to learn online on my own?

学完本单元，你应能By the end of this module you should be able to

  1. 说出这门课怎么运行——每周一页、周一开放周日发布、每周不超过 2 小时、没有迟交处罚但有建议节奏——以及遇到困难时从哪里获得帮助。Describe how this course runs — one page a week, open Monday and publish by Sunday, no more than 2 hours a week, no late penalty but a suggested pace — and where to get help when stuck.
  2. 说出三种 AI 标注（🚫 独立 / ✅ 辅助 / 🔬 实验）的含义与使用 AI 的三条底线，并知道去哪里查看课程认可的工具清单。Explain the three AI designations (🚫 Independent / ✅ Assisted / 🔬 Experiment) and the three hard rules for using AI, and know where the course's approved-tool list lives.
  3. 走完入门四步（认识教师 → 关于本课程与 AI → 读课程说明 → 准备账号与设备），并写下“我为什么学这门课”。Complete the four-step map (Meet the instructor → About this course and AI → Read the course guide → Get set up) and write “Why am I taking this course?”.

这三条也是本单元量规的三行——本单元不计分，量规用来自查。These three are also the three rows of the module rubric — the module is ungraded; the rubric is for self-checking. → 量规Rubric

怎么开始How to start

  1. 打开 第 1 周页面，从头到尾按顺序做——页面本身就是这一周的学习。Open the Week 1 page and work through it in order — the page IS the week's learning.
  2. 四步各有一页，每页末尾都会带你回到第 1 周页面。走完四步，勾自评清单。Each of the four steps has its own page and sends you back to the Week 1 page. After all four, tick the self-review list.
  3. 周日前写下“我为什么学这门课”，先保存在自己的电脑里——第 3 周建好网站后，它会成为你“关于我”页的第一段。在校学生这周不需要登记任何链接。By Sunday, write “Why am I taking this course?” and keep it on your own computer — in Week 3, once your website exists, it becomes the first paragraph of your About-me page. Enrolled students register no link this week.
