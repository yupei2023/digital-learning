认识教师：一个比你早开始学习的学生Meet the instructor: a student who started learning earlier

大家好，我是段玉佩。如果把我十二岁时的照片和现在的照片放在一起，你会看到左边是个学生、右边是名教师。但很多人容易忽略的是：右边这个教师，其实同时仍然是一名学生。我只是比你老一些，早学了一些内容，多一些经验和教训，仅此而已。

Hello, I'm Yupei Duan. Put a photo of me at twelve next to one from today and you see a student on the left and a teacher on the right. What people tend to miss is that the teacher on the right is still a student. I am just a little older than you; I learned some things earlier and collected a few more lessons — that is all.

韩愈说：“闻道有先后，术业有专攻。”所以，老师只不过是更早开始学习的学生。推论一下：如果某个领域你比我更早了解、更精通，那么在那个领域，你就是我的老师。这门课上，我非常期待这种时刻。

Han Yu wrote: “One hears the Way earlier or later; each has their own specialty.” So a teacher is just a student who started learning earlier. It follows that if you know some field earlier and better than I do, then in that field you are my teacher. I look forward to those moments in this course.

三十多个学年Thirty-odd school years

和你在这里见面，已经是我个人的第三十多个学年——从小学一年级算起。大学毕业后，我在北京的一所公立中学做了十年生物老师；然后进入一所民办学校负责学术管理和课程研发；带着十六年公立与民办学校的教学经验，我开始了在美国的博士研究，方向是教育场景中的人机交互。说起数字化学习，它既是我的研究主题，也是我三十多年既当学生又做教师一直在想的问题：怎样才能用电脑和互联网，让我们的学习变得更好？希望这一学年结束时，你和我都有自己更好的答案。

Meeting you here is my thirtieth-something school year, counting from the first grade. After university I taught biology for ten years at a public secondary school in Beijing, then moved to a private school to lead academic management and curriculum design. With sixteen years of teaching behind me I began doctoral research in the United States on human–computer interaction in education. Digital learning is both my research topic and the question I have carried through thirty years as student and teacher: how can computers and the Internet make our learning better? By the end of this year I hope you and I both have a better answer.

一份不完美的作品集An imperfect portfolio

2020 年初，我在美国东部出差，结束后和家人在波士顿会合，准备过完春节回国。那年春天你们都知道发生了什么——航班取消，回不去了。我的硕士课程本来就是完全在线的，于是白天陪家人沿东海岸旅行，晚上等他们睡了，打开电脑异步学习。那门课的四个项目，我在四座城市完成：第一个项目的照片拍摄于波士顿科学博物馆；第二个项目的录音，是在纽约一家旅馆安静的走廊里用手机录的；第三个项目的讲解视频，录在费城租住的房间里；最终项目在华盛顿特区整合完成。

In early 2020 I was on a work trip on the US east coast, then joined my family in Boston for Spring Festival before flying home. You all know what happened that spring — flights were cancelled, and we could not return. My master's programme happened to be fully online, so by day I travelled the coast with my family, and at night, once they slept, I opened my laptop and learned asynchronously. The course's four projects were finished in four cities: the first project's photo was taken at the Boston Museum of Science; the second project's audio was recorded on my phone in a quiet hotel corridor in New York; the third project's explainer video in a rented room in Philadelphia; the final project was assembled in Washington, D.C.

那份作品集有很多瑕疵，非常不完美，但它非常真实：老师在学习新东西的时候，也会经历笨拙的阶段。任何人进入一个新领域，总会经历这样的笨拙。没有人天生就会。

That portfolio has many flaws — it is far from perfect, but it is honest: a teacher learning something new goes through a clumsy stage too. Anyone entering a new field does. Nobody is born knowing.

分数是底线，故事是上限Scores are the floor; the story is the ceiling

我们学习，不只是为了一纸证书；一门课，也不只是为了一个好分数。分数和证书只能说明你对某个领域掌握程度的底线；而你在学习过程中收获的知识、能力、经验、感受，和经历的所有故事，才共同构成你数字化学习能力的上限。这门课上不封顶——海阔凭鱼跃，天高任鸟飞。

We do not learn only for a certificate, and a course is not only for a good score. Scores and certificates mark the floor of what you know about a field; the knowledge, skills, experiences, feelings and stories you collect along the way together form the ceiling of what you can do as a digital learner. This course has no ceiling — the sea is wide for the fish to leap, the sky high for the bird to fly.

我们怎么联系How we stay in touch
这门课没有上课时间，我们大多数时候靠邮件：邮件教师或导师，24–48 小时内回复（节假日除外）；也可以预约一对一视频。在校学生的导师姓名与邮箱在开课信里；教师的邮箱也在开课信里，不写在公开页面上。There is no class time, so most of our contact is by email: write to the instructor or your mentor, and expect a reply within 24–48 hours (holidays excepted); a one-to-one video call can be booked. Enrolled students find their mentor's name and email in the welcome letter; the instructor's address is there too, not on any public page.
