关于本课程与 AI：规范、工具、安全About this course and AI: rules, tools, safety

这门课允许你在多数作业中使用 AI 辅助学习。如何把 AI 用好，本身就是一门学问——那是第二学期第 8 单元的主题，到时我们会亲手把它研究清楚。在那之前，请先遵守下面的规范。

This course allows AI assistance on most of your work. Using AI well is a craft of its own — that is what Module 8 in Semester 2 is about, and we will figure it out hands-on when we get there. Until then, follow the rules below.

AI 使用说明怎么写How to write the AI use note

两三句话，放在作业结尾：用了什么工具、它帮了什么忙。没用就写一句“本文未使用 AI”。例如：“我用 ____ 检查了第三段的语法，采纳了两处改动；观点和例子都是我自己的。”

Two or three sentences at the end of the work: which tool, and what it helped with. If you used none, write “No AI was used.” For example: “I used ____ to check the grammar of paragraph three and accepted two changes; the ideas and examples are my own.”

三条底线Three hard rules

  1. 把 AI 生成的内容当作自己的作业直接提交，属于作弊（见课程说明的“学术诚信”）。Submitting AI-generated content as your own work is cheating (see Academic Integrity in the course guide).
  2. AI 会一本正经地出错（“幻觉”）。凡是事实性内容，至少用一个可靠来源核实后再使用。AI can be confidently wrong (“hallucination”). Verify any factual content against at least one reliable source before using it.
  3. 不向 AI 工具输入自己或他人的个人信息；遵守所用工具的年龄条款（14 岁以下使用需监护人同意）。Never enter your own or anyone else's personal information into an AI tool; follow each tool's age terms (guardian consent under 14).

工具与公平Tools and fairness

  • 除第 8 单元的指定任务外，本课程任何作业都不强制使用 AI。Outside Module 8's designated tasks, nothing in this course requires AI.
  • 翻译工具（如 DeepL、百度、有道、腾讯翻译）不视为生成式 AI；第 4 周会教你怎样正确地用它们。Translation tools (DeepL, Baidu, Youdao, Tencent Translate) do not count as generative AI; Week 4 teaches how to use them well.
  • 家中不便使用 AI 的在校同学，可申请在校完成第 8 单元任务。Enrolled students without AI access at home may complete Module 8 on campus.

安全提示Safety notes

  • 注册任何 AI 工具前先告诉家长；用课程邮箱注册，不绑定手机号，不填学校全名。Tell your family before registering any AI tool; sign up with your course email, no phone number, no full school name.
  • 不把密码、身份证号、家庭住址、照片放进对话框。Never put passwords, ID numbers, home address or photos into a chat box.
  • AI 给出的链接、数据、引文都可能是编造的——点开核实，再引用。Links, figures and quotations from AI may be invented — open and verify before citing.
  • 遇到让你不舒服的内容，关掉对话，告诉家长或导师。If something makes you uncomfortable, close the chat and tell a parent or your mentor.
这周你不需要用任何 AI 工具。You do not need any AI tool this week. 第一学期的重点是学会学习和数字基本功；关于 AI，先记住三种标注和三条底线就够了。Semester 1 is about learning to learn and digital basics; for AI, the three designations and three hard rules are all you need for now.
