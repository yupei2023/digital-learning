10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我凑不齐 5 件怎么办？I can't get to five entries.

先把“作品”想宽一点：计分任务之外，三行日志的对比截图（第一条 vs 最新一条）、外部课程的学习记录、B 周的邮件 / 检索记录 / 工作台导览，都可以作为一条。真的缺哪一周的，如实写“这件当时没做完 / 没做”，把它列成一条也行——“没做”配上一句现在的看法，同样是成长的证据。

Widen what counts as a work: beyond the graded tasks, a before/after screenshot of your logs (first entry vs. latest), your external-course learning record, or a Basics-week email / search record / workspace tour can each be an entry. If something never got finished, say so honestly and list it anyway — “didn't finish” plus how you see it now is also evidence of growth.

某件作品的链接坏了 / 当时的平台打不开。A link is broken / the platform won't open.

先在网站编辑器里找回原页面重新发布；找不回就放当时的截图或文件，并在成长说明第一句写明“原链接已失效，此处为截图”。评分看的是作品与说明，不为坏链接扣分——但要说明。

Try re-publishing the original page from your site editor; failing that, post a screenshot or the file, and open the note with “the original link is gone; this is a screenshot”. Scoring looks at the work and the note — a dead link costs nothing if you say so.

可以放课程之外的作品吗？May I include work from outside this course?

可以放一件（比如你自己业余做的学习类小项目），前提：与“学习”有关、是你自己做的、符合隐私与版权规则。课程内的作品仍要覆盖至少三个单元。

One is fine (say, a learning-related side project of your own), provided it is about learning, made by you, and privacy- and copyright-clean. Course work must still cover at least three modules.

作品集要公开给所有人看吗？Does the portfolio go fully public?

不要求。默认“仅链接可见”：网站保持不被搜索收录，链接只给导师（和你愿意给的人）。第二学期末的作品集 v2 才有“可选公开发布”的选项，而且需要家长书面同意、使用化名。

Not required. The default is link-only: the site stays out of search engines and the link goes to your mentor (and anyone you choose). Only Portfolio v2, at the end of Semester 2, has an optional public release — with written parent consent and a pseudonym.

AI 可以帮我写成长说明吗？Can AI help write the growth notes?

可以辅助文字（改通顺、查错字），附 AI 使用说明。但“当时卡在哪”和“现在怎么看”只有你知道——AI 写出来的那种说明，正是跟做活动里“还差一步”的样子。下周的学期反思则完全不用 AI，包括润色。

For wording, yes (smoothing, typo-checking), with a use note. But only you know where you got stuck then and how you see it now — an AI-written note reads exactly like the activity's “one step short” cards. Next week's reflection uses no AI at all, polishing included.
