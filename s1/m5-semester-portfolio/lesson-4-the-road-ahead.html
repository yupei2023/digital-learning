路与下一步：证书、寒假、第二学期The road ahead: certificates, the break, Semester 2

《路》的结尾说，网“路”可以让所有的“畏途”变为“通途”。第一学期的路到这周就走完了。这一课把收尾的几件事说清楚：分数怎么变成证书，寒假可以做什么，第二学期从哪里开始。

“The Roads” ends by saying the net-road can turn every road of dread into a road of passage. Semester 1's road ends this week. This lesson settles the closing matters: how points become certificates, what the break can hold, and where Semester 2 begins.

寒假：一切都是选做The break: everything optional

寒假没有任何计分。想学，下面这些都在那儿等你；只想休息，休息就是打算。Nothing over the break is graded. If you feel like it, these are waiting; if you'd rather rest, resting is a plan.

  • 主修外部课程：按你 M3 的日程继续（≤ 3 小时的选做部分）。第一次学的时候，记得用上 M4 的小作品——你在成长说明里写过打算怎么用它。The core external course: continue on your M3 schedule (the optional ≤ 3 hours). In your first session, use your M4 artifact — your growth note says how.
  • M4 的第二件作品：换一种做——做过导图的做闪卡，做过闪卡的录两分钟。作品集 v2 的候选。M4's second artifact: make the other kind — cards if you mapped, two minutes of video if you carded. A candidate for Portfolio v2.
  • 录“我最推荐的一个学习工具”（见下）。Record “the one learning tool I'd recommend” (below).

选做 · 我最推荐的一个学习工具（2 分钟录音或录屏）Optional · The one learning tool I'd recommend (2 minutes, audio or screen)

这学期你试过的所有工具里，只许推荐一件。录 ≤ 2 分钟，四句结构：① 它是什么；② 我拿它做过什么（真事）；③ 一个真实发生的好处 + 一个卡点；④ 它适合什么样的人。不露脸、不露全名；发布到网站，作品集 v1 里加一条也行。不计分、不计时——纯粹因为：把工具讲给别人听，是检验你会不会用它的最快办法。Of every tool you tried this semester, recommend exactly one. Record ≤ 2 minutes in four sentences: ① what it is; ② what I actually did with it; ③ one real benefit + one real snag; ④ who it suits. No face, no full name; publish on your site — it may even join Portfolio v1. Ungraded and outside the budget — recommending a tool to someone else is simply the fastest test of whether you can use it.

这段录音写给谁听：同学与导师。学期收尾后，导师会把全班推荐的工具汇成一份「同学们的工具推荐」清单（只写化名、工具名、一句好处、一句卡点），发回给每一个人——第二学期你还会在「学习者推荐榜」上见到同样的做法。课程没有讨论区、不互访网站，这份清单就是你和同学之间真实的那条线。所以第 ④ 句“它适合什么样的人”值得多想一分钟：读到它的人，正在挑工具。Who this recording is for: your classmates and your mentor. After the semester closes, the mentors compile the cohort's picks into one “classmates' tool picks” list — pseudonym, tool name, one benefit, one snag — and send it back to everyone; Semester 2's learners' picks page works the same way. The course has no forum and no visiting each other's sites, so this list is the real line between you and your classmates. Which makes sentence ④ — who it suits — worth an extra minute: the people reading it are choosing a tool.

第二学期从哪里开始Where Semester 2 begins

第二学期第 1 周叫“重启”。第一件事就是重新打开你这本作品集 v1，挑出最能代表成长的一件，说明为什么；然后改版网站、写新学期的目标、汇报寒假主修课的进度（学了算进度，没学也算——如实就行）。也就是说：你这两周做的东西，就是下学期第一周的教材。把它做成你明年一月愿意重读的样子。

Semester 2's first week is called Restart. Its first act is re-opening this very Portfolio v1, picking the one work that best shows your growth, and saying why; then redesigning your site, setting new goals, and reporting your break progress on the core course (studying counts; not studying counts too — honesty is all). In other words: what you build these two weeks is the first textbook of next semester. Make it something January-you will want to re-read.
