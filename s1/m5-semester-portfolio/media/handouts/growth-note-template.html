成长说明三句模板The three-sentence growth note

每件入选作品配一则。三句各一到两行；“当时卡在哪”不许空着。M4 小作品那一条多答一问。

One note per selected work. One or two lines per sentence; “where I got stuck” may not be blank. The M4 artifact's note answers one extra question.

句Sentence写Write检查Check
条目名Entry name第 __ 周 · ____（任务名）· 链接 / 作品本身Week __ · ____ (task) · link / the work itself链接自己点开过一次。Clicked once yourself.
① 当时① Then当时我做了____，卡在____。I made ____, and got stuck on ____.卡点是真发生过的一步。The snag really happened.
② 现在② Now现在再看，____（我会改哪里 / 我怎么看它）。Looking again, ____ (what I'd change / how I see it).不是“收获很大”。Not “gained a lot”.
③ 证据③ Proof它证明我能____（一个具体能力）。It proves I can ____ (one specific ability).是能力，不是态度。An ability, not an attitude.
（仅 M4 小作品）(M4 artifact only)做完之后我用过它吗：____。寒假第一次学主修课时我打算____（再考一遍 / 补分支 / 重录）。Used since: ____. First break session, I will ____ (re-test / fill the branch / re-record).“没用过”也如实写。“Not yet” is fine — say so.
板块总起（一两句，写在 5–7 条之前）Section opener (one or two lines above the entries)
这学期的我，用一句话说：____。下面 __ 件作品，是这句话的证据。This semester's me, in one sentence: ____. The __ works below are that sentence's evidence.
（选写一行）放弃之辩(Optional line) The one you left out
我最后放弃的一件是____，没带它是因为____。——这个答案往往比入选理由更能说明你的标准。The work I finally left out is ____, because ____. — That answer often shows your standard better than any inclusion.
