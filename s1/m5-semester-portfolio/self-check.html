学习状态自测Learning self-check

这是你第 2 周做过的那份自测。它不是考试：没有对错，不计分，不影响证书；答案只在你自己的浏览器里显示，不会上传。它的用处只有一个——给第 16 周的你和第 2 周的你一把同样刻度的尺子。

This is the same self-check you took in Week 2. It is not a test: no right answers, no points, no effect on certificates; results display only in your own browser and are never uploaded. Its one purpose: to hand Week-16 you and Week-2 you the same ruler.

[placeholder] 正式题目待定[placeholder] The official items are not yet chosen
这份自测的正式题目（约 15–20 题）将在开课前确定并替换本页内容；第 2 周的基线页用同一份题。下面的 3 条样题只是示意格式：1–5 分自评，1 = 还做不到，5 = 能稳定做到并能教别人。The official items (about 15–20) will be finalised before launch and will replace this page's content; the Week-2 baseline page uses the same set. The 3 sample items below only show the format: self-rate 1–5, where 1 = can't yet and 5 = do it reliably and could teach it.

样题（示意）Sample items (format only)

  1. 面对一个真实的学习任务，我能从认得的工具里选出合适的一件，并说出选它、不选别件的理由。Given a real learning task, I can choose a suitable tool from those I know, and say why it — and why not the others.
  2. 我能为自己排出每周固定的学习时段，守不住的时候能找到原因并改一处。I can schedule fixed weekly study slots for myself, and when one collapses I can find the cause and change one thing.
  3. 学完一段内容，我能用自测（合上书考自己）查出自己哪里还没懂。After studying something, I can use self-testing (book closed) to find what I have not yet understood.

怎么用它How to use it

  1. 先按现在的真实状态逐项自评——不是“希望的自己”。Rate your actual current state, item by item — not the self you wish for.
  2. 找出第 2 周的基线记录（当时抄在草稿或笔记里的那份），两列并排抄进草稿。Dig out your Week-2 baseline (the copy you made in your draft or notes) and set the two columns side by side.
  3. 标出 ↑ 变化最大、→ 几乎没动、↓ 变低的项——这三个记号就是学期反思第 ①② 段的材料。Mark ↑ the biggest rise, → what barely moved, ↓ any drop — these marks feed parts ① and ② of the reflection.
  4. （在校学生）把两次结果表发给导师（邮件模板见任务 2 页第 5 步）；公开学习者不需要发给任何人。(Enrolled) Email the two-round table to your mentor (template in Task 2, step 5); open learners send it to no one.
第 2 周的记录找不到了？Lost your Week-2 record?
兜底办法：先按现在如实答一遍；再凭记忆估一份“第 2 周的我大概会怎么答”，在旁边注明“基线为回忆估计”。反思评的是对照的动作，不是数字的精确——如实标注就完全不影响评分。The fallback: answer honestly for now, then estimate from memory how your Week-2 self would have answered, marking it “baseline estimated from memory”. The reflection scores the act of contrasting, not numeric precision — honest labelling costs nothing.

说明：在校学生的自测结果经学校同意、家长知情、学生自愿提交导师，不影响成绩，与登记表分开保存；公开学习者的结果只在本地显示，课程不收集。Note: enrolled students' results are shared with mentors with school approval, parent awareness and the student's consent; they never affect grades and are stored apart from the tracking sheet. Open learners' results display locally only — the course collects nothing.
