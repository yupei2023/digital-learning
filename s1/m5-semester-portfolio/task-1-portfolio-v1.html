要做什么What to do

在你网站“作品集”页的顶部建一个“学期作品集 v1”板块：先写一两句总起（这学期的我，用一句话说），下面放 5–7 条。每条 = 作品名（第几周 · 哪个任务）+ 可打开的链接或作品本身 + 三句成长说明。这是第一学期的“总展台”——第二学期第 1 周你会重看它，第二学期末的 v2 会在它上面长出来。

At the top of your site's Portfolio page, build a “Semester Portfolio v1” section: one or two opening sentences (this semester's me, in one line), then 5–7 entries. Each entry = work name (week · task) + a working link or the work itself + the three-sentence growth note. This is Semester 1's exhibition table — Semester 2 opens by revisiting it, and v2 will grow out of it.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 5–7 条，覆盖至少三个不同单元（B 周也算一个“单元”）；建议至少一条是“过程”类证据（计划、微评、日志对比）。5–7 entries covering at least three different modules (the Basics weeks count as one); at least one “process” piece (a plan, a micro-review, a log contrast) is recommended.
  • 其中一条是外部课程：一段进度（学到哪了、四周守住了几次时段）+ 一件相关作品（可以就是 M4 小作品，注明“一件作品两个身份”即可；也可以是日志摘录、练习截图或徽章）。One entry is the external course: a progress paragraph (how far you got, how many slots you kept) + one related work (your M4 artifact is fine — note “one work, two roles”; or a log excerpt, an exercise screenshot, a badge).
  • 每条三句成长说明（当时 · 现在 · 证据）；M4 小作品那条另答“用过吗 / 寒假怎么用”。Three sentences per entry (then · now · proof); the M4 artifact's note also answers “used since / how over the break”.
  • 每个链接自己点开一次；坏了的链接换成截图并注明。Click every link once yourself; replace a dead one with a screenshot and say so.
  • 隐私照旧：化名、不露正脸、不写学校全名；网站保持不被搜索收录；默认仅链接可见。Privacy as always: pseudonym, no face photos, no full school name; the site stays no-index; link-only by default.
  • 用了 AI 改文字的，板块末尾附两三句 AI 使用说明（或写“本板块未使用 AI”）。If AI helped with wording, end the section with a two–three-sentence AI use note (or “No AI was used in this section”).

常见问题FAQ

总起那一句写不出来。I can't write the opening sentence.

先写完 5–7 条，再回头写总起——总起是从条目里长出来的，不是想出来的。实在写不出，就用最朴素的：“这学期我从____变成了____。”

Write the entries first and return for the opener — it grows out of the entries rather than being invented. If it still won't come, use the plainest form: “this semester I went from ____ to ____.”

同一件作品可以既当外部课程那条、又单独一条吗？Can one work be both the external-course entry and its own entry?

不要重复占两条。M4 小作品要么放在外部课程条目里（注明“一件作品两个身份”），要么单独一条、另找日志摘录当外部课程的“相关作品”。

Don't let it fill two slots. Either the M4 artifact sits inside the external-course entry (“one work, two roles”), or it stands alone and a log excerpt serves as the course's related work.

成长说明可以超过三句吗？May a note run past three sentences?

三句是下限也是骨架；每句拉长到两行没问题，但别写成小作文——七则小作文没人读得完，包括明年一月的你。

Three sentences are the floor and the skeleton; stretching each to two lines is fine, but no mini-essays — nobody reads seven of them, including January-you.
