M5 量规M5 Rubric

量规的三行就是本单元的三条目标。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。任务 2（第 ③ 行）评的是投入与形式——成长多少、方向如何，不评分。

The three rows are the module's three objectives. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence. Task 2 (row ③) is scored on engagement and form — the amount and direction of growth are never scored.

字数档位说明：第 ③ 行的两个档位是等工作量——中文 300–500 字，英文 200–350 词；用哪种语言写不影响评分。Length note: row ③'s two bands are equal in workload — 300–500 Chinese characters, or 200–350 English words; which language you write in never affects the score.

规范要求（不计分，但缺少则该任务记“证据不足”）Form requirements (a gate, not points — missing means “No evidence”)

  • 两项任务都发布在自己网站的“作品集”页，链接可打开；在校学生已登记链接（任务 1 = M5-1，第 15 周；任务 2 = M5-2，第 16 周）。Both tasks are published on the learner's Portfolio page and reachable; enrolled students registered the links (Task 1 = M5-1 in Week 15, Task 2 = M5-2 in Week 16).
  • 第 15、16 周的三行日志已写（本单元写回本课程）。The Week 15 and 16 three-line logs are written (about this course again).
  • 隐私照旧：化名、不露正脸、不写学校全名、不含他人可识别信息；网站保持不被搜索收录；作品集默认仅链接可见。Privacy as always: pseudonym, no face photos, no full school name, no identifiable third parties; the site stays no-index; the portfolio is link-only by default.
  • 任务 1 如用 AI 附使用说明；任务 2 全程未用 AI 并附“本文未使用 AI”。Task 1 carries an AI use note if AI helped; Task 2 used no AI and carries the “No AI was used” line.

自测结果表不在规范要求里：在校学生可自愿把两次结果表发给导师（自愿提交、不影响成绩）；不发不影响任何评分。The self-check table is not part of this gate: enrolled students may send the two-round table to their mentor voluntarily (it never affects grades); not sending it affects no score.

合成How the 20 points add up

任务 1 = 第 ① 行（6 分）+ 第 ② 行（6 分）= 12 分；任务 2 = 第 ③ 行（8 分）；单元合计 20 分，是第一学期的总结性评估。导师在登记表上分别记两项任务的分数与等级，并在一周内邮件反馈；本单元评完，导师同时算出学期总分（≥ 60 合格获学期证书，≥ 85 注明 With Distinction）。作品集与反思共用收尾日（见当年“本学期日历”页）；晚交不扣分，只推迟证书与反馈。Task 1 = row ① (6) + row ② (6) = 12; Task 2 = row ③ (8); 20 in total — Semester 1's summative assessment. Mentors record both tasks' scores and levels on the tracking sheet and email feedback within a week; with this module scored, the semester total follows (≥ 60 passes for the semester certificate; ≥ 85 adds With Distinction). The portfolio and reflection share the closing date on the per-year calendar page; lateness loses nothing and only delays the certificate and feedback.
