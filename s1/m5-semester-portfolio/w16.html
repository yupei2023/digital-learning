请从上到下按顺序做。这是第一学期的最后一周。你手里已经有一本作品集；这周先复测“学习状态自测”，把两次结果并排放好，再读一篇教师自己的示范——读本第 15 章《路》——然后写你自己的学期反思。Work top to bottom. This is the last week of Semester 1. The portfolio is in hand; this week you retake the learning self-check, set the two results side by side, read the instructor's own model — Reader Ch. 15, “The Roads” — and then write your own semester reflection.

复测完、动笔前，先在草稿里填三句——这三句就是反思的骨架：After the retake and before writing, fill three sentences in your draft — they are the reflection's skeleton:

  1. 两次自测里，变化最大的一项是____（第 2 周 __ 分 → 现在 __ 分）。The biggest change between the two checks is ____ (Week 2: __ → now: __).
  2. 作品集里能证明这个变化的一件作品是____，因为____。The portfolio work that proves it is ____, because ____.
  3. 几乎没变（或变低）的一项是____——我现在的解释是____。The item that barely moved (or dropped) is ____ — my explanation right now is ____.

第 2 句写不出来，说明第 1 句挑的项还太大——换一项更具体的。If sentence 2 won't come, sentence 1 is too broad — pick a more specific item.

✅ 核心要点（写完再看）✅ Key takeaways (open after writing)

作品集是挑出来的，不是堆出来的——放进去什么，说明你认为什么算成长；成长说明三句：当时 · 现在 · 证据；反思的每个判断都要有对照（两次自测）或证据（一件作品）；“没变”如实写出来，比“进步了很多”更有分量；作品集与反思是全课程唯一有收尾日的作业，晚交只影响证书时间；第二学期第 1 周，从重看这本作品集开始。

A portfolio is chosen, not piled — what goes in says what you count as growth. A growth note is three sentences: then · now · proof. Every claim in the reflection carries a contrast (two checks) or a piece of evidence (one work). An honest “no change” outweighs an empty “I grew a lot”. The portfolio and reflection carry the course's only firm date, and lateness only delays the certificate. And Semester 2's first week begins by re-opening this very portfolio.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我找不到第 2 周的基线记录了。I can't find my Week-2 baseline record.

先去当时保存的地方找：第 2 周做完自测时，页面会请你把结果用邮件发给自己（主题“我的第 2 周基线”）或抄进网站第一条日志——去邮箱搜这个主题、把网站日志翻到最早一条，多半能找回。真找不回，自测页有兜底办法：先按现在如实答一遍，再凭记忆估一份“第 2 周的我大概会怎么答”，在反思里写明“基线为回忆估计”。如实标注就不影响评分——评的是对照的动作，不是数字的精确。

First look where it was saved: when you finished the Week-2 check, the page asked you to email the result to yourself (subject “my Week-2 baseline”) or copy it into your site's first log entry — search your inbox for that subject and scroll to your earliest log. If it truly is gone, the self-check page has a fallback: answer honestly for now, then estimate from memory how your Week-2 self would have answered, and mark the baseline as “estimated from memory” in the reflection. Honest labelling costs nothing — the contrast is scored, not the precision.

复测的分数比第 2 周还低，正常吗？My retake scores lower than Week 2. Is that normal?

很常见，而且往往是好事：第 2 周你还不知道“会”有多少层，现在标准变高了。把这一项写进反思第 ② 段，解释你的标准怎么变的——这正是评分要看的“如实对照”。

Common — and often good news: in Week 2 you didn't yet know how many layers “can do” has, so your standard has risen. Put it in part ② and explain how the standard moved — exactly the honest contrast the rubric looks for.

反思可以让 AI 帮我润色或翻译吗？May AI polish or translate my reflection?

不可以——这是本学期唯一一项全程不用 AI 的任务，润色和翻译都算用。想用英文写就自己写，语言不完美不扣分；评分只看投入与形式。用了 AI 的反思按量规记 0，并按学术诚信规则处理。

No — this is the semester's one task with no AI at any step; polishing and translating both count. Write in English yourself if you wish — imperfect language costs nothing, since only engagement and form are scored. An AI-assisted reflection rates 0 under the rubric and falls under the integrity policy.

错过收尾日了怎么办？I missed the closing date.

照常交，并给导师发一封邮件说明。不扣分；只是证书和期末反馈要等下一批发放。

Hand it in anyway and email your mentor a line. No points lost; your certificate and feedback simply wait for the next batch.

公开学习者怎么拿证书？How do open learners get a certificate?

证书只发给在校学生（有导师按量规评分）。公开学习者用同一量规自评，然后打印“学习完成声明”——它如实记录你完成了什么、自评多少分，由你自己签名。

Certificates go to enrolled students (mentor-scored by rubric). Open learners self-score with the same rubrics and print the self-completion statement — an honest record of what you finished and how you scored yourself, signed by you.
