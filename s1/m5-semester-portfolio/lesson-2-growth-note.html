成长说明三句The three-sentence growth note

作品自己不会说话。同一张思维导图，配上“我做的导图”五个字是展品标签；配上三句成长说明，才是成长的证据。三句是固定的：当时 · 现在 · 证据。

A work cannot speak for itself. The same mind map captioned “my mind map” is a museum label; with a three-sentence growth note it becomes evidence of growth. The three sentences are fixed: then · now · proof.

固定三句The fixed three sentences

句Sentence写什么What goes there不算数的写法What doesn't count
① 当时① Then第几周、做了什么、卡在哪：“第 8 周录学习故事，录到第 5 遍才敢发出去。”Which week, what you did, where you got stuck: “Week 8, the learning story — it took five takes before I dared publish.”只有名字：“这是我的学习故事。”A name only: “this is my learning story.”
② 现在② Now现在再看它，哪里变了：“现在再听，我会把一半的‘然后’去掉——而且我现在敢一遍过。”What changed when you look again: “listening now, I'd cut half the ‘and then's — and today I'd record it in one take.”套话：“现在觉得收获很大。”Boilerplate: “now I feel I gained a lot.”
③ 证据③ Proof它证明我学会了什么——一个具体的能力：“它证明我能把一次经历讲成有条理的三分钟。”What it proves you can do — one specific ability: “it proves I can turn an experience into three orderly minutes.”“证明我很努力。”努力是过程，不是能力。“It proves I worked hard.” Effort is a process, not an ability.

M4 小作品那一条：多答一问The M4 artifact's entry: one extra question

第 14 周你用一件工具为主修课做了一件小东西。它的成长说明在三句之外必须再答：做完之后，你用过它吗？寒假第一次学主修课的时候，打算怎么用它——再考自己一遍 / 补上当时写不出的那个分支 / 重录一版？作品做出来的那一刻不是终点；被拿回去用的那一刻才是。In Week 14 you made one small thing for your core course. Beyond the three sentences, its note must also answer: have you used it since? And the first time you study the course over the break, how will you use it — test yourself again / fill the branch you couldn't write / re-record it? The moment a work is finished is not its destination; the moment it gets used again is.

选了 M3 学习计划那条的：也多答一问。三句之外再写一句：从第 11 周到第 14 周，这份计划改过几次、为什么？改过不是缺点——按证据改计划，正是那两周真正教的东西；“改过 0 次”如实写也算数。If the M3 plan is one of your entries: one more question for it too. Beyond the three sentences, add one line: how many times did the plan change between Weeks 11 and 14, and why? Changing is no flaw — revising a plan on evidence is exactly what those two weeks taught; an honest “zero changes” counts as well.

教师的两则示范Two model notes by the instructor

示范 1 · 学习故事录音（第 8 周 · M1 任务 1）Model 1 · The recorded learning story (Week 8 · M1 Task 1)

当时：第 8 周录的，两分半。录到第 4 遍还在说“嗯……就是……”，最后是照着六步提纲念了一遍才敢发。Then: recorded in Week 8, two and a half minutes. On take 4 I was still saying “um… I mean…”; the published take was me reading straight off the six-step outline.

现在：现在再听，照着念的部分反而最清楚——我当时以为“照提纲念”丢人，现在明白提纲就是拿来照的。Now: listening again, the read-from-outline part is the clearest — I thought reading it aloud was embarrassing; now I know outlines exist to be read.

证据：它证明我能把一次乱糟糟的经历整理成别人听得懂的三分钟——先有结构，再有表达。Proof: it shows I can turn a messy experience into three minutes someone else can follow — structure first, delivery second.

示范 2 · 闪卡组（第 14 周 · M4 任务 2）Model 2 · The flashcard deck (Week 14 · M4 Task 2)

当时：第 14 周给主修课第 3 周的术语做了 10 张卡。做到第 3 张就卡住了——有两个术语我写不出区别，回去重读了一遍才写完。Then: in Week 14 I made 10 cards for my course's week-3 terms. Card 3 stopped me — two terms I could not tell apart, and I had to re-read before I could finish.

现在：现在看，那两张“写不出来”的卡是整组里最值钱的——是它们告诉我哪里没懂。Now: looking back, the two cards I couldn't write are the deck's most valuable — they told me what I hadn't understood.

证据：它证明我能用一件工具把“看着都会”查成“哪里不会”。用过吗 / 寒假怎么用：做完那周考过自己一遍，错 3 张；寒假第一次学主修课，先把这 10 张再考一遍，错的加新卡。Proof: it shows I can use a tool to turn “it all looks familiar” into “here is what I don't know”. Used since / over the break: tested once the week I made it, three wrong; my first break session starts by running all ten again and adding cards for the misses.

三种最常见的“不算数”The three commonest failures

  1. 产品介绍：“用 XMind 做的导图，有三个分支。”——这是它长什么样，不是你变了什么。The product description: “a mind map in XMind with three branches.” That is what it looks like, not what changed in you.
  2. 万能好话：“做得很认真，收获很大。”——放在任何人任何作品下面都成立的话，等于没说。The universal compliment: “worked hard, gained a lot.” A sentence true under anyone's work says nothing about yours.
  3. 只有进度：“外部课程完成了 4 周里的 3 次。”——差一步：补上一件卡过的事和它证明的能力。Progress only: “3 of 4 external-course sessions done.” One step short — add one thing that stuck and the ability it proves.

本单元任务 1 允许 AI 帮你把句子改通顺（附使用说明）。但注意：三句里最要紧的“当时卡在哪”和“现在怎么看”，只存在你的记忆里——AI 编出来的那种，正是上面第 2 种。Task 1 allows AI to smooth your sentences (with a use note). But the load-bearing parts — where you got stuck then, how you see it now — live only in your memory; what AI invents is exactly failure #2 above.
