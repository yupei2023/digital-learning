要做什么What to do

复测学习状态自测，把结果和第 2 周的基线并排；然后写一篇学期反思（中文 300–500 字，英文 200–350 词，等工作量），发布到网站“作品集”页，标题“M5 任务 2 · 我的学期反思”，紧挨着作品集 v1。结尾写一行“本文未使用 AI”。

Retake the learning self-check and set the result beside your Week-2 baseline; then write a semester reflection (300–500 Chinese characters, or 200–350 English words — the same workload) and publish it on your Portfolio page, right beside Portfolio v1, titled “M5 Task 2 · My semester reflection”. End with the line “No AI was used”.

这是一面镜子，不是一场表演。评分只看投入与形式：对照做了没有、证据给了没有、五段齐不齐、是不是自己的话。成长多少不评分——如实写“几乎没变”，和写“变化很大”拿一样的分。唯一拿不到分的写法，是让别人（包括 AI）替你照镜子。This is a mirror, not a performance. Scoring looks only at engagement and form: did you contrast, did you give evidence, are the five parts there, is it your own voice. The amount of growth is not scored — an honest “hardly anything changed” earns the same as “everything changed”. The only failing move is letting someone else (AI included) look into the mirror for you.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 五段齐全（模板在下面）；中文 300–500 字；用英文写的，按等工作量 200–350 词计。All five parts (template below); 300–500 characters in Chinese — or 200–350 words if you write in English (the same amount of work).
  • 第 ① 段有两次自测的分数对照和一件作品集里的作品作证据；第 ② 段如实写“没变或变低”的一项。Part ① carries both self-check scores and one portfolio work as evidence; part ② honestly names what didn't move or dropped.
  • 全程不使用 AI：不代写、不润色、不翻译。想用英文写就自己写——语言不完美不扣分。No AI at any step: no drafting, no polishing, no translating. Write in English yourself if you wish — imperfect language costs nothing.
  • 结尾一行“本文未使用 AI”。The closing line “No AI was used”.
  • 不写他人真实姓名；不贴含他人信息的截图。No one else's real names; no screenshots carrying others' information.

常见问题FAQ

为什么这篇不能用 AI，作品集却可以？Why no AI here when the portfolio allows it?

作品集的证据是作品本身，文字只是标签——AI 帮忙改标签无伤大雅。反思的证据就是文字本身：你怎么看自己。这一份如果不是你写的，它就什么都不是了。

The portfolio's evidence is the works; the words are labels, and AI may polish a label harmlessly. The reflection's evidence IS the words — how you see yourself. If they aren't yours, the piece is nothing.

写不满 300 字。I can't reach 300.

通常是第 ①③ 段写虚了。回到跟做三句：分数、作品名、卡过的那一步，把名词写出来字数自然就够。还差，就把第 ⑤ 段的两个打算各展开一句“为什么是它”。

Usually parts ① and ③ went abstract. Return to the skeleton: the scores, the work's name, the step that stuck — nouns fill space honestly. Still short? Give each plan in ⑤ one sentence of “why this one”.

可以写得比 500 字长吗？May it run past 500?

上限是给你挡“写成小传”的。超一点没人量尺子；超一倍就请删——删到只剩有对照、有证据的句子。

The cap guards against a memoir. A little over harms nothing; double means cutting — keep only the sentences with a contrast or a piece of evidence.
