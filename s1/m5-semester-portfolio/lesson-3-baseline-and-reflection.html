反思怎么写：对照、证据、如实Writing the reflection: contrast, evidence, honesty

“我进步了很多”不是反思，是口号。反思是把两个时间点的自己放在一起，指着具体的东西说：这里变了，这里没变，我打算怎么办。你手里正好有全套材料：两次学习状态自测、一本作品集、十几条日志。

“I improved a lot” is a slogan, not a reflection. A reflection puts two versions of you side by side and points at specific things: this moved, this didn't, and here is what I'll do. You happen to hold the full kit: two self-checks, a portfolio, a dozen logs.

先摆材料，再动笔Lay out the material before writing

在草稿里画一张两列小表：左列抄第 2 周的自测逐项分，右列抄这周复测的。然后用三支“笔”做标记：Draw a two-column table in your draft: Week-2 scores on the left, this week's on the right. Then mark it three ways:

  • ↑ 变化最大的一项——去作品集里找到能证明它的那件作品。↑ the biggest rise — then find the portfolio work that proves it.
  • → 几乎没动的一项——想一想：是没练到，还是本来就会？→ the item that barely moved — untrained, or already there?
  • ↓ 变低的一项（如果有）——变低常常是标准变高了，这本身就值得写一段。↓ any drop — a drop often means your standard rose, which itself deserves a paragraph.

选做，但很值：第 7 周那张“基本功清单”还在吗？（在作品集页上，B1–B6 每周一句“扎实”或“还不稳”。）把它和这次自测放在一起看——当时写“还不稳”的哪几行，如今稳了？稳下来的那一行，往往就是第 ① 段最好用的作品证据。Optional, and worth it: is the Basics inventory from Week 7 still around? (On your Portfolio page — one line per week, B1–B6, marked “solid” or “still shaky”.) Read it beside this round's self-check: which of the “still shaky” lines have steadied? A line that steadied is often the handiest piece of evidence part ① can use.

竖着抄：一行一题，两次分数并排，最后一列做记号。↓ 那行不一定是退步——往往是你的标准变高了。Copy it vertically: one item per row, the two scores side by side, the mark in the last column. A ↓ row is rarely regression — usually your standard rose.

五段模板The five-part template

段Part写什么What goes there一句起头An opening stem

②没变或变低的一项 + 你的解释What didn't move (or dropped) + your explanation“几乎没变的是____。我现在的解释是____。”“What barely moved: ____. My explanation: ____.”

两条铁律Two iron rules

  1. 每个判断配一件证据。说“我更会安排时间了”，就要指着 M3 的日程表或某周日志；指不出来，就把判断改小，改到指得出来为止。Every claim carries one piece of evidence. “I manage time better” must point at the M3 schedule or a week's log; if nothing points, shrink the claim until something does.
  2. “没变”如实写。这篇反思评的是投入与形式——对照做了没有、证据给了没有、五段齐不齐、是不是自己的话。成长多少不评分。所以最划算的策略就是说实话。Write “no change” honestly. This piece is scored on engagement and form — did you contrast, did you give evidence, are all five parts there, is it in your own words. The amount of growth is not scored. Honesty is therefore the winning strategy.

为什么这篇不用 AIWhy this piece uses no AI

反思是一面镜子。让 AI 替你照镜子，镜子里出现的就是别人的脸——句子也许更漂亮，但那份“哪里没变”的诚实没有了，而那正是这篇文章唯一值钱的东西。所以任务 2 全程不用 AI，包括润色和翻译；语言不完美完全不扣分。

A reflection is a mirror. Let AI look into it for you and the face that appears is someone else's — the sentences may be prettier, but the honesty about what hasn't changed is gone, and that honesty is this piece's only value. So Task 2 uses no AI at any step, polishing and translation included; imperfect language costs nothing.
