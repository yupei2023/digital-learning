第一学期 · 第 15–16 周 · 单元 5 · 学期收尾Semester 1 · Weeks 15–16 · Module 5 · the semester's close

学期作品集与反思Semester Portfolio & Reflection

20 分 · 总结性20 pts · summative✅ 辅助 + 🚫 独立✅ Assisted + 🚫 Independent每周 ≤ 120 分钟（外部课程已暂停）≤ 120 min/week (external course paused)两周Two weeks

十六周之前，你给这门课发出了第一封邮件，建起了三页空空的网站。现在，那个网站上躺着一学期的东西：一段学习故事的录音、一篇学习观短文、一封公开信、一张计划表和它的复盘、三件工具的微评、一件亲手做的小作品，还有十几条三行日志。这两周不学新东西——你要做的，是把这十六周的自己排成一排看一看：挑出五到七件最能代表成长的作品，为每一件写清它证明了什么；再对照第 2 周的那个自己，写下这学期的变化。

Sixteen weeks ago you sent this course its first email and built a three-page website with nothing on it. That site now holds a semester: a recorded learning story, a mini-essay, an open letter, a plan and its review, three tool micro-reviews, one small thing made with your own hands, and a dozen three-line logs. These two weeks teach nothing new — instead, you line up your sixteen-week self and look: pick the five to seven works that best show your growth and say what each one proves; then face your Week-2 self and write down what changed.

挑，比全放难。你在 M4 的读本里读过：选择的本质是放弃。作品集也一样——放进去什么，说明你认为什么算成长。

Choosing is harder than keeping everything. You read it in M4's reader: to choose is to give up. A portfolio works the same way — what you put in says what you count as growth.

核心问题Essential questions

  • 这一学期，我作为数字化学习者有何成长？How have I grown as a digital learner this semester?
  • 哪几件作品能证明这些成长——为什么是它们？Which works are the evidence — and why those?
  • 对照第 2 周的自己，下学期我要去哪里？Set beside my Week-2 self, where am I going next semester?

学完本单元，你应能By the end of this module you should be able to

  1. ① 在个人网站的“作品集”页建成“学期作品集 v1”：遴选 5–7 件最能代表这学期成长的作品（覆盖至少三个不同单元，并含“外部课程进度 + 一件相关作品”一条），每件配可打开的链接或作品本身。① On your website's Portfolio page, build “Semester Portfolio v1”: curate 5–7 works that best show this semester's growth (covering at least three different modules, and including one entry of “external-course progress + one related work”), each with a working link or the work itself.
  2. ② 为每件入选作品写一则三句成长说明（当时做了什么、卡在哪 · 现在再看有什么变化 · 它证明我学会了什么）；M4 小作品那一条还要回答：做完之后用过它吗？寒假第一次学主修课时打算怎么用？② Write a three-sentence growth note for every selected work (what I did then and where I got stuck · how I see it now · what it proves I can do); the note on the M4 artifact must also answer: have I used it since — and how will I use it the first time I study my core course over the break?
  3. ③ 复测“学习状态自测”，对照第 2 周的基线，独立写一篇学期反思（中文 300–500 字，英文 200–350 词，等工作量）：变化最大的一项及其作品证据、没变（或变低）的一项、下学期的两件具体打算。③ Retake the learning self-check, set it beside your Week-2 baseline, and independently write a semester reflection (300–500 Chinese characters, or 200–350 English words — the same workload): the biggest change with evidence from the portfolio, one thing that didn't change (or dropped), and two concrete plans for next semester.

预计用时Time

每周 120 分钟以内：整理与写作约 100 · 反思与日志约 20。主修外部课程从本周起暂停——寒假想继续就继续，不想也没关系，寒假的学习不计入任何评分。页面上的视频全部是选看，不计入分钟；标注“选做”的内容也不计入——唯一的例外是第 16 周的学期匿名问卷（自愿）：那 5 分钟已含在当周 100 分钟里，跳过就省下。第一次做作品集、第一次写学期反思会比表里写的慢——这是正常的，没有迟交处罚。Within 120 minutes a week: curate & write ~100 · reflect & log ~20. Your core external course pauses from this week — continue over the break if you like; nothing over the break is graded. Every video on these pages is optional and outside the count, as is anything marked optional — with one exception: Week 16's anonymous survey (voluntary) has its 5 minutes already inside that week's 100, so skipping it simply saves them. A first portfolio and a first semester reflection run slower than the tables say — that is normal, and there is no late penalty.

本单元的 AI 标注：✅ 辅助（任务 1）+ 🚫 独立（任务 2）AI designations: ✅ Assisted (Task 1) + 🚫 Independent (Task 2)
任务 1（作品集与成长说明）允许使用课程认可清单中的 AI 工具辅助文字——例如把成长说明的句子改通顺——并在页面末尾附两三句“AI 使用说明”；完全不用 AI 同样可以拿满分。任务 2（学期反思）独立完成，不使用任何 AI 工具，包括润色和翻译。反思是一面镜子——AI 替你照，照出来的就不是你。评分只看投入与形式，不评“成长大小”：如实写“没什么变化”，同样可以拿满分。Task 1 (the portfolio and growth notes) allows AI tools from the approved list for wording — e.g., smoothing a growth note's sentences — with a two–three-sentence AI use note at the end; full marks are equally reachable without AI. Task 2 (the semester reflection) is done independently — no AI tools at all, including polishing and translation. The reflection is a mirror: if AI looks into it for you, the face it shows is not yours. It is scored on engagement and form, never on “how much you grew”: an honest “not much changed” can earn full marks.
