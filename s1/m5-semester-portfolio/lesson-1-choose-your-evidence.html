挑作品：少即是多Choosing your evidence: less is more

作品集不是仓库，是证词：你选出的每一件，都是在替你说一句“我学会了____”。仓库越满越好；证词越准越好。所以本周的动词是“挑”，不是“放”。

A portfolio is not a warehouse; it is testimony. Every work you select says on your behalf, “I learned to ____.” A warehouse improves by filling; testimony improves by precision. This week's verb is choose, not keep.

四条标准The four criteria

标准Criterion问自己Ask yourself常见的坑The usual trap
1 · 代表成长1 · Shows growth放上它，第 2 周的我和现在的我之间的差，看得见吗？Does it make the distance between Week-2 me and current me visible?挑“最漂亮的”。最漂亮的那件，往往是你本来就会的。Picking the prettiest — often the thing you could already do.
2 · 有故事可讲2 · Has a story这件作品，我卡过吗？卡在哪，后来怎么通的？Did I get stuck on this one? Where, and how did it come loose?挑“最顺利的”。没卡过的作品，成长说明第一句就没得写。Picking the smoothest — with nothing stuck, the note's first sentence has nothing to say.
3 · 覆盖不同单元3 · Covers different modules5–7 件里，是不是至少来自三个单元？有没有一件“过程”类（计划、微评、日志）？Do the 5–7 span at least three modules? Is at least one a “process” piece (a plan, a micro-review, a log)?五件全是 M4——那证明的是两周，不是十六周。Five entries all from M4 — testimony to two weeks, not sixteen.
4 · 含外部课程4 · Includes the external course有没有一条写主修课：一段进度（学到哪了、守住了几次时段）+ 一件相关作品？Is there one entry on the core course: a progress paragraph (how far, how many slots kept) + one related work?忘了它——但它是你这学期唯一一门“真的在外面学”的课。Forgetting it — the one course you truly took out in the world this semester.

外部课程那一条的“一件相关作品”可以就是 M4 的小作品（它本来就为主修课而做）——一条作品两个身份，注明即可；也可以换成外部课程的日志摘录、练习截图或证书徽章。The “one related work” may simply be your M4 artifact (it was made for the core course anyway) — one work, two roles, just say so; or use a log excerpt, an exercise screenshot, or a badge from the course.

日志也算作品：把第一条和最新一条截图放在一起，就是一件“变化看得见”的展品。Logs count too: your first entry and your latest, screenshotted side by side, make one exhibit where change is visible to the eye.

为什么是 5–7 件？Why 5–7?

少于 5 件，三个单元和外部课程盖不住；多于 7 件，看的人（包括下学期的你）抓不住重点——而且你会开始“放”而不是“挑”。M4 读本的那句话在这里再用一次：选择的本质是放弃。每放弃一件，问一句“为什么不带它”，那个答案往往比入选理由更能说明你的标准。

Fewer than five cannot cover three modules plus the external course; more than seven, and the reader (including next-semester you) loses the thread — and you start keeping instead of choosing. M4's line earns a second use here: to choose is to give up. For each work you leave out, ask “why not this one” — that answer often reveals your standard better than any inclusion.
