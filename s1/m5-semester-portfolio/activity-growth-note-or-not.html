成长说明，还是产品介绍？Growth note, or product description?

八条说明，两个筐。点一条，再点一个筐。全部放完点“检查”——每条都有解释，说清它差的那一步（如果差的话）。不计分；解释比对错更重要。

Eight notes, two bins. Tap a note, then tap a bin. When all eight are placed, press Check — every note carries an explanation naming the missing step (if any). Ungraded; the explanations matter more than the score.
