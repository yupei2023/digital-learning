要做什么What to do

本周任务一页装两件事。第一件（① 行，3 分）：把工作台四角搭完整，用第 3 周的录屏手艺录一段约 1 分钟的屏幕导览，再对照六周的手艺写"我的基本功清单"——每周一句话，如实标"扎实"或"还不稳"；导览 + 清单发布到作品集页。第二件（② 行，2 分）：在网站新建"我的外部课程"页，三段写清哪两门、为什么、每周什么时候学——外部课程学习线的第一个触点在这一页正式落定。第一件是六周手艺的合影；第二件是下一程的路书——两件一起，数字基本功收官。

This week's task fits two matters on one page. First (row ①, 3 pts): complete the workspace's four corners, record a ~1-minute screen tour with Week 3's recording craft, and write "My Basics inventory" against the six weeks' crafts — one sentence per week, honestly marked "solid" or "still shaky"; tour + inventory published on the Portfolio page. Second (row ②, 2 pts): create the "My external course" page on your site — three paragraphs stating which two courses, why, and when each week — the External Course Thread's first touchpoint formally settling on this page. The first matter is a group photo of six weeks' craft; the second is the road-book for the journey ahead — together they seal Digital Basics.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 四角就位：云盘（或如实注明的兜底路线）· 日历里每周重复的提醒 · 笔记的固定落点 · "发布后备份"的动作。走哪条路线不影响分数——评"东西找得到"，不评"用了哪家"。Four corners in place: the cloud drive (or an honestly noted fallback route) · a weekly repeating calendar reminder · a fixed home for notes · the back-up-after-publish move. The route chosen never affects the score — "things findable" is judged, brand never.
  • 导览约 1 分钟，四角都露面：只录屏幕、不露脸；录之前把文件名里的真名、邮箱地址、通知弹窗、日历里别人的日程按第 3 周的规矩收拾干净。长一点短一点都不扣分——四个角都到场才是要点。The tour runs about a minute and shows all four corners: screen only, no faces; before recording, clear real names in filenames, email addresses, notification pop-ups and other people's calendar entries by Week 3's rules. A little over or under costs nothing — all four corners present is the point.
  • 清单六行，句句是实话：B1–B6 每周一行，一句话 + "扎实"或"还不稳"。"还不稳"与"扎实"同分——评的是盘点的诚实，不是技能的完美；六个"都会了"反而看不出盘点。Six inventory lines, every one true: one line per week B1–B6, one sentence plus "solid" or "still shaky". "Still shaky" scores exactly as "solid" does — the honesty of the stocktake is judged, never the perfection of the skills; six "all mastered"s show no stocktake at all.
  • 选择页三段齐全：① 哪两门（主修 + 探索，课名照菜单抄）；② 为什么——引用上周的试学小记或那句对比（引自己一句就够；反悔了就写清为何反悔，同样达标）；③ 每周什么时候学——写明固定时段，并与日历里那条提醒一致。📌 需要注册的平台，先告诉家长。The choice page holds all three paragraphs: ① which two (core + explore, names copied from the menu); ② why — quoting last week's sampling notes or the comparing sentence (one line of your own suffices; a change of heart, with its reason stated, meets the bar just the same); ③ when each week — a stated fixed slot, agreeing with the calendar reminder. 📌 Tell your parents before registering on any platform.
  • 菜单外的课走规则：免费 · 大陆能打开 · 非人工智能主题（本学期）· 时长在上限内 · 注册方式清楚（菜单页末尾有全文），理由写在选择页上；在校学生把页面链接发导师确认（确认的具体登记方式随开课信公布——见第 7 周常见问题）。Off-menu courses take the rules: free · opens from the mainland · not AI-themed (this semester) · within the caps · a clear sign-up route (full text at the foot of the menu page), reasons written on the choice page; enrolled students send the link to their mentor for confirmation (the exact confirmation route is published with the welcome letter — see Week 7's FAQ).
  • 已发布 + 已登记 + 已备份：导览与清单进作品集页，"我的外部课程"自成一页；两个链接都登记，任务编号 B6；发布后照微课 1 的规矩做第一次例行备份。公开学习者：两个链接发进自己的邮箱（主题"我的工作台与选课 · 化名"）。Published + registered + backed up: tour and inventory on the Portfolio page, "My external course" standing as its own page; both links registered, task number B6; after publishing, run the first routine backup by micro-lesson 1's rule. Open learners: both links emailed to yourself (subject "my workspace and choice · pseudonym").
  • 🚫 独立：清单的六句话和选择页的"为什么"由你自己写；本周不使用生成式 AI。家长参与选时段、一起定选课，完全欢迎——那是知情，不是代做。🚫 Independent: the inventory's six sentences and the choice page's "why" written by you; no generative AI this week. Parents helping pick the slot and weighing the choice are entirely welcome — that is informed involvement, not doing it for you.

步骤Steps

  1. 四角就位（15 分钟）：跟做点过的火接着烧——把散在桌面和下载文件夹里的课程文件全部搬进新文件夹（只搬课程相关的，大扫除留给选做）；确认云盘或兜底路线真能在第二台设备上取到文件（手机开网页版看一眼）；日历提醒与笔记落点各检查一遍。照四角图逐角对表。最后把 §0 那件事再做一次：重新找一次同一个文件，记下这回用了几秒——两个秒数并排，"搭好了"就从展示变成了检验（这个数字留着写日志第 1 行）。Corners in place (15 min): feed the fires the follow-along lit — move every course file straying on the desktop and in downloads into the new folders (course things only; the grand clean-up stays optional); confirm the cloud drive or fallback truly serves files to a second device (a glance via the phone's web version settles it); check the calendar reminder and the notes landing place once each. Audit corner by corner against the workspace map. Finally, repeat §0's move once: find that same file again and note the seconds — two numbers side by side turn "it is assembled" from a display into a test (keep the number for the log's first line).
  2. 录 1 分钟导览（10 分钟，含一次重录的余量）：打开录屏（第 3 周的手艺），依次展示四个角：云盘（或兜底）的文件夹 → 日历里那条每周提醒 → 笔记落点（露一条带三行来源的笔记最见功力）→ 备份的去处。开口讲解或用鼠标指引都行。日历那个角要格外当心：家里共用的日历常装着"爸爸复查""补习班"这类别人的事、也可能定位到你——导览只需露出"外部课程"那一条，录之前把别的日历暂时隐藏，或只给那一条一个特写。录完自己回放一遍：有没有露出真名、邮箱、弹窗、别人的日程？有就重录——余量就是为它留的。Record the tour (10 min, one retake included): start the recorder (Week 3's craft) and visit the corners in turn: the cloud (or fallback) folders → the weekly reminder in the calendar → the notes landing place (one note wearing its three source lines shows the finest craft) → where the backups go. Narrate aloud or point with the mouse, either. The calendar corner needs particular care: a shared family calendar carries other people's business — "Dad's check-up", "tutoring" — and can locate you too; the tour needs only the "External course" entry showing, so hide the other calendars beforehand, or film that single entry close up. Then play it back once: any real name, email address, pop-up, anyone else's appointment in frame? Re-record — that is what the allowance is for.
  3. 写"我的基本功清单"（10 分钟）：打印或打开讲义《数字基本功自查清单》，逐行照自查问题问自己，B1–B6 每周写一句：答得毫不犹豫，写"扎实 + 一句凭据"（"B5 扎实——上周三张卡一次成"）；有一丝犹豫，写"还不稳 + 一句实情"（"B3 还不稳——回译核对总忘"）。六句写完，你就有了第 16 周复测前最可靠的私人地图。Write the Basics inventory (10 min): print or open the Digital Basics self-checklist handout and put each row's questions to yourself, one sentence per week, B1–B6: an unhesitating answer earns "solid + one piece of evidence" ("B5 solid — last week's three cards came out right first time"); any flicker of doubt earns "still shaky + one plain truth" ("B3 still shaky — I keep forgetting the back-translation check"). Six sentences later you hold the most reliable private map you will own before Week 16's re-check.
  4. "我的外部课程"页（15 分钟）：网站上新建一页，三段：选了哪两门（主修 + 探索）；为什么——回作品集翻出上周的检索报告，把试学小记或那句对比原文引一句，再接一句现在的想法（维持原判或反悔都合法）；每周什么时候学——写明时段（"每周三 19:00–20:00"），确认日历里那条提醒说的是同一个时间。菜单外的课在这一段末尾按规则补理由。The "My external course" page (15 min): a new page on your site, three paragraphs: which two (core + explore); why — return to last week's search report on your Portfolio, quote one line of the sampling notes or the comparing sentence verbatim, then add one line of your present thinking (upholding the verdict or reversing it, both legitimate); when each week — the slot stated plainly ("Wednesdays 19:00–20:00"), agreeing with the calendar reminder. Off-menu reasons append to this paragraph under the rules.
  5. 发布、登记、第一次例行备份（10 分钟）：两处都点"发布"，用访客的眼睛各过一遍（导览里没有隐私吧？清单里没有校名吧？）；两个链接登记到登记表（公开学习者：发进自己的邮箱）；最后，当着自己的面把"发布后备份"做第一次——导览视频文件、清单与选择页正文，各存一份进"网站备份"文件夹或发给自己。视频文件大，邮箱常常发不动——存进本地"网站备份"文件夹或 U 盘同样算数（量规不问你存在哪），邮箱留给两页正文。从今天起，这个动作跟着每一次发布。Publish, register, the first routine backup (10 min): press Publish in both places and review each with a visitor's eyes (no private details in the tour? no school name in the inventory?); register both links (open learners: email them to yourself); and finally, perform the first back-up-after-publish in your own presence — the tour's video file and the texts of inventory and choice page, one copy each into the site-backups folder or your inbox. Video files are large and email often refuses them — a copy in the local site-backups folder or on a USB stick counts exactly the same (the rubric never asks where), leaving the inbox for the two texts. From today, the move rides along with every publish.

"为什么"那段怎么写：两句就见功力Writing the "why": two sentences show the craft

最好的"为什么"不是形容词（"很有趣""质量高"），是证据加决定。第一句引证据——上周你亲手写下的小记或对比："我当时写'视频短、字幕全，还想看第二课'。"第二句下决定——"一周过去我还是想回去，就选它当主修。"反悔的写法同样体面："我当时更想回去第 4 门，但这周想到它全英文、每周要跟八周，改选第 6 门——中文的我更可能坚持完。"评的是理由在场且出自亲身，不评你选得"对不对"——选课没有标准答案，坚持得完的课就是好课。The best "why" is not adjectives ("interesting", "high quality") but evidence plus a decision. Sentence one quotes the evidence — the note or comparison you wrote with your own hand last week: "I wrote 'short videos, full subtitles, wanted lesson 2'." Sentence two decides — "a week on it still calls me back, so it is my core course." A reversal reads just as well: "Course 4 called me back then, but this week I weighed its all-English eight-week road and chose course 6 instead — in Chinese, I am likelier to finish." What is scored is a reason present and first-hand — never whether you chose "correctly": course-choosing has no answer key, and the course you can finish is the good course.

常见问题FAQ

导览录了 1 分 40 秒，超时了怎么办？My tour runs 1 minute 40 — over the limit?

"约 1 分钟"是目标不是闸门——四个角都在、没露隐私，1 分 40 完全达标。倒是可以当一次剪辑练习：哪二十秒是废话？下一条视频你自然就短了。短于 1 分钟同理：四角都清楚，40 秒也满分。

"About a minute" is a target, not a gate — with all four corners present and no private details, 1:40 fully meets the bar. Treat it as an editing exercise if you like: which twenty seconds were padding? Your next video will shorten itself. The same holds under a minute: four clear corners in 40 seconds still earn full marks.

基本功清单写"还不稳"，会不会显得我这六周白学了？Won't "still shaky" on the inventory make my six weeks look wasted?

恰恰相反。"还不稳"写得准，说明你知道自己的手艺边界在哪——这比技能本身更难得。量规白纸黑字：诚实的"还不稳"与"扎实"同分。而且它有实际用处：第 16 周你要复测"学习状态自测"并对照第 2 周的基线写总结——这张清单就是你留给那时候的路标。六周学的手艺，后面十几周天天要用，"还不稳"的自然会稳。

Quite the opposite. A well-aimed "still shaky" proves you know where your craft's edges lie — rarer than the craft itself. The rubric says it in black and white: an honest "still shaky" scores exactly as "solid". And it earns its keep: in Week 16 you retake the learning self-check and write a reflection against Week 2's baseline — this inventory is the waymark you leave for that day. The crafts of these six weeks get daily use for a dozen more; the shaky ones steady themselves.

主修课想选第 4 周任务里读过介绍的那门，可上周没试学它，行吗？May my core course be the one whose introduction I read in Week 4, though I never sampled it last week?

行。选择页评的是"三段齐全、理由亲身"——理由可以引第 4 周读介绍页的印象，也可以引上周浏览菜单时的发现，不非得出自那 40 分钟试学。稳妥起见，发布前再花 10 分钟到那门课里走一走（介绍 → 大纲 → 第一节），给"为什么"添一句新鲜的第一手话——这 10 分钟不计入本周预算，算你为自己的决定加的保险。

Yes. The choice page is scored on "three paragraphs, first-hand reasons" — and the reason may quote Week 4's impression of its introduction, or last week's menu browsing; the 40 sampling minutes hold no monopoly. For safety's sake, spend 10 minutes walking that course before publishing (introduction → syllabus → first lesson) and give the "why" one fresh first-hand line — those minutes sit outside this week's budget, insurance you buy for your own decision.

两门试学的课我都舍不得，主修到底选哪门？I can't give up either sampled course — so which one is the core?

好消息：你多半不用舍。这一页要的本来就是两门——一门主修、一门探索——所以"两门都想要"往往根本不是两难：更想深学、愿意每周花那一小时的当主修，另一门当探索（≤ 1 小时、两三节，只为多见一种课的长相）。真要决定的其实只有一件事：哪一门配得上那每周一小时。还是拿不定，就选此刻更想回去的那门——写下来的选择并非不可更改，第 11 周正式开学前有充分理由仍可调整（见第 7 周"建议节奏"）。

Good news: you probably need not give either up. This page asks for two courses all along — one core, one explore — so "I want both" is usually no dilemma at all: the one you want to study deeply, and would spend the weekly hour on, becomes the core; the other becomes the explore course (≤ 1 hour, two or three lessons, there only to show you one more shape a course can take). The single real decision is which one earns that weekly hour. Still torn? Take the one you want to return to right now — a written choice is not irreversible, and with good reason it can still shift before study formally begins in Week 11 (see Week 7's suggested pace).

主修和探索能选同一组里的两门吗？探索课到底要学到什么程度？May core and explore come from the same group? And how far must the explore course go?

同组两门完全可以——分组是为了帮你逛菜单，不是围栏。探索课的定位很轻：≤ 1 小时、只学两三节，图的是"多见一种课的长相"；不用学完、不用做题、期末也只在评鉴报告里写它一段。所以选探索课尽管大胆——正因为赌注小，才值得选一门和主修风格很不一样的。

Two from one group is entirely fine — the groups exist to help you browse, not to fence you in. The explore course travels light: ≤ 1 hour, two or three lessons, its whole purpose to show you one more shape a course can take; nothing to finish, no exercises, just one paragraph in next semester's evaluation report. So choose it boldly — the small stake is exactly what makes an unlike-your-core choice worth taking.

我是公开学习者，选择页和导师确认怎么办？I am an open learner — what of the choice page and mentor confirmation?

选择页照写照发布——它首先是写给你自己的路书。链接发进自己的邮箱存档（主题"我的工作台与选课 · 化名"），量规自评。菜单外的课不需要谁确认：对照菜单页末尾的规则自己核一遍即可；拿不准某门课是否合规，宁可换一门——规则存在的理由（免费、可达、时长上限）对公开学习者同样成立。

Write and publish the choice page exactly the same — it is first of all a road-book to yourself. Email the link to your own inbox (subject "my workspace and choice · pseudonym") and self-score with the rubric. Off-menu courses need nobody's confirmation: check them yourself against the rules at the menu's foot; where a course's compliance feels doubtful, prefer another — the rules' reasons (free, reachable, within caps) hold for open learners just the same.
