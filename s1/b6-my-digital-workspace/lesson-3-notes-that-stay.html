笔记：想法的家Notes: the home of ideas

上周你听过一句话："收藏夹是门厅，带来源的笔记才是家。"本课就来把这个家安好。要点不在选哪款笔记软件——在于三个习惯：有固定落点（所有课程笔记进同一个地方，别东一条西一条）；抄话带来源（第 6 周的三行卡原样搬进笔记：抄走一段话，趁页面还开着补上标题 · 作者或机构 · 网址 + 日期）；起名能搜到（笔记标题照第 5 周"用途-时间-内容"规则，三个月后靠搜索也能捞回来）。

Last week you heard the line: "bookmarks are a hallway; a note with its source is home." This lesson furnishes the home. The point is not which notes app to choose — it is three habits: one fixed landing place (all course notes into the same spot, never scattered); copied words carry their source (Week 6's three-line card moves in unchanged: the moment a passage travels into your notes, add title · author or institution · URL + date while the page is still open); names that can be searched (note titles by Week 5's purpose-time-content rule, retrievable by search three months on).

落点怎么选：一处就好Choosing the landing place: one is enough

  • 设备自带的备忘录 / 笔记 App——零注册、零安装，本周的推荐起点。建一个"数字化学习"文件夹（或标签），课程笔记全进这里。The built-in memo or notes app — zero sign-ups, zero installs, this week's recommended start. Raise one "Digital Learning" folder (or tag) and let every course note land there.
  • 一个在线文档——习惯在电脑上写的人可用一个长文档当"课程笔记本"，新的记在最上面（和日志页同一个方向）。One online document — for keyboard-first note-takers, a single long document serves as the course notebook, newest entries on top (the same direction as your log page).
  • 纸本 + 拍照——喜欢手写完全合法：写在固定的一个本子上，重要的页拍照存进云盘"笔记"文件夹（照片文件名也按命名规则），就同时有了纸的手感和云的可搜。Paper + photos — handwriting is entirely legitimate: keep one dedicated notebook, photograph the pages that matter into the drive's "notes" folder (photo filenames by the naming rule), and you hold paper's feel with the cloud's searchability.

诚实的说明书：笔记这件事最大的坑，不是没有好工具，是换工具换上瘾——每换一次"更完美的笔记系统"，旧笔记就流离失所一次。本周的要求刻意朴素：一处落点、带来源、能搜到。就这三条，用什么工具都能达标；先用三个月，再谈换家。（想深入方法本身？第 13–14 周"数字化学习技术"会正式试用六类学习工具——包括笔记类——到时候你已经带着三个月的真实笔记去挑了。）The honest manual: the great pit of note-taking is not the lack of a good tool but the addiction to switching — every migration to a "more perfect system" leaves the old notes homeless once more. This week's bar is deliberately plain: one landing place, sources attached, searchable names. Three lines, satisfiable with any tool; give it three months before talking upgrades. (Drawn to the methods themselves? Weeks 13–14, Digital Learning Technologies, formally trials six categories of learning tools — note-taking included — and by then you will shop with three months of real notes in hand.)
