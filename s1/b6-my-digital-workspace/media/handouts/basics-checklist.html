数字基本功自查清单The Digital Basics self-checklist

六周，六样手艺。写"我的基本功清单"时逐行照问题问自己；第 16 周复测前、以后每次觉得"手生了"的时候，也回来照它做一轮。答得毫不犹豫 = 扎实；有一丝犹豫 = 还不稳——两个答案一样值钱，只有不诚实的答案没用。

Six weeks, six crafts. Put each row's questions to yourself as you write your Basics inventory; return for another round before Week 16's re-check, and any time your hands feel out of practice. An unhesitating answer = solid; a flicker of doubt = still shaky — the two answers are worth the same, and only a dishonest one is worthless.

工作台四角 · 快速点检The four corners · quick check

  • ☁ 云盘 / 同步：换一台设备，两分钟内能取到上周的作业吗？（同步 ≠ 备份——误删会被忠实同步，所以要紧文件另有一份不会自动变的副本。）☁ Cloud / sync: from another device, can last week's work be reached within two minutes? (Sync ≠ backup — a slip's deletion syncs faithfully too, so what matters keeps one copy that never changes by itself.)
  • 🗓 日历 / 提醒：外部课程那条每周提醒还在响吗？响了三次都被划掉 = 该换时段，不是该自责。🗓 Calendar / reminders: is the weekly external-course reminder still ringing? Swiped away three weeks running = move the slot, not the blame.
  • 📓 笔记：新想法有唯一的落点吗？抄来的话都带着三行来源吗？标题搜得到吗？📓 Notes: does every new idea have its one landing place? Do copied words wear their three source lines? Do titles answer to search?
  • 🗄 备份：口诀——一份在平台，一份在自己手里。每次点完"发布"，动作跟上。🗄 Backup: the maxim — one copy on the platform, one in your own hands. The move follows every press of Publish.

每月自检（10 分钟）The monthly check (10 minutes)

  • 随机打开三个自己的文件：还认得它是什么、为什么留着吗？Open three of your files at random: still recognisable — what each is, and why it stays?
  • 最近一次备份是什么时候？超过一个月，现在就补一次。When was the last backup? Older than a month — run one now.
  • 桌面和下载文件夹里，散养的文件超过十个了吗？超了就花十分钟归置。Do desktop and downloads hold more than ten free-range files? If so, ten minutes of homing.
  • 密码短语与两步验证（B4）：新注册的账号也都照规矩了吗？Passphrases and two-step verification (B4): do the newest accounts keep the rules too?
本周一句话This week in one sentence
找得到，是本事；记得住，是分工；丢不了，是习惯——工作台把六周的手艺装进同一张台面，剩下的事只有一件：天天用它。Finding is skill; remembering is delegation; never losing is habit — the workspace mounts six weeks of craft on a single bench, and only one thing remains: use it daily.
