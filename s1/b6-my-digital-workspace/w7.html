10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

免费云盘几乎都要手机号，怎么办？Nearly every free cloud drive wants a phone number — now what?

如实回答：很多大牌网盘注册确实要手机号——那是那些工具的规矩，不是你所在地方的问题。三条路，按顺序考虑：① 试仅邮箱注册的云盘（微课 1 的表里有，标着"待实测"）；② 想用要手机号的网盘——先告诉家长（外部课程学习线的同一条约定），家长同意再注册，第 5 周的三问照常；③ 谁都不注册，走零门槛兜底：改完的文件发给自己的邮箱 + 本地"课程文件夹" + U 盘。兜底路线在量规里与云盘同分——工作台评的是"东西找得到"，不是"用了哪家"。

The honest answer: many big-name cloud drives really do ask for a phone number at sign-up — that is those tools' rule, not a fact about where you live. Three routes, in order: ① try an email-only drive (micro-lesson 1's table lists them, marked "to verify"); ② if you want a phone-number drive — tell your parents first (the External Course Thread's same standing agreement) and register only with their go-ahead, Week 5's three questions applying as usual; ③ register nowhere and take the zero-signup fallback: email each finished file to yourself + a local course folder + a USB stick. The fallback scores identically to a cloud drive on the rubric — the workspace is judged on "things findable", never on brand.

只有手机，没有电脑，工作台怎么搭？Phone only, no computer — can the workspace still stand?

能，四个角手机全搭得起来：云盘用 App 或网页版（能网页就网页——本课程不要求装任何 App，第 5 周的规矩照旧）；日历和备忘录手机自带的就很好——它们本来就是本周的推荐起点；备份 = 发给自己的邮箱，手机做起来反而最顺手。屏幕导览用第 3 周学过的手机录屏。诚实的两句：手机文件管理器藏得深（微课 1 提了位置），录屏时注意先清掉通知——弹出的消息也是隐私。

It can — all four corners fit a phone: cloud by app or web (web where possible — this course never requires installing an app; Week 5's rule stands); the built-in calendar and notes apps are excellent — they are this week's recommended starting point anyway; backup = email-to-self, which a phone does most gracefully of all. The tour uses Week 3's phone screen-recording. Two honest notes: phone file managers hide deep (micro-lesson 1 says where), and clear your notifications before recording — a popping message is private information too.

家里电脑是共用的，同步和账号怎么处理？The family computer is shared — what about sync and accounts?

第 5 周的口径原样适用：共用电脑上不登录浏览器或云盘客户端做常驻同步——用完登出，或只走网页版；文件放进以你化名命名的课程文件夹，别散在公共桌面。你的工作台不必住在某台机器里——这正是"最新版住云端（或你的邮箱）"的好处：换一台电脑，登录邮箱，你的东西还在。

Week 5's rule applies unchanged: on a shared computer, no resident sign-ins to browsers or cloud-drive clients — log out after use, or stay with the web version; keep files inside a course folder named with your pseudonym, not loose on the shared desktop. Your workspace need not live inside any one machine — which is precisely the virtue of "the latest version lives in the cloud (or your inbox)": at a different computer, sign in to your email and everything is still there.

上周试学的两门我都不想选，甚至想选菜单外的课。Neither sampled course appeals — I may even want one off the menu.

完全合法，两条路都写在规则里。换菜单内的另一门：菜单还有十门，选择页照常三段——"为什么"一段写清你放弃试学那两门的理由（劝退的发现同样是第一手证据，引用上周小记即可）。选菜单外的课：按菜单页末尾的规则——免费、你那里打得开、非人工智能主题（本学期）、时长在上限内、注册方式清楚——在选择页写明理由；在校学生把页面链接发给导师，导师确认后回信（确认的具体登记方式随开课信在开课前公布——见下"开课前公布"卡）。公开学习者自己按同一规则核对即可。

Entirely legitimate — both routes are written into the rules.
