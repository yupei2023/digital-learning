按四角检查Check the four corners

  1. 文件：在已有云盘或本地课程文件夹建作业、网站备份、笔记三个文件夹，用移动或拖放归位课程文件。不要整理其他人的文件。Files: in existing cloud storage or a local course folder, create assignments, site-backups and notes folders. Move or drag course files into place; leave other people’s files alone.
  2. 时间：在已有日历设每周外部课程提醒，提前 10 分钟，起点为第 11 周（公开学习者：开始 M3 的那周），时段与选课页一致。遇假期将该次学习顺延到下一个工作日。Time: in your existing calendar, set the weekly external-course reminder 10 minutes ahead, starting in Week 11 (open learners: the week you start M3). Match the choice-page slot. Shift holiday sessions to the next working day.
  3. 笔记：固定一个落点，存一条学习笔记，抄来的话带三行来源。Notes: choose one fixed home, save a learning note, and give quoted words their three source lines.
  4. 备份：把课程文件保存为独立副本，打开确认；再找一次原来的旧文件，记下秒数。Backup: save an independent course-file copy and open it. Find the original old file again and note the seconds.

常见问题FAQ

没有云盘、U 盘或第二台设备？No cloud drive, USB stick or second device?

独立副本指不会随着工作文件的修改或删除一起变化的副本：发给自己的附件、不与工作文件夹同步的云端位置，或已有的其他存储位置。相同同步文件夹中的副本、单纯同步都不算。无需购买或安装；没有第二台设备，照样从独立副本打开检查。电脑同盘的非同步练习副本可用于本周练习，但不能防设备损坏，应另外备份重要文件。视频太大又无其他位置？备份页面正文，注明视频原文件存放位置即可；这不是视频的独立备份，以后有条件再补。An independent copy does not change when the working file is edited or deleted: an emailed attachment, cloud storage not synced with the working folder, or other storage already available. A copy in the same synced folder, or sync alone, does not count. No purchase or installation is needed; open the independent copy even without a second device. An unsynced practice copy on the same disk works for this week’s exercise, but cannot protect against device failure; back up important work separately. Video too large and no other location? Back up the page text and state where the original video lives; that is not an independent video backup, and you can add one when feasible.

上周没写选课理由，或这周改主意了？No course reason from last week, or changed your mind?

有上周理由就引一句，再写现在的决定；试学小记也可用。不需要补做 B5 山脊。找不到旧理由，就重读菜单介绍，写“本周补记”及自己的具体理由；不编造旧记录。换课同样达标。Quote a prior course-pick reason or sampling note, then give your current decision. B5 Ridge is not required. If no prior note exists, reread the menu and label your own specific reason “added this week”; never invent a past record. Changing courses earns the same credit.

节假日、卡住或想补交？A holiday, a problem, or a revision?

假期不安排新任务或截止日期；有冲突顺延到下一个工作日。按自己的速度完成，迟交不扣分；更新后邮件导师，附链接与已打码截图，问题通常 24–48 小时回复；分数与一条建议在一周内回复（见量规）。答疑预约方式与平台见开课信。No new tasks or deadlines on university holidays; shift conflicting work to the next working day. Work at your pace; no late penalty. Email your mentor after updating, with links and a masked screenshot if needed; questions usually receive replies in 24–48 hours; scores and one suggestion arrive within a week (see rubric). The welcome letter supplies the video-help booking route and platform.
