工作台点火Workspace ignition

§0 你已经量过自己的"乱度"。现在把四个角各点一次火——每个角做一个最小的真动作：建好文件夹、设一条真会响的提醒、真的备份一个文件、把三个快捷键按进手指。全程可逆——摆错了挪回来，一分钱不花。点完火，任务里的"搭台"就只剩把火烧旺。

§0 took your disorder's measure. Now ignite each corner once — one smallest real move per corner: folders raised, a reminder that will truly ring, one file genuinely backed up, three shortcuts pressed into the fingers. Everything is reversible — a thing misplaced is simply moved back, at no cost. With the corners lit, the task's assembly is merely feeding the fire.

四把火The four fires

  1. 第一把 · 建文件夹（4 分钟）：在你选定的"文件的家"（云盘，或本地"课程文件夹"）里建三个文件夹，照第 5 周"用途-时间-内容"规则起名：数字化学习-作业、数字化学习-网站备份、数字化学习-笔记（中英文名都行，如 DL-assignments）。再把 §0 找到（或没找到）的那个旧文件搬进该去的家——用微课 4 的剪切（Ctrl+X / ⌘X），别复制出第二份来添乱。Fire one · raise the folders (4 min): in your chosen home of files (the cloud drive, or a local course folder) raise three folders named by Week 5's purpose-time-content rule: DL-assignments, DL-site-backups, DL-notes (Chinese or English names both serve). Then move §0's old file (found or finally hunted down) into its rightful home — with micro-lesson 4's cut (Ctrl+X / ⌘X), not a copy that breeds a duplicate.
  2. 第二把 · 设提醒（3 分钟）：照微课 2 的三步，在日历里设一条每周重复的提醒："外部课程 · 一小时"，放在你打算写进选择页的那个时段，提前 10 分钟响，开始日期选第 11 周（主修课开学那周——第 8–10 周还没有课可学，空响只会教你忽略它；微课 2 那条脾气）。再顺手设一条只响一次的："下周一 · 打开第 8 周页面"——让下周的自己被机器接住，也让你这周就亲眼看见提醒真的会响。（第二条是顺手加的，不是硬要求。）Fire two · set the reminder (3 min): by micro-lesson 2's three steps, set one weekly repeating reminder — "External course · one hour" — in the very slot your choice page will state, ringing 10 minutes ahead, with its start date in Week 11 (the week the core course begins — there is nothing to study in Weeks 8–10, and ringing into an empty week only teaches you to ignore it; micro-lesson 2's temperament). Then, while you are there, add one single-shot reminder: "Monday — open the Week 8 page", so next week's you gets caught by a machine, and so you see with your own eyes this week that the ringing works. (The second one is a convenience, not a requirement.)
  3. 第三把 · 备份彩排（3 分钟）：把上周的检索报告正文（或任何一件你舍不得丢的课程作业）备份一次：存进"网站备份"文件夹，或当附件发给自己的邮箱，主题照命名规则写。计时——你会发现不到两分钟。这就是任务最后一步"例行备份"的完整彩排。Fire three · the backup rehearsal (3 min): back up last week's search-report text (or any course work you would hate to lose): into the site-backups folder, or emailed to yourself as an attachment, subject by the naming rule. Time it — under two minutes, you will find. This is the full dress rehearsal of the task's closing "routine backup".
  4. 第四把 · 快捷键三连（2 分钟）：打开笔记（微课 3 的落点），用复制（Ctrl+C）从本页抄走一句你想记住的话，粘贴（Ctrl+V）进笔记，随手补上三行来源；再故意删掉一个词，用撤销（Ctrl+Z）救回来。三个动作各来三遍——手指记住的才是你的。Fire four · three shortcuts (2 min): open your notes (micro-lesson 3's landing place), copy (Ctrl+C) one sentence worth keeping from this page, paste (Ctrl+V) it in, add its three source lines while you're there; then delete a word on purpose and rescue it with undo (Ctrl+Z). Three rounds of each — only what the fingers remember is truly yours.
幕间点拨A word between acts
你刚做的四件事没有一件是"学新知识"——全是把旧手艺装进固定的位置：文件夹用的是第 5 周的命名，备份用的是第 2 周的邮箱，笔记里躺着第 6 周的三行卡。工作台不是新东西，是老手艺各就各位。这正是它可靠的原因：每个角你都已经会了。Not one of the four moves you just made was "learning something new" — each seated an old craft in a fixed place: the folders wear Week 5's names, the backup rides Week 2's email, and Week 6's three-line card lies inside the note. The workspace is nothing new — it is old crafts taking their posts. Which is exactly why it can be trusted: you already command every corner of it.
点火完毕Ignition complete
四个角都冒烟了：文件夹立着、提醒等着响、备份跑过一遍、三个快捷键进了手。开工吧：本周任务——把台子搭完整，录下 1 分钟导览，写下基本功清单，再把"我的外部课程"白纸黑字放上网站。All four corners are smoking: folders standing, a reminder waiting to ring, the backup run once, three shortcuts in hand. To work, then: this week's task — finish the assembly, record the one-minute tour, write the Basics inventory, and set "My external course" on your site in black and white.
