快捷键小集：手上的近路The shortcut set: short-cuts for the hands

快捷键不是炫技——是把每天重复几十次的小动作（复制、粘贴、撤销）从"移动鼠标找菜单"缩短成"手指一按"。一学期算下来省的不止时间，还有注意力：手上的动作越顺，脑子越能留在正事上。本周只要八个，够用一学期。

Shortcuts are not showing off — they shrink the little moves repeated dozens of times a day (copy, paste, undo) from "mouse over to the menu" down to one press. Across a semester that saves more than time; it saves attention — the smoother the hands, the more the mind stays on the actual work. Eight are all this week asks, and eight will last the semester.

诚实的说明书：快捷键靠肌肉记忆，看会没有用，按会才算数——跟做里只练三个（复制 · 粘贴 · 撤销），一周里真用上几次，手自然记住；下周再自然长出下一个。一次背八个的人，通常一个也留不下。手机上没有这张表：触屏世界里长按和多选就是你的"快捷键"。The honest manual: shortcuts live in muscle memory — knowing them counts for nothing, pressing them counts for everything. The follow-along drills just three (copy · paste · undo); a few real uses in a week and the hand keeps them, growing the next one naturally the week after. Whoever memorises all eight at once usually keeps none. No such table exists on a phone: in the touchscreen world, long-press and multi-select are your shortcuts.
