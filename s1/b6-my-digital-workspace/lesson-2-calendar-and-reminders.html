日历与提醒：时间的家Calendar & reminders: the home of time

"我打算每周三晚上学一小时"——这句话说出来的那一刻人人真诚，两周后还记得的寥寥无几。不是意志力的问题，是分工错了：脑子擅长想事，不擅长按时想起事；按时想起，是日历的本职。把"打算"变成日历里一条会响的提醒，约定就从心里搬进了机器里——机器不忘事。

"I plan to study an hour every Wednesday evening" — everyone means it at the moment of saying, and few still remember two weeks on. The failing is not willpower but the division of labour: minds are good at thinking, and bad at thinking of things on time; on-time remembering is a calendar's day job. Turn the plan into a reminder that rings, and the appointment moves out of your head and into a machine — and machines do not forget.

本周只要求一条提醒This week asks for exactly one reminder

  1. 选定时段：想一个每周真正空着的一小时（写作业的黄金时间别用——外部课程排在精力尚可、又不挤功课的位置，比如周三晚 7–8 点或周六上午）。这个时段一会儿要写进"我的外部课程"页——日历里的这条和页面上的那句，说的必须是同一件事。Pick the slot: one hour a week that is genuinely free (not homework's prime time — the external course belongs where energy is decent and schoolwork uncrowded, say Wednesday 7–8 pm or Saturday morning). The slot goes onto your "My external course" page shortly — the calendar entry and the page's sentence must tell the same story.
  2. 设为每周重复：新建日程 → 标题"外部课程 · 一小时" → 重复：每周 → 提醒：提前 10 分钟 → 开始日期选在第 11 周（主修课正式开学那周；新建重复事件时各家日历都让你选开始日期）。设一次，管一学期。Make it repeat weekly: new event → title "External course · one hour" → repeat: weekly → alert: 10 minutes before → start date: Week 11 (the week the core course formally begins; every calendar lets you choose a repeating event's start date). Set once, it serves the semester.
  3. 用哪个日历？手机或电脑自带的就很好——不用注册、不用安装、换设备跟着账号走。这也是课程的推荐起点；等你真觉得不够用了，再换不迟。Which calendar? The one your phone or computer already carries is excellent — nothing to register, nothing to install, and it follows your account across devices. That is the course's recommended start; upgrade only when it genuinely runs short.
这条提醒不是本周的摆设This reminder is no weekly ornament
第 11 周，你要为选定的主修课制定正式的学习计划——用日程工具排出从第 11 周直到寒假的所有学习时段，执行一周后还要复盘调整。用的就是你本周设好的这个日历。本周种一条提醒，第 11 周它就是一整张计划表的地基。In Week 11 you will draw up the formal study plan for your chosen core course — scheduling every study slot from Week 11 to the winter break with a calendar tool, then reviewing and adjusting after a week of running it. The tool will be this very calendar you set up today. Plant one reminder this week, and by Week 11 it is the foundation of an entire plan.

诚实的说明书：提醒不是自律的魔法——它负责"按时想起"，去不去学还是你的事；响了三次都被划掉的提醒，说明时段选错了，换时段，别怪自己。另一条脾气：提醒设得越多，每一条就越不响——什么都提醒等于什么都不提醒。本周只设这一条，让它保持稀罕、保持有效。同一条脾气也解释了为什么起点定在第 11 周：第 8–10 周你还没有主修课要学，让它在那几周空响，等于提前教会自己忽略它。（已经设成本周开始也不必改——第 11 周之前的每一次都只是预告，划掉不算失约。）The honest manual: a reminder is no magic of self-discipline — it handles the on-time remembering; showing up remains yours. A reminder swiped away three weeks running means the slot is wrong: move the slot, spare the guilt. And one more temperament: the more reminders you set, the less each one rings — reminding about everything is reminding about nothing. Set only this one this week; keep it rare, and it stays loud. The same temperament explains the Week-11 start: with no core course to study in Weeks 8–10, a reminder ringing into those empty weeks merely teaches you, in advance, to ignore it. (Already set it to start this week? No need to change — every ring before Week 11 is only a trailer, and swiping it away breaks no promise.)
