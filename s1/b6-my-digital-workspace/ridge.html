山脊Ridge Trail

+1.5 → 4.5 分+1.5 → 4.5 pts约 25 分钟~25 min🚫 独立🚫 Independent

先完成起点营地；这里随时可读。导师按量规计分，起点营地至少 2 分才计山脊。Complete Base Camp first; this page is always readable. Ridge points count when Base Camp earns at least 2.

本关交付：同一作品集里的四角屏幕导览 + 一句答疑准备。Deliverable: a screen tour of all four corners plus one help-call preparation sentence on the same Portfolio page.

  1. 1

    学LEARN 两段微课，练三个动作Two micro-lessons, three moves10 分钟10 min

    微课 4 · 快捷键Lesson 4 · Shortcuts微课 5 · 视频通话礼仪Lesson 5 · Video-call manners

    每页约 5 分钟，含指定视频。读完微课 4，用复制、粘贴、撤销各练一次（Mac 看对应列）；读完微课 5，写一句答疑前要检查什么，放在导览旁。不需要真的预约或安装会议软件。Allow ~5 minutes per page including its specified clip. After lesson 4, practise copy, paste and undo once each (Mac: use the matching column). After lesson 5, add one line beside your tour about what to check before a help call. No booking or meeting-software installation is required.

  2. 2

    做MAKE 录四角导览Record the four-corner tour10 分钟10 min

    发布前：不放真名、正脸照片、学校全名或可定位的信息。屏幕里隐藏邮箱、通知和别人的日程。若误放，先撤下或遮盖，再发布；不因隐私失误扣分。Before publishing: no real names, face photos, full school names or locating details. Hide email addresses, notifications and other people’s appointments. If anything slips through, remove or mask it and republish; privacy slips cost no points.

    约 1 分钟，只录屏幕、不露脸，声音可选。依次展示文件夹、那一条每周提醒、笔记落点、备份副本。只显示外部课程那一条日历，隐藏他人的事。回放检查隐私，不够清楚可重录；时长略有出入不扣分。手机自带录屏同样可用。Aim for ~1 minute: screen only, no face, voice optional. Show your folders, the weekly reminder, notes home and backup copy. Show only the external-course calendar entry; hide other people’s events. Replay to check privacy and retake if unclear; a little over or under the target costs no points. Built-in phone recording works too.

    邮箱备份只展示打开的附件或另存的副本，不录收件箱列表、邮箱地址栏或其他邮件。For an email backup, show only the opened attachment or saved copy, never the inbox list, email address bar or other messages.

  3. 3

    交SUBMIT 更新同一作品集页Update the same Portfolio page5 分钟5 min

    把视频和答疑准备那句话加在原作品集区块，访客模式确认视频可播。发布、备份视频与更新正文，给导师发“B6 已更新”及原链接；公开学习者发给自己。文件过大就用已有的视频托管并嵌入或链接，不要求注册新服务；先检查视频在访客模式可播。视频太大且无独立备份位置时，备份页面正文并注明视频原文件位置即可，不影响本关评分。Add the video and help-call preparation sentence to your existing Portfolio section. Check playback as a visitor. Publish, back up the video and updated text, and email “B6 updated” with the original link to your mentor; open learners email themselves. For a large file, embed or link from video hosting you already use; no new service registration is required. Check visitor playback first. If the video is too large and no independent storage is available, back up the page text and state where the original video lives; this does not reduce the tier score.

山脊清单完成，等待导师评分。想继续？安全恢复测试在登顶。Ridge checklist complete, ready for review. Continue to the Summit for a safe recovery test.

常见问题FAQ

没有云盘、U 盘或第二台设备？No cloud drive, USB stick or second device?

独立副本指不会随着工作文件的修改或删除一起变化的副本：发给自己的附件、不与工作文件夹同步的云端位置，或已有的其他存储位置。相同同步文件夹中的副本、单纯同步都不算。无需购买或安装；没有第二台设备，照样从独立副本打开检查。电脑同盘的非同步练习副本可用于本周练习，但不能防设备损坏，应另外备份重要文件。视频太大又无其他位置？备份页面正文，注明视频原文件存放位置即可；这不是视频的独立备份，以后有条件再补。An independent copy does not change when the working file is edited or deleted: an emailed attachment, cloud storage not synced with the working folder, or other storage already available. A copy in the same synced folder, or sync alone, does not count. No purchase or installation is needed; open the independent copy even without a second device. An unsynced practice copy on the same disk works for this week’s exercise, but cannot protect against device failure; back up important work separately. Video too large and no other location? Back up the page text and state where the original video lives; that is not an independent video backup, and you can add one when feasible.

上周没写选课理由，或这周改主意了？No course reason from last week, or changed your mind?

有上周理由就引一句，再写现在的决定；试学小记也可用。不需要补做 B5 山脊。找不到旧理由，就重读菜单介绍，写“本周补记”及自己的具体理由；不编造旧记录。换课同样达标。Quote a prior course-pick reason or sampling note, then give your current decision. B5 Ridge is not required. If no prior note exists, reread the menu and label your own specific reason “added this week”; never invent a past record. Changing courses earns the same credit.

节假日、卡住或想补交？A holiday, a problem, or a revision?

假期不安排新任务或截止日期；有冲突顺延到下一个工作日。按自己的速度完成，迟交不扣分；更新后邮件导师，附链接与已打码截图，问题通常 24–48 小时回复；分数与一条建议在一周内回复（见量规）。答疑预约方式与平台见开课信。No new tasks or deadlines on university holidays; shift conflicting work to the next working day. Work at your pace; no late penalty. Email your mentor after updating, with links and a masked screenshot if needed; questions usually receive replies in 24–48 hours; scores and one suggestion arrive within a week (see rubric). The welcome letter supplies the video-help booking route and platform.
