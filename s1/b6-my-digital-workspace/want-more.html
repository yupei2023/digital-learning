做Do

  • 下载文件夹大扫除——本周任务只归置了课程文件；周末花二十分钟把下载文件夹清一遍：该进家的进家（照命名规则改名），过期的安装包和重复文件删掉。计时，下次会更快。The downloads clean-up — the task arranged course files only; give the downloads folder twenty weekend minutes: home the keepers (renamed by the rule), delete the stale installers and duplicates. Time it — the next round runs faster.
  • 当一次家里的"备份管理员"——问家人一句："手机里的照片，如果手机今天丢了还在吗？"帮他们把最舍不得的相册备份一份（云端或电脑）。第 5 周你帮家人看过密码，这周帮他们留住照片。Serve a shift as the family backup steward — ask at home: "if the phone vanished today, would its photos survive?" Help back up the album they treasure most (cloud or computer). In Week 5 you guarded the family's passwords; this week, their photographs.
  • 把剩下五个快捷键练进手——跟做只练了三个；把微课 4 表里的另外五个各用十遍。一周后你会发现鼠标去菜单里找"保存"的次数归零了。Grow the other five shortcuts into your hands — the follow-along drilled three; give the table's remaining five ten real uses each. A week on, you will notice the mouse no longer visits the menu for "save".
  • 和工作台立一个"月检"之约——在日历里设一条每月一次的提醒"工作台月检 · 10 分钟"，照讲义末尾的自检问题过一遍：随机开三个文件还认得吗？备份最近一次是什么时候？失修的工作台比没有工作台更误事——因为你以为它还在工作。Make a monthly appointment with the workspace — a recurring calendar reminder, "workspace check · 10 min", running the handout's closing questions: three random files still recognisable? when was the last backup? A workspace fallen into disrepair misleads worse than none at all — because you still believe it is working.
