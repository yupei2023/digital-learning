B6 · 第 7 周B6 · Week 7

起点营地Base Camp

3 分3 pts约 80 分钟~80 min🚫 独立🚫 Independent

必做：作品集一处（四行工作台记录 + 六行基本功清单），另加一页“我的外部课程”。无需录屏也能达标得 3 分；录屏在山脊。Required: a Portfolio section (four-line workspace record + six-line inventory), plus a separate “My external course” page. Full Base Camp earns 3 points without a recording; the tour is on the Ridge.

八个步骤Eight steps

  1. 1

    练PRACTICE 先找一个旧文件Find one old file first1 分钟1 min

    找上周的检索报告或任一旧课程文件，记下花了几秒；没找到也如实记。稍后整理后再找一次。Find last week’s search report or any old course file. Note the seconds; if it is missing, say so. Try again after organising.

  2. 2

    学LEARN 读三段微课Read three micro-lessons15 分钟15 min

    每页约 5 分钟，先看页面指定的视频片段，再读文字。工具打不开时走页面给出的兜底路线，不用安装新软件。Allow ~5 minutes each: watch the specified part of the clip, then read the text. If a tool is unavailable, use the lesson’s fallback; no new software is needed.

    微课 1 · 云盘与同步Lesson 1 · Cloud and sync微课 2 · 日历与提醒Lesson 2 · Calendar and reminders微课 3 · 留得住的笔记Lesson 3 · Notes that stay
  3. 3

    练PRACTICE 搭好工作台四角Set up all four corners17 分钟17 min

    文件：建作业、网站备份、笔记三个文件夹，把课程文件归位。时间：设“外部课程 · 一小时”的每周提醒，提前 10 分钟响，起点为第 11 周；时段与选课页一致。笔记：用已有工具固定一个落点，存一条带三行来源的笔记。备份：把上周报告另存一份到原工作位置之外（邮箱附件或已有备份位置），打开副本确认可读。不要把自动同步当备份。Files: create assignments, site-backups and notes folders; move course files into place. Time: set a weekly “External course · one hour” reminder, 10 minutes ahead, starting in Week 11; match your choice page’s slot. Notes: choose one existing tool and save one note with three source lines. Backup: save last week’s report outside its working location (email attachment or an existing backup location), then open the copy. Automatic sync alone is not backup.

    不用购买 U 盘或找第二台设备；从原位置之外打开独立副本即可。只整理课程文件。整理后再找第 1 步的文件，记下秒数。You need not buy a USB stick or find a second device; open an independent copy outside its original location. Organise only course files. Find step 1’s file again and record the seconds.

    查看课程周次日历See the course-week calendar选读：分步跟做（不用重复）Optional: guided walkthrough (do not repeat)

    在校学生按开课信确认第 11 周日期；公开学习者以自己开始 M3 外部课程学习的那一周为起点。假期当天不学：碰到时将该次学习与提醒顺延到下一个工作日，不用现在编辑整学期。Enrolled students use the welcome letter to confirm Week 11’s date; open learners use the week they begin M3 external-course study. Keep holidays free: when an occurrence falls on one, move that session and reminder to the next working day; you need not edit a whole term now.

  4. 4

    做MAKE 写四行工作台记录Write a four-line workspace record7 分钟7 min

    作品集页新建“我的数字工作台”区块，照下面四行用自己的话写；不需要截图或录屏。记录刚才真正做过的动作，别只列软件名字。Add “My digital workspace” to your Portfolio. Write the four lines below in your own words; screenshots and recording are not required here. Describe what you actually did, rather than just naming apps.

    ① 文件：放在哪里？我怎样找到那个旧文件？
    ② 时间：每周哪一时段、从第几周开始、提前多久提醒？
    ③ 笔记：固定落点在哪里？刚存了什么学习笔记？
    ④ 备份：备份了什么、放在哪里、打开副本的结果？再写一句发布后备份规则。（别写私人路径或邮箱）    ① Files: where do they live, and how did I find the old file?
    ② Time: weekly slot, start week, reminder lead time?
    ③ Notes: fixed home and the learning note just saved?
    ④ Backup: what was copied, where, and what happened when I opened it? Add your post-publish backup rule. (No private paths or email addresses.)
  5. 5

    做MAKE 基本功清单六行Six inventory lines8 分钟8 min

    接在四行记录后，B1–B6 各一行：“扎实 + 一句真实例子”或“还不稳 + 一句具体困难”。两种状态同分。六个技能依次是：邮件与截图、录屏与网站、翻译核对、账号与命名、检索与来源卡、工作台。After the record, add one line each for B1–B6: “solid + one real example” or “still shaky + one specific difficulty.” Both states earn equal credit. Skills in order: email and screenshots; recording and website; checking translation; accounts and naming; search and source cards; workspace.

  6. 6

    做MAKE 我的外部课程：三段My external course: three paragraphs15 分钟15 min

    在自己的网站单独建“我的外部课程”页。① 哪两门：主修 + 探索，写全课名；探索最多 1 小时、两三节。② 为什么：引用 B5 的一句选课理由或试学小记，加一句现在的想法；若旧记录缺失，重读菜单，标“本周补记”写具体理由。换课可以，不必试学过。③ 什么时候：固定时段，与第 11 周起（公开学习者：开始 M3 的那一周）的每周提醒一致。Create a separate “My external course” page on your site. ① Which two: give the full core and explore course names; explore takes at most one hour, two or three lessons. ② Why: quote a B5 course-pick reason or sampling note and add your current thinking; if no prior record exists, reread the menu and label a specific reason “added this week.” Changes are welcome; prior sampling is not required. ③ When: a fixed slot matching your weekly reminder from Week 11 (open learners: the week you begin M3).

    菜单外的课逐条核对：免费、当地能打开、本学期非 AI 主题、时长合规、注册方式清楚；选择页写明理由。在校学生发导师确认，公开学习者自行核对。For an off-menu choice, check all five rules: free, accessible locally, not AI-themed this semester, within duration limits, and clear registration requirements. State reasons on the page. Enrolled students send it to their mentor for confirmation; open learners check it themselves.

  7. 7

    交SUBMIT 发布、登记、备份Publish, register, back up13 分钟13 min

    发布前：不放真名、正脸照片、学校全名或可定位的信息。屏幕里隐藏邮箱、通知和别人的日程。若误放，先撤下或遮盖，再发布；不因隐私失误扣分。Before publishing: no real names, face photos, full school names or locating details. Hide email addresses, notifications and other people’s appointments. If anything slips through, remove or mask it and republish; privacy slips cost no points.

    发布作品集和选课页，用访客模式确认两页可读；两个链接登记到导师登记表（B6）。公开学习者把两个链接发给自己。发布后把两页正文各另存一份到原工作位置之外，打开确认；无需为备份确认再改原文，在本页清单确认即可。Publish your Portfolio and choice page; check both as a visitor. Register both links on your mentor’s tracking sheet (B6). Open learners email both links to themselves. After publishing, save both page texts outside the working location and open them to check; confirm this in this page’s checklist; no edit to the published record is needed.

  8. 8

    思REFLECT 三行日志Three-line log4 分钟4 min

    日志页写：① 整理前后找同一文件各用了几秒，学到了什么？② 还有哪里卡住？③ 你认为“学习”是什么？给出一件亲身经历，留给下周 M1。下次更新日志后也备份。On your log page: ① Seconds to find the same file before and after; what did you learn? ② What still troubles you? ③ What do you think “learning” means? Give one personal experience for next week’s M1. Back up the log after updating it.

常见问题FAQ

没有云盘、U 盘或第二台设备？No cloud drive, USB stick or second device?

独立副本指不会随着工作文件的修改或删除一起变化的副本：发给自己的附件、不与工作文件夹同步的云端位置，或已有的其他存储位置。相同同步文件夹中的副本、单纯同步都不算。无需购买或安装；没有第二台设备，照样从独立副本打开检查。电脑同盘的非同步练习副本可用于本周练习，但不能防设备损坏，应另外备份重要文件。视频太大又无其他位置？备份页面正文，注明视频原文件存放位置即可；这不是视频的独立备份，以后有条件再补。An independent copy does not change when the working file is edited or deleted: an emailed attachment, cloud storage not synced with the working folder, or other storage already available. A copy in the same synced folder, or sync alone, does not count. No purchase or installation is needed; open the independent copy even without a second device. An unsynced practice copy on the same disk works for this week’s exercise, but cannot protect against device failure; back up important work separately. Video too large and no other location? Back up the page text and state where the original video lives; that is not an independent video backup, and you can add one when feasible.

上周没写选课理由，或这周改主意了？No course reason from last week, or changed your mind?

有上周理由就引一句，再写现在的决定；试学小记也可用。不需要补做 B5 山脊。找不到旧理由，就重读菜单介绍，写“本周补记”及自己的具体理由；不编造旧记录。换课同样达标。Quote a prior course-pick reason or sampling note, then give your current decision. B5 Ridge is not required. If no prior note exists, reread the menu and label your own specific reason “added this week”; never invent a past record. Changing courses earns the same credit.

节假日、卡住或想补交？A holiday, a problem, or a revision?

假期不安排新任务或截止日期；有冲突顺延到下一个工作日。按自己的速度完成，迟交不扣分；更新后邮件导师，附链接与已打码截图，问题通常 24–48 小时回复；分数与一条建议在一周内回复（见量规）。答疑预约方式与平台见开课信。No new tasks or deadlines on university holidays; shift conflicting work to the next working day. Work at your pace; no late penalty. Email your mentor after updating, with links and a masked screenshot if needed; questions usually receive replies in 24–48 hours; scores and one suggestion arrive within a week (see rubric). The welcome letter supplies the video-help booking route and platform.
