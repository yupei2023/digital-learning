B6 量规B6 Rubric

量规的三行就是本周的三条目标。第 ① ② 行计分（3 + 2 = 5 分），第 ③ 行不计分、完成即可。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。本任务评的是搭建与盘点的动作，和记录的诚实——清单里写"还不稳"与写"扎实"同分，走兜底路线与用云盘同分；不评你的技能是否完美，不评你选了哪门课，也不评文笔。

The three rows are this week's three objectives. Rows ① ② carry points (3 + 2 = 5); row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence. What this task scores is the act of assembling and stocktaking, and the honesty of the record — "still shaky" on the inventory scores as "solid" does, and the fallback route as the cloud drive does. Never scored: the perfection of your skills, which course you chose, or your prose.

本周的知识点（同步与备份的分别、提醒的脾气、快捷键、视频通话礼仪）不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。隐私规则是全课程的底线（见下方"规范要求"）：导览或页面里误放了隐私，导师提醒改正，不扣分。This week's knowledge points (sync versus backup, reminders' temperament, the shortcuts, video-call manners) are not scored here — the self-quiz checks them, ungraded. The privacy rules are the course-wide floor (Form requirements below): private details slipping into the tour or a page draw a reminder and a fix, never a deduction.
