三关怎么玩How the tiers work

起点营地达标得 3 分，是 5 分里的及格线；山脊、登顶选做。导师按量规评分：起点营地至少 2 分才计山脊；山脊至少 1 分才计登顶。公开学习者用同一量规自评。Base Camp meeting the rubric earns 3 of 5 points, the pass mark; Ridge and Summit are optional. Mentors score with the rubric: Base Camp must earn at least 2 before Ridge counts; Ridge at least 1 before Summit counts. Open learners self-score with the same rubric.

总预算 80 + 25 + 15 = 120 分钟，含指定视频片段、发布和日志；自测及拓展另为选做。两份交付：作品集中的工作台记录与清单，以及单独的“我的外部课程”页。挑战只更新原作品集页。The budget is 80 + 25 + 15 = 120 minutes, including the specified clips, publishing and log. Quiz and extensions are optional extras. Two deliverables: the workspace record and inventory on your Portfolio, and a separate “My external course” page. Challenges update the same Portfolio page.

徽章来自本浏览器的清单，表示已准备好接受评分；它不是成绩，也不会跨设备同步。山路只是任务顺序的比喻，勾选不代表实际掌握。Badges come from this browser’s checklists and mean ready for review; they are not grades and do not sync across devices. The mountain stands for task order; ticking a box does not demonstrate mastery.

起点营地：缺项怎么数Base Camp: counting omissions

以下每组最多算一个缺项。同一问题不重复扣。登记、日志、练习与微课不另计分；无登记但能看到的证据先评，导师提醒补登记。Each group below counts at most once. Never count the same problem twice. Registration, log, practice and lesson reading carry no separate points; score visible evidence even if registration needs a reminder.

  1. 文件记录缺失，或没有具体整理/找回动作。Files line missing, or no specific organising/retrieval action.
  2. 时间记录缺失，或没有与选择页一致、从第 11 周起（公开学习者：开始 M3 的那一周）的每周提醒。Time line missing, or no matching weekly reminder starting in Week 11 (open learners: the week they begin M3).
  3. 笔记记录缺失，或没有固定落点与实际笔记。Notes line missing, or no fixed home and actual note.
  4. 备份记录缺失；或缺打开独立副本的结果；或缺发布后备份规则（本组最多算一项；清单确认实际备份，不另扣分）。Backup line missing; or independent-copy opening result missing; or post-publish backup rule missing (one omission maximum for this group; actual backup is confirmed in the checklist without a separate deduction).
  5. 六行清单不齐，或有行缺“状态 + 具体例子/困难”（整张清单最多算一个缺项）。Inventory incomplete, or a line lacks status plus a specific example/difficulty (at most one omission for the whole inventory).
  6. 主修或探索课名缺失，或菜单外课没按五条规则说明、在校学生未发导师确认。Core/explore name missing, or an off-menu course lacks the five-rule explanation or (enrolled students) has not been sent for mentor confirmation.
  7. 选课理由缺失或只剩“有趣”等形容词，没有自己的具体理由。Course reason missing or just adjectives such as “interesting,” without a specific personal reason.
  8. 选课固定时段缺失或与提醒不一致（与时间记录的同一问题只算一次）。Fixed choice-page slot missing or mismatched with the reminder (count the same scheduling issue only once across this and the time line).

四行记录评动作与可检查的说明，不要求截图；六行清单“还不稳”与“扎实”同分。选课维持或更改同分；云盘、邮箱或其他独立备份路线同分；同一设备上另一位置的非同步副本，本周也满足第 4 组。录屏时长约 1 分钟，不设时长扣分。The four-line record is scored for actions and checkable descriptions; screenshots are not required. “Still shaky” and “solid” inventory entries earn equal credit. Keeping or changing a course earns equal credit, as do cloud, email and other independent backup routes; an unsynced copy in another location on the same device also meets group 4 this week. The tour target is ~1 minute, with no duration deduction.

不计分的共同要求Shared ungraded requirements

发布前：不放真名、正脸照片、学校全名或可定位的信息。屏幕里隐藏邮箱、通知和别人的日程。若误放，先撤下或遮盖，再发布；不因隐私失误扣分。Before publishing: no real names, face photos, full school names or locating details. Hide email addresses, notifications and other people’s appointments. If anything slips through, remove or mask it and republish; privacy slips cost no points.

本周不使用生成式 AI；清单和选课理由由自己写。家长一起挑时段、讨论选课，欢迎。新注册先告诉家长；需要手机号时，先征得家长同意。共用电脑用完登出。No generative AI this week; write your inventory and course reasons yourself. Parents may help weigh choices and study times. Tell a parent before new registrations; obtain their agreement before supplying a phone number. Sign out after using a shared computer.

引用别人介绍配来源；自己的旧记录不必给自己做来源卡。若证据显示代写，按课程学术诚信程序核实，不凭文风猜测。允许修改重交；迟交不扣分。导师收到链接或更新邮件后一周内回复分数与一条建议，两个导师先用同一份样例对齐尺度。Cite quoted course descriptions; your own prior notes need no source card. If evidence suggests substituted authorship, follow the course’s integrity process; do not infer it from writing style. Revisions are welcome, with no late penalty. Mentors reply with a score and one suggestion within a week of receiving the links or update email; both mentors first calibrate with the same sample.

B6 总分 5 分，属于第一学期数字基本功 B1–B6 共 30 分。B6 contributes 5 points to Semester 1’s 30-point Digital Basics B1–B6 total.
