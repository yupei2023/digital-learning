标注，与带截图提问Annotate, and ask with a screenshot

一张原始截图会说话，但说得含糊：画面里有十个按钮，别人不知道你指哪一个。标注就是替截图把话说清——画个框、加个箭头，重点自己跳出来。学会标注，你就解锁了这门课全年的“求助姿势”：一张标注好的截图，加三句话。

A raw screenshot talks, but it mumbles: ten buttons in the frame, and nobody knows which one you mean. Annotation makes the screenshot speak plainly — a box here, an arrow there, and the point leaps out by itself. Master it and you unlock this course's all-year way of asking for help: one annotated screenshot, plus three sentences.

三样标注，够用一年Three marks cover the whole year

标注Mark它说什么What it says在哪里点Where to find it
框The box“看这里。”把问题所在圈出来，读图的人第一眼就落在对的地方。“Look here.” Circle the trouble spot and the reader's first glance lands exactly right.手机：截图预览 → 编辑/标记 → 方框工具。电脑：截图预览或“照片/画图”里的形状工具。Phone: screenshot preview → Edit/Markup → the rectangle tool. Computer: the capture preview, or the shapes tool in Photos/Paint.
箭头（或一小句字）The arrow (or a short label)“从这里到这里。”指出点了什么、期待去哪里；一小句文字（“点了没反应”）常常比箭头更省事。“From here to there.” Shows what was pressed and where you expected to go; a short label (“pressed — nothing happened”) often beats the arrow itself.同一套标记工具里，箭头与文字都在。The same markup kit holds both arrow and text.
打码The mask“这部分不给看。”用马赛克或色块盖住真名、账号、别人的消息——发出去之前，隐私先藏好。“This part is not for viewing.” Mosaic or a solid block over real names, account details, other people's messages — privacy hidden before anything travels.手机标记工具里的马赛克/荧光笔调成实色；没有就画一个涂满的框。裁剪掉那一块也算打码。The mosaic tool, or a highlighter set to solid; failing both, draw a filled box. Cropping the region away counts too.

这门课的求助姿势：一张图 + 三句话This course's way of asking: one image + three sentences

遇到问题时，最没用的求助是“老师，我打不开”；最有用的求助长下面这样——一封规范邮件（上周的六个部位照旧），正文三句话，附一张标注好的截图：When something breaks, the least useful plea is “it doesn't work”; the most useful one looks like this — a proper email (last week's six parts, unchanged), a three-sentence body, one annotated screenshot attached:

  1. 我想做什么：“我想把'学习日志'页发布出来。”What I was trying to do: “I was trying to publish my Learning-log page.”
  2. 我做了什么：“我点了右上角的'发布'按钮。”What I did: “I pressed the Publish button, top right.”
  3. 出现了什么：“出来截图里这个提示，我没看懂——红框处。”What happened: “This message in the screenshot appeared and I can't read it — see the red box.”
为什么导师喜欢这样的求助Why mentors love this kind of ask
“我打不开”有一百种可能，导师要来回问三封邮件才摸到现场；一张标注好的截图把现场直接搬了过去，多数问题一封回信就解决。帮别人省力，就是帮自己提速——这也是 24–48 小时回复节奏下最划算的做法。“It doesn't work” has a hundred possible meanings, and pinning one down costs three rounds of email; an annotated screenshot carries the scene itself across, and most problems die in a single reply. Saving the helper's effort is how you speed up your own answer — the smartest play under the 24–48-hour rhythm.
