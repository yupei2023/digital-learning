留证据工具包The evidence kit

截图、标注、录屏都是可逆的动作：截坏了删掉重来，录砸了再录一条——所以这一页不设彩排，直接上手，把第 0 节开的头做完。三幕全部在你自己的设备上完成，不发给任何人。

Screenshots, annotation and recording are all reversible: a bad capture is deleted and retaken, a botched recording re-recorded — so this page skips the rehearsal and goes straight to your hands, finishing what §0 began. All three acts stay on your own devices, sent to no one.

第一幕 · 电脑一张，手机一张（7 分钟）Act 1 · One on the computer, one on the phone (7 min)

  1. 电脑：用微课 1 表里你这台电脑的截法，框选本页的标题部分截一张。然后立刻做一件事：找到它——剪贴板预览？桌面？截图文件夹？知道图去了哪里，和会截一样重要。Computer: with your machine's method from the micro-lesson 1 table, region-capture this page's title. Then do one thing at once: find the file — clipboard preview? desktop? a screenshots folder? Knowing where it went matters as much as capturing it.
  2. 手机：用你手机的按键组合把任意页面截一张，到相册里找到它。两台设备都截到了？本周目标 ③ 已完成一半。Phone: capture any page with your phone's button combo, then find it in the gallery. Both devices done? Objective ③ is half finished.
  3. 顺手练一次删除重截：把不满意的那张删掉，再截一张更准的——体会一下“可逆”是什么感觉。Practise the delete-and-retake once while you are here: remove the worse capture and take a tighter one — feel what “reversible” means.

第二幕 · 标注一张，配一句问题（6 分钟）Act 2 · Annotate one, add the one-line question (6 min)

  1. 挑一张刚才的截图，打开标记工具：画一个框圈住画面里的某个按钮或某句话。Pick one of your fresh screenshots and open the markup tool: draw a box around some button or sentence in the frame.
  2. 在旁边写一句能让陌生人看懂的问题，句式照微课 2：“我想＿＿，我点了＿＿，出现了红框里的＿＿。”写在图上或备忘录里都行。Beside it, write one question a stranger could act on, in the micro-lesson 2 pattern: “I wanted ____, I pressed ____, and the thing in the red box appeared.” On the image or in a note, either way.
  3. 再练一次打码：假设图里有你的真名，用马赛克或色块把任意一小块盖住。以后每张要发出去的截图，都先过这一道。Rehearse the mask too: pretending your real name is in the frame, cover any small region with mosaic or a solid block. Every screenshot that ever leaves your device passes this gate first.

第三幕 · 录 30 秒（12 分钟）Act 3 · Record 30 seconds (12 min)

跟着视频找到手机的录制入口；电脑上练过的同学用电脑也行——设备任选，录成一段就算完成。Follow the clip to your phone's recording switch — or use the computer if that is where you practised; either device counts, one finished clip completes the act.

  1. 录前三件事（微课 3）：清场 · 开勿扰 · 想好一句话。今天的一句话就用：“这是我打开本课程网站的过程。”The three pre-record steps (micro-lesson 3): clear the stage · do-not-disturb on · know your one sentence. Today's sentence: “this is me opening the course website.”
  2. 录约 30 秒：先把浏览器打开（Windows 用 Game Bar 的同学，这一步必须在按录制之前——它只录当前应用，录不了桌面），再开始录：进入课程首页 → 点进本周页面 → 停止录制。长一点短一点都不要紧。Record about 30 seconds: open the browser first (Game Bar users on Windows: do this before pressing record — it films only the current app, never the desktop), then record: the course home → this week's page → stop. A little over or under is fine.
  3. 回放一遍：画面里有没有闯进来的通知或无关窗口？有就删掉重录一条——这也是练习的一部分。Play it back: did a notification or stray window wander in? Delete and re-record if so — that retake is part of the practice.
