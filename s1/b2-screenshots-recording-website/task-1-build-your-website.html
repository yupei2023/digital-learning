要一个按钮一个按钮的操作说明？Want click-by-click instructions?
另有一页从零建站的分步手册：注册、选模板、加页面、改文字与图片、发布、取到对的那个网址、把网站设为不被搜索引擎收录，逐个按钮写清楚，另附新手最常掉的七个坑。它是参考手册，不是一课——不计入本周的 120 分钟：本周的分钟里已经包含了“动手”的时间（跟做 20 + 任务 58），这一页只是你动手时摊在旁边的那张纸，做到哪一步翻哪一步。There is a separate page: a step-by-step build guide — signing up, choosing a template, adding pages, editing text and images, publishing, fetching the right address, and setting the site hidden from search engines, button by button, with the seven pits beginners fall into. It is a manual, not a lesson — and it is outside the week's 120 minutes: the doing is already paid for in those minutes (20 follow-along + 58 task); this page is only the sheet lying open beside you, read at whichever step you are on.

要做什么What to do

用开课信指定的建站平台注册一个账号（只需邮箱、免费；平台名与注册说明开课前随信公布，公开学习者见课程说明的「你的建站平台」框），选一个模板，建三页——关于我 · 学习日志 · 作品集；在日志页写下第一条日志；用“访客的眼睛”做一遍隐私检查；然后发布 → 设为不被搜索引擎收录 → 复制链接 → 登记。最后给三页各存一张截图作备份，并录一段约 30 秒的网站导览（不计分，留在自己设备上）。从本周起，这门课的“提交”就是“发布到自己网站 + 登记链接”。

Register on the builder named in the welcome letter (email only, free; the name and sign-up notes arrive with the letter before launch — open learners find them in the course guide's “Your site builder” box), pick a template, and make three pages — About me · Learning log · Portfolio; write the first entry on the log page; run the visitor's-eyes privacy check; then publish → set it hidden from search engines → copy the link → register it. Finish with one backup screenshot per page and a ~30-second tour of the site recorded (ungraded, kept on your own device). From this week on, “submitting” in this course means “publish on your own site + register the link”.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 三页齐全、页名清楚：关于我（About me）· 学习日志（Learning log）· 作品集（Portfolio）。中英文页名任选，认得出就行。All three pages, clearly named: About me · Learning log · Portfolio. Chinese or English page names, whichever — recognisable is what counts.
  • 第一条日志三样俱全：第 2 周写在草稿里的三行日志（搬上来）；“我的第 2 周基线”的逐项结果（从你发给自己的那封邮件里抄来——搜主题“我的第 2 周基线”就能找到）；本周的三行日志。抄基线时把每行写成“第 1 项 · 3 分”的样子（或加一句“对照第 2 周自测页”）——过两个月回看，还认得每个数字说的是哪一项。The first log entry carries all three parts: the Week-2 three-line log moved in from your draft; the per-item “My Week-2 baseline” results, copied from the email you sent yourself (search that exact subject to find it); and this week's three lines. When copying the baseline, write each line as “item 1 · 3 pts” (or add “see the Week-2 self-check page”) — so months later every number still explains itself.
  • 隐私四条：英文名或化名（与上周邮件一致）· 不放正脸照片 · 不写学校全名 · 不放别人的信息。误放了不扣分，改掉重新发布即可——但发布前先查最省心。The four privacy rules: English name or pseudonym (matching last week's email) · no face photos · no full school name · no one else's information. A slip costs no points — edit and republish — but checking before publishing is the calmer road.
  • 已发布 + 不被搜索引擎收录：两个开关都要拨——发布让网站存在，不收录让它只对拿链接的人可见。Published + hidden from search engines: both switches thrown — publishing makes the site exist, no-index keeps it visible only to holders of the link.
  • 链接已登记：登记到导师的登记表，任务编号 B2——登记表网址将在开课信中公布。公开学习者：把链接发进自己的邮箱（主题“我的网站 · 化名”）。The link registered: on your mentor's tracking sheet, task number B2 — the sheet's address is announced in the welcome letter. Open learners: email the link to yourself (subject “my website · pseudonym”). 开课前公布published before launch
  • 🚫 独立：全程不使用 AI——包括“关于我”和日志的文字。写得朴素完全没关系，它必须是你的。🚫 Independent: no AI anywhere — About-me and log text included. Plain is perfectly fine; it just has to be yours.

每页写什么（够用就好）What goes on each page (enough is enough)

关于我：三小段——我是谁（化名）；我为什么学这门课（上周附件里那段整理一下就能用）；这学期最想学会的一件事。学习日志：第一条日志（上面三样）；给这一页定个格式：一周一条，最新的在最上面。作品集：本周只放一句占位，比如“作品从第 8 周起陆续入住 Works arrive from Week 8 on”——第 15–16 周你会在这一页整理出“作品集 v1”。About me: three short parts — who I am (pseudonym); why I am taking this course (last week's email paragraph, tidied — you drafted it in Act 1 of the follow-along); the one thing I most want to learn this semester. Learning log: the first entry (the three parts above), and a habit for the page — one entry per week, newest on top. Portfolio: a single placeholder line this week, e.g. “Works arrive from Week 8 on” — in Weeks 15–16 this page becomes Portfolio v1.

步骤Steps

  1. 注册并选模板（8 分钟）：按开课信的说明注册（只需邮箱；注册前和家里人说一声；公开学习者：平台名与注册说明见课程说明的「你的建站平台」框）。每个按钮在哪里，见建站手册第 1–2 步（不计入本周分钟）。进模板库，给自己 5 分钟，从“简洁 / 个人 / 作品集”类里挑一个就出发——模板随时能换肉，别在这里耗掉半小时。Register and pick a template (8 min): sign up as the welcome letter describes (email only; tell your family first; open learners: the builder's name and notes live in the course guide's “Your site builder” box). Where every button sits: Steps 1–2 of the build guide (outside this week's minutes). In the gallery, give yourself five minutes and pick from the clean/personal/portfolio shelf — the flesh on a template changes any time, so don't spend half an hour here.
  2. “关于我”（8 分钟）：把模板页改名“关于我”，删掉假字，把跟做第一幕写好的三小段搬进来。头像位不放正脸照——图案、风景、涂鸦都好。About me (8 min): rename a template page, clear the dummy text, and move in the three parts you drafted in Act 1 of the follow-along. No face in the avatar slot — a pattern, a landscape, a doodle all serve.
  3. “学习日志” + 第一条日志（12 分钟）：建页、改名，写下第一条：第 2 周三行日志 + 基线逐项结果 + （最后再补）本周三行。给条目标个“第 2–3 周”，以后一周一条往上摞。Learning log + the first entry (12 min): make the page, rename it, write the first entry — the Week-2 three lines + the baseline results + (added last) this week's three. Label it “Weeks 2–3” and stack one entry per week above it from now on.
  4. “作品集”占位（4 分钟）：建页、改名、写一句占位。三个房间到齐。The Portfolio placeholder (4 min): page, name, one line. All three rooms stand.
  5. “访客的眼睛”检查（6 分钟）：想象一个陌生人拿到链接：TA 能看到真名吗？正脸？学校？别人的信息？逐页扫一遍，对照发布检查单。拿不准的内容，就多问一句：这条信息能帮陌生人定位到真实的我吗？能定位的删掉或打码（小区门口的照片——能，删）；定位不到的放心留（最喜欢的乐队——不能，留）。The visitor's-eyes check (6 min): imagine a stranger holding your link — can they see a real name? a face? a school? anyone else's details? Sweep every page against the launch checklist. When unsure, ask one more question: could this piece of information help a stranger locate the real me? If it could — the photo of your estate gate, say — delete or mask it; if it could not — a favourite band — it may stay.
  6. 发布 · 设不收录 · 复制并登记链接（6 分钟）：点发布；到网站设置里关掉“允许搜索引擎收录”（开关在哪里）；复制网址（当心：编辑器的网址不是网站的网址，两者的分别），登记到登记表（网址见开课信），再发进自己邮箱存一份。然后用手机（或无痕窗口——浏览器菜单里的“无痕 / 隐私浏览”，打开后网站不认识你）打开链接——亲眼确认访客看到的样子。看到要改的地方？记下来，下一步就改。Publish, no-index, copy and register (6 min): press Publish; in the site settings turn search-engine indexing off (where that switch lives); copy the address (careful: the editor's address is not the site's — telling them apart), register it on the tracking sheet (address in the welcome letter), and email yourself a spare. Then open the link on your phone (or in a private window — the browser menu's “incognito / private browsing”, where the site doesn't know you) — see with your own eyes what a visitor sees. Something to fix? Note it down; the next step is where you fix it.
  7. 改一改，再发布一次（4 分钟）：上一步用访客的眼睛看出来的问题——模板假字没删干净、手机上字压字、哪一页少了标题——回编辑器改掉，然后再点一次“发布”。这是七个坑里最常见的那一个：编辑器里改好了，网址上不会自己跟着变。手机视图也在这一步顺手扫一眼——你的导师多半就是在手机上打开你的网站。Fix, then publish again (4 min): whatever the visitor's eyes turned up — dummy text still standing, overlapping lines on a phone, a page missing its title — go back into the editor, change it, and press Publish a second time. This is the commonest of the seven pits: a change made in the editor does not travel to the address by itself. Sweep the mobile view while you are here — your mentor will most likely open your site on a phone.
  8. 备份截图（4 分钟）：给发布后的三页各截一张图，存进一个叫“我的网站备份”的相簿或文件夹。以后每次大改并发布后，都补一轮——用的正是第 2 周学的手艺；这个习惯 B6 会升级成完整的备份体系。Backup screenshots (4 min): capture each published page once and keep the images in an album or folder called “site backups”. Repeat after every big edit-and-publish — Week 2’s craft at work, and a habit B6 later grows into a full backup system.
  9. 录一段约 30 秒的网站导览（6 分钟）：按微课 3 的“录前三件事”——清场、开勿扰、想好一句话（今天这句就是“这是我的网站，三个房间”）——然后录：首页 → 关于我 → 学习日志 → 作品集，停。回放一遍，画面里闯进通知或无关窗口就删掉重录。录好的视频留在你自己的设备上，不用上传、不用发给任何人；免费建站平台传视频普遍受限，这一段只是给第 7 周那段 1 分钟工作台导览做的热身。Record a ~30-second tour of your site (6 min): follow micro-lesson 3's three pre-record steps — clear the stage, do-not-disturb on, know your one sentence (today's is “this is my site, and these are its three rooms”) — then record: front page → About me → Learning log → Portfolio, stop. Play it back; if a notification or stray window wandered in, delete and re-record. The clip stays on your own device — nothing to upload, nobody to send it to; free builders are generally stingy about video, and this half-minute exists as the warm-up for Week 7's one-minute workspace tour.

评分 · 量规第 ① ② 行（3 + 2 = 5 分）Scoring · Rubric rows ① ② (3 + 2 = 5 pts)

第 ① 行看房子本身：三页齐、已发布、已设不收录、链接已登记。第 ② 行看第一条日志：三样俱全地立在日志页上。评的是投入与格式规范——模板普通、文字朴素都不扣分；隐私误放也不扣分（改掉即可），房间缺了才扣。完整量规Row ① scores the house itself: three pages, published, no-indexed, link registered. Row ② scores the first log entry: all three parts standing on the log page. Engagement and correctness of form — an ordinary template and plain prose lose nothing, a privacy slip loses nothing (just fix it); missing rooms are what cost. Full rubric → 量规页rubric.html

常见问题FAQ

基线邮件找不到了，第一条日志怎么办？I can't find the baseline email — what about the first entry?

先在邮箱里搜主题“我的第 2 周基线”（一字不差是当时的要求，正是为了今天能搜到）。真找不到：把还记得的逐项分凭记忆写上，注明“基线为回忆估计”，并回自测页照实再做一遍补发给自己。日志照写，不影响本周评分。

Search your inbox for the exact subject “我的第 2 周基线 My Week-2 baseline” — word-for-word was the rule precisely so today's search would land. Truly gone? Write what you remember, mark it “baseline estimated from memory”, and redo the self-check page honestly, re-mailing yourself. The entry still counts; this week's score is unaffected.

模板里一堆用不上的页面和板块。The template came with pages and sections I don't need.

删掉或隐藏就行——模板是毛坯房，多余的隔断拆掉不心疼。只要最后站着三个房间：关于我、学习日志、作品集。

Delete or hide them — a template is an unfinished house, and spare partitions come down guilt-free. All that must stand at the end: About me, Learning log, Portfolio.

改完的内容，访客那边怎么没变？I edited, but visitors still see the old version.

十有八九是忘了再点“发布”。编辑器里的修改只是草稿，每次改完都要发布一次才对外生效——这是所见即所得建站最常见的“坑”，掉进去一次就记住了。

Nine times in ten, Publish wasn't pressed again. Edits in the editor are drafts; each round of changes goes live only on the next Publish — the single most common builder trap, and one fall teaches it forever.

注册或发布时平台要我升级付费。The platform is asking me to upgrade and pay.

这门课全程只用免费版：免费子域名、带平台小广告都完全没问题。任何“升级”弹窗一律点掉；关不掉、或找不到免费发布的路——截图 + 三句话，邮件导师。

This course runs on the free tier throughout: a free subdomain and the platform's small ads are entirely fine. Dismiss every upgrade pop-up; if one won't close or the free publish path hides itself — screenshot + three sentences, email your mentor.

我是公开学习者，怎么“评分”？I am an open learner — how do I get scored?

发布并把链接发给自己之后，打开量规页逐行自评，分数记在日志里。第 15 周整理作品集时，这个网站本身就是你第一件、也是最大的一件作品。再一个建议（选做）：把链接发给家里人，带他们逛一遍三个房间，讲讲“不被搜索引擎收录”是怎么回事——能讲清楚才是真学会，你的网站也就有了第一位真访客。

After publishing and emailing yourself the link, self-score row by row against the rubric and note the result in your log. Come Week 15, this site is itself your first and largest artifact. One more suggestion (optional): send the link to your family and walk them through the three rooms, explaining what hidden-from-search means — explaining it clearly is the proof you own it, and it gives your site its first real visitor.

网站能让家里人看吗？Can my family see the site?

当然——把链接发给他们就行（不被搜索收录只挡搜索引擎，不挡拿链接的人）。带他们逛逛你的三个房间；愿意的话，把“我为什么学这门课”读给他们听。

Of course — hand them the link (no-index blocks search engines, not link-holders). Walk them through your three rooms; read them your “why I am taking this course” if you like.
