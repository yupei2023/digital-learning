这一页怎么用How to use this page

先说清一件事：Wix 是本课程首选的建站平台，开课前教师会实测确认（能不能顺畅打开、注册是不是只要邮箱、有没有“不被搜索引擎收录”的开关）。实测通过，开课信与课程说明的「你的建站平台」框就写 Wix，这一页从头到尾都作数；万一没通过，课程改用备选平台，只有本页最后一节需要换——前面的“为什么”一句都不用改。One thing first: Wix is this course's first choice of builder, and the instructor tests it before launch (does it open smoothly, is sign-up really email-only, is there a hide-from-search switch). If it passes, the welcome letter and the course guide's “Your site builder” box will name Wix and this page holds end to end; if it does not, the course moves to the fallback platform and only this page's last section changes — not one sentence of the “why” above it.

这不是一课，是一本说明书：手放在键盘上的时候翻开它，做到哪一步看哪一步。它不占本周的 120 分钟——微课 25 分钟讲“为什么”，跟做 25 分钟加任务 50 分钟才是“动手”的时间，这一页只是你动手时摊在旁边的那张纸。读它不算学习时间，用它才算。This is not a lesson, it is a manual: open it with your hands already on the keyboard and read only the step you are on. It takes none of the week's 120 minutes — the 25 minutes of micro-lessons carry the why, the 25-minute follow-along and the 50-minute task are the doing; this page is just the sheet lying open beside you while you do it. Reading it is not study time; using it is.

要记住的东西在微课 2 的五个动作里：选模板 → 改文字与图片 → 发布 → 复制链接 → 设为不被搜索引擎收录。那五个动作换到任何一家建站平台都成立，值得记；这一页只是把它们翻译成 Wix 上的按钮名——按钮名会变，动作不会。What is worth remembering lives in micro-lesson 2's five moves: pick a template → edit text and images → publish → copy your link → set it hidden from search engines. Those five hold on any builder, so they are the part to keep; this page only translates them into Wix's button names — and button names change while the moves do not.

开工前，摆好四样东西Before you start, lay out four things

  1. 一台电脑。诚实地说：Wix 的编辑器是为大屏幕和鼠标做的，从零建站请用电脑——挑模板、加页面、改设置这些事在手机上很难做。建好以后，手机上可以做一些轻量修改（改文字、换图片、再发布一次），但这项功能 Wix 自己写明“尚未对所有用户开放”，你的账号有没有要试了才知道。只有手机怎么办？见本页最后一节的“只有手机”。A computer. Honestly: the Wix editor is built for a large screen and a mouse, so build from scratch on a computer — choosing a template, adding pages and changing settings are all hard work on a phone. Once the site exists, light edits from a phone are possible (retype text, swap an image, publish again), but Wix's own help says that feature “is not yet available for all users”, so whether your account has it is something only trying will tell. Phone only? See “Phone only” at the foot of this page.
  2. 第 1 周准备好的邮箱，以及你能想起来的密码。The email address you set up in Week 1, and a password you can actually recall.
  3. 你的化名——和第 2 周邮件里用的那一个一模一样，全课程一致。Your pseudonym — the very one from the Week-2 email, kept consistent all course.
  4. 三页的内容先想好（在纸上或备忘录里都行）：关于我三小段、第一条日志的三样、作品集的一句占位。先想内容再开编辑器，能省下一半时间——对着空白页现编，是最耗人的建站方式。The words for the three pages, thought out first (paper or a notes app both serve): the three short parts of About me, the three parts of the first log entry, one placeholder line for Portfolio. Words before editor saves you half the time — inventing text while staring at a blank canvas is the most exhausting way to build a site.

还有一件不属于电脑的事：注册前和家里人说一声（第 1 周第 4 步约定过）。And one thing that is not about equipment: tell your family before you register (as agreed in Week 1, Step 4).

第 1 步 · 注册（只要邮箱）Step 1 · Sign up (email only)

  1. 打开 wix.com，点注册 Sign Up。Open wix.com and click Sign Up.
  2. 用邮箱 + 自设密码注册。页面上还会给出“用 Google / Apple / Facebook 账号继续”的按钮——本课程一律用邮箱：那三个在大陆不一定用得了，而且会把你的建站账号绑在另一个账号上。密码至少 6 位（本课程建议 12 位以上），可以用大小写字母、数字和 _ - ! @ # $ % ^ & *，但不能含英文句点。Register with an email address and a password you choose. The page also offers “continue with Google / Apple / Facebook” — this course uses email, always: those three may not work from where you are, and they tie your builder account to another account. The password needs at least 6 characters (this course suggests 12 or more), may use letters, digits and _ - ! @ # $ % ^ & *, and may not contain a full stop.
  3. 不需要手机号，不需要银行卡。 按 Wix 自己的帮助文档，注册就是这三步，没有手机号那一栏。万一屏幕上真的要手机号或要钱：停下来，截图，配三句话（我想做什么 · 我做了什么 · 出现了什么）邮件问导师——这正是本周微课 2 教的求助姿势。No phone number, no bank card. By Wix's own help pages, signing up is those three steps with no phone field anywhere. If your screen really does ask for a phone number or for money: stop, screenshot it, and email your mentor with the three sentences (what I wanted to do · what I did · what appeared) — exactly the way of asking micro-lesson 2 teaches.
  4. 把界面改成中文（强烈建议，能省很多猜按钮的力气）：右上角头像 → 账号设置 Account Settings → 账号语言 Account language → 选中文。改完之后，后台和编辑器都会跟着变成中文。本页保留英文按钮名，是因为你可能在英文界面里，也可能在别人的电脑上——两边名字都给你。Switch the interface to Chinese (strongly suggested — it removes a great deal of button-guessing): avatar at the top right → Account Settings → Account language → pick Chinese. The dashboard and the editor both follow. This page keeps the English names as well, because you may be in the English interface, or on someone else's computer — so you get both.

第 2 步 · 建站并挑一个模板Step 2 · Create the site and pick a template

  1. 登录后进入你的工作台，点创建新网站 Create New Site。Once logged in you land in your workspace; click Create New Site.
  2. 平台会问你几个问题（做什么用的网站、叫什么名字），然后多半会热情地推荐“让 AI 帮你生成一个网站”。本周不要用它——第 3 周是 🚫 独立单元，网站上的每一句话都必须是你写的。选“从模板开始 Choose a template”那条路。It asks a few questions (what the site is for, what to call it) and will then enthusiastically offer to generate a site for you. Not this week — Week 3 is a 🚫 Independent module, and every sentence on the site has to be yours. Take the Choose a template path instead.
  3. 在模板库里，往个人 Personal、作品集与简历 Portfolio & CV、博客 Blog 这几类里找，挑一个简洁的：白底、字大、图少。鼠标停在模板上 → 点编辑 Edit，编辑器就开了。In the gallery, look under Personal, Portfolio & CV or Blog, and choose a plain one: white ground, large type, few images. Hover the template → click Edit, and the editor opens.
  4. 给自己定个 5 分钟闹钟。 模板只是骨架，文字和图片随时能换。但有一条 Wix 的硬规则要知道：网站一旦建好，模板就换不了了——想换只能另建一个新站（免费版可以建很多个，不花钱）。所以挑模板的标准不是“完美”，是“简洁、我看得懂它哪块是哪块”。Set yourself a five-minute timer. A template is only the skeleton; text and images change any time. But one hard Wix rule is worth knowing: once the site is created, the template cannot be swapped — changing it means building a second site instead (the free plan allows many, at no cost). So the standard for choosing is not “perfect” but “plain, and I can tell which block is which”.

第 3 步 · 三个房间（加页、改名、删多余的页）Step 3 · The three rooms (add, rename, delete the spares)

  1. 点编辑器左边的页面与菜单 Pages & Menu（图上的 ④）。你会看到模板自带的一串页面。Click Pages & Menu down the left side of the editor (④ in the sketch). The template's own list of pages appears.
  2. 加一页：点面板左上角的＋ 空白页 + Blank Page（也可以把鼠标停在某个现成版式上点添加页面 Add Page），输入页名，点完成 Done。新加的页会自动出现在网站的导航菜单里。Add one: click + Blank Page at the panel's top left (or hover a ready-made layout and click Add Page), type the page name, and click Done. A new page joins the site's navigation menu by itself.
  3. 把三个房间凑齐，页名清清楚楚：关于我 About me · 学习日志 Learning log · 作品集 Portfolio。中文名英文名都行，认得出就好。Assemble the three rooms with unmistakable names: About me · Learning log · Portfolio. Chinese or English, whichever — recognisable is what counts.
  4. 模板自带一堆用不上的页（“服务”“价目表”“预约”……）：在同一个面板里，点那一页右边的更多操作 ⋯ More Actions → 删除 Delete（或先隐藏 Hide，拿不准的时候更稳妥）。模板是毛坯房，多余的隔断拆掉不心疼。Templates arrive with pages you will never use (“Services”, “Pricing”, “Book Online”…): in the same panel, click More Actions ⋯ beside the page → Delete (or Hide first, the safer move when you are unsure). A template is an unfinished house; spare partitions come down guilt-free.

第 4 步 · 改文字与图片Step 4 · Edit the text and the images

  • 文字：在画布上双击那句话，光标就进去了，像改文档一样改。模板里的假字（一段读不通的英文，或“在这里写下你的标题”）必须全部换掉——留一句在页面上，访客第一眼就看到你没做完。Text: double-click the line on the canvas and the cursor drops in; edit it as you would a document. The template's dummy words (a paragraph of nonsense Latin, or “Write your title here”) all have to go — one left standing tells a visitor at a glance that the work is unfinished.
  • 图片：双击图片 → 更换图片 Change Image → 上传你自己的。隐私第 2 条在这里落地：不放正脸照片，画一个、拍风景、用喜欢的图案都行。Images: double-click the image → Change Image → upload your own. This is where privacy rule 2 lands: no face photos — a drawing, a landscape, a pattern you like all serve.
  • 图片先缩小再上传。免费版给你 500 MB 存储、1 GB 流量；手机拍的原图一张常有 3–8 MB，几张就把网站拖慢了（尤其是同学用手机流量打开的时候）。做法：先把图截个屏再用截图，或在手机相册里选“导出小尺寸 / 中等”。这也是本周截图技能的一个正经用处。Shrink images before uploading. The free plan gives 500 MB of storage and 1 GB of bandwidth; one photo straight off a phone is often 3–8 MB, and a handful of those make the site crawl (most of all for a visitor on mobile data). The fix: screenshot the photo and upload the screenshot, or export it at a small or medium size from the phone's gallery. It is a genuine use for this week's screenshot skill.
  • 「保存」不是「发布」。 编辑器里的保存 Save只是把草稿存起来；Wix 的帮助文档写得很直白：保存不会让改动上线。这一条是下一步的全部内容。Save is not Publish. Save in the editor only stores the draft; Wix's own help says it plainly — saving does not make changes live. That sentence is the whole of the next step.

第 5 步 · 发布，然后再看一眼手机Step 5 · Publish, then look again on a phone

  1. 点编辑器右上角的发布 Publish（图上的 ③）。网站从“只有你能看的草稿”变成“有网址的真网站”。Click Publish at the editor's top right (③ in the sketch). The site turns from a draft only you could see into a real site with an address.
  2. 弹出的小窗里点查看网站 View Site 亲眼看一遍，再点完成 Done 回到编辑器。这个弹窗里就有你的网址——先别关，第 6 步要用。In the little window that appears, click View Site and look at it with your own eyes, then Done to return to the editor. Your web address is in that window — don't dismiss it yet; Step 6 needs it.
  3. 切到手机视图看一遍：编辑器顶部靠右的手机图标（图上的 ②），或按 Ctrl + J（Mac 上 Cmd + J）。桌面上排得整整齐齐的页面，到了窄屏上常常字压字、图出框——而你的同学和家人多半是用手机打开你的链接的。在手机视图里把挤到一起的东西挪开，然后再点一次发布。Switch to mobile view and look: the phone icon near the top right (② in the sketch), or press Ctrl + J (Cmd + J on a Mac). A page that lines up beautifully on a desktop often overlaps and spills on a narrow screen — and a phone is how your classmates and family will almost certainly open your link. Pull the crowded pieces apart in mobile view, then press Publish again.
  4. 记住这个小圈：改一改 → 再点一次发布。它就是五级台阶图上从 ③ 弯回 ② 的那根箭头，你到第 16 周还在走它。Remember the small circle: edit a little → press Publish again. It is the arrow curving from ③ back to ② on the five-move ladder, and you will still be walking it in Week 16.

第 6 步 · 复制“对的”那个网址Step 6 · Copy the right address

这一步每年都有人栽跟头：登记上去的是编辑器的网址，导师一点开只看到“请登录”。你手里其实有两个网址，长得很不一样：This is the step people trip on every year: the address registered turns out to be the editor's, and the mentor who clicks it sees only a login wall. You are in fact holding two addresses, and they do not look alike:

到哪里拿这个网址，三条路都行：① 刚才发布后那个小窗里；② 网站后台顶部的网站操作 Site Actions → 查看网站 View Site，然后复制浏览器地址栏里的那一串；③ 后台左边设置 Settings → 网站基本信息 Site Basics → 网站地址 Site Address。拿到之后：登记到登记表（网址开课前公布），再发一份进自己的邮箱（主题“我的网站 · 化名”）——两个地方存，丢不了。Where to fetch it, any of three ways: ① from the little window that appeared after publishing; ② the dashboard's Site Actions at the top → View Site, then copy what the browser's address bar shows; ③ Settings in the dashboard's left column → Site Basics → Site Address. Once you hold it: register it on the tracking sheet (address announced before launch) and email a copy to yourself (subject “my website · pseudonym”) — kept in two places, it cannot be lost.

第 7 步 · 设为不被搜索引擎收录（这一步不能漏）Step 7 · Hide it from search engines (the step you cannot skip)

这是本课程的隐私底线，也是量规第 ① 行要看的一项。整站和单页各有一个开关，位置不同——建议两个都拨，一个漏了另一个兜着。This is the course's privacy floor, and one of the things rubric row ① checks. There is a switch for the whole site and another for a single page, in different places — throwing both is the suggestion, so that a miss on one is caught by the other.

整站（主要的那个开关）：进网站后台 Dashboard（不是编辑器）→ 左边找 SEO 那一项（Wix 现在写作 SEO & GEO；SEO 只是“搜索引擎相关设置”的英文缩写，认准带“搜索 / search”的字样就行）→ 往下拉到工具与设置 Tools and settings → 点 SEO 设置 SEO Settings → 关掉“允许搜索引擎收录本网站 Let search engines index your site”那个开关 → 点隐藏网站 Hide Site 确认。The whole site (the switch that matters most): go to the Dashboard (not the editor) → find SEO in the left column (Wix currently labels it SEO & GEO; SEO is only the English shorthand for search-engine settings, so look for the word “search”) → scroll to Tools and settings → click SEO Settings → turn off “Let search engines index your site” → click Hide Site to confirm.

单页（一页一页地保险）：回编辑器 → 左边页面与菜单 Pages & Menu → 点某一页右边的更多操作 ⋯ More Actions → SEO 基础设置 SEO basics → 关掉“允许搜索引擎收录本页 Let search engines index this page”。三页各做一次。Page by page (belt as well as braces): back in the editor → Pages & Menu at the left → More Actions ⋯ beside a page → SEO basics → turn off “Let search engines index this page”. Once for each of the three pages.

它到底挡住了谁？ 挡住的是搜索引擎——别人搜你的化名、搜你的句子，搜不出这个网站。不挡拿到链接的人：你、导师、你想给他们看的家里人，照样一点就开。还有一句 Wix 帮助里的实话：如果网站已经被搜索引擎收录过，关掉开关后要等一段时间才会从搜索结果里消失——所以这一步要在发布后尽快做，别拖到下周。拨完开关，别忘了再发布一次。Who exactly does it stop? Search engines — nobody searching your pseudonym or a sentence of yours will turn up this site. It does not stop people holding the link: you, your mentor, the family you choose to show it to all open it with one click. And one honest line from Wix's own help: if the site has already been indexed, turning the switch off means waiting a while before it drops out of search results — so do this step soon after publishing, not next week. Once the switch is thrown, remember to publish again.

第 8 步 · 备份截图（用本周刚学的手艺）Step 8 · Backup screenshots (with the skill you just learned)

发布完成后，用手机或无痕窗口以访客身份把三页各打开一次，各截一张图，存进一个叫“我的网站备份”的相簿或文件夹。文件名照第 2 周的命名规则（课程 - 周次 - 内容 - 化名）来，例如：Once published, open all three pages as a visitor — phone or private window — capture each one, and keep the images in an album or folder called “site backups”. Name the files by Week 2's naming rule (course - week - what - pseudonym), for example:

数字化学习-W3-网站关于我-晨星.png　·　数字化学习-W3-网站学习日志-晨星.png　·　数字化学习-W3-网站作品集-晨星.png（英文写法同样可以：DL-W3-site-about-Chenxing.png）。以后每次大改并发布之后，都补一轮——第 7 周（B6）会把这个习惯升级成完整的备份体系。DL-W3-site-about-Chenxing.png　·　DL-W3-site-log-Chenxing.png　·　DL-W3-site-portfolio-Chenxing.png (the Chinese form works equally well: 数字化学习-W3-网站关于我-晨星.png). Repeat the round after every big edit-and-publish — in Week 7 (B6) this habit grows into a full backup system.

账号安全：改密码与两步验证（第 5 周 B4 会用到）Account security: password and two-step verification (Week 5, B4, uses this)

本周不必做，先知道位置就好；第 5 周（B4 账号、密码与隐私）会请你回到这里真动手。两件事都在同一个页面上：右上角头像 → 账号设置 Account Settings。Nothing to do this week — knowing where they live is enough; Week 5 (B4, Accounts, passwords and privacy) will send you back here to actually do it. Both live on the same page: avatar at the top right → Account Settings.

  • 改密码Change the password
    在哪里：账号设置页 → 修改密码 Edit Password → 输旧密码、输两遍新密码 → 保存。 要留意的：至少 6 位（本课程建议 12 位以上）；不能含英文句点。页面会劝你留一个“找回密码用的手机号”——本课程不要求，可以不填。Where: Account Settings → Edit Password → old password, new password twice → Save. Worth knowing: at least 6 characters (this course suggests 12+); no full stop allowed. The page will suggest adding a recovery phone number — this course does not require it; leaving it empty is fine.
  • 两步验证（第二道门）Two-step verification (the second door)
    在哪里：同一页往下拉到两步验证 2-Step Verification，点启用 Enable。 要留意的：Wix 给四种方式——官方手机 App、验证器 App、短信、邮箱。本课程推荐邮箱或验证器 App，都不用交出手机号。选邮箱的前提：账号设置页上你的邮箱已显示“已验证”。Where: scroll down the same page to 2-Step Verification and click Enable. Worth knowing: Wix offers four routes — its own app, an authenticator app, SMS, and email. This course suggests email or an authenticator app; neither hands over a phone number. Email requires that your address already shows as confirmed on the Account Settings page.

新手最常掉的七个坑 · 在这家平台上各按哪里The seven pits — which button ends each one here

这七个坑本身在微课 2 里已经讲过（换哪家平台都一样）；下面是它们在这一家平台上的具体按钮。The seven pits themselves are in micro-lesson 2, and they are the same on every builder; below are the actual buttons that end each one on this platform.

  • 明明改好了，网址上还是旧的I edited it, the address still shows the old page
    为什么：只点了“保存”，没再点“发布”。编辑器里是草稿，网址上是你上一次发布的那一版。 怎么办：回编辑器点发布。这是所见即所得建站第一大坑，掉进去一次就记住了。Why: Save was pressed, Publish was not. The editor holds a draft; the address holds whatever you last published. What to do: Go back and press Publish. The commonest trap on any such builder — one fall teaches it for good.
  • 导师说链接点开是“请登录”My mentor says the link shows a sign-in page
    为什么：登记的是编辑器网址（带 editor 或 manage），不是网站网址。 怎么办：按第 6 步重新取一次，先用无痕窗口验证，再登记。Why: The editor's address (with editor or manage in it) got registered instead of the site's. What to do: Fetch it again by Step 6, verify it in a private window, then register.
  • 页面上还有一段读不通的英文A paragraph of nonsense English is still on the page
    为什么：模板自带的假字没删干净——它长得像正文，很容易看漏。 怎么办：用访客身份把三页从头读到尾一遍，只找“不是我写的句子”。Why: Template dummy text that was never cleared; it looks like body text, so the eye slides over it. What to do: Read all three pages end to end as a visitor, hunting only for sentences you did not write.
  • 菜单里有“价目表”“预约”这类页The menu has pages like “Pricing” or “Book Online”
    为什么：模板自带的页面还在。 怎么办：左侧页面与菜单 → ⋯ → 删除或隐藏（见第 3 步）。Why: The template's own pages are still there. What to do: Pages & Menu → ⋯ → Delete or Hide (see Step 3).
  • 手机上字压字、图跑出屏幕On a phone the text overlaps and images run off-screen
    为什么：只在电脑上检查过。手机版是另一套排布，不会自己排整齐。 怎么办：编辑器里切到手机视图调好，再发布一次（见第 5 步）。Why: It was only ever checked on a desktop. The phone layout is a second arrangement and does not tidy itself. What to do: Fix it in mobile view and publish again (see Step 5).
  • 网站打开很慢，或提示存储快满了The site loads slowly, or storage is nearly full
    为什么：上传了手机原图。免费版只有 500 MB 存储、1 GB 流量。 怎么办：删掉大图，换成截图或小尺寸导出的版本（见第 4 步）。Why: Full-size phone photos were uploaded. The free plan has 500 MB of storage and 1 GB of bandwidth. What to do: Remove the large images and upload screenshots or small-size exports instead (see Step 4).
  • 到处弹窗劝我升级付费Upgrade pop-ups keep appearing
    为什么：免费版的常态。本课程全程只用免费版。 怎么办：一律点掉。关不掉、或找不到免费发布的路：截图 + 三句话，邮件导师。Why: Normal life on a free plan. This course stays on the free plan throughout. What to do: Dismiss them all. If one won't close, or the free path to publishing hides itself: screenshot + three sentences, email your mentor.

免费版会给你什么，不会给你什么（说在前面）What the free plan does and does not give you (said in advance)

  • 网址是“账号名.wixsite.com/网站名”这种形式，不是你自己的域名。本课程不需要自有域名——那要花钱，而且免费网址一样打得开、一样能登记。The address takes the form “account-name.wixsite.com/site-name”, not a domain of your own. This course does not need one: a custom domain costs money, and the free address opens and registers just as well.
  • 页面上会有平台自己的广告条，免费版去不掉（要去掉得付费升级）。这不扣分，本课程也不要求你去掉它——它是你没花一分钱的证明。The platform puts its own banner ad on your pages, and the free plan cannot remove it (only a paid upgrade can). It costs you no points, and this course does not ask you to remove it — it is the receipt for having spent nothing.
  • 500 MB 存储、1 GB 流量：三页文字加几张缩小过的图，远远用不完。500 MB of storage and 1 GB of bandwidth: three pages of text and a few shrunken images come nowhere near it.
  • 模板建站后不能换（想换只能另建一个新站——免费版可以建很多个，不花钱）。The template cannot be changed after the site is created (changing it means building another site — the free plan allows many, at no cost).
  • “不被搜索引擎收录”的开关免费版就有——这是本课程挑平台的硬条件之一。The hide-from-search switch is available on the free plan — one of this course's hard conditions when picking a platform.

只有手机怎么办Phone only

诚实的回答：能建，但很吃力，而且有几步大概率做不了。挑模板、加页面、调手机版排布、找 SEO 设置，这些在小屏幕上都很难。可行的做法，按优先顺序：The honest answer: it can be done, but it is hard work, and a few steps will most likely be out of reach. Choosing a template, adding pages, adjusting the phone layout and finding the SEO settings are all difficult on a small screen. What to try, in order:

  1. 借一次电脑，把骨架搭完：注册、选模板、建三页、发布、设不收录——大约半小时。学校机房、图书馆、家人的电脑都行。骨架一旦立起来，以后每周写日志用手机就够了。Borrow a computer once and put up the skeleton: register, pick a template, make the three pages, publish, set no-index — about half an hour. A school lab, a library, a family machine all serve. Once the skeleton stands, a phone is enough for the weekly log from then on.
  2. 实在借不到：用手机浏览器打开建站平台，横过来用（横屏能多出很多操作空间）。改文字、换图片、再发布，这几件事手机上能做（Wix 写明这项功能“尚未对所有用户开放”，所以要试了才知道你的账号有没有）。If no computer can be borrowed: open the builder in the phone's browser and turn it sideways (landscape buys a great deal of room). Retyping text, swapping images and publishing again are doable on a phone (Wix says the feature “is not yet available for all users”, so only trying tells whether your account has it).
  3. 卡在哪一步都别自己扛：截图 + 三句话，邮件导师。手机建站的卡点是设备的限制，不是你的失误——导师会给你另一条路走完本周，分数不受影响。Wherever you get stuck, do not carry it alone: screenshot + three sentences, email your mentor. A phone-only wall is the device's limit, not your mistake — your mentor will find you another route through the week, and your score is unaffected.
