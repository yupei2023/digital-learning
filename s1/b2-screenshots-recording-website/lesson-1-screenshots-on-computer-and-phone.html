截图：电脑与手机Screenshots on computer and phone

截图就是给屏幕拍照：屏幕上是什么样，照片里就是什么样。它是数字世界里最便宜的“证据”——出错的页面、想留住的作业、要给别人看的画面，一按就存下来了。第 0 节你已经试过一次；这一课把各设备上的按法补全，并告诉你截好的图去了哪里。

A screenshot is a photograph of your screen: whatever the screen shows, the picture keeps. It is the cheapest evidence in the digital world — an error page, a piece of work worth keeping, something you want another person to see, saved at one press. You already tried once in §0; this lesson completes the moves on each device and tells you where the images go.

什么时候用哪种Which capture for which moment

选区域是日常主力：只框住有用的部分，别人一眼看到重点，你也少暴露无关的窗口。整屏用于“整个画面都重要”的时刻——比如报错时的完整现场。长截图（滚动截屏）能把一整页聊天或长网页接成一张，本周用不到，想学的话“想学更多”里有。Region capture is the everyday workhorse: frame only what matters, the reader sees the point instantly, and none of your unrelated windows travel along. Full screen is for moments when the whole scene matters — the complete crime scene of an error, say. Long screenshots (scrolling capture) stitch a whole chat or long page into one image; not needed this week, but Want-more has it.

截图前的一秒钟The second before you capture
扫一眼画面里有没有不该带走的东西：真名、账号、别人的消息、通知弹窗。有，就先关掉或缩小它，或者截完用标注打码（微课 2）。证据要留，隐私要藏——这两件事本周会一直并排出现。Sweep the frame for anything that should not travel: real names, account details, other people's messages, notification pop-ups. Close or shrink them first — or mask them afterwards with annotation (micro-lesson 2). Keep the evidence, hide the private — the two walk side by side all week.

这门课会怎么用截图How this course will use screenshots

  • 本周：网站发布后，三页各存一张截图——最简单的备份。This week: one screenshot per published page — the simplest backup.
  • 求助时（全年）：一张标注好的截图 + 三句话，导师能在一封回信里解决大多数问题（微课 2）。Whenever you ask for help (all year): one annotated screenshot + three sentences, and most problems die in a single reply (micro-lesson 2).
