录屏Screen recording

上周你学会了给屏幕拍照；这一课学给屏幕录像。为什么放在本周、而不是和截图一起放在第 2 周？两个理由，都很实在：一，录屏文件动辄几十兆，当邮件附件太重，第 2 周那封信要的是一张轻巧的图；二，本周你刚建好一个网站——终于有了一样值得录下来的东西。“点了这里之后它闪了一下又弹回来”——这种会动的问题，十张截图不如三十秒录像。电脑和手机都自带录屏，不用装任何软件。

Last week you learned to photograph the screen; this lesson films it. Why here, and not beside screenshots in Week 2? Two plain reasons: a recording runs to tens of megabytes and is far too heavy for an email attachment, and Week 2’s letter wanted one light image; and this week you have just built a website — at last something worth recording. “I tap here, it flashes and bounces back” — for problems that move, thirty seconds of video beat ten screenshots. Both your computer and your phone record the screen out of the box; nothing to install.

录之前的三件事Three things before you press record

  1. 清场：关掉无关窗口和标签页，只留要录的画面——观众少分心，你也少暴露。Clear the stage: close unrelated windows and tabs, leaving only the scene to be filmed — less distraction for the viewer, less exposure for you.
  2. 静音通知：打开勿扰模式。录到一半弹出一条私人消息，是录屏最常见的“翻车”——这一条既是效率，更是隐私。Silence the notifications: turn on do-not-disturb. A private message popping up mid-recording is the classic recording accident — this rule is efficiency and privacy in one.
  3. 想好一句话：这段录像要说明什么？想清楚再按录，30 秒就能说完；没想清楚，3 分钟也说不完。录音则遵守课程礼仪：环境安静，不录到别人。Know your one sentence: what is this clip supposed to show? Decide before pressing record and 30 seconds suffices; skip the deciding and three minutes will not. If sound is on, course etiquette applies: a quiet space, no one else in the recording.
截图还是录屏？一句判断Screenshot or recording? One test
画面停着能说清的，用截图（轻、快、好标注）；非得动起来才说得清的——点击之后的反应、消失得太快的提示、一套操作步骤——才用录屏。先想截图，再想录屏，是留证据的省力顺序。If a still frame tells the story, screenshot it (light, fast, easy to annotate); only when the story moves — what happens after a click, a message that vanishes too fast, a sequence of steps — does recording earn its place. Screenshot first, recording second: the economical order of evidence.

这门课会怎么用录屏How this course will use recording

  • 本周任务最后一步：网站发布之后，录一段约 30 秒的网站导览——从首页走到三个房间，停。这是你的网站第一次“动起来”被留下来。The last step of this week’s task: once the site is published, record a ~30-second tour of it — from the front page through the three rooms, then stop. The first time your site is kept in motion.
  • 第 7 周（B6）预告：任务里要录一段 1 分钟的“数字工作台屏幕导览”——本周这 30 秒，就是它的热身；到那时你已经录过一次，剩下的只是走得慢一点、说得清楚一点。Week 7 (B6), a preview: its task includes a one-minute screen tour of your digital workspace — this week's 30 seconds is the warm-up.
  • 求助时：会动的问题，录屏 + 三句话，和第 2 周学的截图求助同一套姿势。但先想一想：截图能说清的，就别录屏——邮件里几十兆的附件，常常根本寄不出去。When asking for help: for problems that move, a recording + three sentences — the same posture as Week 2’s screenshot ask. But think first: if a screenshot would say it, do not record — a tens-of-megabytes attachment often never leaves the outbox at all.
