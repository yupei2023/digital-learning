B2 量规B2 Rubric

量规的三行就是本周的三条目标。第 ① ② 行计分（3 + 2 = 5 分），第 ③ 行不计分、完成即可。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。本任务是 🚫 独立任务——评投入与格式规范，不评文笔，不评网站漂不漂亮。

The three rows are this week's three objectives. Rows ① ② carry points (3 + 2 = 5); row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence. This is an 🚫 Independent task — scored on engagement and correctness of form, never on prose or on a beautiful design.

本周的三样知识点（新手最常掉的七个坑、“不被搜索引擎收录”的含义、什么时候录屏比截图好用）不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。隐私规则是全课程的底线（见下方“规范要求”）：误放了提醒改正，不扣分。This week's three knowledge points (the seven beginner pits, what no-index means, and when a recording beats a screenshot) are not scored here — the self-quiz checks them, ungraded. The privacy rules are the course-wide floor (see Form requirements below): a slip draws a reminder and a fix, never a deduction.
