做Do

  • 学一次长截图——第 2 周“想学更多”里有两段视频教滚动长截图；用它把你自己的“学习日志”页整页截下来存进备份相簿，一页装不下的日志也能收成一张。Learn the long screenshot — Week 2's Want-more carries two clips on scrolling capture; use one to take your whole Learning-log page into the backup album, however far it runs past the screen.
  • 给“关于我”配一个不露脸的头像——画一张、拍一处风景、拼一个图案。让页面像你，但认不出你。Give About-me a faceless avatar — draw one, shoot a landscape, compose a pattern. Let the page feel like you without revealing you.
  • 用英语再写一版“关于我”——不必发布，存成草稿就好；第二学期改版时它会派上用场。Draft About-me in English — no need to publish; keep it as a draft. The Semester-2 redesign will thank you.
