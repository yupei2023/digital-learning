截图录屏与我的网站Screenshots, Recording & My Website

5 分5 pts🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min一周One week

想一想：上周你发出的第一封邮件、写在草稿里的三行日志、那份“第 2 周基线”——它们现在各在一个角落里。这学期你还会做出更多东西：短文、音频、思维导图、学习计划。你的学习会留在哪里？聊天记录会被刷走，草稿纸会丢，收藏夹会乱。这周，你要在互联网上为自己的学习安一个家：一个属于你自己的网站，三个房间——关于我 · 学习日志 · 作品集。从本周起到学期结束，你做的每一件作品都住进去。

Think back: last week's first email, the three-line log in your draft, that “Week-2 baseline” — each is sitting in a different corner. This semester you will make much more: essays, audio, mind maps, study plans. Where will your learning live? Chat threads scroll away, scraps of paper vanish, bookmark folders tangle. This week you give your learning a home on the internet: a website of your own, with three rooms — About me · Learning log · Portfolio. From now to the end of the semester, everything you make moves in.

在那之前，先学三样“留证据”的小功夫：截图、标注、录屏——电脑和手机上都会。它们是这门课的日常工具：遇到问题时，一张标注好的截图顶得上十句描述；网站发布后，给自己的页面各存一张截图，就是最简单的备份。

First, though, three small evidence skills: screenshots, annotation, screen recording — on both the computer and the phone. They are this course's everyday tools: when something goes wrong, one well-marked screenshot beats ten sentences of description; and once your site is published, a screenshot of each page is the simplest backup there is.

  • 我的学习会留在哪里？Where will my learning live?

学完本周，你应能By the end of this week you should be able to

  1. ① 用课程指定的建站平台建成并发布三页个人网站（关于我 · 学习日志 · 作品集）：完成“选模板 → 改文字与图片 → 发布 → 复制链接 → 设为不被搜索引擎收录”五个动作，并把链接登记到导师的登记表（公开学习者：把链接存进自己的邮箱）。① On the course's website builder, build and publish your three-page personal site (About me · Learning log · Portfolio): the five moves — pick a template → edit text and images → publish → copy your link → set it hidden from search engines — and register the link on your mentor's tracking sheet (open learners: file the link in your own inbox).
  2. ② 在“学习日志”页写下第一条日志：把第 2 周写在草稿里的三行日志搬上来，把“我的第 2 周基线”的逐项结果抄进来（双保险），再写本周的三行日志。② Write the first entry on your Learning-log page: move in the Week-2 three-line log from your draft, copy in the per-item results of “My Week-2 baseline” (the second safety net), then add this week's three lines.
  3. ③（不计分 · 完成即可）练成本周的“留证据”基本功：在电脑和手机上各截一张图，给其中一张加标注并配一句说得清楚的问题，录一段约 30 秒的录屏；网站发布后，给三页各存一张截图作备份。③ (ungraded · completing it is enough) The week's evidence kit: one screenshot on the computer and one on the phone, one of them annotated with a clear one-line question, a screen recording of about 30 seconds; after publishing, one backup screenshot of each page.

这三条也是本单元量规的评分行（第 ③ 条不计分，完成即可）。These three are also the rubric rows (row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough). → 量规Rubric

本周你还会学到：怎样提一个带截图的问题（这门课全年的“求助姿势”）、截图与录屏里的隐私打码、“不被搜索引擎收录”是什么意思。这些不进量规——第 3 周页面的自测题帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: how to ask a question with a screenshot (this course's all-year way of asking for help), how to mask private information in screenshots and recordings, and what “hidden from search engines” means. None of it is graded — the Week 3 self-quiz checks it for you, ungraded.

本单元的 AI 标注：🚫 独立AI designation: 🚫 Independent
本周全程不使用 AI——包括“关于我”和日志的文字。这类任务是给你自己的一面镜子：评的是投入与格式规范，不评文笔好坏、不评网站漂不漂亮。“关于我”写得朴素、模板选得普通，完全没关系——这个家必须是你自己搭的。No AI anywhere this week — including the About-me text and the log. Tasks like this are a mirror for yourself: scored on engagement and correctness of form, never on elegant prose or a beautiful design. A plain About-me and an ordinary template are perfectly fine — this home just has to be built by you.
