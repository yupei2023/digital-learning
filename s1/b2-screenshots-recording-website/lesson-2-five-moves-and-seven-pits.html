建站五个动作，与新手七个坑The five moves, and the seven pits

上一课讲了三个房间各住什么；这一课讲怎么把房子盖起来。所见即所得建站说到底只有五个动作——学会它们，换哪家平台都不用重学。后半页是七个坑：新手最常花掉半小时才爬出来的七件怪事，先看一遍，遇上时你就知道那不是你的错，也知道该按哪里。

The last lesson said what lives in each of the three rooms; this one is about putting the house up. What-you-see-is-what-you-get building comes down to five moves — learn them and no change of platform costs you a relearn. The second half is the seven pits: the seven odd things that most often cost a beginner half an hour. Read them once now, and when one happens you will know it is not your fault, and which button ends it.

建站的五个通用动作The five universal builder moves

本课程指定的建站平台与注册说明将在开课信中公布（开课前寄出；公开学习者看课程说明的「你的建站平台」框，内容相同）。不管指定哪家，所见即所得建站都是同样五步：The course's designated builder and its sign-up notes are announced in the welcome letter, sent before launch (open learners: the course guide's “Your site builder” box carries the same). Whichever it is, what-you-see-is-what-you-get building runs on the same five moves:

  1. 选模板：从平台的模板库挑一个简洁的当底子——挑的是“骨架”，肉都可以换。Pick a template: choose a clean one from the gallery as your skeleton — everything on the bones can change.
  2. 改文字与图片：点到哪句改哪句，像编辑文档一样；把模板自带的假字换成你的真话。Edit text and images: click a line, retype it, like editing a document; swap the template's dummy words for your real ones.
  3. 发布：点“发布”按钮，网站从“只有你能看的草稿”变成“有网址的真网站”。以后每次修改，都要再点一次发布才生效。Publish: one press turns the draft only you could see into a real site with an address. Every later edit needs another press of Publish to go live.
  4. 复制你的链接：发布后平台会给你一个网址——复制它，登记到登记表，也发进自己的邮箱存一份。Copy your link: publishing hands you an address — copy it, register it on the tracking sheet, and email yourself a spare.
  5. 设为不被搜索引擎收录：在网站设置里找到“搜索引擎”一项（英文界面上常叫 SEO——它只是“搜索引擎”相关设置的英文名，认准带“搜索”两个字的选项就行），关掉“允许收录”。找不到就搜平台帮助里的“隐藏于搜索引擎”，或截图问导师。Set no-index: in the site settings find the search-engine setting (often labelled SEO — just the English shorthand for search-engine options; look for the word “search”) and turn indexing off. Can't find it? Search the builder's help for “hide from search engines” — or screenshot and ask your mentor.

这五个动作在某一家平台上具体点哪个按钮？另有一页从零建站的分步手册逐步写清（含注册、加页面、发布、取对网址、设不收录，以及新手最常掉的七个坑）。它是参考手册，不计入本周的 120 分钟——需要哪一步翻哪一步。Which button, on a particular builder, performs each of the five moves? A separate step-by-step build guide spells it out (signing up, adding pages, publishing, fetching the right address, hiding from search, plus the seven pits beginners fall into). It is a manual and sits outside the week's 120 minutes — read only the step you are on.

新手最常掉的七个坑The seven pits beginners fall into

下面七件事，几乎每个第一次建站的人都会遇上一两件。它们看起来像“我把网站搞坏了”，其实全是所见即所得建站的常规脾气——先读一遍，遇上时你会认得它，也知道该按哪里。（每一条在建站手册里都有对应平台上的具体按钮。）Almost everyone building a first site meets one or two of these. They feel like “I have broken my website”, and every one of them is just the normal temperament of a what-you-see-is-what-you-get builder. Read them once; you will recognise the moment when it comes, and know which button ends it. (Each has its platform-specific buttons in the build guide.)

  • 明明改好了，网址上还是旧的I edited it, the address still shows the old page
    为什么：只点了“保存”，没再点“发布”。编辑器里是草稿，网址上是你上一次发布的那一版。 怎么办：回编辑器点发布。这是所见即所得建站第一大坑，掉进去一次就记住了——也正是上面五个动作里“② 和 ③ 每周都要再走一遍”的意思。Why: Save was pressed, Publish was not. The editor holds a draft; the address holds whatever you last published. What to do: Go back and press Publish. The commonest trap on any such builder — one fall teaches it for good, and it is exactly what “steps ② and ③ get walked again every week” means above.
  • 导师说链接点开是“请登录”My mentor says the link shows a sign-in page
    为什么：登记的是编辑器的网址，不是网站的网址。编辑器网址通常很长、带 editor 或 manage 字样，只有登录了的你能打开。 怎么办：发布之后从“查看网站”那条路取一次网址，先用手机或无痕窗口验证它真能打开，再登记。两个网址长什么样。Why: The editor’s address got registered instead of the site’s. The editor address is usually long, carries editor or manage, and opens only for the signed-in you. What to do: After publishing, fetch the address through “View site”, verify it opens on a phone or in a private window, and only then register it. What the two addresses look like.
  • 页面上还有一段读不通的英文A paragraph of nonsense English is still on the page
    为什么：模板自带的假字没删干净——它长得像正文，很容易看漏。 怎么办：用访客身份把三页从头读到尾一遍，只找“不是我写的句子”。Why: Template dummy text that was never cleared; it looks like body text, so the eye slides over it. What to do: Read all three pages end to end as a visitor, hunting only for sentences you did not write.
  • 菜单里有“价目表”“预约”这类页The menu has pages like “Pricing” or “Book Online”
    为什么：模板自带的页面还在——模板是给店铺、婚礼、乐队做的，多出来的房间很正常。 怎么办：在编辑器的“页面”面板里把它们删掉或隐藏，只留三个房间。Why: The template’s own pages are still there — templates are built for shops, weddings and bands, so spare rooms are normal. What to do: In the editor’s Pages panel, delete or hide them until only the three rooms stand.
  • 手机上字压字、图跑出屏幕On a phone the text overlaps and images run off-screen
    为什么：只在电脑上检查过。手机版是另一套排布，不会自己排整齐。 怎么办：编辑器里切到手机视图调好，再发布一次。你的导师多半在手机上打开你的网站——这一步不能省。Why: It was only ever checked on a desktop. The phone layout is a second arrangement and does not tidy itself. What to do: Fix it in mobile view and publish again. Your mentor will most likely open your site on a phone — this step is not optional.
  • 网站打开很慢，或提示存储快满了The site loads slowly, or storage is nearly full
    为什么：上传了手机原图——一张现代手机照片动辄好几兆，免费版的存储和流量都很有限。 怎么办：删掉大图，换成截图或小尺寸导出的版本（本周你正好会截图）。Why: Full-size phone photos were uploaded — a modern phone picture easily runs to several megabytes, and free plans are thrifty with both storage and bandwidth. What to do: Remove the large images and upload screenshots or small-size exports instead (you can screenshot, as of last week).
  • 到处弹窗劝我升级付费Upgrade pop-ups keep appearing
    为什么：免费版的常态。本课程全程只用免费版：免费子域名、带平台小广告都完全没问题。 怎么办：一律点掉。关不掉、或找不到免费发布的路：截图 + 三句话，邮件导师（第 2 周学的求助姿势）。Why: Normal life on a free plan. This course stays on the free plan throughout: a free subdomain and the platform’s small ads are entirely fine. What to do: Dismiss them all. If one won’t close, or the free path to publishing hides itself: screenshot + three sentences, email your mentor (Week 2’s way of asking).
七个坑里，六个的解法只有一句话Six of the seven end in one sentence
回编辑器改一改，再点一次发布。这就是五级台阶图上那根从 ③ 弯回 ② 的箭头——你到第 16 周还会一直走它。真正需要求助的只有最后一条（关不掉的付费弹窗），而求助的姿势，第 2 周已经教过了。Go back into the editor, change it, and press Publish again. That is the arrow curving from ③ back to ② on the five-move ladder — the loop you will still be walking in Week 16. Only the last pit (an upgrade pop-up that will not close) genuinely needs help, and asking for help was Week 2’s lesson.
