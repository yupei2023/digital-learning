请从上到下按顺序做。本周结束时，互联网上会多出一个属于你的网站——听起来是大事，做起来是五个动作。还没找到开课信里的建站平台信息？先看第 10 节第一条。Work top to bottom. By the end of this week the internet will hold one more website — yours. It sounds like a big deal; it is five moves. Can't find the builder info in your welcome letter? See the first question in §10.

0 · 先动手（1 分钟）0 · Hands first (one minute)

别看任何教程，现在就试着把这个页面截下来——电脑用键盘，手机用按键，怎么按你说了算。截到了？恭喜，微课 1 你已经会了一半。没截到？也很好——“到底该按哪里”这个问题，微课 1 第一段就回答。No tutorial yet — right now, try to take a screenshot of this very page: keyboard on the computer, buttons on the phone, your call which to press. Got one? Congratulations — you already own half of micro-lesson 1. Didn't? Also good — “which buttons, exactly?” is the first thing micro-lesson 1 answers.

为什么本周敢让你先动手？因为截图和上周的邮件不一样：邮件发出去收不回，截图截坏了删掉重来就是——错不了，也错不起什么。可逆的动作，就该先试后学；不可逆的动作（比如上周的正式邮件），才先彩排后登台。Why does this week dare to start with your hands? Because a screenshot is not last week's email: an email cannot be unsent, but a bad screenshot is deleted and retaken — nothing to lose. Reversible actions deserve trying before studying; irreversible ones (like last week's formal email) get the rehearsal first.

本周你还会学到：怎样提一个带截图的问题、截图与录屏里的隐私打码、“不被搜索引擎收录”是什么意思。这些不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: how to ask a question with a screenshot, masking private information in screenshots and recordings, and what “hidden from search engines” means. None of it is graded — the self-quiz checks it for you, ungraded.

诚实的估计：第一次建网站，实际用时常是表里的 1.5 倍——在模板之间来回犹豫、把一句话改了又改，都算学习。没有迟交处罚。微课页与跟做页的短视频（最长 4 分 20 秒）已算在相应分钟里；单元首页那段“网站是如何被打开的”是选看，不计入。An honest estimate: a first website often takes 1.5× the table — dithering between templates and rewriting one sentence five times all count as learning. No late penalty. The short clips on the lesson and follow-along pages (4:20 at most) are inside those minutes; the module-home clip on how websites get opened is optional and outside the count.

  1. 微课 1 · 截图：电脑与手机（7 分钟）Micro-lesson 1 · Screenshots on computer and phone (7 min)——每台设备上的两三种截法、截好的图存在哪里、什么时候用哪种。 — the two or three ways on each device, where the images land, and which way fits which moment.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

建站平台到底是哪一个？Which website builder is it, exactly?

建站平台的名字与注册说明将在开课信中公布（开课前寄出）；公开学习者、以及一时找不到信的同学，看课程说明的「你的建站平台」框——信里写什么，那里就写什么。本周页面教的五个动作——选模板、改文字与图片、发布、复制链接、设不收录——在各家所见即所得建站平台上都长得差不多，所以不管指定的是哪家，这一页都用得上。目前首选 Wix（开课前实测确认）：从零建一个 Wix 网站 · 分步手册把这五个动作落到具体按钮上，并在最后一节写了备选平台的对照——参考手册，不计入本周分钟。

The builder's name and sign-up notes are announced in the welcome letter, sent before the course opens; open learners — and anyone who cannot lay hands on the letter — find the same information in the course guide's “Your site builder” box. The five moves this week teaches — pick a template, edit text and images, publish, copy your link, set no-index — look nearly the same on every what-you-see-is-what-you-get builder, so the page serves you whichever one is named. The first choice is Wix (confirmed by a pre-launch test): the step-by-step Wix build guide lands those five moves on actual buttons and closes with a side-by-side for the fallback platform — a manual, outside this week's minutes.

注册要花钱吗？要手机号吗？Does signing up cost money? Does it need a phone number?

不花钱，也不需要手机号——课程指定的平台免费、只需邮箱注册，这是课程选平台的硬规则。任何一步向你要钱或要手机号，停下来，截图发邮件问导师（对，就用本周学的求助姿势）。

No money, no phone number — the course's builder is free and signs up with email alone; that is a hard rule of how the course picks platforms. If any step asks you for money or a phone number, stop, screenshot it, and email your mentor (yes — exactly this week's way of asking).

我只有手机，没有电脑。I only have a phone, no computer.

课程建议在电脑上建站（编辑器在大屏上好用得多）；只有手机也能完成：用手机浏览器打开建站平台，横屏操作更稳。截图和录屏本来就要在手机上练一遍——这部分你反而更方便。至于目标 ③ 里“电脑截一张”那格：等下次碰到电脑（学校机房、家人电脑）再补，先空着不影响任何分数——目标 ③ 本来就不计分。实在卡住：截图 + 三句话，邮件导师。

A computer is recommended for building (the editor is far happier on a big screen), but phone-only works: open the builder in the phone's browser, and landscape orientation steadies things. The screenshot and recording practice happens on the phone anyway — there you are ahead. As for the “one on the computer” box in objective ③: fill it whenever a computer next crosses your path (a school lab, a family machine) — an empty box costs nothing, since objective ③ is ungraded anyway. Truly stuck? Screenshot + three sentences, email your mentor.

模板挑不好，怕选错。I can't choose a template — what if I pick wrong?

给自己定个 5 分钟闹钟，从简洁的模板里挑一个就出发。模板不是终身大事：文字和图片随时能改，第二学期第 1 周还有一次专门的“网站改版”。本周评的是三页齐不齐、日志有没有，不评漂亮。

Set a five-minute timer, pick a clean template, and go. A template is not a life sentence: text and images change any time, and Semester 2 opens with a whole week for redesigning the site. This week grades whether the three pages and the log exist — never beauty.

网站发布后，是不是谁都能搜到我？Once published, can anyone search me up?

不是。你会把网站设为“不被搜索引擎收录”：搜索引擎搜不到它，只有拿到链接的人（你和导师）能打开。再加上隐私四条——英文名或化名、不放正脸照片、不写学校全名、不放别人的信息——网站上没有能指向真实的你的东西。完全公开是第二学期末的选项，且需要家长书面同意。

No. You will set the site to be hidden from search engines: search cannot find it, and only people holding the link (you and your mentor) can open it. Add the four privacy rules — English name or pseudonym, no face photos, no full school name, no one else's information — and nothing on the site points to the real you. Going fully public is a Semester-2-end option, and only with written parental consent.

这周正好赶上长假怎么办？What if a holiday week lands on top of this?

按课程的假期规则：长假那一周是缓冲周，不开新单元，用来补做和休息（具体见日历页）。网站不会跑——假期结束接着建就是。没有迟交处罚。

The course's holiday rule applies: a holiday week is a buffer week — no new module, just catch-up and rest (see the calendar). The website will wait; pick it up after the break. No late penalty.

发布之后才发现页面上有真名 / 照片。I published, then spotted my real name / a photo on a page.

别慌——这正是网站比邮件友善的地方：网站可以改了再发布。回编辑器删掉，重新发布，访客看到的就是新版。自己发现并当天改好，不扣分；这样的得体补救，本身就是本周的内容。

Don't panic — this is exactly where a website is kinder than email: a site can be edited and republished. Go back to the editor, remove it, publish again, and visitors see the new version. Caught by you and fixed the same day, it costs nothing; graceful recovery like this is part of this week's content.

网站打不开了 / 内容不见了。My site won't open / my content vanished.

立即邮件导师，附上截图（打不开的报错页也截下来）——这是课程说明里写好的流程。这也是“发布后存备份截图”的意义：就算页面真丢了，你手里还有它的样子，重建快得多。第 7 周（B6）会教更完整的备份习惯。

Email your mentor at once with a screenshot (capture the error page too) — the course guide's standing procedure. This is also why the backup screenshots exist: even if a page truly vanishes, you still hold what it looked like, and rebuilding is far faster. Week 7 (B6) teaches the fuller backup habit.

要发的截图里有我的真名 / 账号信息。The screenshot I need to send shows my real name / account details.

先处理再发送：用标注工具把敏感部分打码（马赛克或色块盖住），或干脆裁掉那一块。这条规则对发给导师的求助截图、放上网站的截图一律适用——微课 2 有演示。

Treat it before it travels: mask the sensitive part with the annotation tool (mosaic or a solid block), or crop that region away entirely. The rule covers help screenshots to your mentor and anything placed on your site alike — micro-lesson 2 shows how.

仍不清楚？先看上面的常见问题，再邮件导师或教师——记得带上一张标注好的截图（24–48 小时内回复；导师邮箱见开课信）。Still unclear? Check the FAQ above, then email your mentor or the instructor — with an annotated screenshot attached (reply within 24–48 h; mentor addresses are in the welcome letter).
