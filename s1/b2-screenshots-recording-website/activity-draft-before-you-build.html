开工前：先写字，再建站Before you start: words first, builder second

建站这件事最容易吃掉时间的地方，不是“不会用工具”，而是一边挑模板一边想说什么：光标停在模板的假字上，脑子一片空白，半小时就过去了。所以这一页把两件事拆开——现在先在草稿里把话写好，待会儿进编辑器只做一件事：把写好的话搬进去。两幕都不计分，写得朴素完全没关系。

What eats the time in site-building is almost never the tool; it is deciding what to say while choosing a template — cursor blinking on the dummy text, mind blank, half an hour gone. So this page splits the two jobs: write the words now, in a draft, and let the editor session do one thing only — move them in. Both acts are ungraded, and plain writing is entirely fine.

第一幕 · 三页的文字，先写在草稿里（12 分钟）Act 1 · The three pages' words, in a draft first (12 min)

打开手机备忘录、电脑记事本、或者一张纸——哪里都行，只要待会儿复制得动。给自己 12 分钟，把下面三块填完就停。Open your phone's notes, a text file, or a sheet of paper — anywhere you can copy from later. Give yourself twelve minutes, fill the three blocks, and stop.

  1. 「关于我」· 三小段（5 分钟）：① 我是谁——只写英文名或化名（跟第 2 周邮件里用的那个一致）；② 我为什么学这门课——第 2 周那封邮件的第二段整理一下就能用；③ 这学期最想学会的一件事。每段两三句，够了。About me · three short parts (5 min): ① who I am — English name or pseudonym only, the same one as in Week 2's email; ② why I am taking this course — the second paragraph of that email, tidied, serves perfectly; ③ the one thing I most want to learn this semester. Two or three sentences each is enough.
  2. 「学习日志」第一条 · 三块（5 分钟）：② 第 2 周写在草稿里的三行日志（原样抄来）；② “我的第 2 周基线”的逐项结果（去邮箱搜主题“我的第 2 周基线”，抄成“第 1 项 · 3 分”的样子）；③ 本周的三行日志——最后一行等网站发布后再补，因为那一刻的感觉现在还没发生。Learning log, first entry · three blocks (5 min): ① the Week-2 three-line log from your draft, copied as it stands; ② the per-item results of “My Week-2 baseline” (search your inbox for that exact subject, and write them as “item 1 · 3 pts”); ③ this week's three lines — leave the last one until after you publish, because that feeling has not happened yet.
  3. 「作品集」· 一句占位（2 分钟）：例如“作品从第 8 周起陆续入住 Works arrive from Week 8 on”。就一句，这个房间本周只要有门牌。Portfolio · one placeholder line (2 min): for example “Works arrive from Week 8 on”. One sentence; this room needs only a name plate this week.
为什么先写字？Why words first?
写字和排版是两种活儿，同时做，两样都做不好。文字先写完，进编辑器时你就只剩“复制—粘贴—看一眼”这一个动作——任务里那 8 分钟的“关于我”才真的够用。这个先后顺序，第 11 周做学习计划、第 15 周整理作品集时你还会再用一次。Writing and arranging are two different jobs, and doing them at once does neither well. With the words finished, the editor session shrinks to copy — paste — glance, which is what makes the task's eight minutes for About-me actually enough. You will reach for this same order again in Week 11's study plan and Week 15's portfolio.

第二幕 · 火眼金睛：这句话能放上网吗？（8 分钟）Act 2 · Sharp eyes: does this belong on the web? (8 min)

八张卡片，每张判断一下：这样写可以发布，还是先改一改？点按钮立即看解释。不计分，答错正是练习的目的——现在错，好过发布之后才发现。Eight cards. Judge each: is this ready to publish, or does it need one fix first? Press a button for the instant explanation. Ungraded — wrong answers are the point, and being wrong here beats being wrong after publishing.

八张都判完了？“先改一改”的五张，正是待会儿“访客的眼睛”那一步要找的东西——现在认得出来，那六分钟就够用了。All eight judged? The five that need a fix are precisely what the visitor's-eyes step will be hunting for — recognise them now, and those six minutes will be enough.
