建站发布检查单The site-launch checklist

每次点“发布”之前、之后，各过一遍对应的半张表。本学期每周更新网站都用它。

Before every press of Publish, run the top half; after it, the bottom half. Every weekly site update this semester uses this sheet.

时机When检查什么Check what一句话记住Remember it as
发布前 · 隐私四条Before · the four privacy rules① 全站只用英文名或化名；② 没有正脸照片；③ 没有学校全名（也没有班级、地址）；④ 没有别人的信息（名字、照片、聊天记录）。① Pseudonym or English name only, site-wide; ② no face photos; ③ no full school name (nor class, nor address); ④ no one else's information (names, photos, chats).“访客的眼睛”：陌生人拿到链接，认不出真实的我。Visitor's eyes: a stranger with the link cannot find the real me.
发布前 · 内容三间房Before · the three rooms关于我 · 学习日志 · 作品集三页都在；模板的假字清干净了；日志最新一条在最上面。About me · Learning log · Portfolio all standing; the template's dummy text cleared; the newest log entry on top.三个房间，件件是我的东西。Three rooms, everything in them mine.
发布时 · 两个开关At launch · the two switches点了“发布”（每次修改后都要再点）；“不被搜索引擎收录”保持开启。Publish pressed (and pressed again after every edit); hidden-from-search kept on.发布让它存在，不收录让它安静。Publishing makes it exist; no-index keeps it quiet.
发布后 · 三个动作After · the three moves用手机或无痕窗口（浏览器的“隐私浏览”模式）以访客身份打开链接看一遍；链接登记到登记表（公开学习者：发进自己邮箱）；给每页存一张备份截图。Open the link as a visitor (phone, or the browser's private/incognito window); register the link on the tracking sheet (open learners: email it to yourself); save one backup screenshot per page.亲眼看 · 登记 · 留底。See it, register it, keep its picture.
出错了怎么办When something slips
发布后才发现真名 / 照片：回编辑器删掉 → 重新发布 → 访客看到的就是新版，自己当天改好不扣分。网站打不开 / 内容丢了：立即邮件导师，附截图（报错页也截）。A real name or photo spotted after launch: back to the editor → remove → publish again — visitors see the new version, and a same-day self-fix costs nothing. The site won't open or content vanished: email your mentor at once, screenshot attached (capture the error page too).
求助的固定姿势The standing way to ask for help
一张标注好的截图（红框圈重点 · 隐私先打码）+ 三句话：我想做什么 · 我做了什么 · 出现了什么。主题照旧：“事由 · 化名（学生编号）”。One annotated screenshot (red box on the point, private parts masked first) + three sentences: what I tried · what I did · what happened. Subject as always: matter · pseudonym (student ID).
