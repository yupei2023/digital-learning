我的网站三页Your three-page website

很多年前，这门课的教师让他的学生们用课上学到的东西各建一个自己的网站，并不断思考一个问题：怎么设计，才能体现你的所学和你的个性？今天轮到你了——而且比那时容易得多：课程指定的建站平台是“所见即所得”的，你看到什么，访客就看到什么，全程不用写一行代码。

Years ago, the instructor of this course had his students each build a website with what they had learned, holding one question in mind the whole way: how do you design it so it shows what you have learned — and who you are? Now it is your turn, and far easier than it was then: the course's builder is what-you-see-is-what-you-get — whatever you see, your visitor sees, and not one line of code is written.

三个房间，各住什么Three rooms, and what lives in each

你的网站是一栋三个房间的小屋。每个房间本周只需“住进第一件东西”——学期里它们会自己长满。Your site is a small house of three rooms. Each room needs only its first occupant this week — the semester fills them by itself.

建站的五个通用动作The five universal builder moves

本课程指定的建站平台与注册说明将在开课信中公布（开课前寄出；公开学习者看课程说明的「你的建站平台」框，内容相同）。不管指定哪家，所见即所得建站都是同样五步：The course's designated builder and its sign-up notes are announced in the welcome letter, sent before launch (open learners: the course guide's “Your site builder” box carries the same). Whichever it is, what-you-see-is-what-you-get building runs on the same five moves:

  1. 选模板：从平台的模板库挑一个简洁的当底子——挑的是“骨架”，肉都可以换。Pick a template: choose a clean one from the gallery as your skeleton — everything on the bones can change.
  2. 改文字与图片：点到哪句改哪句，像编辑文档一样；把模板自带的假字换成你的真话。Edit text and images: click a line, retype it, like editing a document; swap the template's dummy words for your real ones.
  3. 发布：点“发布”按钮，网站从“只有你能看的草稿”变成“有网址的真网站”。以后每次修改，都要再点一次发布才生效。Publish: one press turns the draft only you could see into a real site with an address. Every later edit needs another press of Publish to go live.
  4. 复制你的链接：发布后平台会给你一个网址——复制它，登记到登记表，也发进自己的邮箱存一份。Copy your link: publishing hands you an address — copy it, register it on the tracking sheet, and email yourself a spare.
  5. 设为不被搜索引擎收录：在网站设置里找到“搜索引擎”一项（英文界面上常叫 SEO——它只是“搜索引擎”相关设置的英文名，认准带“搜索”两个字的选项就行），关掉“允许收录”。找不到就搜平台帮助里的“隐藏于搜索引擎”，或截图问导师。Set no-index: in the site settings find the search-engine setting (often labelled SEO — just the English shorthand for search-engine options; look for the word “search”) and turn indexing off. Can't find it? Search the builder's help for “hide from search engines” — or screenshot and ask your mentor.

这五个动作在某一家平台上具体点哪个按钮？另有一页从零建站的分步手册逐步写清（含注册、加页面、发布、取对网址、设不收录，以及新手最常掉的七个坑）。它是参考手册，不计入本周的 120 分钟——需要哪一步翻哪一步。Which button, on a particular builder, performs each of the five moves? A separate step-by-step build guide spells it out (signing up, adding pages, publishing, fetching the right address, hiding from search, plus the seven pits beginners fall into). It is a manual and sits outside the week's 120 minutes — read only the step you are on.

隐私四条（上墙的规矩）The four privacy rules (posted on the wall)

  1. 用英文名或化名——就是你上周邮件里用的那个，全课程一致。Use an English name or pseudonym — the same one from last week's email, consistent all course.
  2. 不放正脸照片。想要头像？画一个、拍风景、用喜欢的图案都行。No face photos. Want an avatar? Draw one, shoot a landscape, pick a pattern you like.
  3. 不写学校全名，也不写班级、地址这类能定位到你的信息。No full school name — nor class numbers, addresses, or anything else that pins you down.
  4. 不放别人的信息：同学的名字、家人的照片、聊天记录，都不上网站。No one else's information: classmates' names, family photos, chat records — none of it goes on the site.

再加保险：网站默认不被搜索引擎收录——搜索搜不到，只有拿到链接的人能看。完全公开是第二学期末的选项，需家长书面同意。还有一层道理：化名保护的不只是你的安全，还有你日志里的诚实——追不到真人的页面，才敢写真话。Plus the safety layer: the site stays hidden from search engines — unsearchable, visible only to holders of the link. Going fully public is a Semester-2-end option, with written parental consent. One more layer to it: the pseudonym protects more than your safety — it protects the honesty of your log. Only a page that cannot be traced to the real you dares to tell the truth.

为什么这栋“房子”值得一年的照料Why this house deserves a year of keeping
从本周起，这门课的“提交”都是同一个动作：发布到自己的网站，把链接登记给导师。第 7 周你会在网站上写外部课程的选择，第 11 周放学习计划，第 13–14 周放作品，第 15–16 周在作品集页整理出“作品集 v1”。别人交作业交进老师的抽屉，你交进自己的房子——学期结束时，抽屉是空的，房子是满的。From this week on, “submitting” in this course is always the same move: publish on your own site, register the link with your mentor. Week 7 puts your external-course choice on the site, Week 11 your study plan, Weeks 13–14 your artifact, Weeks 15–16 curate Portfolio v1 on the portfolio page. Others hand their work into a teacher's drawer; you place yours in your own house — and when the semester ends, the drawer is empty but the house is full.
