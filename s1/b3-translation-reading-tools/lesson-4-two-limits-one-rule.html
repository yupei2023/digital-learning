两个局限与一条规则Two limits and one rule

工具箱清点过了，核对术学会了，最后一课把"边界"划清楚：翻译工具常在哪两种地方失手，以及全年只需要记住的那一条使用规则。知道边界在哪，工具才真正是你的。

The toolbox is inventoried, the checking move learned; this last lesson draws the boundary — the two places where translation tools routinely stumble, and the single rule of use the whole year runs on. A tool only truly becomes yours once you know where its edges are.

局限一 · 成语与专有名词会走样Limit 1 · Idioms and proper names garble

第 0 节你已经亲眼见过：a piece of cake 变成了一块蛋糕。成语、俗语按字面直译，就是单元首页那些搞笑路牌的诞生方式。更隐蔽的是专有名词：课程名 Learning How to Learn 可能被译成一串怪话，人名 Oakley 可能变成"橡树地"，平台名、证书名也常被硬译。对策：读课程介绍时，凡是首字母大写的名字，回到原文看一眼、保留英文原样记——名字不需要翻译，需要认得。You saw it with your own eyes in §0: a piece of cake turned into pastry. Idioms rendered word-by-word are exactly how the module home's comic signs were born. Sneakier still are proper names: the course title Learning How to Learn can come out as a strange little phrase, the surname Oakley can turn into "oak field", and platform and certificate names get flattened too. The counter-move: whenever a capitalised name appears in a course intro, glance at the original and keep the name in English — names don't need translating, they need recognising.

局限二 · 长句会丢逻辑Limit 2 · Long sentences lose their logic

三行长的英文句子——从句套从句、几个 not 和 unless 纠缠在一起——工具常常译出一句"每个词都对、合起来不知所云"的中文，最危险的是否定被弄丢或弄反："并非不重要"变成"不重要"，意思正好颠倒。对策有两个：把长句拆成两三个短句分别翻译，逻辑常常就回来了；对压着整段意思的那个长句，做回译核对（微课 2）——逻辑被弄丢时，回译句和原句一对照就现形。A three-line English sentence — clauses nested in clauses, a few nots and unlesses tangled together — often yields Chinese where every word is right and the whole means nothing; most dangerous of all, a negation gets dropped or flipped: "not unimportant" becomes "unimportant", the meaning exactly reversed. Two counter-moves: split the long sentence into two or three short ones and translate them separately — the logic usually walks back in; and give the load-bearing sentence the back-translation check (micro-lesson 2) — lost logic shows itself the moment the round trip is compared.

一条规则（背下来）The one rule (memorise it)

「翻译工具帮你读，不替你写；关键句子回译核对；不粘贴个人信息。」"Translation tools help you read, never write for you; back-translate and check the key sentences; never paste personal information."

  • 帮你读，不替你写——读英文材料，放手用；但你发布的、署你名字的文字（日志、短文、报告里的三句话）必须出自你的手。让工具替你写，你的声音就没了，而这门课要听的恰恰是你的声音；在计分任务里那也是学术诚信问题。Help you read, never write for you — for reading English, use it freely; but words published under your name (your log, essays, the report's three sentences) must come from your own hand. Let the tool write and your voice disappears — and your voice is exactly what this course wants to hear; on graded work it is also an integrity matter.
  • 关键句子回译核对——不必句句核，但压着整段意思的那句，走一遍微课 2 的四步。工具会错，而且错得一本正经；核对的责任在你。Back-translate and check the key sentences — not every line, but the sentence the passage leans on gets micro-lesson 2's four steps. The tool errs with a straight face; the duty of checking is yours.
  • 不粘贴个人信息——翻译框不是记事本：你粘进去的每个字都会被送上别人的服务器。真名、住址、学校名、账号密码、聊天记录，先删掉或换成化名再翻译。这条习惯下周 B4 会展开讲成一整周。Never paste personal information — the translation box is not a notepad: every character you paste travels to someone else's server. Real names, addresses, school names, account details, chat logs — delete or pseudonymise them before translating. Next week B4 grows this habit into a full week.
所以它为什么不算生成式 AI？So why doesn't it count as generative AI?
按本课程的规定：翻译工具不替你产生新内容，只把同一句话换一种语言——所以《AI 使用规范》把它单列，🚫 独立周也可以用。但"能用"不等于"随便用"：上面那条规则，就是它在这门课里的使用执照。执照只盖在翻译键上：同一个页面上的「AI 润色 / AI 写作」按钮属于生成式 AI——本周不用它们；翻译按钮本身不是。By this course's definition: a translation tool invents no new content — it re-dresses the same sentence in another language — so the AI Use Policy lists it separately, and 🚫 Independent weeks admit it. But "admitted" is not "unregulated": the rule above is its licence in this course. And the licence covers the translate button only: the "AI polish / AI writing" buttons on the same pages are generative AI — not for this week; the translate button itself is not.
