翻译与阅读工具Translation & Reading Tools

5 分5 pts🚫 独立🚫 Independent≤ 120 分钟≤ 120 min一周One week

翻开这门课的外部课程菜单看看：这学期你要真正在线学的那门课，很可能是英文讲的——Oakley 教授的学习科学、edX 的网课入门、开放大学的数字技能。第 6 周你就要去试学了。英文不该是门槛。你的教师当年也是从"英文只是一门考试的名称"起步的，后来英文变成了他获取更广阔知识的工具——桥就是这一类工具搭起来的。

Open this course's external course menu: the online course you will really take this semester is quite likely taught in English — Professor Oakley on the science of learning, edX's primer on online courses, the Open University's digital skills. In Week 6 you go sampling. English should not be the gate. Your instructor once knew English only as the name of an exam; later it became his tool for reaching far wider knowledge — and tools of exactly this kind built that bridge.

本周学的就是这类工具：翻译工具与阅读工具——百度翻译、有道翻译、微信里那个被低估的翻译键，还有查发音例句的词典、替你朗读的浏览器。但工具会出错，而且错得一本正经。所以本周真正的功夫只有一件：知道工具能帮你到哪里，并学会亲手核对它——把译文再译回去、和原文对照的"回译核对"，就是全年受用的那一招。

This week's subject is precisely those tools: translation and reading tools — Baidu Fanyi, Youdao, the underrated translate button inside WeChat, plus dictionaries for pronunciation and examples and browsers that read aloud for you. But the tools get things wrong, and wrong with a straight face. So the week's real craft is one thing: knowing where a tool stops helping, and checking it with your own hands — the back-translation check, translating the translation back and comparing, is the move that serves you all year.

核心问题Essential question

  • 翻译工具能帮我到哪里？Where do translation tools stop helping?

学完本周，你应能By the end of this week you should be able to

  1. ① 用翻译工具读完一段真实的英文课程介绍，并在自己的网站上发布一份"读后小报告"：一张截图（翻译工具使用中，原文与译文同框，个人信息已打码）+ 三句话指出译文译错或走样之处 + 一句"这门课我想不想试，为什么"；链接登记到导师的登记表（公开学习者：把链接存进自己的邮箱）。① Read a real English course introduction with a translation tool and publish a "reading report" on your own site: one screenshot (the tool at work, source and translation in the same frame, private details masked) + three sentences on what the translation got wrong or bent + one sentence, "would I try this course, and why"; register the link on your mentor's tracking sheet (open learners: file the link in your own inbox).
  2. ② 对介绍里的一个关键句完成"回译核对"，并把三行证据写进报告：原句 · 回译句 · 一处差异（对照后没发现明显差异的，写明对照过程，同样算完成）。② Run the back-translation check on one key sentence and write its three lines of evidence into the report: the original · the back-translation · one difference (finding no clear difference is fine too — state how you compared).
  3. ③（不计分 · 完成即可）练成本周的"翻译工具包"跟做三幕：整段粘贴并通读译文、回译核对一个句子、用词典深挖两个单词（发音 + 例句）。成果留在你自己的设备上。③ (ungraded · completing it is enough) The week's translation-kit follow-along: paste and read a whole passage, back-translate one sentence, dig into two words with a dictionary (pronunciation + examples). Everything stays on your own devices.

这三条也是本单元量规的评分行（第 ③ 条不计分，完成即可）。These three are also the rubric rows (row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough). → 量规Rubric

本周你还会学到：翻译工具的两个常见失手处（成语与专有名词会走样；长句会丢逻辑）、单词该查词典还是整段该用翻译的判断、朗读工具在哪里、以及"不把个人信息粘进翻译框"的习惯。这些不进量规——第 4 周页面的自测题帮你检查，不计分。You will also learn this week: the tools' two common failure spots (idioms and proper names garble; long sentences lose their logic), when a word wants a dictionary and a passage wants a translator, where the read-aloud tools live, and the habit of never pasting personal information into a translation box. None of it is graded — the Week 4 self-quiz checks it for you, ungraded.

本单元的 AI 标注：🚫 独立——那翻译工具呢？AI designation: 🚫 Independent — then what about translation tools?
课程《AI 使用规范》写得明白："翻译工具不视为生成式 AI；数字基本功单元会教你怎样正确地用它们。"本周就是那一课。翻译工具不替你想内容，只替你换语言——所以在 🚫 独立周照用不误，但要按一条规则用：翻译工具帮你读，不替你写；关键句子回译核对；不粘贴个人信息。报告里的三句话和那句表态，必须出自你自己的手。The course's AI Use Policy says it plainly: "Translation tools do not count as generative AI; Digital Basics teaches how to use them well." This week is that lesson. A translation tool invents no content for you — it only changes the language — so it is welcome even in an 🚫 Independent week, under one rule: translation tools help you read, never write for you; back-translate and check the key sentences; never paste personal information. The report's three sentences and the one-line verdict must come from your own hand.
