翻译工具包The translation kit

翻译是可逆的动作：译错了删掉重来，一分钱代价没有——所以这一页不设彩排，直接上手。下面是一段专门给你埋了"陷阱"的练习英文（本课程自己写的，内容就在说本周的事），三幕各练一样：整段通读、回译核对、词典深挖。全程在你自己的设备上，不发给任何人。

Translating is reversible: a wrong turn is deleted and redone at zero cost — so this page skips the rehearsal and goes straight to your hands. Below is a practice passage written by this course with traps deliberately buried in it (it happens to describe this very week); the three acts train one move each: the full read, the back-translation check, the dictionary dig. Everything stays on your own devices, sent to no one.

练习材料（复制整段用）——里面埋着：一个成语、一个专有名词、一个够长的句子。The practice passage (copy it whole) — buried inside: one idiom, one proper name, one properly long sentence.

Welcome to Week 4! Learning to read English with a translation tool is not rocket science, but it is not foolproof either. Professor Barbara Oakley, who created a famous online course about learning, often reminds her students that a tool which never explains its own mistakes should not be trusted blindly, unless you have checked its key sentences yourself.

第一幕 · 整段粘贴，通读译文（8 分钟）Act 1 · Paste the passage, read the translation (8 min)

  1. 先自己读一遍原文，认得几成算几成——猜一猜大意再往下走，这一步别跳过。Read the original yourself first, catching however much you catch — guess at the gist before moving on. Do not skip this step.
  2. 打开百度翻译或有道翻译的网页版，把整段粘进去，通读中文译文，看和你猜的差多远。Open Baidu's or Youdao's web version, paste the whole passage, read the Chinese through, and see how far it lands from your guess.
  3. 找陷阱：not rocket science 被译成什么了？Oakley 教授的名字还是名字吗？找到一处走样就算破案。三处都被译对了？也算破案——把「它译对了」记进备忘录：你亲眼确认了它对，这和抓到错一样是核对。再把整段粘进第二家工具对照，两家不一致的地方仍是现成的线索。Hunt the traps: what became of not rocket science? Is Professor Oakley's name still a name? Finding one bend closes the case. All three traps translated correctly? That closes the case too — note "it got these right": confirming with your own eyes is checking, exactly as catching is. Then run the passage through a second tool; wherever the two disagree is still a ready-made lead.

第二幕 · 回译核对一个句子（9 分钟）Act 2 · Back-translate and compare one sentence (9 min)

  1. 挑最后那个长句（从 Professor Barbara Oakley 开始的那句）——它是整段的关键句，也最容易丢逻辑。Take the closing long sentence (the one beginning with Professor Barbara Oakley) — the passage's key sentence, and the likeliest to lose its logic.
  2. 把它的中文译文单独复制，粘回工具（换第二家工具更好），译回英文——得到回译句。Copy its Chinese translation alone, paste it back into a tool (a second tool is better), and render it into English — your back-translation.
  3. 原句、回译句并排，找一处不同：unless 的意思还在吗？"不该盲目相信"有没有变成"该相信"？在备忘录里写下三行：原句 · 回译句 · 一处差异——本周任务要交的正是这个格式。（对照后真没发现明显差异？写「回译基本一致，我用＿＿和＿＿对照的」——这三行同样算齐，本周任务里这就是「诚实核对」那条路。）Lay original and back-translation side by side and find one difference: did unless survive? Did "should not be trusted blindly" quietly become "should be trusted"? Note three lines in a memo: original · back-translation · one difference — exactly the format this week's task collects. (No clear difference after comparing? Write "the round trip held; I compared ____ and ____" — the three lines still count in full: the honest-audit route the task itself honours.)

第三幕 · 词典深挖两个单词（8 分钟）Act 3 · Dig into two words with a dictionary (8 min)

先看一段桌面端的省事玩法——上周练的截图，这周直接变成翻译的入口：First, a desktop shortcut — last week's screenshot skill turning straight into a translation doorway:

超实用的截屏翻译功能，网易有道词典 · 2:31 · B 站 UP 主 潜心专研的小张同学 · 2021（已计入跟做分钟）——框选屏幕上任意一块英文直接翻译。选装：不装也完全不影响本幕，网页版词典一样查。Youdao's screenshot translation · 2:31 · Bilibili uploader 潜心专研的小张同学 · 2021 (counted in the follow-along minutes) — box any English on screen and translate it in place. Optional install: the web dictionary serves this act just as well. · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗

  1. 从练习段里挑两个词——推荐 foolproof 和 blindly——单独粘进百度或有道，页面自动变词典。Pick two words from the passage — foolproof and blindly recommended — and paste each alone into Baidu or Youdao; the page flips into dictionary mode.
  2. 每个词做三件事：点 🔊 听发音并跟读一遍；看词性；读一条例句，挑你觉得"这词就该这么用"的抄进备忘录。For each word, three things: tap 🔊, listen, echo it once; note the part of speech; read the examples and copy the one that shows the word at its most alive.
  3. 收尾：用翻译框旁的 🔊（或 Edge 的"大声朗读"）把整段英文听一遍，眼睛跟着走。听完这一遍，这段英文就是你的了。Close the loop: with the 🔊 beside the translation box (or Edge's Read Aloud), hear the whole passage once, eyes following along. After that listen, the passage belongs to you.
