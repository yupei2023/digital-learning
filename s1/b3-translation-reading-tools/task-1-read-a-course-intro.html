任务 · 用翻译工具读一段英文课程介绍Task · Read an English course intro with a translation tool

截图 · 三句话 · 回译 · 表态Screenshot · 3 sentences · check · verdict发布 + 登记链接Publish + register the link约 50 分钟（首次更久）~50 min (longer first time)

要做什么What to do

到外部课程菜单挑一门语言含英文的课（九门可选），打开它的介绍页，用任一翻译工具把那段英文介绍读懂；对一个关键句做回译核对；然后在你的网站上发布一份读后小报告——一张截图、三句话、回译三行、一句表态——再把链接登记到登记表。这是你第一次"工具阅读"真材实料的英文，也是给第 6 周试学、第 7 周选课提前踩的点。

Go to the external course menu, pick a course with English (nine qualify), open its introduction page, and actually understand its English blurb with a translation tool; run the back-translation check on one key sentence; then publish a reading report on your site — one screenshot, three sentences, the three back-translation lines, one verdict — and register the link. It is your first tool-assisted read of the real thing, and your advance scouting for Week 6's sampling and Week 7's choice.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 真实材料：菜单里一门语言含英文的课的介绍（外部页面，或按第 4 周页面第 10 节的兜底路线，连读菜单内 2–3 门课的英文介绍合成一段），读的段落约 3–6 句。Real material: the introduction of an English-language course on the menu (the external page, or the fallback route in the Week 4 page's §10 using 2–3 of the menu's own English blurbs read as one passage), a passage of roughly 3–6 sentences.
  • 一张截图：翻译工具使用中，原文与译文同框，看得出用的哪个工具；真名、账号、聊天记录先打码或裁掉（上周的手艺）。One screenshot: the tool at work, source and translation in one frame, the tool identifiable; real names, accounts and chats masked or cropped first (last week's craft).
  • 三句话，各指到具体词句，句式建议：「原文说＿＿，译文说成＿＿，我是这样发现的＿＿。」笼统的"翻得还行 / 不太好"不算。Three sentences, each pointing at specific words, in the suggested frame: "the original says ____, the translation made it ____, and here is how I caught it: ____." A vague "it's okay / not great" does not count.
  • 回译三行：原句 · 回译句 · 一处差异（对照后没有明显差异的，写明用了哪两个工具怎么对照的——诚实的核对记录同样算齐）。The three back-translation lines: original · back-translation · one difference (no clear difference found? state which two tools you compared and how — an honest audit counts in full).
  • 一句表态：这门课我想不想试，为什么——你自己的话，一句就够。One verdict: would I try this course, and why — your own words, one sentence suffices.
  • 已发布 + 已登记：报告发布在你网站的作品集页（第 3 周三个房间里「作品」住的那间；新条目或新建一页都行），链接登记到登记表，任务编号 B3；本周的三行日志照常另写在日志页，一周一条不变。公开学习者：链接发进自己的邮箱（主题"我的读后小报告 · 化名"）。Published + registered: the report lives on your site's Portfolio page (the room your works moved into in Week 3; a new entry or a new page, either way), the link on the tracking sheet, task number B3; the week's three-line log still goes on the log page as usual, one entry per week. Open learners: email the link to yourself (subject "my reading report · pseudonym").
  • 🚫 独立：三句话与表态不用生成式 AI 写、不请人代写；翻译工具按一条规则用——帮你读，不替你写。🚫 Independent: the three sentences and the verdict written by no generative AI and no other person; the translation tool used under the one rule — it helps you read, never writes for you.

三句话公式（这个任务的救生圈）The three-sentence frame (this task's lifebuoy)
「原文说＿＿，译文说成＿＿，我是这样发现的＿＿。」每句咬住一处具体走样。认真核对后真的挑不出？换成诚实的对照记录（用了哪两个工具、怎样对照、最接近可疑处在哪里）——量规第 ① 行写明：同样满分。"The original says ____, the translation made it ____, and here is how I caught it: ____." Each sentence bites one concrete bend. Truly nothing to catch after honest checking? Swap in the honest audit (which two tools, how you compared, the closest suspect) — rubric row ① says it in writing: the same full marks.

步骤Steps

  1. 挑课并打开介绍页（8 分钟）：在菜单里找"语言含英"的行——比如 Learning How to Learn（英文版）、How to Learn Online、Digital Skills for Work and Life。凭直觉挑一门你可能感兴趣的，点开介绍页。打不开？换一门（这些链接都标着"待实测"，打不开不是你的错）；都打不开走第 4 周页面第 10 节的兜底路线。Pick a course and open its intro (8 min): on the menu, find the rows whose language includes English — say Learning How to Learn (the English edition), How to Learn Online, or Digital Skills for Work and Life. Follow your curiosity, open the page. Won't open? Switch courses (every link is marked "to verify"; a dead page is not your failure); if none opens, take the fallback route in the Week 4 page's §10.
  2. 用工具通读（12 分钟）：先自己把英文介绍读一遍，认得几成算几成；再整页翻译或分段粘贴，通读译文。顺手把"原文 + 译文同框"的画面截下来（先打码再存，B2 的规矩）。Read it with the tool (12 min): first read the English yourself, catching what you catch; then whole-page translate or paste by paragraph and read the Chinese through. Capture the source-plus-translation frame while you are there (mask before saving — B2's rule).
  3. 回译核对关键句（10 分钟）：挑压着整段意思的那句（常是第一句，或带 you will 的那句），走微课 2 的四步，写下三行：原句 · 回译句 · 一处差异。（关键句请单独粘进翻译框再回译——整页翻译的页面上不方便只复制一句；通读用整页、核对用分段，各干各的活。）Back-translate the key sentence (10 min): take the load-bearing sentence (often the first, or the one carrying you will), walk micro-lesson 2's four steps, and write the three lines: original · back-translation · one difference. (Paste the key sentence into the translation box by itself — whole-page translation makes copying a single line awkward; whole-page for reading, pasted passages for checking, each to its own job.)
  4. 写三句话 + 一句表态（12 分钟）：三句话各咬住一处具体走样——硬译的名字、变味的成语、丢了的逻辑、别扭的中文都算；讲义翻译核对卡上有句式。最后一句写"这门课我想不想试，为什么"。Write the three sentences + the verdict (12 min): each sentence bites into one specific bend — a flattened name, a mistranslated idiom, dropped logic, Chinese no human would say; the translation check card handout carries the sentence frames. Close with "would I try this course, and why".
  5. 发布并登记（8 分钟）：把截图和文字发布到你的网站——放进作品集页：加一条"第 4 周 · 读后小报告"最省事，愿意也可以新建一页再从作品集链过去；本周的三行日志照常另写在日志页（第 4 周第 7 节），一周一条不变。重新点"发布"，用访客的眼睛看一遍，然后把报告的链接登记到登记表（公开学习者：发进自己的邮箱）。Publish and register (8 min): put the screenshot and text on your site — into the Portfolio page: a "Week 4 · reading report" entry is the easy road, a new page linked from Portfolio welcome too. The week's three-line log still goes on the log page as usual (Week 4 §7), one entry per week. Press Publish again, view it with a visitor's eyes, then register the report's link (open learners: email it to yourself).

评分 · 量规第 ① ② 行（3 + 2 = 5 分）Scoring · Rubric rows ① ② (3 + 2 = 5 pts)

第 ① 行看报告本身：截图同框、三句话具体、一句表态、已发布已登记。第 ② 行看回译三行：核对做了没有、差异指得具体不具体。评的是投入与核对的诚实——英语水平不打分，"没发现明显走样"如实写出对照过程同样满分。完整量规Row ① scores the report itself: the framed screenshot, three specific sentences, the verdict, published and registered. Row ② scores the back-translation lines: whether the check happened and how concretely the difference is named. What earns points is engagement and honest checking — English proficiency is never scored, and "no clear bend found", with the comparison honestly described, earns full marks. 常见问题FAQ

三句话必须都是"译错"吗？译文看着挺通顺。Must all three sentences be outright errors? The translation reads smoothly.

不必。"走样"都算：课程名或人名被硬译、语气比原文重或轻、长句译得每词都对合起来费解、一句不像中文的中文。还可以换第二个工具对照，两家不一致处就是现成的素材。真的一处都挑不出，如实写核对过程——诚实是这个任务的满分姿势。

No. Any "bend" counts: a flattened course or person name, tone heavier or lighter than the original, a long sentence right in every word and baffling as a whole, a line of Chinese no one would say. Or run a second tool and harvest wherever the two disagree. Genuinely nothing to catch? Write up the check honestly — honesty is this task's full-marks posture.

我想读的课不在菜单上。The course I want to read isn't on the menu.

本周先用菜单里的——它们是第 6–7 周选课的正式候选，本周读介绍正好一举两得。菜单外的课第 7 周有正式的申请路径（菜单页末尾写了条件）；真有心仪的，先记在日志里。

Use the menu this week — those are the official candidates for Weeks 6–7, so reading one now does double duty. Off-menu courses have a proper route in Week 7 (conditions at the foot of the menu page); if one already calls to you, note it in your log for now.

介绍页全是英文，我一打开就慌。The page is wall-to-wall English and I panic on sight.

这正是本周存在的理由。先做三次深呼吸，然后只做一件事：整页翻译，把它变成中文通读一遍——看，读懂了。然后再回到英文，找那句关键句。工具的意义就是把"全是英文"变成"我能下手"。

This is exactly why this week exists. Three slow breaths, then one move only: whole-page translate and read it in Chinese — see, understood. Then return to the English for the key sentence. The tool's whole purpose is turning "wall-to-wall English" into "I can work with this".

报告写中文还是英文？Chinese or English for the report?

中文（或你顺手的语言）。本周评的是"会用工具、会核对"，不是英文写作。想挑战自己，表态那句用英文写一版也很好——但请自己写，别让工具替你写（那条规则）。

Chinese (or whatever language is natural). This week grades tool use and checking, not English composition. Want a stretch? An English version of the verdict is lovely — written by you, not by the tool (the rule).

截图里有我的账号 / 聊天记录。My screenshot shows my account / chats.

先处理再发布：打码或裁掉（B2 的手艺）。用微信翻译的，把英文粘进"文件传输助手"再翻译，画面天生干净。也别忘了本周的新习惯：要翻译的文字里不留个人信息。

Treat before publishing: mask or crop (B2's craft). If you translate in WeChat, paste the English into File Transfer Helper first — a naturally clean frame. And remember this week's new habit: no personal information inside the text you translate.

我是公开学习者，怎么"评分"？I am an open learner — how do I get scored?

发布报告并把链接发给自己之后，打开量规页逐行自评，分数记进日志。一个建议（选做）：把译文里最好笑的一处走样讲给家里人听——能把"工具为什么会错"讲明白，这一课就真的学会了。

After publishing and emailing yourself the link, self-score row by row against the rubric and note it in your log. One suggestion (optional): tell your family about the funniest bend the tool produced — if you can explain why the tool errs, the lesson is truly yours.
