单词与整段Words vs passages

很多同学只有一件兵器：不管遇到什么，整个粘进翻译框。其实"读英文"有三种不同的时刻，各有各的趁手工具：卡在一个词——查词典；面对一整段——用翻译；想听它读出来——用朗读工具。用错了工具不算错，只是费劲：拿整段翻译查一个词，就像用地图软件找自家钥匙。

Many learners carry one weapon only: whatever appears, paste all of it into the translation box. But "reading English" really has three distinct moments, each with its own right tool: stuck on one word — a dictionary; facing a whole passage — a translator; wanting to hear it — a read-aloud tool. The wrong tool isn't wrong, just wasteful — checking one word with a passage translator is like hunting your house keys with a map app.

三种时刻，三件工具Three moments, three tools

时刻The moment用什么Use what看什么 / 听什么Look for / listen for
卡在一个词Stuck on one word词典模式：在百度翻译或有道翻译里只粘这一个词，页面自动变成词典（手机系统词典、词典 App 也一样）。Dictionary mode: paste just that word into Baidu or Youdao and the page turns into a dictionary (system dictionaries and dictionary apps do the same).按顺序看四样：🔊 发音（点小喇叭跟读一遍）→ 词性（动词还是名词，句子结构立刻清楚）→ 释义（挑贴合上下文的那条，别拿第一条就走）→ 例句——例句最值钱，它教你这个词"怎么活着用"。Four things in order: 🔊 pronunciation (tap the speaker, echo it once) → part of speech (verb or noun — the sentence's skeleton snaps into view) → the sense that fits your context (never grab the first and run) → the examples — worth the most, they show the word alive.
面对一整段Facing a whole passage翻译工具：整段粘贴，或整页翻译（浏览器 / 微信公众号右上角）。A translator: paste the passage, or translate the whole page (browser, or the top-right menu on a WeChat article).先自己把原文读一遍（认得几成算几成），再看译文——顺序反过来，你就只是在读中文，英语一点没练到。关键句另做回译核对（微课 2）。Read the original first — however much you catch — and only then the translation; reverse the order and you are merely reading Chinese, with the English untouched. Key sentences get the back-translation check (micro-lesson 2).
想听它读出来Wanting to hear it朗读工具：Edge 浏览器的"大声朗读"（右键或地址栏图标）；百度 / 有道翻译框旁的 🔊 也能朗读整段。A read-aloud tool: Edge's Read Aloud (right-click, or the address-bar icon); the 🔊 beside Baidu's and Youdao's boxes also reads whole passages.眼睛跟着读、耳朵听连读——一段英文"听一遍再读一遍"，比闷头看两遍记得牢；生词的发音顺路就学了。Eyes following, ears on the flow — hearing a passage once then reading it beats staring at it twice, and new words teach you their sound on the way past.
一句判断口诀The one-line judgment
一个词，查词典；一段话，用翻译；想听声，用朗读。三件常常连着用：整段翻译先通读 → 关键词回到词典深挖 → 朗读一遍收尾。跟做活动就按这个顺序走。One word — dictionary; one passage — translator; one listen — read-aloud. The three usually chain: translate the passage for the gist → return to the dictionary for the key words → close with one read-aloud pass. The follow-along walks exactly this order.
