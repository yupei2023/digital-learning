你的翻译工具箱Your translation toolbox

你的教师年轻时，英文对他"只是一门考试的名称"；后来它变成了他获取更广阔知识的工具——他在网上看世界名校的公开课、读英文资料、把科普文章翻译给更多人。今天的你比他当年幸运得多：好用的翻译工具就摆在你手边，免费、打开就能用。这一课先把工具箱清点一遍——五件工具，各有各的位置。

When your instructor was young, English was to him "only the name of an exam"; later it became his tool for reaching far wider knowledge — open courses from famous universities, English sources, science writing he translated onward for others. You are luckier than he was: capable translation tools sit at your fingertips, free and ready. This lesson takes inventory — five tools, each with its own place.

名单为什么是这五件？课程选工具的硬规则只有三条：免费、不要手机号、不用翻墙（标"待实测"的开课前逐一实测）。这五件都是中文世界里常用的翻译服务——这是关于工具的事实，不是关于你人在哪里。有些国际服务在某些地区打不开，所以不进名单；你那边有别的好工具，尽管用，本周这套方法对任何一家都成立。Why these five? The course applies three hard rules and no more: free, no phone number, no VPN (anything tagged "to verify" is access-tested before launch). All five happen to be translation services widely used in the Chinese-speaking world — that is a fact about the tools, not about where you are. Some international services will not open in some places, so they stay off the list; if a different good tool opens where you are, use it — everything this week teaches works on any of them.

还有一颗按钮要认准：这些工具的页面上如出现「AI 润色」「AI 写作」一类按钮，那就是生成式 AI 的入口——本周不用它们；翻译按钮本身不是（微课 4 讲界线在哪里）。One more button to recognise: if a tool's page shows an "AI polish" or "AI writing" button, that is a doorway into generative AI — leave those alone this week; the translate button itself is not one (micro-lesson 4 draws the line).

不需要注册，这件事本身就是知识No account needed — and that fact is itself a lesson

上面每一件工具的网页版都不登录就能用。有的翻译 App 会劝你注册、要你的手机号——记住：能不给的信息就不给，网页版够用就用网页版。哪些信息属于"不该给的"，下周 B4《账号、密码与隐私》整整讲一周。Every web version above works without logging in. Some translation apps will coax you to register, to hand over a phone number — remember: information you can withhold, withhold; when the web version serves, use the web version. Which information counts as "not to be given" is next week's whole subject, B4 Accounts, Passwords & Privacy.
