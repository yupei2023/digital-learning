B3 量规B3 Rubric

量规的三行就是本周的三条目标。第 ① ② 行计分（3 + 2 = 5 分），第 ③ 行不计分、完成即可。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。本任务是 🚫 独立任务——评投入与核对的诚实，不评英语水平，不评文笔。

The three rows are this week's three objectives. Rows ① ② carry points (3 + 2 = 5); row ③ is ungraded — completing it is enough. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence. This is an 🚫 Independent task — scored on engagement and honest checking, never on English proficiency or prose.

本周的知识点（两个局限、单词与整段的判断、朗读工具、不粘贴个人信息）不进量规——自测题帮你检查，不计分。隐私规则是全课程的底线（见下方"规范要求"）：截图误放了提醒改正，不扣分。This week's knowledge points (the two limits, the word-vs-passage judgment, read-aloud tools, never pasting personal information) are not scored here — the self-quiz checks them, ungraded. The privacy rules are the course-wide floor (see Form requirements below): a slip in a screenshot draws a reminder and a fix, never a deduction.
