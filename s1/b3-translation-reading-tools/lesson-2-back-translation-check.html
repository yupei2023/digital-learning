回译核对The back-translation check

一个看似无解的问题：你正因为读不懂英文才用翻译工具，那你怎么知道它译得对不对？总不能拿看不懂的原文去核对吧。有解——而且很巧妙：让工具把它自己的译文再译回英文，然后把"回译的英文"和"原来的英文"摆在一起对照。两句英文长得像，译文大概靠得住；差得远的那个地方，就是值得怀疑的地方。你不需要精通英文，只需要会"找不同"。

A seemingly unsolvable problem: you reached for the tool precisely because you can't read the English — so how do you know whether its translation is right? You can hardly check against an original you can't read. There is a way, and an elegant one: make the tool translate its own translation back into English, then lay the back-translation beside the original. Where the two Englishes resemble each other, the translation probably holds; where they part ways is exactly where to be suspicious. You don't need mastery of English — only the skill of spot-the-difference.

四步走一圈The four steps of the loop

  1. 挑关键句：不必句句核对——挑那句"整段的意思压在它身上"的话（课程介绍里常是第一句，或带 you will / learn to 的那句）。Pick the key sentence — no need to check every line: take the one the whole passage leans on (in a course intro, often the first sentence, or the one carrying "you will" / "learn to").
  2. 取它的中文译文，单独复制出来。Take its Chinese translation and copy it out on its own.
  3. 译回去：把这句中文粘回工具（或换第二个工具更好），译成英文——这就是"回译句"。Translate it back: paste the Chinese into the tool (a second tool is even better) and render it into English — this is the back-translation.
  4. 对照找不同：原句和回译句并排放，找出走样处——多了什么、丢了什么、哪个词变了味。差异处就写进本周报告的"回译三行"：原句 · 回译句 · 一处差异。Compare and spot the difference: original and back-translation side by side — what appeared, what vanished, which word changed flavour. The difference becomes this week's three report lines: original · back-translation · one difference.

诚实说明：它是报警器，不是判决书Honestly: it is an alarm, not a verdict

回译核对不是万能的，两点要心里有数：回译"通过"不等于译文一定对——工具去和回犯同一个错时，两句英文照样很像；回译"对不上"也不一定是译错——同一个意思本来就有很多种英文说法。所以把它当报警器用：响了，去查词典、换工具再译一次；没响，也保留一分清醒。这份"不全信工具"的习惯，比任何一个工具都值钱。

The check is not almighty; keep two caveats in mind. A "pass" does not prove the translation right — when the tool makes the same mistake out and back, the two Englishes still match. A mismatch does not prove it wrong — one meaning has many honest English clothes. So treat it as an alarm: when it rings, reach for the dictionary or a second tool; when it stays silent, keep an ounce of doubt anyway. That habit — never fully trusting the tool — is worth more than any tool itself.
