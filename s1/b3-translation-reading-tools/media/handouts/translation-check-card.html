翻译核对卡The translation check card

读英文材料时把这张卡放在手边。这学期的外部课程、以后每一段英文，都用它。

Keep this card beside you whenever you read English. This semester's external course, and every English passage after it, runs on the same card.

这一栏Section内容What it says一句话记住Remember it as
一条规则The one rule翻译工具帮你读，不替你写；关键句子回译核对；不粘贴个人信息（真名、地址、账号——删掉或化名替换再翻译）。Translation tools help you read, never write for you; back-translate and check the key sentences; never paste personal information (real names, addresses, accounts — delete or pseudonymise first).读可以，写不行；关键句要核；隐私不进翻译框。Reading yes, writing no; key sentences get checked; privacy never enters the box.
回译四步The four-step check① 挑关键句（整段意思压在它身上的那句）→ ② 单独复制它的中文译文 → ③ 粘回工具译回英文（换第二家工具更好）→ ④ 原句与回译句并排找不同。① Pick the key sentence (the one the passage leans on) → ② copy its Chinese translation alone → ③ paste it back and render into English (a second tool is better) → ④ lay original and back-translation side by side, spot the difference.译过去，译回来，找不同。对不上的地方 = 值得怀疑处。Out, back, spot the difference. Disagreement = the spot to distrust.
两个局限The two limits成语与专有名词会走样——首字母大写的名字保留英文原样记；长句会丢逻辑（尤其否定）——拆成两三个短句再译。Idioms and proper names garble — keep capitalised names in English; long sentences lose their logic (negations especially) — split into two or three short ones and retranslate.名字不译，长句拆短。Names stay; long sentences split.
三句话公式The three-sentence frame每句咬住一处具体走样：「原文说＿＿，译文说成＿＿，我是这样发现的＿＿。」硬译的名字、变味的成语、丢了的逻辑、不像中文的中文，都是素材。认真核对仍挑不出？如实写对照记录（哪两个工具、怎样对照、最接近可疑处）——量规同样满分。Each sentence bites one concrete bend: "the original says ____, the translation made it ____, and here is how I caught it: ____." Flattened names, mistranslated idioms, dropped logic and inhuman Chinese are all fair game. Nothing to catch after honest checking? Write the audit instead (which two tools, how, closest suspect) — the rubric pays it full marks.指到词句，不说"还行"。Point at words; never say "it's fine".
工具箱速查Toolbox quick list整段：百度翻译 / 有道翻译网页版（免登录）；手机快译：微信长按消息 / 扫一扫 / 公众号整页翻译；单词：同一工具只粘一个词即变词典（🔊 发音 + 例句）；朗读：Edge"大声朗读"或翻译框旁的 🔊；DeepL 网页版：如果打得开再用（待实测）。页面上的「AI 润色 / AI 写作」按钮属于生成式 AI——不用；翻译按钮不是。Passages: Baidu / Youdao web (no login); phone speed: WeChat long-press / Scan / whole-article translate; words: paste one word into the same tool for dictionary mode (🔊 + examples); listening: Edge's Read Aloud or the 🔊 by the box; DeepL web: only if it opens (to verify). "AI polish / AI writing" buttons on those pages are generative AI — skip them; the translate button is not.一个词，查词典；一段话，用翻译；想听声，用朗读。One word — dictionary; one passage — translator; one listen — read-aloud.
回译核对是报警器，不是判决书The check is an alarm, not a verdict
回译通过 ≠ 一定译对（去和回可能犯同一个错）；回译对不上 ≠ 一定译错（同一个意思有很多种英文说法）。响了就查词典、换工具；没响也留一分清醒。A pass does not prove it right (the same mistake can happen out and back); a mismatch does not prove it wrong (one meaning wears many honest English clothes). When it rings, check further; when silent, keep an ounce of doubt.
求助的固定姿势The standing way to ask for help
一张标注好的截图（红框圈重点 · 隐私先打码）+ 三句话：我想做什么 · 我做了什么 · 出现了什么。主题照旧："事由 · 化名（学生编号）"。One annotated screenshot (red box on the point, private parts masked first) + three sentences: what I tried · what I did · what happened. Subject as always: matter · pseudonym (student ID).
