请从上到下按顺序做。本周结束时，你的网站上会多出一份"读后小报告"——你第一次借工具读完一段真实的英文，并亲手抓住了译文走样的地方。先说清一件事：翻译工具不属于生成式 AI（课程《AI 使用规范》原文如此），所以这个 🚫 独立周照用不误——本周教的正是怎样把它用对。Work top to bottom. 0 · 先动手（1 分钟）0 · Hands first (one minute)

别看任何微课，现在就打开任何一个翻译工具的网页（百度翻译、有道翻译都行，不用登录），把下面这句英文粘进去，看看它给你什么中文：No lesson yet — open any translation tool's web page right now (Baidu or Youdao, no login needed), paste in the sentence below, and see what Chinese comes back:

译文里下猫下狗了吗？"a piece of cake"变成一块蛋糕了吗？（它其实是"小菜一碟"。）恭喜——你已经亲眼见过本周最重要的事实：工具会错，而且错得一本正经。为什么本周敢让你先动手？因为翻译和上周的截图一样是可逆的动作：译错了删掉重译就是，不花任何代价。可逆的动作，先试后学。Did it rain actual cats and dogs in the Chinese? Did "a piece of cake" turn into pastry? (It really means "dead easy".) Congratulations — you have now seen this week's central fact with your own eyes: the tool errs, and errs with a straight face. Why does this week dare to start with your hands? Because translating, like last week's screenshots, is reversible: a bad translation is deleted and redone at zero cost. Reversible actions get tried before studied.

如果它全译对了呢？也恭喜——译对了也算发现：把「它译对了」记下来，你现在亲眼确认了它对，这本身就是一次核对。常被引用的名句，工具早背熟了；它栽跟头的地方在少见的说法和更长的句子上。不信加试下面这句（本课程自己写的，没人引用过）——回来的中文若是每个词都对、合起来却让你皱眉，那就是微课 4 要讲的「长句丢逻辑」：And if it got everything right? Congratulations too — a correct translation is also a finding: write down "it got this one right"; you have now confirmed it with your own eyes, and confirming is itself a check. Much-quoted lines the tools memorised long ago; where they stumble is the rarely-said and the long-winded. Test the sentence below (written by this course, quoted by no one) — if the Chinese that returns is right in every word yet furrows your brow as a whole, that is micro-lesson 4's "long sentences lose their logic":

Not that reading English without a tool is impossible — but unless a translation has never once misled you, you should not assume that a sentence the tool renders smoothly is a sentence it has rendered right.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

这是 🚫 独立周，用翻译工具算不算违规？This is an 🚫 Independent week — is using a translation tool a violation?

不算。课程《AI 使用规范》写明："翻译工具不视为生成式 AI；数字基本功单元会教你怎样正确地用它们。"本周任务恰恰要求你用翻译工具。真正的界线是那条规则：工具帮你读可以，替你写不行——报告里的三句话、一句表态，必须自己写；用生成式 AI（聊天机器人）代写才是违规。

No. The course's AI Use Policy states: "Translation tools do not count as generative AI; Digital Basics teaches how to use them well." This week's task requires the tool. The real line is the rule: helping you read is fine, writing for you is not — the three sentences and the verdict must be your own; having a generative AI chatbot write them is what violates.

课程介绍页打不开怎么办？The course's introduction page won't open — now what?

菜单里的外部链接都标着"待实测"，打不开是正常情况，不是你的错。按顺序补救：① 换菜单里另一门语言含英文的课（有九门可挑）；② 都打不开时，把菜单页右上角切成 English，菜单里每门课自带的英文介绍就是你的阅读材料——每门只有一两句，连读 2–3 门、合成一段来读，就凑足了任务要求的 3–6 句，顺手也提前完成第 6 周「浏览至少三门课」的踩点。照常翻译、核对、写报告，并在报告里加一句"外部页面未能打开，用的是菜单内介绍"。两条路的分数一模一样。

Every external link on the menu is marked "to verify" — a page refusing to open is a known condition, not your failure. Recover in order: ① switch to another English-language course on the menu (nine to choose from); ② if none opens, flip the menu page to English at the top right — each course's own English blurb there becomes your reading material — each is only a sentence or two, so read 2–3 courses' blurbs back to back as one passage: that makes the task's 3–6 sentences and quietly banks Week 6's "browse at least three courses" scouting. Translate, check and report as usual, adding one line: "external pages unreachable; menu blurb used". Both routes score identically.

DeepL 打不开。DeepL won't open.

正常。DeepL 网页版在菜单里就标着"在你那边打得开再用"——它译得讲究，但在有些地方就是打不开，这跟你的操作无关。用百度、有道或微信，任务不受任何影响；本周的任何一步都不需要 DeepL。

Expected. DeepL's web version is listed strictly as "use it if it opens where you are" — a careful translator that simply does not open in some places, through no fault of yours. Baidu, Youdao or WeChat serve fully; nothing this week depends on DeepL.

我英语挺好的，能不能不用翻译工具直接读？My English is decent — may I just read it straight, no tool?

直接读，非常好——但请照常把工具打开一次：本周练的不只是"读懂"，更是"核对工具"。英语好的你恰恰是最佳裁判：你能挑出的走样处比别人更多、更准，三句话会写得漂亮。把工具当被告，你当法官。

Read it straight — excellent. But still open the tool once: this week trains not just reading, but auditing the tool. Good English makes you the ideal judge — you will catch more bends, more precisely, and your three sentences will shine. The tool is the defendant; you are the bench.

译文看起来全对，三句话挑不出错。The translation looks fine — I can't find three faults.

把"错"放宽成"走样"：课程名或人名被硬译了吗？语气比原文更硬或更软？哪句中文读着别扭、不像人话？还可以换第二个工具译同一段，两个译文不一致的地方就是可疑处。全都试过仍然挑不出？如实写"我用____和____对照，没发现明显走样，最接近可疑的是____"——诚实的核对记录同样拿满分（量规第 ① ② 两行都写明了这一条）。

Widen "wrong" to "bent": did a course name or a person's name get literally translated? Is the tone harder or softer than the original? Which Chinese sentence reads like no human wrote it? You can also run the passage through a second tool — wherever two translations disagree is a suspect spot. Still nothing? Write honestly: "I compared ____ and ____, found no clear bend; the closest suspect is ____" — an honest audit trail earns full marks (rubric rows ① and ② both say so).

要截的图里有我的微信聊天记录 / 账号信息。My screenshot would show my WeChat chats / account details.

先处理再发布——上周的规矩原样适用：用标注工具打码，或干脆裁掉那一块；用微信翻译的，最好新建一个"文件传输助手"对话专门粘英文，画面干净。另外记住本周的新习惯：要翻译的文字里也不粘个人信息——真名、地址、账号，先删掉或用化名替换（下周 B4 细讲原因）。

Treat before it travels — last week's rule applies unchanged: mask with the annotation tool or crop the region away; if you translate inside WeChat, paste your English into the File Transfer Helper chat so the frame stays clean. And note this week's new habit: no personal information inside the text you translate either — real names, addresses, account details get deleted or replaced with a pseudonym first (B4 explains why next week).

整页翻译好，还是分段粘贴好？Whole-page translation or paragraph-by-paragraph pasting?

通读用整页翻译（浏览器或微信的整页翻译最快），核对用分段粘贴——关键句单独粘，才好做回译。两样都用，各干各的活。长句译不通时，把它拆成两三个短句再译，逻辑常常就回来了（微课 4 讲为什么）。

Read with whole-page translation (browser or WeChat, fastest); check with pasted passages — a key sentence pasted alone is what back-translation needs. Use both, each for its own job. When a long sentence translates into nonsense, split it into two or three short ones and the logic often returns (micro-lesson 4 explains why).

这周正好赶上长假怎么办？What if a holiday week lands on top of this?

按课程的假期规则：长假那一周是缓冲周，不开新单元，用来补做和休息（具体见日历页）。英文介绍不会跑——假期结束接着读就是。没有迟交处罚。

The course's holiday rule applies: a holiday week is a buffer week — no new module, just catch-up and rest (see the calendar). The English will wait; pick it up after the break. No late penalty.
