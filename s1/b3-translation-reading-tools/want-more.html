做Do

  • 把表态那句自己写成英文——任务里「这门课我想不想试，为什么」那句，亲手写一版英文加进报告（自测第 3 题说的正是这个练习）。写得朴素完全没关系——那是你的声音；让工具替你写，就出了那条规则的界。Write your verdict sentence in English yourself — the task's "would I try this course, and why", hand-written in English and added to the report (the very exercise self-quiz Q3 points to). Plain is perfectly fine — it is your voice; letting the tool write it steps over the rule.
  • 给家里人当一回翻译顾问——找一样家里的英文材料（说明书、包装、邮件），用本周的工具读给他们听，并告诉他们哪句你核对过。能讲清"工具为什么会错"，这一课就真的学会了。Be the family's translation consultant for a day — take some household English (a manual, a label, an email), read it to them with this week's tools, and point out which sentence you verified. Explaining why the tool errs is the proof of learning.
  • 回译一句你喜欢的中文——把一句喜欢的话译成英文再译回来，看它绕一圈回来变成了什么。变味最重的那个词，就是最"中文"的那个词。Round-trip a favourite Chinese line — into English and back, and see what returns. The word that suffers most in transit is the most untranslatably Chinese one.
  • 让浏览器给你读一页英文——用 Edge 的"大声朗读"听完你任务里那门课的整个介绍页，跟读一分钟。第 6 周试学时，你的耳朵会认得这门课的声音。Have the browser read you a page — Edge's Read Aloud through your task course's whole intro page, echoing along for a minute. Come Week 6's sampling, your ears will already know the course's voice.
