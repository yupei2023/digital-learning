有意义的数字化学习Meaningful Digital Learning

我读线上硕士的那两年，每一个周三到来时，心率都会慢慢加快——作业的截止时间是周三深夜。我仿佛听见自己的心脏坐着一列由远而近的火车，裹挟着越来越多的压力向我驶来；直到提交了作业，心率才恢复正常。然后，下一个周三又来了。后来我的身体适应了这个节奏：周四、周五下班后集中看书，周六、周日写作业，周一、周二修改。家人主动承担了更多家务，给我腾出时间。没有那张写在一周里的时间表，没有家人的支持，我拿不到那个学位。

In the two years of my online master's degree, every Wednesday my heart rate crept up — assignments were due late Wednesday night. I could almost hear my heart riding a train coming from far away, carrying more and more pressure towards me; only after I submitted did it settle. Then the next Wednesday came. In time my body learned the rhythm: reading Thursday and Friday evenings, writing on the weekend, revising Monday and Tuesday. My family quietly took on more of the housework to clear my time. Without that timetable written into a real week, and without their support, I would not have that degree.

这两周，你要为自己的主修外部课程做同一件事：写一条目标，把它排进你真实的一周，然后真的按表学一周，再回来看看哪里按上了、哪里没有。计划不是交给导师的作业，是你自己要用的工具。

These two weeks you do the same thing for your core external course: write one goal, place it into your real week, then actually study by that plan for a week — and come back to see what held and what didn't. The plan is not homework for your mentor; it is a tool you will use.

核心问题Essential questions

  • 怎样让我的数字化学习更高效？How can I make my digital learning more effective?
  • 怎样制定并实现一份适合自己的学习计划？How do I build a personal learning plan — and keep it?

学完本单元，你应能By the end of this module you should be able to

  1. ① 为自己的主修外部课程（或其指定子集）写出一条 SMART 学习目标，并说明它为什么对自己重要。① Write one SMART goal for your core external course (or its named subset), and say why it matters to you.
  2. ② 用一个日程工具，把从本周到寒假的学习时段排进自己真实的一周，并如实记录这一周的执行情况。② Using a scheduling tool, place your study slots from this week to the winter break into your real week, and keep an honest record of how the plan went for one week.
  3. ③ 对照计划复盘这一周，指出按计划做了什么、没做什么，做出至少一处有理由的调整。③ Review the week against the plan — what went to plan, what didn't — and make at least one justified change.

预计用时Time

每周 120 分钟以内，其中 60 分钟是你的主修外部课程——从第 11 周起，它是每周必做的一部分。剩下 60 分钟：看/读约 20 · 做约 30 · 反思与日志约 10。标注“选做”的内容不计入。Within 120 minutes a week, of which 60 minutes are your core external course — from Week 11 it is part of every week's required work. The other 60: watch & read ~20 · do ~30 · reflect & log ~10. Items marked optional don't count.

本单元的 AI 标注：✅ 辅助AI designation for this module: ✅ Assisted
两项任务都允许使用课程认可清单中的 AI 工具辅助（例如帮你把目标写得更清楚、检查语句），在作业结尾附两三句“AI 使用说明”：用了什么工具、它帮了什么忙。完全不用 AI 同样可以拿满分。目标必须是你自己要做的事，日程必须是你自己的一周，复盘里的理由必须是你这一周真实发生的事。认可清单在第 1 周《关于本课程与 AI》页公布。Both tasks allow AI tools from the course's approved list (e.g., to sharpen the wording of your goal or check sentences); end your work with a two–three-sentence AI use note: which tool, what it helped with. Full marks are equally reachable without AI. The goal must be something you will actually do, the schedule must be your own week, and the reasons in your review must be what really happened. The approved list is published on the Week 1 “About this course and AI” page.
