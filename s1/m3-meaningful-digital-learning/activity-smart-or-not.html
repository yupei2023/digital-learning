这条目标 SMART 吗？Is this goal SMART?

八条学习目标。点一张卡片，再点一个筐：它最缺的是什么？有两条的答案是“看情况”——放进去之后，要写出“对我来说现实的条件是____”（只保存在你自己的浏览器里）。全部放完后点“检查”。每一条都有解释——解释比对错更重要。

Eight study goals. Tap a card, then tap a bin: what does it lack most? Two of them are “it depends” — after placing them, write “the condition that makes it realistic for me is ____” (saved only in this browser). When all eight are placed, press Check. Every card has an explanation — the explanation matters more than the score.

卡片Cards

🔍 不够清晰、不可测（S / M）🔍 Not clear or measurable (S / M)

⛰️ 做不到或不现实（A / R）⛰️ Not doable or realistic (A / R)

⏳ 没有时间截点（T）⏳ No deadline (T)

✅ 五个字母都有✅ All five letters

🤔 看情况（写出条件）🤔 It depends (name the condition)
