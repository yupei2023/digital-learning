做Do

  • 一周“专时专用”实验：把主修课的时段设成手机“勿扰”；每次学完，在日志里记下实际开始和结束的时间。周日看一眼：表上的 60 分钟，实际是多少？A one-week “protected time” experiment: set your phone to Do Not Disturb for the core-course slot; after each session, note the actual start and end time in your log. On Sunday, look: the 60 minutes on paper — how many were they really?
