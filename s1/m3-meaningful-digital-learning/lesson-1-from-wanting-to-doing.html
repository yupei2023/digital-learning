从“想学”到“学到”From wanting to doing

热情做成了一半Passion is half the job

第 8、9 周我们说过，把守学习大门的有两个门卫：情绪和动机。你为主修课写下“我的外部课程选择”那一天，多半是带着热情的——那门课的介绍页让你眼睛一亮。当你怀揣着热情、热爱，或者内部动机去开始一件事的时候，你已经把这件事做成了一半了。

In Weeks 8 and 9 we said two gatekeepers guard the door of learning: emotion and motivation. The day you wrote “my external course choice” on your site, you were probably carrying some enthusiasm — the course page had made your eyes light up. When you start something with passion, love, or internal motivation, you have already done half the job.

那另一半呢？另一半是一个合适的目标，和一份写在真实的一周里的计划。热情负责开门；目标和计划负责让你每周都走进去。

And the other half? The other half is a proper goal and a plan written into a real week. Passion opens the door; the goal and the plan get you through it every week.

为什么大多数计划会失败Why most plans fail

回到你在“起点”写的第 3 行：上次的计划是怎么停下来的？我看过很多学生的计划，也停过很多次自己的计划，原因几乎逃不出下面四个。

Go back to line 3 of your starting point: how did your last plan stop? I have seen many students' plans, and stopped many of my own; the causes almost never escape these four.

#原因Cause长什么样What it looks like解药Remedy
1目标模糊A fuzzy goal“我要学好英语。”——学到什么算“好”？到哪天？谁也说不出，于是永远没有“做到了”的那一刻。“I'll get good at English.” — How good is good? By when? Nobody can say, so the moment of “done” never arrives.清晰的目标，清晰的结果；模糊的目标，模糊的结果。用微课 2 的五个字母检查它。Clear goals, clear results; fuzzy goals, fuzzy results. Check it with micro-lesson 2's five letters.
2切分不当Chunks the wrong size要么一口吞下一整门课，被吓退；要么切得太碎，每一步都没有完成感。Either you try to swallow a whole course and get scared off, or you slice it so thin that no step feels like an achievement.想象一段楼梯：一步跨上一层楼太难，但一级一级的台阶谁都能上。“切分就是力量”——台阶别太高，也别太矮。Picture a staircase: leaping a whole floor is impossible, but anyone can climb it step by step. “Chunking is power” — steps neither too tall nor too short.
3没写下来Never written down目标只在脑子里，一周后连原话都记不清；没有一个东西每天提醒你“今天有一级台阶要上”。The goal lives only in your head; a week later you can't even recall the wording, and nothing reminds you daily that there is a step to climb.写下来，放在每天都会看到的地方——这门课里，就是你的网站和你的日历。Write it down where you will see it every day — in this course, that means your website and your calendar.
4没有时间（其实是：没有时段）No time (really: no slot)“有空就学。”——一周过去了，从来没有“空”。“I'll study when I'm free.” — A week passes and “free” never comes.给它一个截止日，再给它一个每周固定的时段。微课 3 教你把它放进真实的一周。Give it a deadline, then a fixed weekly slot. Micro-lesson 3 shows you how to place it in a real week.

切分就是力量：一步跨一层楼太难，一级一级谁都能上。台阶别太高，也别太矮。Chunking is power: leaping a whole floor is impossible; anyone can climb step by step. Steps neither too tall nor too short.

四个原因有一个共同点：都不是“我不够努力”。它们是设计问题——目标怎么写、怎么切、放在哪里。设计问题，用设计解决。

The four causes share one thing: none of them is “I didn't try hard enough”. They are design problems — how the goal is written, how it is chunked, where it lives. Design problems are solved by design.

报了课，不等于学到了Enrolling is not learning

读本第 10 章的第一个锦囊叫“为你所用”。线上的好课太多了，一辈子也学不完。看到这门好，报个名；看到那份资料好，下载下来——报名课程、把资料存进硬盘，这种意识是好的，但你报了课，不等于你学到了本领；你下载了资料，不等于你读到了有意义的内容。资料，只有用了，才是你的；课程，只有学了，才是你的。

The first tip in Reader Ch. 10 is “make it serve you”. There are more good courses online than a lifetime can hold. You see a good one and enrol; you see a good file and download it — the instinct is fine, but enrolling in a course is not learning the skill, and downloading a file is not reading anything worthwhile. A resource is yours only once you use it; a course is yours only once you learn it.

这两周就做一件事：把那门你已经“报了”的课，变成一门你在“学”的课。

These two weeks do one thing: turn the course you have already “enrolled in” into a course you are “learning”.

带着这句话去看微课 2：Take this sentence into micro-lesson 2:一个好目标，不是让你更有雄心的句子，而是一个让你在某一天能够确定地说“做到了”的句子。A good goal is not a sentence that makes you more ambitious; it is a sentence that lets you say, on a definite day, “done”.
