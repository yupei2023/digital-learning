10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我的主修课还没选定 / 想换一门。I haven't chosen my core course / I want to switch.

先在 菜单 上选，再做本周任务——目标必须是一门具体的课。在校学生换课请先邮件导师确认（第 7 周的规则）。已经发布了任务 1 才换课的：回到同一页改目标和日程，重截一张图，不用另建、不用重新登记——链接没变；在日志第 3 行写一句“换成了____，因为____”。

Choose from the menu first, then do the task — the goal must be about one specific course. Enrolled students: email your mentor to confirm a switch (the Week 7 rule). Switching after Task 1 is published: edit the goal and schedule on the same page and re-capture the screenshot — no new entry, no re-registration, the link is unchanged — and add a line to log line 3: “switched to ____, because ____”.

我是公开学习者，没有“寒假开始那天”。目标里的截止写什么？I am an open learner without a “winter break”. What deadline goes in the goal?

把“寒假开始”换成从本周起第 4 周的周日（也就是第 14 周末），或者你自己定的一个具体日期——写进目标的 T，也设成日历里重复事件的结束日。之后想继续，再排新的一段。

Replace “the start of the winter break” with the Sunday four weeks from now (the end of Week 14), or any concrete date you set yourself — put it in the goal's T and as the end date of the repeating event. Schedule a new stretch afterwards if you want to continue.

我的一周已经排满了，哪来 1 小时？My week is already full — where is the hour?

先把固定不可动的事写下来，通常会发现两三个 30 分钟的空档（通勤、晚饭后、周末上午）。实在没有，就把目标缩小到菜单标注的子集，1 小时改成两个 30 分钟——这本身就是一个 SMART 的“R”。

Write down the immovable things first; two or three 30-minute gaps usually appear (commute, after dinner, weekend morning). If truly none, shrink the goal to the menu's named subset and use two 30-minute slots — that is the “R” in SMART at work.

“菜单标注的子集”是什么？What is “the menu's named subset”?

有的课全部学完要十几个小时，每周 1 小时到寒假学不完，所以菜单为它标了一个小一些的部分——比如《如何学习》的“模块 1–2”。写目标时，目标就指向这个子集的完成。每门课的子集都写在 外部课程菜单 上。

Some courses take a dozen hours in full — more than one hour a week until the break — so the menu names a smaller part, e.g. “modules 1–2” of Learning How to Learn. Your goal points at completing that subset. Every course's subset is on the external course menu.

我的时段排在周一，这周已经过了怎么办？My slot is on Monday and this week's has already passed.

这周任选一个空档补上第一次，从下周起按表。补不上也没关系——写进下周的复盘，那也是信息。

Use any free gap this week for the first session and follow the plan from next week. If you can't, that's fine — put it in next week's review; it is information too.

用什么日历？一定要装软件吗？Which calendar? Do I need to install something?

不用装。手机和电脑自带的“日历”就够了，不需要注册。想用别的工具也可以，只要不需要手机号注册、大陆能打开。

Nothing to install. The built-in Calendar on your phone or computer is enough and needs no account. Another tool is fine if it needs no phone number to sign up and opens from the mainland.

截图会暴露我的日程，怎么办？The screenshot exposes my schedule.

只需要看得见“主修课”的时段。其他事件改名为“私事”，或用截图标注工具涂掉（第 3 周学过截图标注，第 5 周学过隐去敏感信息）。不要出现他人的姓名。

Only the course slots need to be visible. Rename other events “private” or block them out with the annotation tool (screenshot annotation was Week 3, redacting sensitive information was Week 5). No one else's names.
