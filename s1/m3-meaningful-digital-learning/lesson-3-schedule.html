工具操作三步（第 11 周做这一节）The three tool steps (do this section in Week 11)

用手机或电脑自带的“日历”就够了——不用注册，不用装软件，各地都能打开。另一个工具也行，只要不需要手机号注册。

Your phone's or computer's built-in Calendar is enough — no account, nothing to install, and it opens wherever you are. Another tool is fine as long as it needs no phone number to sign up.

  1. 先写固定的事，再找空档。把这一周里不可动的东西列出来：上课、通勤、晚饭、睡觉、已经答应别人的事。剩下的白格子，才是你真正拥有的时间——通常比你以为的少，但一定有。Fixed things first, then the gaps. List what cannot move this week: classes, commute, dinner, sleep, promises already made. The white squares left over are the time you actually own — usually less than you thought, but never zero.
  2. 新建一个重复事件。名字就写“主修课”（不要写课程全名——截图要发布）。时长 60 分钟，或者两个 30 分钟；重复：每周；结束：寒假开始那天（公开学习者：你自己定的日期，见第 11 周常见问题）。放进一个白格子里，最好是你一周里最不容易被挤掉的那个（周末上午、晚饭后）。第 7 周你已经在日历里设过一条“外部课程 · 一小时”的每周重复提醒——那条就是底子：把它改名为“主修课”、把起点和时长改成现在的安排即可，不必另设一条（提醒越多，每一条就越不响）。在事件的“备注”里写上第 10 周你在公开信里给自己定的那条对策（比如“手机放到另一个房间”）——提醒响起时它会一起出现。Create one repeating event. Name it just “core course” (not the full course name — the screenshot gets published). Length 60 minutes, or two events of 30; repeat: weekly; ends: the day the winter break begins (open learners: the date you set yourself — see the Week 11 FAQ). Put it in a white square — ideally the one least likely to get squeezed (weekend morning, after dinner). In Week 7 you already set a weekly repeating reminder, “External course · one hour” — that one is the foundation: rename it “core course” and change its start date and length to match this plan, rather than creating a second (the more reminders, the less each one rings). In the event's note, write the mitigation you set yourself in your Week 10 open letter (e.g., “phone in the other room”) — it will appear with the reminder.
  3. 设提醒，然后截图。提醒设在开始前 10 分钟。截“周视图”，让人一眼看到这个时段在一周里的位置；其他事件改名为“私事”或涂掉；不要有他人的姓名。Set a reminder, then screenshot. Reminder 10 minutes before. Capture the week view so the slot's place in the week is obvious; rename other events “private” or block them out; no one else's names.

你的截图大概就长这样：灰的是固定的事，白的是你真正拥有的时间，绿的是排进去的两个“主修课”格子。手机日历的周视图是竖的，也一样。Your screenshot will look roughly like this: grey is fixed, white is the time you actually own, green is the two “core course” squares you placed. A phone's week view is vertical; same idea.

手机日历：新建事件 → 打开“重复”→ 选“每周”→ 设“结束重复”日期。电脑日历（macOS / Windows 自带）：双击某个时段 → 同样有“重复”一栏。找不到就在设置里搜“重复”。Phone calendar: New event → Repeat → Weekly → set an end date. Computer calendar (built into macOS / Windows): double-click a slot → the same Repeat field. If you can't find it, search the settings for “repeat”.

专时专用（第 12 周读）Protected time (read in Week 12)

读本第 10 章的第二个锦囊。线上学习时，时间流逝得特别快：你从一个问题出发，一边检索，一边跳到了另一个问题上；为了解决那个问题，又跳到别的资料上；查一个概念的时候，又看到一条突发新闻……绕了一大圈，好几个小时过去了，你甚至忘了最初要解决的问题是什么。怪不得人们把上网叫做“冲浪”。

The second tip in Reader Ch. 10. Time runs strangely fast when you learn online: you start from one question, search, and jump to another; to solve that one you jump to some other material; while looking up a term you see a piece of breaking news… a big loop later, hours have gone and you have forgotten what you set out to solve. No wonder people call it “surfing”.

解药是专时专用：这四十分钟，就是用于主修课的；每天到了这个时间点，就开始；时间一到，才可以再去看别的。你在日历里建的那个重复事件，就是在替你说这句话。一旦线上学习形成了习惯，习惯会帮你节省更多的心力——你不再需要每次都“决定”学不学。

The remedy is protected time: these forty minutes belong to the core course; when the clock reaches this point, you start; only when it ends may you look at anything else. The repeating event you created is saying exactly this on your behalf. Once online learning becomes a habit, the habit saves you effort — you no longer have to “decide” each time whether to study.

流程操作：让一件轻松的事带着它走（第 12 周读）Routine: let something easy carry it (read in Week 12)

第三个锦囊。我见过很多在线学习者，刚开始一个项目的时候目光炯炯、兴致盎然，一两周之后新鲜劲儿没了，中途放弃。我的办法，是把线上学习和一件我本来就喜欢、也有规律的事连成一个流程：开始学习之前，先喝一杯咖啡。喝咖啡对我来说是放松，喝完咖啡，我就会自动进入打开电脑、开始学习的状态——也因为这个流程，我平常咖啡喝得比较多。

The third tip. I have seen many online learners begin a programme bright-eyed and eager, lose the novelty after a week or two, and quit. My method is to chain online learning to something I already enjoy and already do regularly: before studying, a cup of coffee. Coffee relaxes me, and once the cup is empty I slide automatically into opening the laptop and starting — which is also why I drink rather a lot of coffee.

事情一旦进入一个流程，你只要开启了它，就更容易进入自动进行的状态。找一件你每周本来就会做、和那个时段挨着的事——晚饭后收拾完桌子、周六早上的一杯牛奶、跑步回来冲完澡——让它成为“主修课”的开关。第 12 周微课 4 会告诉你，为什么这个“开关”常常是复盘里最值得改的那一处。

Once a thing is part of a routine, starting the routine tends to carry you through. Find something you already do every week right next to that slot — clearing the table after dinner, a Saturday-morning glass of milk, the shower after a run — and make it the switch for “core course”. Micro-lesson 4 in Week 12 explains why this switch is so often the one thing most worth changing in your review.

关于截图的隐私Screenshot privacy

  • 只需要看得见“主修课”的格子；其他格子改名“私事”，或用截图标注工具涂掉（第 2 周学过标注，第 5 周学过隐去敏感信息）。Only the “core course” squares need to be visible; rename the rest “private” or block them out with the annotation tool (annotation was Week 2, redaction was Week 5).
  • 不出现任何他人的姓名；不出现学校全名。No one else's names; no full school name.
  • 你写的是自己的时间，不是家人的。You are scheduling your own time, not your family's.
