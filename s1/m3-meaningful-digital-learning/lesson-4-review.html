复盘：没按计划做，不是失败，是信息Review: a slipped plan is information, not failure

治疗拖延症，这比你想象的更简单也更有价值 · 约 6 分钟 · B 站 UP 主 _愚人金_（Coursera《学会如何学习》中英字幕转载）Tackling procrastination — easier and more valuable than you think (Learning How to Learn 3.1.2) · ~6 min · Bilibili uploader _愚人金_ · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗

跳出一两个，休息一下再来One or two, rest, try again

读本第 03 章里，我五年级学双摇：先看六年级的选手怎么跳，然后试——能跳出一个、两个，但不能连续。没关系，休息一下再来。这次三个、四个。休息一下再来。一个晚上，就能连着跳很多个了。

In Reader Ch. 03, in grade 5, I learned the double-under: watch the sixth-graders first, then try — one, two, but not in a row. Fine; rest, try again. Three, four this time. Rest, try again. In one evening I could string many together.

注意那个晚上发生了什么：我没有一上来就成功。每一次“跳不过去”，都告诉我一点东西——手要再快一点，跳要再高一点。你上周的表也一样。没按上的那几次，不是在告诉你“你不行”，而是在告诉你：那个时段不对、那个目标太大、或者那一刻缺一个开关。没按计划做，不是失败，是信息。

Notice what happened that evening: I did not succeed at the first try. Every failed jump told me something — turn faster, jump higher. Last week's timetable works the same way. The slots you missed are not saying “you can't”; they are saying: that slot is wrong, that goal is too big, or that moment lacked a switch. A slipped plan is not failure. It is information.

复盘三问The three review questions

  1. 按计划做了什么？数一数：表上几个时段，用了几个。用了的那次，学到哪里了。What went to plan? Count: how many slots on the timetable, how many used. In the ones you used, how far did you get.
  2. 没做什么？没用上的时段，写下那一刻你在做什么——不是“我忘了”，而是“那天晚饭后表哥来了，我们打了两小时游戏”。What didn't? For each missed slot, write what you were doing at that moment — not “I forgot”, but “my cousin came over after dinner and we gamed for two hours”.
  3. 挡住我的是什么？把第 2 问的答案归一下类：是时间不对（那个时段总有事）？目标太大（一节课 40 分钟，我只有 30）？没有开关（到点了没反应）？还是内容卡住了（第 2 节完全听不懂）？What got in the way? Sort your answers to question 2: wrong time (that slot always collides), goal too big (a lesson takes 40 minutes, I had 30), no switch (the time came and nothing happened), or stuck on content (lesson 2 made no sense)?

改一处：四种改法Change one thing: four kinds of change

只改一处。改得多，下周就不知道是哪一处起了作用。第 3 问的答案是哪一类，就对应哪一种改法：

Change one thing only. Change several and next week you won't know which one worked. Match the kind of change to your answer to question 3:

挡住我的是What got in the way改法The change理由长这样The reason looks like
时间不对Wrong time换时间——挪到一个更稳的白格子Move the slot to a steadier white square“周三晚饭后两次都被作业挤掉了，改到周六早上 9 点，那个时段这两周都空着。”“After dinner on Wednesday got squeezed out by homework both times; moved to Saturday 9 a.m., which was empty both weeks.”
目标太大Goal too big缩小目标——改成菜单标注的子集Shrink the goal to the menu's named subset“每节要 40 分钟，我一次只有 30，两次都没学完一节。改成只学模块 1，寒假前能完整学完。”“Each lesson takes 40 minutes; I have 30 per slot and never finished one. Changed to module 1 only, which I can complete before the break.”
一步太大One step too big拆更小——60 分钟拆成两个 30，或一节视频拆成“看 + 做小测”两次Split it smaller — 60 into two 30s, or one lesson into “watch” and “quiz” on two days“一坐一小时到 40 分钟就走神了。拆成周三、周六各 30 分钟。”“An hour in one sitting and my mind wandered at minute 40. Split into 30 on Wednesday and 30 on Saturday.”
没有开关No switch加一个流程触发——把它接在一件本来就会做的事后面（微课 3）Add a routine trigger — chain it to something you already do (micro-lesson 3)“到点了提醒响了，我在刷手机，就划掉了。改成收拾完饭桌就打开电脑，手机放到另一个房间。”“The reminder rang while I was on my phone and I swiped it away. Now: clear the table, open the laptop, phone in the other room.”
内容卡住Stuck on content换方法——读本第 09 章的“多语对照”（英文课，把那个概念用中文再学一遍，或反过来），或你在 M1 里选定的那个策略（提取、间隔、自我解释）Change the method — Ch. 09's “two languages side by side” (for an English course, learn that concept again in Chinese, or the other way round), or the strategy you chose in M1 (retrieval, spacing, self-explanation)“第 2 节的‘组块’听了两遍没懂。改成先看中文字幕版再看英文版，看完合上页面用自己的话写一遍。”“I listened to lesson 2 on ‘chunking’ twice and didn't get it. Now: Chinese subtitles first, then the English, then close the page and write it in my own words.”

如果挡住你的是内容（听不懂），那不是表的问题，先试第五种改法——换方法；仍不懂，看那门课自己的常见问题或论坛，再把问题写进日志第 2 行——导师每周读日志，会帮你想“怎么学”，但不会替你讲那门课。

If what stopped you was the content (you couldn't follow), the timetable is not the problem — try the fifth kind of change first, a different method; if still stuck, check the course's own FAQ or forum, then put the question in line 2 of your log — your mentor reads it weekly and will help with how to learn, not with teaching that course.

先判断：哪一段能得 6 分？Judge first: which one earns 6?

两位同学复盘同一周：表上周三晚饭后、周六上午各 30 分钟。先自己判断，再点开答案。

Two students review the same week: 30 minutes after dinner on Wednesday and 30 on Saturday morning. Decide for yourself before opening the answer.

A · “表上两个时段，我用了一个。周三没做。挡住我的是时间不对。我改了：以后周三一定按时做，因为我要更自律。”“Two slots on the plan, I used one. Wednesday didn't happen. What got in the way: wrong time. My change: from now on I will do Wednesday on time, because I need to be more disciplined.”
B · “表上两个时段，用了周六那个，学到视频 1.3。周三没做——那一刻我在写数学卷子，写到 9 点半。挡住我的是时间不对。我改了这一处：周三晚饭后改到周日上午 10 点，因为这两周周三晚上都有作业写到 9 点半，而周日上午两次都空着。”“Two slots, I used Saturday's and got to video 1.3. Wednesday didn't happen — at that moment I was doing a maths paper until 9:30. What got in the way: wrong time. The one thing I changed: Wednesday after dinner → Sunday 10 a.m., because both Wednesdays this fortnight had homework until 9:30, and Sunday morning was empty both times.”
答案Answer

B 得 6 分。A 是“发展中”（4 分）：有调整，但“我要更自律”是愿望，不是这一周发生的事；“以后一定按时”也没有落到日历上——表没变。B 的三问都答了，理由里有两件具体的事（作业到 9 点半、周日上午空着），改动是一个新的时段，截图能看出来。注意 B 并没有比 A 更努力——他只是看了自己的一周。

B earns 6. A is Developing (4): there is a change, but “be more disciplined” is a wish, not an event from this week, and “on time from now on” never reaches the calendar — the timetable is unchanged. B answers all three questions, the reason holds two concrete events (homework till 9:30, Sunday morning empty), and the change is a new slot a screenshot can show. Notice B is not trying harder than A — B simply looked at the week.

理由怎么写才算“有理由”What counts as a reason

不算Not a reason：“我会更努力。”“我要更自律。”“下周一定做到。”——这些是愿望，不是理由；它们没有指向这一周发生的任何一件事。“I'll try harder.” “I'll be more disciplined.” “Next week for sure.” — These are wishes, not reasons; they point at nothing that happened this week.
算A reason：“因为周三那次____（发生了什么），所以我把____改成____。”——一个理由里一定有一件具体的事。“Because on Wednesday ____ (something that happened), I changed ____ to ____.” — A reason always contains one concrete event.

不用跟别人比No need to compare with anyone

复盘不是检讨。你不用跟排满了表、每次都做到的同学比——你只需要跟上周的自己比：这周的表，比上周更像你真实的一周了吗？一步一个脚印，让下一周的自己是更好的自己，就够了。

A review is not a confession. You need not measure yourself against the classmate whose every slot got used — only against last week's you: is this week's timetable more like your real week than last week's? One footprint at a time, so that next week's you is a slightly better you — that is enough.
