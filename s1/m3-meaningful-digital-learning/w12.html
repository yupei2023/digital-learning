单元收尾（先合上页面写，再展开对照）Module wrap-up (write first with the page closed, then open and compare)

合上所有页面，写一句：我上次的计划（起点第 3 行）是因为四个原因里的哪一个停下来的；这一次的表，为它做了什么不同的事。写在日志第 3 行后面，一句就够，不用另发。写完再点开下面的要点对一对——不是看谁背得全，是看你自己的那句话里有没有把“上次”和“这次”接上。

Close every page and write one sentence: which of the four causes stopped my last plan (starting-point line 3), and what this timetable does differently about it. Put it after log line 3 — one sentence, nothing extra to publish. Only then open the points below and compare — not to see who remembers most, but to check whether your sentence connects “last time” to “this time”.

✅ 核心要点（写完再看）✅ Key takeaways (open after writing)

热情做成了一半，另一半是目标和计划；目标要 SMART，尤其要现实、有截止；计划只有写进真实的一周才算存在；专时专用，用一个固定动作触发它；没按计划做不是失败，是信息——改一处，说理由，再来；全按上了也是信息——说出撑住它的是什么，预备好下一周。

Passion is half the job; the other half is a goal and a plan. A goal must be SMART — above all realistic and with a deadline. A plan exists only once it is written into a real week. Protect the time and trigger it with a fixed routine. A slipped plan is information, not failure — change one thing, say why, go again; a plan that held is information too — say what held it, and be ready for next week.

10 · 常见问题10 · FAQ

我上周一次都没按表学。还能做复盘吗？I didn't use a single slot last week. Can I still do the review?

能，而且这正是复盘最有用的时候。诚实写“0 次”，然后回答：那几个时段我在做什么？改一处，说理由。量规看的是调整和理由，不是上周用了几次。

Yes — this is exactly when a review is most useful. Write “0” honestly, then answer: what was I doing in those slots? Change one thing, with a reason. The rubric scores the change and the reason, not how many slots you used.

我全按表做了，没什么要改的。Everything went to plan; nothing to change.

很好——按上了也是信息。两条路都达标，选一条：不改，但复盘里多写三样——是什么让它撑住的（指向具体的事：“周六早上家里没人”）、下周最可能被挤掉的时段是哪个（“周三，下周有月考”）、预案是什么（“挪到周四晚饭后”），预案写进日历那个事件的备注，新截图能看到；或者改一处让它更稳——这周哪一次最险些被挤掉？（“周三那次我 9 点 40 才开始，因为作业写到 9 点半。”）把它挪到更稳的位置或加一个流程触发，理由就是那件事。两条路都一样：“为了寒假前不掉链子”是愿望，不算理由；理由里要有这一周的一件事。

Good — a plan that held is information too. Two routes both meet the rubric; pick one. Keep it, but add three things to the review — what held it (something concrete: “nobody is home on Saturday morning”), which slot is most likely to get squeezed next week (“Wednesday — there is a test next week”), and your fallback (“move to Thursday after dinner”), written into the event's note in your calendar so the new screenshot shows it. Or change one thing to make it steadier — which session came closest to being squeezed out? (“On Wednesday I started at 9:40 because homework ran to 9:30.”) Move that slot or add a routine trigger; that event is your reason. Either way, “so it survives until the break” is a wish, not a reason — the reason must contain one event from this week.

“改一处”可以是改目标吗？Can the “one change” be the goal itself?

可以——比如从全课缩小到菜单标注的子集。但理由要指向这一周发生的事（“两次都没做完一节，因为一节要 40 分钟而我只有 30”），并同步更新日程。

Yes — e.g., shrinking from the full course to the menu's named subset. But the reason must point to what happened (“both times I couldn't finish a lesson: it takes 40 minutes and I have 30”), and the schedule must be updated to match.

期中自检的两句话是匿名的吗？Are the two midpoint sentences anonymous?

不是——它们写在你自己的日志里，导师会读。它们不计分，也不影响任何评价；写真话最有用。公开学习者写给自己就好。

No — they sit in your own log and your mentor reads them. They are ungraded and affect nothing; honesty is what makes them useful. Open learners write them for themselves.
