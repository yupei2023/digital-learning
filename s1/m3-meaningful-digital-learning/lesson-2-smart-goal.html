SMART 目标怎么写How to write a SMART goal

冲浪练习：过程与结果 · 约 4 分钟 · B 站 UP 主 _愚人金_（Coursera《学会如何学习》中英字幕转载）Process versus product (Learning How to Learn 3.1.4) · ~4 min · Bilibili uploader _愚人金_ · 在 B 站打开 ↗Open on Bilibili ↗

SMART 是五个英文单词的首字母。每一个字母，都是在问你一个问题。

SMART is five initials. Each letter asks you one question.

字母Letter它问你It asks对主修课来说For your core course
SSpecific · 清晰Specific到底要完成什么？不要太大，也别太小。What exactly will be done? Not too big, not too small.哪门课的哪一部分：全课，还是菜单标注的子集（如“模块 1–2”）。Which course, which part: the whole thing, or the menu's named subset (e.g., “modules 1–2”).
MMeasurable · 可测Measurable怎么知道做到了？How will you know it is done?能数的东西：多少节视频、几次小测、一个证书或徽章。Something countable: how many videos, how many quizzes, a certificate or badge.
AActionable · 可操作Actionable这周能做的第一个动作是什么？What is the first action you can take this week?“每周三、周六各 30 分钟”——是一个能排进日历的动作。“30 minutes every Wednesday and Saturday” — an action that fits a calendar.
RRealistic · 现实Realistic以你真实的一周，大概率做得到吗？In your real week, is it likely to happen?每周 1 小时、到寒假前——不是“每天 2 小时”。One hour a week until the break — not “two hours a day”.
TTimely · 有截止Timely哪一天算到期？By which day?“寒假开始前”。（公开学习者：换成一个你自己定的具体日期，见第 11 周常见问题。）“Before the winter break begins.” (Open learners: a concrete date you set yourself — see the Week 11 FAQ.)

别处你可能见到 A = Achievable（可达成）、R = Relevant（相关）。本课用“可操作”“现实”，因为目标要排进日历；五个问题的意思是一样的。Elsewhere you may see A = Achievable and R = Relevant. This course uses “actionable” and “realistic” because the goal has to fit a calendar; the five questions mean the same thing.

还有一个字母表里没有的问题，也许最重要：它为什么对我重要？不是对老师、对家长——对我。这一句写不出来，前面五个字母写得再漂亮，也撑不到寒假。

One more question, not in the acronym and maybe the most important: why does it matter to me? Not to a teacher or parent — to me. If you cannot write this line, the five letters above, however neat, will not last until the break.

同一个目标，两种写法One goal, two ways of writing it

模糊版Fuzzy
“这学期我要学好《学会如何学习》这门课。”“This semester I will do well in Learning How to Learn.”
达标版SMART
“寒假开始前，我要完成《学会如何学习》模块 1–2 的全部视频和两次单元小测（S、M、T）；每周三晚饭后和周六上午各学 30 分钟（A、R）。它对我重要，因为我背单词总是背了就忘，我想亲手试一次‘间隔练习’到底有没有用。”“Before the winter break I will finish all the videos and both unit quizzes of modules 1–2 of Learning How to Learn (S, M, T); 30 minutes after dinner on Wednesdays and 30 minutes on Saturday mornings (A, R). It matters to me because vocabulary never sticks for me, and I want to test for myself whether ‘spaced practice’ really works.”

注意达标版多出来的东西：一个能数的终点（视频 + 两次小测）、一个日期（寒假前）、两个能排进日历的时段，和一个只属于这个人的理由。

Notice what the SMART version adds: a countable end point (videos + two quizzes), a date (before the break), two slots that fit a calendar, and a reason that belongs to this one person.

先判断：哪一段能得 5 分？Judge first: which one earns 5?

两位同学写的目标，都是关于同一门中文课《学会学习——大学生学习指导》。先自己判断，再点开答案。

Two students' goals, both about the same Chinese course. Decide for yourself before opening the answer.

A · “这学期认真学完《学会学习》，每周都学，争取拿到证书。它对我重要，因为学习很重要。”“This semester I will seriously finish Learning to Learn, study every week, and try to get the certificate. It matters because learning is important.”
B · “寒假前学完《学会学习》第 1–4 讲的视频并通过每讲的测验（子集，约 4 小时）；每周日上午 10 点学 1 小时。它对我重要，因为明年选科我想选物理，想先看看自己能不能一个人把一门网课学下来。”“Before the break, finish the videos of lectures 1–4 of Learning to Learn and pass each lecture's quiz (a subset, ~4 h); one hour every Sunday at 10 a.m. It matters to me because I want to choose physics next year, and I want to see first whether I can get through an online course on my own.”
答案Answer

B 得 5 分。A 是“发展中”（4 分）：“认真学完”没有可测的终点，“争取拿到证书”不是自己能控制的结果，“每周都学”没有时段，“学习很重要”是套话。B 每个字母都能找到，理由是他自己的。注意 B 还做了一件聪明事：把目标缩小到子集——这不是偷懒，这是 R。

B earns 5. A is Developing (4): “seriously finish” has no measurable end, “try to get the certificate” is not an outcome the student controls, “every week” names no slot, and “learning is important” is boilerplate. In B every letter is present and the reason is the student's own. Notice B did something clever: shrinking the goal to a subset — not laziness, but R.

“为什么对我重要”怎么写Writing the “why it matters to me” line

读本第 04 章分过两种动机：外部动机（奖赏、证书、别人的认可）和内部动机（做这件事本身让我高兴）。两种都可以写，写真的那种就行。三个开头供你选：

Reader Ch. 04 separates two kinds of motivation: external (rewards, certificates, others' approval) and internal (doing the thing itself pleases me). Either is fine — write the true one. Three openings to choose from:

  • “因为我想亲手试一次____到底有没有用。”（好奇）“Because I want to test for myself whether ____ really works.” (curiosity)
  • “因为明年我想____，这门课是第一块台阶。”（目标）“Because next year I want to ____, and this course is the first step.” (a goal)
  • “因为上次____我没做到，这次我想看看换个方法行不行。”（不服气）“Because last time I failed at ____, and I want to see if a different method works.” (stubbornness)

AI 使用说明怎么写The AI use note

本单元是 ✅ 辅助。让 AI 帮你把目标改得更清楚是允许的——但“它为什么对我重要”那一句，AI 写不出来，因为它不是你。如果用了，文末加两三句：用了什么工具，它帮了什么忙，你采纳了什么、没采纳什么。没用就写“本文未使用 AI”。范例见 M1 微课 4。

This module is ✅ Assisted. Letting AI sharpen the wording of your goal is allowed — but AI cannot write the “why it matters to me” line, because it is not you. If you used it, add two or three sentences at the end: which tool, what it helped with, what you kept and what you didn't. Otherwise write “No AI was used”. Example in M1 micro-lesson 4.
