任务 1 · 我的学习计划Task 1 · My learning plan

一条目标 + 一张截图One goal + one screenshot中文或英文Chinese or English约 25 分钟（首次排表会更久）~25 min (longer the first time)

要做什么What to do

为你的主修外部课程写一条 SMART 目标，加一句“它为什么对我重要”；把从本周到寒假、每周 1 小时的学习时段排进你自己的日历，截一张周视图。发布到你网站的“作品集”页，标题写“M3 任务 1 · 我的学习计划”。然后本周就按这张表学第一次。

Write one SMART goal for your core external course, plus one line on why it matters to you; place one hour a week, from this week to the winter break, into your own calendar and take a week-view screenshot. Publish on your Portfolio page as “M3 Task 1 · My learning plan”. Then study your first hour by that timetable this week.

第 12 周你会回到这一页，在下面补一行“我的学习策略”，并追加一周复盘（任务 2）。所以这一页是活的——它要陪你到寒假。

In Week 12 you return to this page, add a “my strategy” line beneath, and append a one-week review (Task 2). So this page is alive — it stays with you until the break.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 目标写的是主修课或其指定子集，不是“这门课”或“学习”本身。The goal is about the core course or its named subset — not this course, not “learning” in general.
  • 五个字母都能在目标里找到；特别是可测的终点和截止日。All five letters are present — above all a measurable end point and a deadline.
  • 截图是周视图，能看到写着“主修课”的重复时段；其他事件改名“私事”或已涂掉；没有他人姓名。The screenshot is a week view showing a repeating “core course” slot; other events renamed “private” or blocked out; no one else's names.
  • 结尾附 AI 使用说明（或“本文未使用 AI”）。End with an AI use note (or “No AI was used”).

步骤Steps

  1. 用微课 2 的表格逐字母检查你的目标；用“先判断”那两段对照一遍。再对照你在跟做活动里为“看情况”那两张卡写下的“对我来说现实的条件”——你的目标满足它吗？主修课是英文的：你在第 8 张卡上写的那个条件，就是你目标里的 R。Check your goal letter by letter with micro-lesson 2's table; compare it with the two sample goals there. Then check it against the “realistic for me” conditions you wrote for the two “it depends” cards in the activity — does your goal satisfy them? If your course is in English: the condition you wrote on card 8 is the R in your goal.
  2. 按微课 3 的三步，在自带日历里建好重复事件，设提醒，截周视图。（公开学习者：“寒假开始那天”换成什么日期，见第 11 周常见问题。）Follow micro-lesson 3's three steps: create the repeating event in the built-in calendar, set the reminder, capture the week view. (Open learners: see the Week 11 FAQ for what date replaces “the start of the winter break”.)
  3. 截图隐私：其他事件改名“私事”或用标注工具涂掉；不要出现他人姓名或学校全名。Screenshot privacy: rename or block out other events; no one else's names, no full school name.
  4. 发布——三行做法，不依赖别的页面：① 打开你的网站编辑器，进入“作品集”页；② 新建一条，标题“M3 任务 1 · 我的学习计划”，粘贴模板并填好，插入截图；③ 点“发布”，复制这一页的链接。Publish — three self-contained steps: ① open your site editor and go to the Portfolio page; ② add an entry titled “M3 Task 1 · My learning plan”, paste and fill the template, insert the screenshot; ③ press Publish and copy the page's link.
  5. （在校学生）把链接登记给导师：登记表 → [网址将在开课信中公布]。要填三样：学生编号、任务编号（M3-1）、作品链接。(Enrolled) Register the link: Tracking sheet → [address in the welcome letter]. Three fields: student number, task number (M3-1), link.
  6. 按表学主修课第一次（60 分钟）。学完顺手在日志页下记一行：日期 · 用了 / 没用这个时段 · 学到哪儿了。写三行日志——从这周起写的是主修课。Do your first core-course hour by the timetable (60 min). Right after, add one line under your log: date · used / missed the slot · how far I got. Write the three-line log — from this week on it is about the core course.
