任务 2 · 一周复盘Task 2 · One-week review

追加在任务 1 那一页下面Appended under Task 1改一处，说理由One change, one reason约 30 分钟~30 min

要做什么What to do

回到你网站上“M3 任务 1 · 我的学习计划”那一页，在下面追加两样东西：

Return to “M3 Task 1 · My learning plan” on your site and add two things underneath:

  1. 把模板里空着的那一行填上：我为主修课选定的学习策略（来自 M1）——第 9 周短文里你说要多用的那一种（提取、间隔、自我解释……），或者你这两周真的在用的那一种。Fill the empty line in the template: the strategy I chose for my core course (from M1) — the one you said you would use more in your Week 9 essay (retrieval, spacing, self-explanation…), or the one you have actually been using these two weeks.
  2. 一周复盘：按计划做了什么、没做什么、挡住我的是什么、我改了这一处并说理由，附改过之后的日程截图。The one-week review: what went to plan, what didn't, what got in the way, the one thing I changed and why — with a screenshot of the revised schedule.

硬性要求Hard requirements

  • 三问都要答；“没做什么”要写那一刻你在做什么，不写“我忘了”。Answer all three questions; for “what didn't”, write what you were doing at that moment, not “I forgot”.
  • 只改一处，改法要和“挡住我的是”指向同一件事；理由里必须有一件这一周真实发生的事。“我会更努力”按“发展中”计。全按表做了的可以不改——但要写出：撑住它的是什么、下周最危险的时段、预案（预案写进日历备注）。Change one thing, and the change must match what got in the way; the reason must contain one real event from this week. “I'll try harder” rates Developing. If everything went to plan you may keep it — but write what held it, the riskiest slot next week, and your fallback (in the calendar note).
  • 改动必须落到日历上，新截图能看出来（换了时段 / 拆成两段 / 结束日期变了……）。The change must land on the calendar, visible in the new screenshot (slot moved / split in two / end date changed…).
  • 结尾附 AI 使用说明（或“本文未使用 AI”）。End with an AI use note (or “No AI was used”).

步骤Steps

  1. 对着上周的截图，先在草稿里答完第 12 周页面第 4 节的三句话。With last week's screenshot open, first answer the three sentences from section 4 of the Week 12 page in your draft.
  2. 用微课 4 的表格选一种改法——改法要对着“挡住我的是”那一栏——写出带“因为”的理由。全按表做了的，写撑住它的是什么、最危险的时段和预案。Pick one kind of change from micro-lesson 4's table — the change must face what got in the way — and write the reason with a “because”. If everything held, write what held it, the riskiest slot, and the fallback.
  3. 在日历里真的改掉它（挪时段 / 拆两段 / 改结束日期 / 在事件备注里写上换的方法或预案），重新截周视图，隐私处理同任务 1。Actually make the change in the calendar (move / split / new end date / the new method or the fallback written in the event note), re-capture the week view, same privacy handling as Task 1.
  4. 发布：① 打开你的网站编辑器，找到“M3 任务 1”那一条；② 填上“我的学习策略”那一行，在下方粘贴复盘模板并填好，插入新截图；③ 点“发布”，复制链接（还是同一页的链接）。Publish: ① open your site editor and find the “M3 Task 1” entry; ② fill the “my strategy” line, paste and fill the review template beneath, insert the new screenshot; ③ press Publish and copy the link (the same page's link).
  5. （在校学生）登记：登记表 → [网址将在开课信中公布]，任务编号 M3-2。(Enrolled) Register: Tracking sheet → [address in the welcome letter], task number M3-2.
