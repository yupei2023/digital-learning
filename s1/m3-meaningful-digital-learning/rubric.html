M3 量规M3 Rubric

量规的三行就是本单元的三条目标。在校学生由导师评分；公开学习者用同一张表自评。评级四级：达标 / 发展中 / 尚未达标 / 证据不足。

The three rows are the module's three objectives. Mentors score enrolled students; open learners self-score with the same table. Four levels: Meets / Developing / Not yet / No evidence.

目标（任务 · 分值）Objective (Task · Pts)达标Meets发展中Developing尚未达标Not yet证据不足No evidence
① 为自己的主修外部课程（或其指定子集）写出一条 SMART 学习目标，并说明它为什么对自己重要① Write one SMART goal for your core external course (or its named subset), and say why it matters to you
任务 1 · 5 分Task 1 · 5 pts		5 · 目标指向主修课（或子集）的完成；S / M / A / R / T 五项都能在句子里找到（有可测的终点和截止）；“为什么对我重要”一句是自己的理由，不是套话。Points at completing the core course (or subset); all five of S / M / A / R / T are visible in the sentence (a measurable end point and a deadline); the “why it matters” line is a real, personal reason.4 · 目标是主修课，但缺一项（常见：没有截止，或“学完”没有可测的终点）；或“为什么重要”是套话。About the core course but one letter missing (usually no deadline, or “finish” with no measurable end); or the “why” is boilerplate.2 · 目标模糊（“学好这门课”），或不是主修课，或缺两项以上。Vague (“do well in the course”), or not the core course, or two or more letters missing.0 · 未发布 / 打不开 / 不是本人所写。Not published / unreachable / not the learner's own.
② 用一个日程工具，把从本周到寒假的学习时段排进自己真实的一周，并如实记录这一周的执行情况② Using a scheduling tool, place your study slots from this week to the winter break into your real week, and keep an honest record of how the plan went for one week
任务 1 + 任务 2 · 4 分Task 1 + Task 2 · 4 pts		4 · 截图可见：写着“主修课”的重复时段、每周 1 小时（或两个 30 分钟）、从本周排到寒假、放在真实的一周里（能看到它避开了固定的事）；复盘或日志能说明这周用了哪些时段（0 次也算，只要诚实写明）。Screenshot shows a repeating “core course” event, one hour a week (or two 30-minute slots), from this week to the break, inside a real week (it visibly avoids fixed commitments); the review or log states which slots were used this week (zero counts, if stated honestly).3 · 时段有，但只排了一两周，或看不出是重复事件，或截图看不出放在真实的一周里；执行情况有提及但不具体。Slots exist but only for a week or two, or not visibly repeating, or the screenshot doesn't show a real week; execution mentioned but not specific.2 · 只有文字描述没有日程截图，或截图与目标不对应；执行情况没有说明。Text only, no screenshot; or the screenshot doesn't match the goal; execution not reported.0 · 未发布 / 打不开 / 截图含他人信息或违反隐私规则（先要求删除重发）。Not published / unreachable / screenshot contains others' information or breaks privacy rules (ask for removal and re-post first).
③ 对照计划复盘这一周，指出按计划做了什么、没做什么，做出至少一处有理由的调整③ Review the week against the plan — what went to plan, what didn't — and make at least one justified change
任务 2 · 6 分Task 2 · 6 pts		6 · 三问都答了（做了什么 · 没做什么 · 挡住我的是什么）；改了一处，改法与“挡住我的是”指向同一件事，理由指向这一周真实发生的事；改动已落到日程上（换方法的写进事件备注），新截图能看出来。或：全按表做了，说明了是什么让它撑住的（指向具体的事）、下周最可能被挤掉的时段和预案——预案写进日历备注，新截图能看到。All three questions answered (what held · what didn't · what got in the way); one change that matches what got in the way, with a reason that points to what actually happened this week; the change is on the calendar (a change of method goes in the event note) and visible in the new screenshot. Or: everything went to plan, and the review says what held it (something concrete), which slot is most at risk next week, and the fallback — written into the calendar note and visible in the new screenshot.4 · 有调整但理由笼统（“更努力”“更自律”），或理由具体但改法不对着原因（内容卡住却去换时段），或改动没有落到日程上，或复盘只答了一两问；不改的人只写了“撑住了”，没有最危险的时段和预案。A change with a vague reason (“try harder”, “be more disciplined”), or a specific reason but a change that does not face the cause (stuck on content, yet the slot was moved), or a change not carried onto the calendar, or only one or two questions answered; or a plan kept with “it held” but no at-risk slot and no fallback.2 · 只有汇报（“这周学了两次”），既没有调整，也没有说明为什么不改；或调整与这一周的事无关。A report only (“studied twice this week”), with neither a change nor a reason for keeping the plan; or a change unrelated to the week.0 · 未发布 / 打不开 / 不是本人的一周。Not published / unreachable / not the learner's own week.

规范要求（不计分，但缺少则该任务记“证据不足”）Form requirements (a gate, not points — missing means “No evidence”)

  • 作品发布在自己的网站“作品集”页，链接可打开；在校学生已登记链接。Published on the learner's Portfolio page and reachable; enrolled students registered the link.
  • 当周三行日志已写（第 11–14 周写的是主修课）。The week's three-line log is written (Weeks 11–14 are about the core course).
  • 使用 AI 者附了 AI 使用说明；明显由 AI 代写的复盘按学术诚信规则处理。AI users added a use note; a review clearly written by AI falls under the integrity policy.
  • 截图规范：不含他人姓名或信息；课程以外的事件已改名或遮住。Screenshot rules: no one else's names or information; non-course events renamed or covered.

合成How the 15 points add up

任务 1 = 第 ① 行（5 分）+ 第 ② 行（4 分）= 9 分；任务 2 = 第 ③ 行（6 分）；单元合计 15 分。第 ② 行在第 12 周复盘登记后再评（“执行一周”的证据在复盘里）。导师在登记表上分别记两项任务的分数与等级，并在一周内邮件反馈。Task 1 = row ① (5) + row ② (4) = 9; Task 2 = row ③ (6); 15 in total. Row ② is scored after the Week 12 review is registered (the evidence for “follow it for one week” lives there). Mentors record both tasks' scores and levels on the tracking sheet and email feedback within a week.
