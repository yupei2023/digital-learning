2 · 导师是谁，不是谁2 · What a mentor is — and is not

导师的工作，一句话The job, in one sentence
每周打开自己那 30 位学生的网站，按量规给分，一周内把一封具体的、能照着做的反馈邮件送回去；当周就联系没出现的人。Every week: open your 30 students' websites, score by the rubric, send each one a specific, actionable feedback email within a week — and contact anyone missing that same week.

课程材料里从头到尾只用导师这个词，给学生和家长的文字里也只用它。原因有三个，每一个都改变做法：The course says mentor everywhere, and only that word is used with students and families. There are three reasons, and each one changes what a mentor actually does:

不是什么Not this 是什么This 对做法的影响What changes
不是老师Not a teacher 课程内容是页面教的。每一页都写全了目标、材料、任务、量规、自评——一个没有老师的人也能学完。The pages teach the content, completely enough for someone with no teacher: objectives, materials, task, rubric, self-review. 学生问“这一节讲的是什么意思”，导师指回页面（哪一节、哪张卡），不重讲一遍。Asked “what does this lesson mean”, a mentor points back at the page — which micro-lesson, which card — instead of re-teaching it.
不是助教Not a TA 导师不是教师的手，不替教师批一堆东西。他是这 30 个人每周固定见到的那一个人。A mentor is not the instructor's extra pair of hands. They are the one person these 30 students reliably meet each week. 反馈里要有“我读了你写的那一句”。批量、模板、群发式的评语，在这门课里是失败的反馈。Every reply must show that this student's own words were read. A batch reply, however polished, counts as a failed reply here.
不是监工Not an enforcer 没有迟交处罚，也没有催交。学生落下了，导师做的是确认没有障碍，不是施压。There is no late penalty and no chasing. When someone falls behind, the mentor checks for obstacles, not for compliance. “这周没登记，是遇到什么卡住了吗？”——永远是这句，不是“请尽快补交”。Always “did something get in the way?”, never “please submit as soon as possible”.
一个比方A metaphor
在这门全程异步的课里，导师是每周准时亮一次的那盏灯——学生不一定每周都需要他，但需要知道灯每周都会亮。
这个比方不准的地方：灯只管照亮，不作判断；而导师还要打分。所以每封回信的顺序是先看见人，再报分数——分数放在第三位，不是第一位。In a fully asynchronous course, the mentor is the light that comes on at the same hour every week — students may not need it weekly, but they need to know it will be on.
Where the metaphor breaks down: a light only illuminates, it does not judge, and a mentor does score. So every reply goes person first, score third — never score first.

Three things eat it, and there are three countermeasures: read only what the rubric names (there is plenty else on a student's site — skip it); always start from the phrase bank, never from a blank page; and stop at fifteen minutes on any one student, log it for Sunday, move on — the mentor is not the only remedy. Also spread the thirty replies over two or three evenings: the twenty-fifth reply written in one sitting shows its fatigue.

一个比方A metaphor
规范要求是门，量规行是分。门只管你能不能进屋，屋里的东西才计价。
这个比方不准的地方：真的门关上就得等下一次，而这里的门可以反复推——学生补好之后随时补评，分数随时可以往上改。“随时补评”这四个字要写进每一封“发展中”的回信。Form requirements are the door; rubric rows are the goods inside. The door only decides whether you get in; only what is inside carries a price.
Where the metaphor breaks down: a real door shuts you out until next time, while this one can be pushed open again at any moment — fix it and the work is re-scored, upward, whenever that happens. “I'll re-score whenever you fix it” belongs in every Developing reply.

全课程最硬的一条：诚实路线同样满分The course's hardest rule: the honest route also earns full marks

学生认真做了、如实报告“我查了，没有发现”，与“我发现了三处”同样是满分。An honest “I checked and found nothing” earns exactly the same full marks as “I found three things”.

这条规矩在每个单元里都长出具体的样子：“我对照了两个翻译工具，没发现明显走样”是满分；“我找遍了设置，这个平台没有两步验证”是满分；“表上排了 4 个时段，我一个都没用上”，接着改一处并说明理由，也是满分——那一行评的是如实记录 + 有理由的调整，执行次数根本不是评分项。The rule takes a concrete shape in every module: “I compared two translation tools and found no clear distortion” is full marks; “I searched the settings and this platform has no two-factor option” is full marks; “I put four slots on the calendar and used none of them”, followed by one justified change, is also full marks — that row scores honest recording plus a reasoned adjustment, and the number of sessions completed is not a criterion at all.

为什么这条必须硬：如果只有“有发现”才拿满分，那么英语弱的学生会去编造一个译错，找不到两步验证开关的学生会写“已开启”，什么都没做的学生会编一次复盘。一个学生绝不能因为编造一个“发现”而得利——这就是这条规矩的全部意义。Why it has to be absolute: if only “findings” earn full marks, the weaker reader invents a translation error, the student whose platform has no two-factor switch writes “enabled”, and the student who did nothing invents a review. No student may ever profit from inventing a finding. That is the whole point.

反过来同样成立：“都会了”“都设置好了”“都很顺利”这类无差别的完美报告，不是满分，而是看不出盘点。分界线从来不是“有没有发现”，是看不看得见核对的动作——工具、过程、最可疑的那一处，三样在不在。The reverse holds too: a uniformly flawless report — “all done”, “all fine”, “I'm good at all six” — is not full marks, because no stocktaking is visible. The line was never “did they find something”; it is can I see the checking happen — the tool, the process, and the closest suspect, all three present or not.

三条容易记反的口径Three things people get backwards

  1. 写得笨拙不是问题，写得“太好”才需要核对。朴素、句子磕绊、问题幼稚——都不扣分。只有与该生平时文风差距悬殊的流畅文本，才启动第 7.8 条的询问流程。Clumsy writing is fine; unusually polished writing is what prompts a check. Plain sentences, awkward phrasing, naive questions — none of these lose points. Only prose far out of line with that student's usual voice starts the conversation in 7.8.
  2. 🚫 独立任务评的是投入与格式规范，不评文笔、不评英语、不评审美、不评观点。模板普通、版式笨拙、立场导师不认同——都不影响分数。🚫 Independent tasks score engagement and correctness of form — never style, English level, taste, or opinion. A plain template, an awkward layout, a position the mentor disagrees with: none of it moves the score.
  3. 家长在旁陪同不算代做。一起设置账号、一起挑时段、一起看菜单，全部正常。判断标准是“这段文字是不是这个学生写的”，不是“有没有人在旁边”。A parent sitting alongside is not doing the work. Setting up an account together, choosing time slots together, reading the menu together — all normal. The test is whose words these are, not whether someone was in the room.

5 · 怎么写反馈5 · How feedback is written

① 我看到了什么（具体，最好引用学生自己的一句原话）→ ② 为什么是这个等级 → ③ 一个下一步。① What I saw (specific — quote their own words) → ② why this level → ③ one next step.
  • 长度：3–5 句。第 2–7 周（数字基本功）写 4–6 句，因为那六周里导师的回信本身就是学生的第二份“规范邮件”范文——主题、称呼、正文、结尾、署名、附件提及，六个部位一个不缺地写出来。Length: three to five sentences; four to six in Weeks 2–7, where the reply doubles as a worked example of a well-formed email — subject, greeting, body, closing, signature, and a mention of the attachment, all six parts present.
  • 语言：一个 15 岁的人读完就能动手。不用“建议进一步深化”“论证有待加强”这类话。Language: a fifteen-year-old must be able to act on it. No “consider deepening your analysis”.
  • 下一步只给一个，并且带上时间感：“五分钟的事”“十分钟补得完”。Exactly one next step, with a sense of scale: “five minutes”, “ten minutes and it's done”.
  • 每一封都以“随时补评”收住（除非已经满分）。学生知道分数还能往上走，才会真的回去改。Close every non-full-marks reply with “I'll re-score whenever you fix it”. Students go back and fix things only when they know the score can still move.
  • 每封回信至少引用学生自己的一句原话。这门课没有讨论区、没有互评——导师是唯一会读学生作品的人。那一句引用比任何“做得好”都有效。Every reply quotes at least one of the student's own sentences. With no forum and no peer review, the mentor is the only reader; that single quote outperforms any amount of praise.

第 2–7 周还有一个固定的四段结构：① 称呼化名 + 肯定一处具体的动作 → ② 隐私检查结果（通过也要说）→ ③ 分数（①/3 + ②/2 = /5）+ 一条建议 → ④ 一句对下周的期待。“通过也要说”这一条不要省：一个学生第一次把东西放到互联网上，“我检查过了，可以放心”这句话本身就是他需要的。Weeks 2–7 also have a fixed four-part anatomy: ① their pseudonym plus one specific thing they did → ② the privacy result, stated even when clean → ③ the score (①/3 + ②/2 = /5) and one suggestion → ④ one line about next week. Never skip the clean privacy result: for a student's first public page, “I checked, you're fine” is the sentence they need.

6 · 导师不做的事6 · What a mentor never does

这一节是硬边界。每一条都能在课程文件里找到出处，也都能在一个周二晚上做到。学生和家长同样有权知道这张表——它划的是导师的权限，不是学生的义务。These are hard boundaries. Each traces back to a course document, and each is doable on a Tuesday evening. Students and families are entitled to this table too: it limits what a mentor may do, not what a student must do.

不做Never 具体是什么意思What it means 那么做什么Instead
不替学生做Never do the work 不替写邮件、不替建站、不替改页面、不替做回译、不替装软件、不替选来源、不替挑作品、不替选课、不替远程操作设置。Not writing the email, not building the site, not editing the page, not doing the back-translation, not installing the software, not picking the sources, not choosing the portfolio pieces, not choosing the course, not operating anyone's settings remotely. 指回微课、讲义、任务页上的公式；用“截图 + 三句话”教他怎么描述问题。Point at the micro-lesson, the handout, the formula on the task page; teach the screenshot-plus-three-sentences way of describing a problem.
不评判家庭Never judge a family 不评设备条件、不评家里有没有电脑、不评家长要不要保管密码、不评家庭作息、不评“为什么没人帮你”。No comment on the equipment, on whether there is a computer at home, on whether parents hold the passwords, on the household's hours, or on “why is nobody helping you”. 只坚持课程的规矩（例如“密码不进网络”），其余是家事：“这是你们家自己的决定。”Hold the course's own rule (for example, “passwords never go online”); the rest belongs to the family: “that's your family's call.”
不碰密码Never touch a password 任何情况下不索要、不保管、不“帮忙记”学生的密码、验证码、恢复代码。排障只看打码后的截图。Under no circumstances asking for, storing, or “keeping a note of” a student's password, verification code or recovery code. Troubleshooting uses redacted screenshots only. 这正是第 5 周教的骗局姿势——导师自己就是那一课的示范：“以后任何人（包括我）要你的密码，都不给。”This is exactly the posture Week 5 teaches, and the mentor is the live demonstration: “from now on, anyone who asks for your password — me included — gets nothing.”
不给外部课程背书Never endorse a course 不说哪门课更好、不当课程测评员、不替学生选主修课、不评价他选得对不对——反悔并写明理由的与维持原判的同分。Not saying which course is better, not reviewing courses, not choosing a student's main course, not judging their choice — changing your mind with a stated reason scores the same as sticking with it. 帮“怎么学”，不帮“学什么”。内容问题一律：“先看那门课自己的常见问题或论坛。”然后给学习方法那一半。Help with how to learn, never with what that course teaches. Content questions get “start with that course's own FAQ or forum”, and then the study-method half.
不追自愿项Never chase a voluntary item 学习状态自测的结果表是自愿提交的。不发的学生不提醒、不追问、不记差。选做录音同样。The self-check result table is voluntary. Students who don't send it are not reminded, not asked, and not marked down. The same goes for the optional recording. 收到就在登记表打 ✓，与成绩分开保存；没收到什么也不做，也不在回信里提。Tick it on the sheet if it comes, stored apart from grades; if it doesn't, do nothing and say nothing.
不把迟交当扣分Late is never a deduction 课程没有迟交政策，量规里没有这一项。周日只是“本周完成”的参考点，不是闸门。There is no late policy and no such rubric row. Sunday is a reference point for “done this week”, not a gate. 备注栏记登记日期，照常评分、照常反馈。Note the date; score and reply as normal.
不评自测内容Never comment on self-check content 自测答了什么、分数高低、变高变低——不看、不评、不与成绩并置、不在任何场合引用。What a student answered, how high or low, whether it went up or down — not read, not commented on, never set beside grades, never quoted anywhere. 学生问“我答得不好怎么办”，固定口径：“没有对错，不计分，不影响任何评分；照实答最有用。”Asked “what if I answered badly”, the standing line is: “there are no right answers, it carries no points, it affects no score — answering honestly is what makes it useful.”
不当标准答案库Never be the answer key 学生来源里的知识性错误不是评分对象；快捷键、云盘选哪个、软件怎么装，指回微课。Factual errors inside a student's sources are not what is being scored; keyboard shortcuts, which cloud drive, how to install something — all point back at the micro-lesson. 明显危险的信息（健康偏方一类）可温和提示“用一眼三看再看它一次”，并说一类更可靠的来源——不代查。For plainly dangerous material (folk health remedies and the like), a gentle “run it through the three-look check again” plus a category of more reliable source — never looking it up for them.
不做证书Never issue certificates 总分表和证书名单由导师算、导师报；证书由教师签发。Mentors compute and submit the totals and the certificate list; the instructor signs and issues the certificates. 学生问证书什么时候到：“收尾日之后的下一批发放。”Asked when a certificate arrives: “in the next batch after the closing date.”
不预告后面还没开放的内容Never preview material that has not opened yet 课程材料是按周开放的，有些内容要到第二学期才展开。见第 8 节。Course material opens week by week, and some of it only opens in Semester 2. See Section 8. 用第 8 节里那几句现成的答法；拿不准现在能不能说的，先问教师，再回答。Use the ready-made answers in Section 8; when unsure whether something can be said yet, ask the instructor before answering.
不越过教师回答四类问题Four questions a mentor never answers alone 课程政策与数据使用中超出页面所写范围的问题、成绩申诉、家长投诉、无障碍便利安排。Questions about course policy or data use beyond what the pages already say; grade appeals; complaints from a parent; accessibility arrangements. 一律转教师，见第 9 节。All four go to the instructor — see Section 9.

7 · 遇到这些情况，导师会怎么做7 · If this happens, here is what a mentor does

下面十一种情况，课程早就为它们准备好了做法。把它们公开写出来，是为了让学生也知道：遇到了会发生什么，而不是会被怎么看。每一条的可抄用句子都放在引号里——它们是写给导师的模板，也是学生可以预期收到的口气。The course has a prepared response for each of the eleven situations below. They are published so that students know what will happen, rather than how they will be judged. Each ready-to-use sentence is quoted — a template for the mentor, and the tone a student can expect to receive.

7.1 链接打不开7.1 A link that won't open

这是技术故障，不是“没交”，在确认之前更不是“证据不足”。当天回信，附上导师看到的画面截图；最常见的原因是改完页面没有再点一次“发布”。修好再评，不算迟交。一周后仍打不开，才按“证据不足”记，并写进周日汇报。This is a technical fault, not a missing submission, and certainly not “no evidence” before it is confirmed. Reply the same day with a screenshot of what you see; the usual cause is a page edited and never re-published. Score once it is fixed; not late. Only after a week does it become “no evidence”, and it goes into Sunday's report.

“链接我这边打不开（截图附上）。最常见的原因是改完没有再点一次‘发布’——去编辑器里再点一次试试？还不行就把你看到的画面截图发我。修好随时告诉我，不算迟交。”“The link doesn't open on my side (screenshot attached). The usual cause is editing without pressing ‘publish’ again — could you try that? If it still fails, send me a screenshot of what you see. Tell me whenever it's fixed; it does not count as late.”

7.2 网站坏了、内容丢了7.2 A site that broke or lost content

先请学生不要重建——先截图。然后按第 7 周教的备份路线找回。找不回的部分，允许用截图加一句“原页面已失效”顶上，照常评分。这不是学生的错，不扣分。同一周多个学生出问题，当天报教师，可能是平台问题。Ask them not to rebuild yet — screenshot first. Then recover using the backup route Week 7 taught. Anything unrecoverable may be replaced by a screenshot plus “original page lost”, and scores normally. This is not the student's fault and costs no points. Several students in one week means the instructor hears about it the same day; it may be the platform.

“先别急着重建——先把你看到的画面截一张图发我。第 7 周教过的那份备份还在吗？找回来的部分照常评分；实在找不回的，用截图加一句‘原页面已失效’顶上就好，这一行的分不受影响。”“Don't rebuild yet — send me a screenshot of what you're seeing first. Do you still have the backup from Week 7? Whatever comes back is scored as normal; for anything that doesn't, a screenshot plus ‘original page lost’ is enough, and the points for that row are unaffected.”

7.3 页面上出现了真名、正脸照片或学校全名7.3 A real name, face photo or full school name on a page

这是当天要办的事，优先于一切评分。当天回信，指明是哪一页、哪一处（学生自己常常找不到）；请他删改后重新点一次“发布”；不扣分——课程页面上就是这么写的；登记表记提醒日期，改好补 ✓；一周后仍未改，再提醒一次，同时报告教师（涉及未成年人隐私，教师可能需要联系家庭）。他人的信息——同学的名字、合影、聊天记录截图——同样处理，那是在保护他的朋友。Same day, ahead of all scoring. Reply that day, naming the page and the exact spot (students often cannot find it themselves); ask for the edit and a fresh “publish”; no deduction — the course pages say so; log the reminder date and tick it when fixed; if it is still there after a week, remind again and tell the instructor, who may need to contact the family. Other people's information — a classmate's name, a group photo, a chat screenshot — is handled the same way, and that protects their friends.

“一件要紧的小事：____页上出现了你的真名／一张能看到正脸的照片／学校的全名。请把它换成化名／换成风景或你画的头像／删掉，然后重新点一次‘发布’——这不扣分，是为了保护你。改好回我一句就好。改完用手机打开自己的链接看一眼，就当是‘访客的眼睛’那一步。”“One small but important thing: your real name / a photo showing your face / your school's full name appears on the ____ page. Please swap it for your pseudonym / a landscape or a drawing / delete it, then press ‘publish’ again — this costs you nothing; it protects you. Just reply when it's done. Afterwards, open your own link on a phone and look at it once, as the ‘visitor's eyes’ step.”

7.4 截图里露出了真实密码7.4 A real password visible in a screenshot

这是安全事故，不是扣分项。顺序不能颠倒：先让密码失效，再谈别的。当天回信；请学生现在就把那个账号的密码换掉；再把报告里那一处删掉、重新发布；不扣分；同日告知教师知悉；导师自己不保存、不转发那张截图，看完即止。A security incident, never a deduction. The order matters: kill the password first, everything else second. Reply the same day; ask them to change that account's password right now; then remove the image and re-publish; no deduction; tell the instructor the same day; and the mentor neither saves nor forwards that screenshot.

“有件事要今天就办：你的报告里出现了一个真实密码（在____处）。发布过的密码等于没有密码——请你现在就把那个账号的密码换掉，然后把报告里那一处删掉重新发布。这不扣分，也不是批评：处理它就是本周最真实的一次练习。办好回我一句。另外记一条规矩：以后任何人（包括我）要你的密码或验证码，都不给。”“Something to do today: a real password appears in your report (at ____). A published password is no longer a password — please change that account's password right now, then delete that part of the report and publish again. This costs no points and is not a telling-off: handling it is the most real practice you'll get this week. Reply when it's done. And one rule to keep: from now on, anyone who asks for your password or a verification code — me included — gets nothing.”

7.5 视频在大陆播不了7.5 A video that won't play from the mainland

这是课程的问题，不是学生的问题。课程承诺所有视频大陆可达、所有外部链接开课前实测过——打不开就是课程欠了学生一次。回信告诉学生：每段视频都有完整的文字版，只看文字版一样能学完、一样满分；请他说明是哪一段、什么设备、什么网络；当周记进周日汇报，教师换片源。This is the course's problem, not the student's. The course promises every video is reachable from the mainland and every external link is tested before launch — if it doesn't open, the course owes the student one. Tell them every video has a full text version and the text version alone completes the lesson at full marks; ask which video, which device, which network; and put it in Sunday's report so the instructor can replace the source.

“打不开不是你的问题——每段视频页面上都有完整的文字版，只看文字版完全能学完这一节，分数一样。方便的话告诉我是哪一段、用的什么网络，我这周报上去换片源。谢谢你帮全班踩了路。”“This isn't on you — every video page carries a full text version, and the text alone completes the lesson, at the same marks. If it's easy, tell me which video and what network you were on, and I'll report it this week so the source gets replaced. Thanks for scouting it for everyone.”

7.6 学生落后两周或更多7.6 Two or more weeks behind

落后不是纪律问题，是障碍问题；课程特意设了缓冲周就是为了补做。做法：先做减法——“先做本周的，再回头补以前的”，不要让他从落下的第一周开始爬；问一句开放的问题；从落下的几周里只挑一件最要紧的（通常是网站还没建好，或者外部课程还没选）；提出约 15 分钟视频；第二周的周一起报告教师。全程不提分数、不提证书、不提“再不交就……”。Falling behind is an obstacle problem, not a discipline problem; the buffer weeks exist for exactly this. Do the subtraction first — “this week first, the backlog after” — so they never have to climb from the first week they missed. Ask one open question. Pick just one thing out of the backlog that matters most (usually the site isn't built yet, or no external course has been chosen). Offer fifteen minutes on video. From the second Monday, tell the instructor. Never mention points, certificates, or consequences.

7.8 疑似 AI 代写或他人代做7.8 Suspected AI-written or ghost-written work

判断的方向要正：写得差不是证据；写得“太好”才需要核对。触发信号是不一致——文风与该生平时的日志、邮件差距悬殊。流程的顺序不能变：先问，不定性（一封温和的邮件，只问过程）；学生自认使用则按该单元评分表处理（多为该行 0 分），并报告教师；仍不确定则不自行裁定，把两边的材料交给教师——判定学术诚信不是导师的职权。全程不在回信里使用“作弊”“抄袭”“造假”这类定性词。Point the suspicion the right way: poor writing is never evidence; unusually polished writing is what prompts a check. The trigger is inconsistency with that student's own logs and emails. The order is fixed: ask first, never accuse — a gentle email about process only. If the student confirms, score per the module's sheet (usually zero on that row) and tell the instructor. If doubt remains, do not rule: hand both sides to the instructor, because integrity determinations are not a mentor's authority. The words “cheating”, “plagiarism” and “faking” never appear in a reply.

“想和你确认一下：这一篇（这组卡／这段说明）是你自己写的吗？本周的任务标注‘独立’，用自己的话写、写得朴素完全没关系——如果用了工具，告诉我，我们重来一次就好。回邮件说说你是怎么写的就行。”“I'd like to check something with you: did you write this piece (these cards / this note) yourself? This week's task is marked ‘independent’, and writing it plainly, in your own words, is entirely fine — if you used a tool, tell me and we'll simply do it again. Just reply and describe how you wrote it.”

7.9 家里不同意注册某个平台7.9 A family that will not consent to a registration

家长有权说不，没有任何任务值得让学生违背家里的决定。先谢谢学生告诉你，不追问理由；再找替代路线——课程为大多数场景都准备了：外部课程可以换成不需要注册的；云盘可以走“发给自己的邮箱 + 本地文件夹 + U 盘”的兜底路线（与用云盘同分）；工具可以换同一类里不用注册的那一件。只有建站平台没有替代（第 3 周整个单元建在它上面）→ 立刻转教师，由教师与家庭沟通。家长直接来信 → 客气地回执，具体政策一律转教师。A family may say no, and no task is worth a student going against that. Thank the student for telling you, without asking why. Then find the alternative — the course has one for most cases: external courses that need no sign-up; the cloud-drive fallback of email-to-self plus a local folder plus a USB stick (which scores the same as a cloud drive); a no-sign-up option in most tool categories. Only the website builder has no substitute (all of Week 3 is built on it) — escalate that to the instructor at once, who will talk to the family. If a parent writes directly, acknowledge warmly and send every policy question to the instructor.

“谢谢你告诉我，家里的决定我们完全尊重。____这一项有别的路：____。换了路线分数完全一样，课程本来就写了这一条。”“Thank you for telling me — your family's decision is fully respected. There is another route for ____: ____. The marks are exactly the same on the alternative route; the course says so itself.”

7.10 学生把自己锁在门外了7.10 Locked out of an account

按任务页常见问题的路径引导（忘记密码 → 恢复通道）；全程不索要任何密码、验证码；解决之后明确告诉学生不扣分。Walk them through the task page's FAQ route (forgotten password → recovery channel); never ask for any credential; and once it is resolved, say explicitly that nothing was deducted.

7.11 学生写下明显的困扰7.11 A student writes something that shows distress

只读不评，不追问，不承诺保密，不给心理建议。当天转教师，只转述学生的原话和出现的位置，不加导师自己的推测。见第 9 节。Read it, do not respond to it, do not probe, do not promise confidentiality, do not counsel. Same day to the instructor: the student's exact words and where they appeared, with none of the mentor's inference. See Section 9.

8 · 按进度开放的材料8 · Material that opens on a schedule

这门课的材料是按周开放的——课程说明页上写明了：每周一开放本周页面。有些内容要到第二学期才展开。这不是保密，是顺序：把后面的东西提前摊开，前面那一步就白做了。所有内容最后都会公开，一样不少。This course's material opens week by week — the course guide says so: each week's pages open on Monday. Some of it only opens in Semester 2. This is not secrecy, it is sequencing: spread the later material out early and the earlier step is wasted. All of it becomes public in the end, none of it withheld.

对导师来说，这变成三条很短的做法：For a mentor this comes down to three short rules:

  1. 按已经开放的页面回答，不预告后面的内容。Answer from the pages that are already open, and don't preview what comes later.
  2. 学生问到 AI，按《关于本课程与 AI》那一页回答，不超出那一页——三种标注、三条底线、认可工具清单，都在上面。When a student asks about AI, answer from the “About this course and AI” page and go no further — the three designations, the three hard rules and the approved-tool list are all there.
  3. 拿不准某件事现在能不能说，先问教师，再回答。多等一天没有代价。When unsure whether something can be said yet, ask the instructor before answering. Waiting a day costs nothing.

万一说漏了If a mentor slips
