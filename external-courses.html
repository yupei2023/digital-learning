外部课程菜单External course menu

这学期，你将真正在线学一门课。从下面的菜单里选两门：一门主修（学完，或学完标注的指定子集），一门探索（只学两三节）。主修课每周 1 小时，从第 11 周到寒假（寒假想继续就继续，不计分）；探索课总共不超过 1 小时。

This semester you will really learn a course online. Pick two from the menu below: one core course (finish it, or its named subset) and one explore course (two or three lessons only). The core course takes one hour a week from Week 11 to the winter break (continuing over the break is optional and ungraded); the explore course is under one hour in total.

“指定子集”是什么？有的课全部学完要十几个小时，每周 1 小时到寒假学不完，所以菜单为它标出了一个小一些的部分——比如“模块 1–2”。写学习目标（第 11 周任务 1）时，目标就指向这个子集的完成。

What is a “named subset”? Some courses take a dozen hours in full — more than one hour a week until the break allows — so the menu names a smaller part, e.g. “modules 1–2”. When you write your learning goal (Week 11, Task 1), the goal points at completing that subset.

📌 需要注册的平台，先告诉家长。“手机号”一列写明注册是否需要手机号（外部课程不受本课“只用邮箱”规则限制，但如实标注，供家长知情）。

📌 Tell your parents before registering on any platform. The “phone” column states whether sign-up needs a phone number (external courses are not bound by this course's email-only rule, but it is stated for parents' awareness).

所有链接标 待实测：开课前会从大陆网络（非校园网、无 VPN）逐一测试能否打开、能否播放第一节视频；打不开请邮件告诉我们。本学期菜单不含人工智能主题的课程。

Every link is marked to verify: before launch each is tested from a mainland connection (no campus network, no VPN) for opening and playing the first video; tell us if one fails. No AI-themed course is on this semester's menu.

自选菜单外的课Choosing a course not on the menu

可以，只要它：免费；大陆能打开；不是人工智能主题（本学期）；时长在上限内（主修 ≤ 8 小时，或指定一个子集并写明小时数）；注册方式清楚。第 7 周前在网站“我的外部课程”页写明理由；在校学生由导师确认。

Yes — as long as it is free, opens from the mainland, is not AI-themed (this semester), fits the caps (core ≤ 8 h, or a named subset with hours stated) and has a clear sign-up route. Write your reasons on your “My external course” page by Week 7; enrolled students get their mentor's confirmation.

时长上限Caps

  • 主修课 ≤ 8 小时：第 11–14 周每周 1 小时（4 小时）+ 第二学期第 2–3 周每周 30 分钟（1 小时）保证在预算内；寒假 ≤ 3 小时选做。不打算用寒假的同学，选 ≤ 5 小时的课或子集。Core ≤ 8 h: 1 h a week in Weeks 11–14 (4 h) + 30 min a week in Semester 2 Weeks 2–3 (1 h) inside the budget; ≤ 3 h optional over the break. If you don't plan to use the break, choose a course or subset of ≤ 5 h.
  • 探索课 ≤ 1 小时，只学两三节。Explore ≤ 1 h, two or three lessons.
  • 每周总学习时长（本课 + 外部课程）≤ 2 小时。Weekly total (this course + external) ≤ 2 hours.

这门课怎样用到它Where the course uses it

  • 第 6 周：浏览至少三门课的介绍页，挑两门各试学 20 分钟。第 7 周：在网站上新建“我的外部课程”页，写三段——选了哪两门、为什么、每周打算什么时候学。Week 6: browse at least three course pages and sample two for 20 minutes each. Week 7: create a “My external course” page on your site — which two, why, and when each week.
  • 第 11–12 周（M3）：为主修课写 SMART 目标、排日程、执行一周后复盘。→ M3Weeks 11–12 (M3): a SMART goal for the core course, a schedule, a one-week review. → M3
  • 第 11–14 周：每周 1 小时按表学；三行日志写的是主修课。内容问题先看那门课自己的常见问题或论坛；仍不懂，写进日志，导师每周都读。Weeks 11–14: one hour a week by your plan; the three-line log is about the core course. Content questions: the course's own FAQ or forum first; if still stuck, log it — your mentor reads it weekly.
  • 第 15–16 周：作品集里写一段主修课进度和一件作品。第二学期：用五个标准评鉴主修课与探索课，写“给下一位学习者的推荐”；证书或徽章入年度作品集。Weeks 15–16: portfolio entry on progress and one artifact. Semester 2: evaluate both courses with the Five Criteria and write a recommendation for the next learner; certificates or badges join the year portfolio.
