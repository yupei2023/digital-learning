## 一、我们请您做三件事
## 1. Three things we ask of you

1. **每周守住两个约一小时的时段。** 写进家里的日历（例如周三晚上、周六上午），那两小时不派家务、不安排走亲访友。异步课程最容易被"随时都可以做"吃掉。**Protect two roughly one-hour slots each week.** Put them in the family calendar — Wednesday evening and Saturday morning, say — and keep errands and visits out of them. Asynchronous courses are most often eaten by "you can do it anytime".
2. **每周聊五分钟，问过程，不问分数。** 孩子每周会写三行日志：学到了什么 · 哪里卡住了 · 下周怎么办。就这三行聊。请不要问"做完了吗""得了几分"。**Talk for five minutes a week — about the process, not the score.** Each week your child writes three lines: what I learned · where I got stuck · what I'll do next. Talk about those. Please avoid "Are you done?" and "What did you score?"
3. **不替孩子做作业，也不替孩子写邮件。** 陪着他、听他讲、当他的第一位读者，但让作品和邮件出自他自己的手。**Do not do their work, and do not write their emails.** Sit with them, listen, be their first reader — but let the work and the email be theirs.

**加分项：**在注册账号和隐私设置这两件事上做孩子的搭档——注册前看一眼是哪个平台；网站发布后陪他检查一遍有没有真实姓名、正脸照片、学校全名。

**A bonus:** be your child's partner on two things — account registration and privacy settings. Glance at the platform before they sign up, and after they publish, check the site together for a real name, a face photo, or a full school name.

## 二、这是一门什么课
## 2. What this course is

这门课教的不是某一款软件，而是**怎样学习**——尤其是怎样在线上、独自一人的情况下把学习做成。

This course does not teach a piece of software. It teaches **how to learn** — especially how to make learning happen online, alone.

- **第一学期：站稳脚跟。** 写邮件、截图录屏、用翻译工具读英文、管好账号密码、检索并记录来源、搭好自己的数字工作台；然后是"什么是学习""什么是数字化学习""怎样让学习真正发生"。**Semester 1 — getting equipped.** Email, screenshots and screen recording, reading English with translation tools, accounts and passwords, searching and recording sources, a personal digital workspace — then what learning is, what digital learning is, and how to make it happen.
- **第二学期：判断、创造、自主。** 判断网上信息可不可信、管理数字足迹与数字健康、和家人商定家庭媒体公约、为下一届同学做一节微课，最后独立完成一个毕业项目。**Semester 2 — judging, creating, self-directing.** Weighing online information, digital footprint and wellbeing, a family media agreement, a micro-lesson for next year's students, and an independent capstone project.

## 三、这门课怎么运行
## 3. How the course runs

| | |
|---|---|
| **学制 Length** | 两个学期，每学期 16 个教学周。Two semesters, 16 teaching weeks each. |
| **形式 Format** | 异步在线自学——没有固定上课时间，没有直播。Asynchronous online self-study — no class meetings, no live sessions. |
| **周次 Weeks** | 只说"第几周"，不绑定日期；遇到法定节假日顺延，并设有缓冲周，不开新内容。Weeks, not dates; work rolls forward past public holidays, and buffer weeks carry no new content. |
| **每周时长 Weekly time** | **必做不超过 120 分钟**，连外部课程也算在内；"选做"不计入。**Required work never exceeds 120 minutes a week**, external course included; optional work is outside that budget. |
| **节奏 Rhythm** | 每周一开放当周页面。建议：周三前看完材料，周六前完成任务，周日前发布并写日志。Pages open Monday. Suggested pace: materials by Wednesday, task by Saturday, publish and log by Sunday. |
| **迟交 Late work** | **没有迟交处罚。** 落后了就先做当周的，再用缓冲周补。**There are no late penalties.** If they fall behind: do the current week first, catch up in a buffer week. |
| **个体学习 Individual** | 没有小组作业，没有讨论区，没有互相回帖。Every artifact is the child's own: no group work, no forum, no peer replies. |
| **导师 Mentors** | 两位导师，各负责约 30 名学生，我是其中之一。我每周查看进度、按量规评分、邮件反馈；哪一周没交，我当周就联系；也可预约一对一视频答疑。Two mentors, each supporting about 30 students; I am one. Every week I check progress, score by rubric, and reply by email; if work is missing I make contact that same week; 1:1 video is available by appointment. |

在线课程并不比线下轻松：它省掉通勤，换来的是对自我管理的更高要求。所以第一节的第一条最重要。

An online course is not the easy option: it saves the commute and asks far more self-management in return. That is why the first item in Section 1 matters most.

## 四、孩子的一周
## 4. Your child's week

每个教学周只有**一张网页**，这张页面本身就是这一周的学习。它固定包含：本周目标 · 预计用时 · 材料（文字加一段短视频）· 跟着做的小练习 · 本周任务与量规 · 可勾选的自评清单 · 不计分的自测题 · 三行反思日志 · 选做的拓展 · 建议节奏 · 常见问题。

Each teaching week is **one web page**, and that page *is* the week's learning. It always carries: objectives · time estimate · materials (text plus a short video) · a follow-along exercise · the week's task and rubric · a checkable self-review list · an ungraded self-quiz · the three-line reflection log · optional extras · suggested pace · FAQ.

视频每段不超过 5 分钟、配中英字幕，放在各地都能直接打开的平台上，**每段视频都另附一份完整文字版**——看不了视频也能学完。

Videos run under five minutes, carry bilingual captions, and sit on a host that opens directly wherever you are. **Every video has a full text version beside it**, so the week works even if the video does not.

第 3 周，孩子会建一个属于自己的个人网站，三个页面：**关于我 · 学习日志 · 作品集**。从那以后这个网站就是他的作业本。第二学期再加"项目"和"我的一年"两页。

In Week 3 your child builds a personal website with three pages: **About me · Learning log · Portfolio**. From then on that site is their notebook. Semester 2 adds *Project* and *My year*.

第一学期里，孩子还会从一份课程菜单中挑两门公开免费的在线课程真正去学：一门"主修"学完，一门"探索"只试两三节。主修课安排在学期后段，每周一小时，这一小时也算在那 120 分钟之内。**孩子在注册任何一个外部课程平台之前，我们要求他先告诉您。**

During Semester 1 your child also picks two free public online courses and actually takes them: one *core* course to finish, one *explore* course sampled for two or three lessons. The core course runs one hour a week later in the semester, inside the 120 minutes. **Before registering on any external platform, we ask your child to tell you first.**

## 五、作品怎么评，放在哪里
## 5. How work is assessed, and where it lives

每一件计分的作品都附一张量规。量规的每一行就是这个单元的学习目标，每一行分四级：

Every graded task comes with a rubric. Each row *is* one of that module's objectives, rated at four levels:

| 等级 Level | 含义 What it means |
|---|---|
| **达标 Meets** | 作品清楚地做到了这条目标。The work clearly does what the objective asks. |
| **发展中 Developing** | 方向对了，还差一点火候。On the right track, not there yet. |
| **尚未达标 Not yet** | 这条目标还没有做到。The objective has not been met yet. |
| **证据不足 No evidence** | 不是做得不好，而是作品里看不出来——通常漏了一步，补上就行。Not "poor work" but "nothing to read" — usually a missing step that can simply be added. |

作品**发布在孩子自己的网站上**，把那一页的链接登记给我；我按量规评分，一周内邮件反馈。每学期满分 100 分：**≥60 合格并获学期证书，≥85 标注"优秀"**，两个学期都合格的孩子另获年度证书。

Work is **published on your child's own website**, and its link registered with me; I score by rubric and reply by email within a week. Each semester is out of 100: **60 or above passes and earns the semester certificate, 85 or above is marked "with distinction"**, and passing both semesters earns the year certificate.

课程中还有几次**不计分的"学习自检"**，帮孩子看清自己的变化。它不进成绩，不影响证书，结果只显示在孩子自己的浏览器里。

The course also includes a few **ungraded "learning self-checks"**. They never enter the grade, never affect the certificate, and display only in your child's own browser.

## 六、隐私与安全
## 6. Privacy and safety

- **化名，不用真名。** 孩子在网站上使用英文名或化名。**Pseudonyms, not real names.**
- **不放正脸照片，不写学校全名**，也不发布任何他人的个人信息。**No face photos, no full school name**, and no one else's personal information.
- **网站默认"不被搜索引擎收录"。** 建站那一周我们会一步一步教孩子把这个开关关上：网站能通过链接打开，但搜不到。**The site is set to no-index by default.** We walk your child through that switch step by step: the site opens by link but does not appear in search results.
- **要不要公开发布，是第二学年末才出现的一个选项，并且必须有家长的书面同意。** 在那之前不存在"公开"这个动作。**Making the site public is only an option at the very end of Semester 2, and only with written parental consent.** Until then, going public is simply not an action that exists.
- **所有工具都免费，注册只需一个邮箱——不要手机号，不需要 VPN。** 这是选工具的硬规则。**All tools are free and sign up with an email address only — no phone number, no VPN.** This is a hard rule, not a preference.
- **AI 的使用有明确规范。** 第一学期不强制使用任何 AI 工具。课程规定：不得把 AI 生成的内容当作自己的作业提交；事实性内容必须另找可靠来源核实；**任何时候都不得向 AI 工具输入自己或他人的个人信息**。**AI use has explicit rules.** Nothing in Semester 1 requires an AI tool. AI-generated content may never be submitted as one's own; factual claims must be verified against a reliable source; and **personal information — your child's or anyone else's — is never typed into an AI tool.**
- **页面上的互动（打勾、自测、进度条）不需要任何账号**，进度只保存在孩子自己的浏览器里，我们不收集。**The interactive parts of each page require no account**; progress stays in your child's own browser and is not collected by us.

如果网站出了问题、内容丢失，或孩子在网上遇到任何让他不舒服的事，请立刻给我发邮件。**这类事情从来没有"小题大做"。**

If the site breaks, content is lost, or your child meets anything online that makes them uncomfortable, please email me at once. **On matters like these there is no such thing as overreacting.**

## 七、孩子卡住时，走这三步
## 7. When your child is stuck

1. **让他把问题说清楚**：一句话说明"我想做什么、我做了什么、结果怎样"，配一张截图。**Have them state the problem** — one sentence covering what they were trying to do, what they did, and what happened, plus a screenshot.
2. **让他自己给我发邮件。** 写邮件本身就是第一学期的正式内容。我在 24–48 小时内回复（节假日除外）。**Have them email me themselves** — writing that email is itself part of what Semester 1 teaches. I reply within 24–48 hours (holidays excepted).
3. **等回复的时候，让他先做下一件事。** 卡住的那一件放着，不是失败，是排队。**While waiting, let them move on.** A stuck item set aside is not a failure; it is an item in a queue.

**只有一种情况请您直接出手：涉及安全、隐私、金钱，或让孩子感到不安的事。** 请立刻联系我，不必等孩子自己开口。如果孩子出现明显反常——突然沉默、忽然拒绝把手机放在客厅——请先温和地问，然后尽快联系我。

**There is one case where you should step in directly: anything involving safety, privacy, money, or something that has upset your child.** Contact me at once; do not wait for your child to raise it. If you notice a marked change — a sudden silence, a flat refusal to leave the phone in the living room — ask gently first, then contact me promptly.

## 十、最后
## 10. In closing

这一年结束的时候，我希望您看到的不只是一个网站和两张证书，而是一个**能给自己定目标、能自己开口求助、卡住了会想办法的孩子**。

您扮演的角色比您以为的重要，也比您以为的轻松：不是当老师，不是当监工，只是当那个守住时间、认真听讲、并且忍住不替他动手的人。

Your part matters more than you think and asks less of you than you fear: not to be the teacher, not to be the supervisor, only to be the person who protects the time, listens properly, and holds back from taking over.
