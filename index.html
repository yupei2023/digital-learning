关于课程About the course

学习如何学习，也学习如何在数字世界中有判断地使用技术。从数字基本功出发，理解学习与技术，最后用作品和反思记录成长。按顺序学习，也可以从当下最需要的单元开始。课程公开、免费，面向各地的学习者。Learn how to learn — and how to use technology thoughtfully in a digital world. Begin with digital foundations, examine learning and technology, then document your growth through work and reflection. Follow the sequence or start with the module you need now. The course is public and free, open to learners anywhere.

一起支持自主学习Supporting independent learning, together

自主学习并不意味着独自承担。家长、学校管理者与导师各有不同的角色，也共享同一个目标：为学习者创造时间、信任与持续支持。Independent learning does not mean carrying everything alone. Parents, school administrators, and mentors play different roles while sharing one goal: creating time, trust, and sustained support for learners.
