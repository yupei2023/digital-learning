Module pages themselves say only “Week n”, never a date — dates live here and only here.

假期规则（先于任何日期）The holiday rule (which precedes any date)

  • 任何单元开放日、任务收尾日都不落在法定假日；落上即顺延到下一个周日。No opening or closing date falls on a statutory holiday; it rolls to the next Sunday.
  • 第一学期通常经过中秋、国庆、元旦；国庆周设为缓冲周，不开新单元。Semester 1 typically meets Mid-Autumn, National Day and New Year; National Day week is a buffer week with no new module.
  • 第二学期通常经过清明、劳动节、端午；清明与劳动节中至少一个设缓冲周。Semester 2 typically meets Qingming, Labor Day and Dragon Boat; at least one of Qingming and Labor Day becomes a buffer week.
  • 晚交不扣分；错过收尾日只推迟证书与期末反馈的发放。Late work loses no points; missing the closing date only delays certificates and end-of-semester feedback.
公开学习者不受这页约束Open learners are not bound by this page
公开学习者按自己的节奏、以“周”为单位推进，任何时候开始都可以；这页只服务在校学生的证书与反馈时间表。Open learners advance week by week at their own pace and may start any time; this page only serves enrolled students' certificate and feedback schedule.
